LESSON 03

Overall Structure

Describe how a text is built: what each part does, in order
描述一段文字是怎样组织的：每一部分在做什么，先后顺序如何
DIVIDESplit the text into two or three parts at its turning points.
LABELName what each part does with a function word: introduces, claims, describes, questions…
MATCH IN ORDERChoose the answer whose every part is true, in the right order.
What is it?

What is an Overall Structure question?

Definition

The question asks how the whole text is organized.

A typical structure:

  1. Presents a claim.
  2. Gives an example supporting that claim.

It is a Craft and Structure question: you are not asked what the text says, but how it is put together.

问的不是“说了什么”，而是“怎么组织的”。要把内容抽象成“功能”。

How often?

0–2 questions per test (about 1.5%). Not every module has one; when it appears, it comes right after the Words in Context questions.

The stem

Which choice best describes the overall structure of the text?

出现频率不高，但位置靠前，一般紧跟词汇题。

Content vs. function

From content to function

Structure answers never repeat the details of the text. They use general function words. Your job is to turn each part of the text into one of these.

The text says (content)The answer says (function)
“How deep these effects extend is unclear.”introduces an unresolved question
“Hypothesizing that … Okolo and colleagues sampled soils…”presents a research team’s hypothesis
“They found, though, that … carbon and nitrogen decreased to comparably low levels.”describes an observation that conflicts with the hypothesis
“I have just seen a most beautiful thing … A straight black cypress”describes a distinctive sight in nature
“Why, beautiful still finger, are you black?”ponders what meaning the sight has

Useful function words: introduces · presents a claim · describes · gives an example · explains a cause · raises a question · offers a hypothesis · reports a finding · contrasts · concedes · criticizes · speculates · concludes. 常用功能词：引出、提出观点、描述、举例、解释原因、提问、提出假设、报告发现、对比、让步、批评、推测、总结。

Two kinds of texts

Typical structures

Informational texts

  • question / problem → hypothesis → finding (supports or conflicts)
  • claim → example
  • common view → however → new view
  • phenomenon → explanation

科学/社科：问题→假设→发现；观点→例子；旧观点→转折→新观点。

Poems and stories

  • description → reflection or question
  • a criticism of the speaker → the speaker’s reply
  • an extended comparison (night = a woman)
  • past → present; observation → feeling

诗歌/小说：描写→思考；被批评→回应；通篇比喻；过去→现在。

How to approach it

Four steps for Overall Structure

1

Read the whole text for its point

Read the introduction line too. 完整读一遍，包括开头的介绍。

2

Divide it at the turning points

Look for but, however, though, yet, a new sentence that starts a new job, a question, or a change of time. Most texts have two or three parts. 在转折、提问、时间变化处切分，一般 2–3 部分。

3

Label each part with a function word

Write a short label: “claim,” “example,” “question,” “finding that disagrees.” 给每部分贴一个功能标签。

4

Check every part of every choice

Choices have two or three parts. One wrong part = a wrong choice. Check the order too. 选项每一部分都要对，顺序也要对，“半对”就是错。

Skill Builder

Think-aloud: give each sentence a job label, then join the labels

Read the text

Mini passage · teacher-written1For a long time, marine biologists believed that octopuses live alone and avoid one another. long-held view 2Recently, however, divers have observed groups of a dozen or more octopuses sharing the same small area of seafloor for months. new observation 3These observations suggest that some octopuses may be more social than scientists once thought. revised view

Which choice best describes the overall structure of the text?
A) It describes a debate, then explains why it cannot be settled.
B) It presents a long-held belief, then discusses observations that challenge it.
C) It compares two species, then recommends further study.
D) It describes an observation, then gives a method for testing it.

Think aloud · 边读边想

1 · Split the text Signal “Recently, however” splits it into two parts: S1 | S2–S3. 先用转折词把文章切成两段。

2 · Label each part (function, not content) S1 = a long-held belief. S2 = new observations that do not fit. S3 = what the observations suggest (a new view). 贴标签：旧观点 → 新观察 → 修正后的看法。

3 · Predict: “first …, then …” “First an old view, then evidence that challenges it.” 用“先……再……”说出结构。

4 · Test every part of every choice B ✓ both halves and the order match. A: no debate. C: one species, no recommendation. D: no method is given. 每个选项的前半和后半都要核对。

Skill Builder

Poems have structure too: look for the turn

Read the poem

Mini poem · teacher-writtenMy grandmother’s kitchen was always warm,
bread rising slowly by the window,
her radio humming songs older than me.
Now the house is sold, the window dark—
I wonder who stands there in the mornings.

Which choice best describes the overall structure of the text?
A) The speaker describes a place in the past, then reflects on what it is like now.
B) The speaker compares two kitchens, then chooses the better one.
C) The speaker describes a meal, then explains how to cook it.
D) The speaker criticizes her grandmother, then apologizes.

Think aloud · 边读边想

1 · Find the turn Lines 1–3 use past tense (“was”). Line 4 starts with Now: present tense. That is the turn. 时态和 Now 标出“转折点”。

2 · Label each part Lines 1–3: a warm memory of a place. Lines 4–5: the present (sold, dark) + a question. 前：回忆；后：现在 + 疑问。

3 · Predict “past description → present reflection.” 过去的描写 → 现在的思考。

4 · Test the choices A ✓. B: one kitchen, no choice. C: no recipe. D: no criticism, no apology — the tone is nostalgic, not critical. 诗歌常见结构：描写 → 思考/提问；过去 → 现在。

Quick Check

Quick check · Name the structure小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.S1: Many people think cats cannot be trained. S2: However, a shelter taught 30 cats to sit on command.
common view → evidence against it 常见看法 → 反驳证据
2.S1: Bees are important to farms. S2: For example, almonds depend almost entirely on bees.
claim → example 观点 → 例子
3.S1: The river kept flooding the town. S2: Engineers built a wall, and the floods stopped.
problem → solution 问题 → 解决
4.S1: Why do leaves change color? S2: In autumn, trees stop making the green pigment.
question / phenomenon → explanation 现象 → 解释
5.A poem first describes a storm, then asks what the storm means.
description → reflection / question 描写 → 思考
6.S1: Researchers predicted X. S2: They found, though, that Y.
hypothesis → finding that conflicts with it 假设 → 不一致的发现
7.A choice says “then praises the inventor,” but the text only lists facts about him. Keep or cut?
Cut: wrong attitude — no praise in the text. 原文没有赞美。
8.A choice is correct for sentences 1–2 but says “then proposes a solution,” which never happens. Keep or cut?
Cut: half right, half wrong = wrong. 半对半错 = 错。

Step 1 · Read the text and the question先读短文，再读题目在问什么

What is the question asking?

Worked Example

Step 2 · Find the evidence in the text回到短文，找到依据

1

Part 1 (lines 1–3)

Human activities affect carbon and nitrogen in soil, “though how deep these effects extend is unclear.” → an unresolved question.

2

Part 2 (lines 4–10)

“Hypothesizing that … differences … are not restricted to the topsoil layer,” Okolo’s team sampled soils. → the team’s hypothesis (and its test).

3

Part 3 (lines 10–12)

“They found, though, that … carbon and nitrogen decreased to comparably low levels beyond 30 cm.” Same low levels everywhere = no difference by land use → a finding that conflicts with the hypothesis.

4

Your summary

question → hypothesis → conflicting finding.

Step 3 · Test every choice逐个检验选项

A
CUT ✗
Parts 1 and 2 fit, but part 3 is wrong: the text never says the team refined its hypothesis.
B
KEEP ✓
unresolved question → hypothesis about it → an observation that conflicts with it. Every part matches.
C
CUT ✗
The text gives only one hypothesis, not competing hypotheses, and no hypothesis is shown to be correct.
D
CUT ✗
The data do not validate the hypothesis; “though” signals that they conflict with it.

Answer: B. The word though in line 10 marks the turn: the finding goes against the hypothesis. A and D fail because of the last part. 关键是第三部分的 though：发现与假设相反。

Worked Example 3

Form 3 · Module 2 · Question 7分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Overall structure”: give each part of the text a job label, then put them in order. 给每部分贴“功能标签”，再按顺序连起来。

2

Locate and paraphrase · 找关键句

S1: Mitchell uses her cover images “to emphasize ideas expressed in her music” — a general statement about her. S2 begins “For the cover of her album …”: one specific album. 第一句是概括性说法；For the cover of… 开始讲一个具体例子。

3

Predict · 预测

“First a general claim about Mitchell, then one example that supports it.” 预测：观点 → 例子。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

A “presents a claim about Mitchell, then gives an example supporting that claim” ✓ — both parts and the order match. 两部分和顺序都对上。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ The text never mentions Mitchell’s influence on other artists.
C ✗ The text describes a connection Mitchell feels to Van Gogh; it never notes a difference between them.
D ✗ Only one song (the title song) is discussed, and the cover is described before the song.

Answer: A. Sentence 1 is a general claim: Mitchell uses her cover images “to emphasize ideas expressed in her music.” “For the cover of her album Turbulent Indigo…” begins one example: the Van Gogh–style self-portrait emphasizes the title song’s ideas. → claim, then example. 第一句是总观点，For the cover of… 开始举例。

Wrong answers

How wrong structure choices are built

One part is wrong

The first part is right, the last is invented: “then refines the hypothesis,” “then validates.” Check the last part extra carefully.

前半对、后半错最常见，最后一部分要特别仔细。

Something that is not there

“competing hypotheses,” “a difference between them,” “other artists,” “a human behavior.” If you cannot point to it, cut it.

选项里出现原文没有的东西就排除。

Wrong order

The parts exist, but in the reverse order (“describes the songs, then the cover”).

顺序颠倒。

Wrong attitude

“regrets,” “boasts,” “concedes,” “questions the accuracy”: the verb gives the speaker an attitude the text does not show.

动词赋予了原文没有的态度。

Summary

What to remember about Overall Structure全文结构要点

01 · 识别题干

Stem: Which choice best describes the overall structure of the text? 0–2 per test, right after Words in Context.

02 · 关注功能

Think about function, not content: what does each part do?

03 · 划分层次

Divide the text at turning points (but, however, though, a question, a new step), usually into 2–3 parts.

04 · 逐段核对

Label each part, then check every part of every choice, in order. One wrong part makes the whole choice wrong.

05 · 核对顺序

Match the order of the answer to the order of the text. Reject a choice if even one part describes the wrong function.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
