Contrast clues

Contrast clues: find the opposite, then flip it

SignalExamples of signal words
Contrastbut, however, yet, although, though, while, whereas, despite, in contrast, on the other hand, unlike, instead, rather than
Correctionnot … but …, rather, in fact, actually: the second half corrects the first
A viburnum plant experiencing insect damage may develop erineum—a discolored, felty growth. A viburnum plant, on the other hand, will have leaves with smooth surfaces and uniformly green coloration.

How to use it

1. Find the idea on the other side of the signal: a damaged plant. 2. Flip it: not damaged. 3. Match: healthy (3-2-1).

先找转折另一边的意思，再取反。

Careful

Ask what exactly is being contrasted. Here it is damaged vs. undamaged, so “healthy” fits; “simple” or “beneficial” do not.

弄清楚对比的是哪一点。

Negation

Negative words change the direction

When a negative word stands next to the blank, decide first what the whole phrase must mean, then work out the blank.

TextWhole phrase must meanSo the blank means
It is by no means ___ to recognize Bosch’s influence; indeed, Banisadr himself cites Bosch… 4-1-4recognizing it mattersnot important → unimportant
Poet is an occupation which is not ___ in Delany’s work: nearly a dozen characters are poets. 4-2-5poets are commonunusual → atypical
The delays are unlikely to ___ her projection entirely but will extend its time frame. 2-2-3the projection survives, just laterdestroy it → invalidate
Jemisin declines to ___ the conventions…; readers like her willingness to thwart expectations. 3-2-3she breaks the conventionsfollow → conform to

Double negatives (by no means un-…, not atypical) are common in hard questions. Say the whole phrase in plain words first: “not atypical” = “typical.” 双重否定先翻译成肯定的大白话。

Most nearly mean

The second stem: “most nearly mean”

What it looks like

As used in the text, what does the word “trace” most nearly mean?

There is no blank. One word in the text is the target, and the choices are possible meanings. The word is usually familiar, used in a less common sense, often in an older literary text.

没有空格，问原文某个词在此处的意思。常考熟词僻义，多出现在文学选段里。

How to do it

  1. Cover the word and treat it as a blank.
  2. Predict a word from the context, as in Lesson 1.
  3. Put each choice in place of the word. Keep the one that fits this sentence, not the word’s most common meaning.

把目标词当作空格，用上下文预测，再把选项代回去。不要选这个词最常见的意思。

disputing every inch of our passage with wagons and baskets → not “arguing” but getting in the way (6-1-4).

How to approach it

Steps for contrast and negation questions

1

Read the whole text and circle signal words

but, however, on the other hand, rather; not, no, never, by no means, unlikely, declines, fails. 圈出转折词和否定词。

2

Find the clue on the other side

What does the text say on the other side of the contrast, or after the colon? 找转折另一侧或冒号后的线索。

3

Decide the meaning of the whole phrase, then the blank

Flip for contrast; flip again for each negative word. Write your prediction down. 先定整句意思，遇转折、否定各翻转一次。

4

Match and reread

Read the full sentence with your choice, including the negative word. Does it say what the text means? 连同否定词一起代回去读。

Skill Builder

Contrast think-aloud: find the other side, then flip it

Read the text · say “blank”

Mini passage · teacher-writtenAfter the six-hour climb, most of the hikers collapsed onto the grass, too tired to speak. Mina, however, seemed completely _______, as if she had just woken from a long nap.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word?
A) weary   B) refreshed   C) cautious   D) annoyed

Contrast rule: the blank is the opposite of the idea on the other side of however — but only on the point being contrasted (here: tired vs. not tired). 转折题：找到转折另一边的意思再取反，而且只在“被对比的那一点”上取反。

Think aloud · 边读边想

1 · Circle the signal however → Mina is different from “most of the hikers.” however 表明 Mina 与其他人相反。

2 · What is on the other side? The others were “too tired to speak.” 另一边：其他人累得说不出话。

3 · Flip it, and check with a second clue Opposite of very tired = full of energy. A second clue agrees: “as if she had just woken from a long nap.” 取反：精力充沛；后半句“像刚睡醒”再次确认。

4 · Match refreshed ✓. weary (= tired) describes the other hikers — a classic trap. cautious and annoyed are not about tiredness at all. weary 是“没翻转”的陷阱；另外两个与对比点无关。

Skill Builder

Negative words: decide what the whole phrase means

Words that flip the meaning · 否定/反向词

not / never / no 不 · hardly, scarcely 几乎不 · by no means 绝不 · far from 远非 · unlikely to 不太可能 · fail to 未能 · decline to / refuse to 拒绝 · lack 缺乏 · rarely, seldom 很少 · prefixes un- / in- / dis- 不

Two negatives = a positive. “not uncommon” = common. “hardly surprising” = expected. 双重否定 = 肯定。每遇到一个否定词就把方向翻一次。

Example 2The new rule is unlikely to _______ the problem entirely, but it should make it smaller.

1 · Whole phrase → blank The problem will still exist, just smaller. So the rule will not eliminate it. 问题仍在只是变小 → 不能“彻底消除”。

Think aloud · hardly + blank

Example 1The result was hardly _______: almost every scientist had predicted it years earlier.

1 · Whole phrase Everyone predicted it → the result was expected. 整句意思：在预料之中。

2 · Remove the negative “hardly ___” = expected, so ___ = surprising / unexpected. 去掉 hardly，空格就取反：令人意外的。

Example 3Snow is far from _______ in this mountain town: it falls on more than 150 days a year.

1 · Whole phrase → blank Snow 150 days a year → snow is common. “far from ___” = common, so ___ = rare / unusual. 远非罕见 = 很常见，空格取反：罕见的。

Skill Builder

“Most nearly mean”: replace the word, keep the sentence’s meaning

Think aloud · cover the word

Mini passage · teacher-writtenVisitors to the temple are asked to observe a few simple rules: remove your shoes, speak quietly, and do not take photographs.

As used in the text, what does the word “observe” most nearly mean?
A) watch   B) follow   C) notice   D) study

1 · Cover the word Visitors are asked to ___ rules, and the rules are actions to do. 先遮住目标词。

2 · Predict to obey, to do what the rules say · 遵守。

3 · Match follow ✓. “watch / notice / study” are the usual meanings of observe, but you cannot “watch” a rule. 常见词义 watch 是陷阱，要用语境义。

Precision: close is not enough · 精确

When two choices point the same direction, choose the one that matches the exact idea and the strength of the text.

Text: the critic “had a few doubts” about the film.

✅ reservations (some doubts)same idea, same strength · 保留意见

❌ contempt (strong hatred)too strong · 程度过强

❌ indifference (not caring)wrong idea: doubts ≠ not caring

两个选项方向相同时，比较“具体意思”和“程度强弱”，选和原文最一致的那个。

Quick Check

Quick check · Contrast, negation, precision小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.Most early critics dismissed the novel, but later readers found it _______.
Opposite of “dismissed” → valuable / admirable. 前人否定，后人肯定。
2.Unlike his talkative brother, Sam was _______ and rarely spoke in class.
Opposite of talkative → reserved / quiet / reticent. 有两个线索：unlike + rarely spoke。
3.The findings are not _______; in fact, three other studies reported the same pattern.
Whole phrase: the findings are typical → blank = unusual / anomalous. 双重否定。
4.Although the town is small, its library is surprisingly _______.
Contrast with “small” → large / extensive.
5.The company failed to _______ its goal; sales fell short by 20 percent.
What it did not do → meet / achieve the goal.
6.“The judge’s ruling was sound.” “sound” most nearly means:
reasonable / well-founded (合理的), not “noise.” 熟词僻义。
7.Text: the plan was “slightly delayed.” Choice: abandoned. Good match?
No: delayed ≠ given up; also much too strong.
8.Rather than _______ the old theory, the new data actually support it.
Opposite of “support” → challenge / undermine. rather than 两边相反。
Worked Example

Step 1 · Read the text and the question先读短文，再读题目在问什么

What is the question asking?

The blank describes recognizing Bosch’s influence on Banisadr’s paintings. But notice: “It is by no means ___”. We must decide what the whole phrase means.

注意 by no means（绝不）放在空格前面。

Worked Example

Step 2 · Find the evidence in the text回到短文，找到依据

1

Find the clue

“indeed, Banisadr himself cites Bosch as an inspiration.” Indeed supports the first sentence: Bosch’s influence is real and obvious.

2

Meaning of the whole phrase

Recognizing Bosch’s influence is reasonable / worthwhile. The next sentence (However…) only says another work may have had a greater impact.

3

Remove the negative

“by no means ___” = “worthwhile” → the blank = not worthwhile, unimportant.

4

Check

“It is by no means unimportant” = it is important. ✓

Worked Example

Step 3 · Test every choice逐个检验选项

A
CUT ✗
“by no means substantial” = not important at all. That conflicts with “indeed, Banisadr himself cites Bosch.”
B
CUT ✗
satisfying: the text is about whether the influence is real, not whether noticing it is pleasant.
C
KEEP ✓
“by no means unimportant” = it does matter to recognize Bosch’s influence, which Banisadr himself confirms.
D
CUT ✗
“by no means appropriate” = it would be wrong to recognize the influence. Opposite of the text.

Answer: C. First decide what the whole phrase must mean (recognizing Bosch’s influence matters), then remove the negative. 双重否定：by no means unimportant = important。

Worked Example 2

Form 6 · Module 2 · Question 3分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Fill the blank: what did skeptical colleagues say the kea’s use of stones “might simply be”? 空格是“怀疑者”对鹦鹉用石头这一行为的看法。

2

Locate the key lines · 找线索

Signal: “…might simply be ___, but Bastos and her team showed that the kea was using the stones deliberately.” but 后：研究团队证明鹦鹉是“故意的”。

3

Predict · 预测

The skeptics said the opposite of “deliberately” → “by chance, not on purpose.” 取反：偶然的、无意的。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

accidental = not on purpose ✓. It is the exact opposite of “deliberately.” accidental 正好是 deliberately 的反义。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ intriguing = interesting. Being interesting is not the opposite of deliberate, and skeptics would not doubt it.
B ✗ obvious: the contrast is about whether the act was on purpose, not whether it was easy to see.
D ✗ observable: the stone use was clearly observed; the question was whether it was intentional.

Answer: C. Contrast. The skeptics said the kea’s use of stones might be ___, but Bastos showed the kea “was using the stones deliberately.” The opposite of deliberately is accidental. but 后是 deliberately（故意的），反义 → accidental。

Worked Example 3

Form 3 · Module 2 · Question 1分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Fill the blank: what kind of viburnum plant has smooth, green leaves? 空格描述一种叶子光滑、均匀绿色的植物。

2

Locate the key lines · 找线索

Sentence 1: a plant “experiencing insect damage” gets a discolored, felty growth. Sentence 2: “A ___ plant, on the other hand, will have … smooth … green” leaves. on the other hand 表对比：受虫害 vs. 空格。

3

Predict · 预测

Opposite of “damaged by insects” → “not damaged, in good condition.” 取反：没受损的、健康的。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

healthy ✓. Check the contrast point: damaged vs. undamaged — not helpful vs. harmful, not simple vs. complex. 对比的是“受损 vs. 健康”。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ struggling describes the damaged plant, not its opposite.
B ✗ beneficial = helpful to others. The contrast is damaged vs. not damaged, not helpful vs. harmful.
C ✗ simple: nothing in the text is about how complex the plant is.

Answer: D. Contrast. First: a plant with insect damage gets a discolored, felty growth. “On the other hand”: a ___ plant has smooth, uniformly green leaves. Opposite of a damaged plant = healthy. 受虫害的植物 vs. 健康的植物。

Precision

Precision: the meaning must fit exactly

Right direction, wrong word

In 2-2-3, dispute and invalidate are both “against” the projection, but delays (events) cannot dispute; only people dispute. Ask: who or what does the action?

方向对还不够，要看主语能不能做这个动作。

Common meaning trap

In “most nearly mean” questions, one choice is usually the word’s most common meaning (dispute → arguing; trace → sketch). It is almost always wrong. Read the sentence.

最常见的意思往往是陷阱。

Summary

What to remember about contrast, negation, and precision语境词义（二）要点

01 · 转折信号

Contrast signals (but, however, on the other hand, rather, unlike): find the clue on the other side, then flip it.

02 · 明确对比对象

Make sure you know what is being contrasted: However may contrast two whole ideas, not the blank.

03 · 否定表达

Negation (not, by no means, unlikely to, declines to): decide what the whole phrase means, then remove the negative.

04 · 熟词生义

Most nearly mean: cover the word, predict from context, and avoid the word’s most common meaning.

05 · 方向与搭配

Precise = right direction and right fit with the words around it.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
