Warm-up · 热身

Agree, disagree, or partly agree?同意、反对，还是部分同意？

Cross-Text questions are about the relationship between two views. 关键是两种观点的关系。

Amy: “Homework helps students remember what they learn in class.”
Ben: “Some homework helps, but too much just makes students tired, and they remember less.”
How would Ben most likely respond to Amy?
What is it?

What is a Cross-Text Connections question?

Definition

The question gives two short texts on the same topic, labeled Text 1 and Text 2. You decide how the author (or the researchers) of one text would most likely respond to or characterize an idea in the other.

两篇同一话题的短文，问一方会如何回应/评价另一方的观点。每套 1 题，位置在结构、目的题之后。

The stems

Based on the texts, how would the author of Text 2 most likely respond to the overall argument presented in Text 1?

…how would Putirka and Xu (Text 2) most likely characterize the conclusion presented in Text 1?

…how would the author of Text 2 respond to the underlined claim in Text 1?

几乎总是“Text 2 怎么看 Text 1”。注意题目针对的是 Text 1 的哪一部分。

Relationships

Common relationships between the two texts

RelationshipWhat Text 2 doesTypical answer words
Disagreesoffers evidence or reasoning against Text 1’s viewdisputing, challenging, arguing that it is based on a misconception, questionable
Partly agreesaccepts part of Text 1 (often with although, indeed) but rejects its conclusionconceding … but asserting; suggesting a revision instead of rejection
Cools the excitementadds facts that make Text 1’s claim seem too strong or too earlyoverly optimistic, premature, overstates
Agrees / extendssupports Text 1 or adds an explanationwould agree, supports, helps explain

Most SAT pairs disagree in some way. Text 2 usually answers Text 1. Look for the word that shows the turn: However, but, argue that … unduly, or a different explanation for the same facts. 大多数是“不同意”或“部分同意”。

How to approach it

Four steps for Cross-Text Connections

1

Read the stem first: who responds to what?

Underline “Text 2” and the exact target in Text 1: the conventional wisdom, the conclusion, the underlined claim. 先看题干：谁回应谁的哪个观点。

2

Summarize Text 1’s target in one sentence

“Text 1 says: human societies developed in fixed stages.” 一句话概括 Text 1 被问到的观点。

3

Summarize Text 2’s view on the same point

“Text 2 says: societies were always flexible, switching back and forth.” 一句话概括 Text 2 在同一点上的看法。

4

Predict the response, then match

Agree or disagree? Fully or partly? Why? The correct choice must use only what Text 2 actually says. 预测态度和理由，答案必须来自 Text 2 的内容。

Skill Builder

Two texts, one question: summarize each side first

Mini pair (teacher-written)

Text 1 Many students study with music playing. Some researchers claim that background music helps students remember new vocabulary, because it keeps the brain alert.

Text 2 A psychologist tested music with 300 students. Instrumental music caused no harm, but songs with lyrics lowered vocabulary scores, since the brain tried to process two sets of words at once.

Q: How would the psychologist (Text 2) most likely respond to the claim in Text 1?

Think aloud · 出声思考

  1. 1 · Who responds to what? Text 2 → the claim in Text 1 (“music helps memory”). 确认方向：Text 2 回应 Text 1 的主张。
  2. 2 · Text 1 in one line. Claim: music helps vocabulary memory. Reason: keeps the brain alert. T1：主张 + 理由。
  3. 3 · Text 2 in one line. Instrumental music: no harm. Songs with lyrics: lower scores. T2：无歌词无害，有歌词反而降低成绩。
  4. 4 · Relationship + predict. Not a total “no”: Text 2 qualifies the claim. Predict: “Too general: songs with lyrics actually hurt memory.” 关系是“限定”：说法太笼统，有歌词的音乐反而有害。
Skill Builder

Words in the answer choices: how does one author judge the other?

Word in the choice中文Text 2 would say this when…
overly optimistic / premature过于乐观 / 为时过早Text 1 is excited, but Text 2 shows the evidence is not strong enough yet.
questionable / flawed值得怀疑 / 有缺陷Text 2 points out a problem in Text 1’s method or reasoning.
based on a misconception基于误解Text 1 assumes something that Text 2 shows is false.
oversimplified / too general过于简单化 / 太笼统Text 2 shows the claim is true only under certain conditions (qualify 限定).
concede … but承认…但是Text 2 accepts part of Text 1 and rejects another part.
supports / helps explain支持 / 帮助解释Text 2 adds evidence or a reason for what Text 1 describes (complement 补充).

Mini pair check: “oversimplified” fits (✓); “based on a misconception” is too strong, because Text 2 agrees instrumental music causes no harm. 先判断方向（同意/反对），再判断程度（全部/部分）。“限定”不等于“全盘否定”。

Skill Builder

Five traps in Cross-Text choices (mini pair)

Trap · 错误特征Wrong choiceWhy it is wrong
One-text answer
只符合一篇		“Songs with lyrics lower vocabulary scores.”True in Text 2, but it does not say how Text 2 responds to Text 1. 没回答两文关系。
Overstatement
夸大分歧		“Music never helps learning.”Text 2 only limits the claim; it never says “never.” 限定 ≠ 彻底否定。
Misattribution
立场错配		“Text 1 warns that lyrics are distracting.”That is Text 2’s view, given to the wrong author. 把观点安到了错误的作者身上。
Unsupported agreement
虚构共识		“Both authors agree that music with lyrics is best.”Text 1 never mentions lyrics. 一方没说，不等于同意。
Wrong point
争点错误		“They disagree about how many students study with music.”They disagree about whether music helps memory. 争点错了。

最后检查：答案能分别在两篇找到依据，并且回应方向没有写反。

Quick Check

Quick check · What is the relationship?小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.T1: Dinosaurs died out because of an asteroid. T2: The asteroid hit, but huge volcanic eruptions had already weakened many species. → T2 on T1?
Complements / qualifies: adds another cause. 补充另一个原因，不是全盘否定。
2.T1: This new battery will replace all car batteries within two years. T2: It has only been tested in a lab for three weeks. → T2 on T1?
Premature / overly optimistic. 为时过早、过于乐观。
3.T1: City trees lower summer temperatures. T2: Streets with many trees were 4°C cooler than streets with none. → T2 on T1?
Supports it with data. 用数据支持。
4.T1: People choose foods mainly by price. T2: In surveys, shoppers ranked taste far above price. → T2 on T1?
Disagrees: the claim rests on a mistaken assumption. 反驳。
5.Choice: “Both authors agree that the battery is safe.” (Neither text mentions safety.) OK?
No: unsupported agreement. 虚构共识。
6.Choice: “T2 would say trees have no effect on temperature.” (Item 3) OK?
No: reversed. T2 supports T1. 方向写反。

1

Text 1: “exciting progress,” “an especially promising discovery.” Text 2: “caused excitement. However, … the gene likely functions very differently in humans”; how it works in humans “remains unclear.” T1 很乐观；T2 用 However 泼冷水。

3

Text 2: “Text 1 is too hopeful: we don’t yet know how EGR works in humans.” 预测：T1 太乐观了。

4

Compare with the choices · 对照选项

B: “overly optimistic given additional observations from Srivastava and her team” = too hopeful + the team’s own warning. overly optimistic = 过于乐观，理由来自团队的补充观察。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Text 2 says the team has not identified how EGR works in humans.
C ✗ Text 2 agrees the finding caused excitement; Text 1’s excitement is not unexpected, just too strong.
D ✗ Text 1 is enthusiastic, not dismissive.

Answer: B. Text 1 calls the EGR discovery “especially promising” and “exciting progress” toward understanding human regeneration. Text 2: the discovery “caused excitement. Text 1 很乐观；Text 2 指出人类中的功能可能完全不同 → 过于乐观。

Wrong answers

How wrong Cross-Text choices are built

Wrong direction

Says Text 2 agrees when it disagrees, or rejects completely when it only disagrees in part (the Nutcracker pair).

态度方向错，或把“部分同意”说成“完全否定”。

Wrong target

Answers a part of Text 1 that the question did not ask about.

回应的不是题目问的那个观点。

Not in Text 2

A reason that sounds scientific but Text 2 never gives: “nutrient replenishment,” “larger organisms.”

Text 2 没提过的理由。

Wrong reason

The attitude is right (“questionable”), but the reason after because is wrong.

态度对，理由不对：because 后面也要核对。

Summary

What to remember about Cross-Text Connections双篇关系要点

01 · 识别题干

Stem: Based on the texts, how would the author of Text 2 most likely respond to / characterize … in Text 1? About one per test.

02 · 定位讨论对象

First find the exact target in Text 1 (the conventional wisdom, the conclusion, the underlined claim).

03 · 概括双方观点

Summarize Text 1’s target and Text 2’s view on the same point, one sentence each.

04 · 态度与理由

Decide the relationship: disagree, partly agree, “too optimistic,” or agree; then check the reason.

05 · 核对讨论对象

Check that the answer addresses the exact idea named in the question, and that its reason comes from Text 2.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
