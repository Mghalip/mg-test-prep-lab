What is it?

What is a Command of Textual Evidence question?

Definition

The text makes a claim about a literary work (or reports someone’s claim). The four choices are quotations. You choose the one that is the clearest direct evidence for the claim.

原文提出一个观点（多为对文学作品的评论），四个选项都是引文，选出最能直接证明这个观点的一句。

How often?

0–3 per test. Most come from poems, novels, plays, and short stories, but some quote a historian, a researcher, or a survey response.

The stems

Which quotation from “The Bet” most effectively illustrates the claim?

Which quotation from a work by a historian would best illustrate the student’s claim?

Which response from a survey … best supports the researchers’ explanation?

标志：quotation / response + illustrates / supports the claim。和第 10 课 “Which finding, if true…” 区分开。

The key idea

The quotation must show the claim, every part of it

Wrong quotations are usually on the same topic and often sound beautiful. The question is not “is this quotation about the topic?” but “does this quotation show what the claim says?”

ClaimSimplifiedParts the quotation must show
a banker is described as being very upset 5-1-13banker = very upsetstrong negative emotion
Mansfield contrasts the character’s pleasant appearance with her unpleasant attitude 4-2-12pretty + unpleasanttwo parts: something lovely about her looks and something unpleasant in her attitude
the speaker describes having contradictory feelings while experiencing a spring day 6-2-14spring + mixed feelingstwo opposite feelings

Count the parts. Words like contrasts, mixed feelings, ambivalence, contradictory, both … and, while also mean the quotation must contain two sides. A quotation with only one side is wrong, even if that side is very strong. 数清观点有几部分。出现“对比、矛盾、复杂情感”时，引文必须两面都有。

How to approach it

Four steps for Command of Textual Evidence

1

Find the claim

It is usually the sentence with the blank, or the student’s / journalist’s claim in the last sentence. 找到观点句。

2

Simplify it to key words and count its parts

“readers have not understood themselves” · “pleasant looks + unpleasant attitude.” 简化成关键词，数清几部分。

3

Read each quotation and ask: does it show the key words?

Paraphrase each quotation in plain words first; old or poetic language can hide the meaning. 把每句引文翻成大白话，再和关键词对照。

4

Choose the one that matches every part

Cut quotations that are only on the topic, show only one part, or show the opposite. 只沾话题、只有一半、意思相反的都排除。

Skill Builder

What the question really asks

The job 任务

The text makes a claim (观点) about a novel, poem, play, or a person. The blank asks for a quotation (引文, the exact words from the work) that shows the claim is true.

The claim is usually the sentence right before the blank, often after “as when the narrator says…” or “for example”.

Rule: the right quotation shows every part of the claim. Same topic is not enough. 正确引文必须体现观点的每一个部分；只是话题相同还不够。

题目先给出一个关于作品或人物的观点，再让你选一句原文引文来“证明”这个观点。

Skill Builder

Think aloud: split the claim, then test every part

Mini passage (teacher-written)

In a short story, the narrator describes her grandfather as proud of his garden but too stubborn to accept help with it: ______

A) “Each morning he walked between the bean rows, touching every leaf as if it were a medal he had won.”

B) “When I offered to carry the heavy watering can, he only said, ‘I have done this for forty years.’”

C) “He showed every visitor his tomatoes, the reddest on the street, and when my mother offered to pull the weeds, he locked the gate.”

D) “The garden was small: three rows of beans and a single apple tree.”

How a good reader thinks 思考过程

1 · Split the claim. Part 1 = proud of the garden. Part 2 = refuses help. 把观点拆成两部分：骄傲 + 拒绝帮助。

2 · Predict. I need him showing off the garden and saying no to help. 预测：既要炫耀花园，又要拒绝帮助。

3 · Test each part. A: proud ✓, help ✗. B: refuses help ✓, proud ✗ (only “forty years”). D: only describes the garden ✗ ✗. C: shows off tomatoes ✓ + locks the gate on help ✓.

4 · Answer C. Only C has both parts. A and B are half matches, the most common trap. 只有 C 两部分都有；A、B 只对一半，是最常见的陷阱。

Feeling words in claims, and four traps

Claim words → what the quotation must show

Claim word中文Look for…
nostalgic怀旧的missing the past, “I remember…”
resentful怨恨的anger about unfair treatment
indifferent漠不关心not caring, no reaction

Wrong-answer traps 陷阱

❌ Half match: shows only one part of the claim.只对一半：观点有两部分，引文只体现一部分。

❌ Same topic, no claim: mentions the garden but no pride, no refusal.只是话题相同，没有体现观点。

❌ Opposite: claim says “upset,” quotation says “delighted.”方向相反。

❌ Wrong person: the feeling belongs to another character.人物错位：情感属于别的人物。

Quick Check

Quick check · Does the quotation show the claim?小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.Claim: the speaker misses her childhood home. “I still dream of the blue door and the smell of bread in the hall.”
✅ Yes: dreaming of the old home = missing it (nostalgic). 梦见旧家 = 怀念。
2.Claim: Tom is nervous before the race. “Tom tied his shoes and walked to the line.”
❌ Same topic only: no sign of nervousness (shaking hands, fast heartbeat…). 只有动作，没有紧张的表现。
3.Claim: Mei is kind but impatient. “Mei gave the boy her umbrella, then tapped her foot as he fumbled with it.”
✅ Both parts: gave the umbrella (kind) + tapped her foot (impatient). 给伞（善良）+ 跺脚（不耐烦），两部分都有。
4.Claim: the narrator admires her teacher. “Ms. Lin explained the problem twice.”
❌ Shows the teacher’s action, not the narrator’s admiration. 只写老师的动作，没写叙述者的钦佩。
5.Claim: the old man is lonely. “He set two cups on the table every evening, though no one had visited in years.”
✅ Yes: waiting for visitors who never come = loneliness. 每晚摆两只杯子却无人来访 → 孤独。
6.Claim: the poem contrasts the noisy city with the quiet sea. “The city roared with cars and voices all night.”
❌ Half match: only the city; the quiet sea is missing. 只有城市，缺少大海，只对一半。
Worked Example

Step 1 · Read the text and the question先读短文，再读题目在问什么

What is the question asking?

The claim: Whitman suggests that readers “have not fully understood themselves.” We need the quotation that says this.

观点：读者没有真正了解自己。

Worked Example

Step 2 · Find the evidence in the text回到短文，找到依据

1

Simplify

Key words: you (the reader) + don’t know yourself.

2

Check A

“You have not known what you are, you have slumber’d upon yourself all your life” → you have not known yourself; you have been asleep to yourself. ✓

3

Check B, C, D

B praises the reader as immense; C is about what the speaker should have done; D says others (“None”) have not understood you, and the speaker does.

4

Decide

Only A is about the reader not understanding himself or herself.

Worked Example

Step 3 · Test every choice逐个检验选项

A
KEEP ✓
“You have not known what you are” = readers have not understood themselves. The sleep image (“eyelids … closed”) repeats the idea.
B
CUT ✗
Calls the reader “immense and interminable”; says nothing about self-understanding.
C
CUT ✗
About the speaker’s regret (“I should have…”), not the reader.
D
CUT ✗
Close trap: “None has understood you” is about other people not understanding the reader, not the reader not understanding himself.

Answer: A. D uses the word “understood,” but the wrong person does the understanding. Match the whole claim, not one word. D 有 understood 这个词，但主语不对：是别人不理解你，而不是你不了解自己。

Worked Example 2

Form 5 · Module 1 · Question 13分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Which quotation … most effectively illustrates the claim?” → I must find the claim in the text and choose words from the story that show it. 题目要一句能“证明”观点的引文，先找观点。

2

Locate the claim · 找观点

Lines 2–3: “a banker is described as being very upset about something.” Paraphrase: the banker = strong negative feelings. Two parts: banker + very upset. 观点：银行家非常难过/心烦。两个要素：人物 + 强烈负面情绪。

3

Predict · 预测

A good quotation shows the banker crying, shouting, unable to sleep or eat: clear signs of strong distress. 预测：引文应写他哭、失眠等明显的痛苦表现。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

Look for a feeling, not just an action. D: “his tears and emotion kept him for hours from sleeping” = crying + lying awake → very upset. Matches the prediction. D 有眼泪和几个小时睡不着，与预测一致。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Shows the banker acting cautiously, not upset.
B ✗ Describes the quiet house at night; nothing about his feelings.
C ✗ Opposite: the banker is “delighted.”

Answer: D. Claim: the banker is very upset. D: “his tears and emotion kept him for hours from sleeping.” Crying and lying awake for hours = very upset. 流泪、几个小时睡不着 → 非常难过。

Worked Example 3

Form 1 · Module 2 · Question 12分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Again “most effectively illustrates the claim” → find the claim, split it into parts. 同样是引文支持观点题。

2

Locate the claim · 找观点

Cather portrays Alexandra as having “a deep emotional connection to her natural surroundings.” Part 1 = strong feelings. Part 2 = nature (land, plants, animals). 两部分：深厚的情感 + 自然环境。

3

Predict · 预测

The quotation should show Alexandra feeling something deep (love, joy, her heart) about the land, insects, birds or sky. Farming facts alone are not enough. 预测：她对土地、动植物有很深的感情；只讲农业知识不够。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

A: the country “meant” much to her; insects were “like the sweetest music”; her “heart” was hiding “with the quail and the plover.” Feeling ✓ + nature ✓. A 两部分都有：情感词（heart, sweetest music）+ 自然（虫、鸟、山脊）。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ About talking with farmers and learning about crops: knowledge, not emotion.
C ✗ She drives through the dark country, but no feelings are described.
D ✗ Shows her practical skill with markets and livestock, not an emotional bond with nature.

Answer: A. Claim: Alexandra has a deep emotional connection to nature. A: the country “meant” much to her; insects sounded “like the sweetest music”; she felt “as if her heart were hiding down there … with the quail and the plover.” Feelings + nature. A 同时有“情感”（heart, sweetest music）和“自然”（虫鸣、鹌鹑、山脊）。

Worked Example 4

Form 4 · Module 2 · Question 12分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Most effectively illustrates the claim” → the claim is in the sentence before the blank: “as when Mansfield writes of the teenager, ___.” 观点在空格前的句子里。

2

Locate the claim · 找观点

Mansfield “contrasts the character’s pleasant appearance with her unpleasant attitude.” Contrast = both halves must appear together. “对比” → 外表好 + 态度差，两部分要同时出现。

3

Predict · 预测

One quotation with a nice word about how she looks (pretty, lovely) and a bad word about how she acts or feels (rude, bored, disgusted). 预测：一句里既有“外表美”的词，又有“态度差”的词。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

B: she shook the puff “as though she loathed it” (unpleasant) and “dabbed her lovely nose” (pleasant). A, C, D show only the bad attitude: half matches. B 同时有 loathed 和 lovely；A、C、D 只有态度差，只对一半。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Shows a strange reaction to flowers, but nothing about her pleasant appearance.
C ✗ Shows impatience (unpleasant attitude) only; no pleasant appearance.
D ✗ Shows rudeness and discomfort only; no pleasant appearance.

Answer: B. Claim: pleasant appearance vs. unpleasant attitude. B: she shook her powder puff “as though she loathed it” (unpleasant) and “dabbed her lovely nose” (pleasant). Both parts appear in one quotation. loathed（态度差）+ lovely nose（外表好），两部分都有。

Wrong answers

How wrong quotations are built

Right topic, wrong point

About the same person or place, but does not show the claim: Alexandra driving through the country at night.

话题相同，观点不对。

Only one side

For two-part claims (contrast, mixed feelings), shows only one side: only “repellant,” only “pleasure.”

对比类观点只给了一边。

Same word, different meaning

Repeats a key word but changes who or what: “None has understood you” vs. “you have not understood yourself.”

关键词相同，但主语或对象变了。

Opposite

Shows the reverse feeling: “delighted” for “very upset”; support where the claim says difficulty.

情感或态度相反。

Summary

What to remember about Command of Textual Evidence文字证据要点

01 · 识别题干

Stem: Which quotation … most effectively illustrates the claim? 0–3 per test, mostly literary works.

02 · 拆解主张

Find the claim, simplify it to key words, and count its parts.

03 · 改写引文

Paraphrase each quotation in plain words before comparing.

04 · 覆盖两面

For contrast / mixed / contradictory / ambivalence claims, the quotation must contain both sides.

05 · 排除干扰

Cut quotations that are only on the topic, show only one part, change the subject, or show the opposite.
