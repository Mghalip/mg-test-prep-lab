Warm-up · 热身

Write the headline给这段新闻选标题

A good headline = the main idea: topic + what the text says about it. 好标题 = 主旨。

For years, scientists thought reef sharks were mostly solitary hunters. But tracking data from 40 reef sharks show that the sharks return to the same group of companions night after night.
Which headline best states the main idea?
What is it?

What is a Central Ideas question?

Definition

You choose the statement that best expresses the main idea of the text: the point that most of the details support.

It belongs to Information and Ideas: the answer is a paraphrase of what the text says, not a description of how it is written.

问全文主旨。属于“信息与观点”：答案是对原文意思的同义改写。

The stems

Which choice best states the main idea of the text?

What is the main idea of the text?

How often?

1–2 per test, at the start of the Information and Ideas group.

与“写作目的”区分：目的问“作者在做什么（To…）”，主旨问“说了什么（一个陈述句）”。

The main idea

What makes a good main idea?

Covers most details

Every sentence in the text should fit under it. If a choice matches only one sentence, it is too narrow.

能涵盖大部分细节。

Includes the emphasis

If the text turns (but, however, although), the part after the turn is usually what the author emphasizes. Good answers often keep both sides: “Although X, Y.”

包含作者强调的重点，转折后面是重点。

Adds nothing new

No new ideas, no stronger claims (most effective, always, proves), nothing that contradicts the text.

不加新内容，不说得更绝对，不与原文矛盾。

Notice: several correct answers on the SAT use the pattern “Although [one side], [main point].” This mirrors texts that admit something and then make their point. 正确答案常见 “Although…, …” 结构，对应原文的让步 + 重点。

Literary texts

Main ideas in literary texts

Ask three questions

  1. Who is the focus? (Mary; Buck; the speaker)
  2. What is the situation? (working in a garden; meeting Thornton’s friends; going to bed)
  3. What does the text show about their feelings or attitude?

文学选段：谁？处境？情感/态度？

Words that carry the feeling

very much absorbed · more pleased every hour · a fascinating sort of play → Mary feels very satisfied.

Thornton alone held him · the rest of mankind was as nothing → Buck values Thornton above everyone else.

抓住表达情感的词，翻译成简单的结论。

How to approach it

Four steps for Central Ideas

1

Read the whole text and find the topic

Who or what is it about? 找话题。

2

Find what the text says about the topic

Look at the first and last sentences and at any turn (but, however, although). 看首尾句和转折。

3

Say the main idea in one sentence

Use your own words. If the text turns, include both sides: “Although…, …” 用一句话说出主旨。

4

Choose the closest match and check its size

Cut choices that are a single detail, too broad, too strong, or not in the text. 选最接近的，并检查范围和语气。

Skill Builder

Topic is not enough: find the main point

Mini passage 1 (informational)

Octopuses are usually described as loners that live and hunt alone. However, divers in one bay in Australia have found groups of a certain octopus species sharing dens on the sea floor. The animals signal to one another by changing color, and they sometimes push each other out of the dens. Such behavior suggests that at least some octopuses are more social than scientists believed.

Q: Which choice best states the main idea of the text?

Think aloud · 出声思考

  1. 1 · Topic (话题). Octopuses and how social they are. A topic is not yet an answer. 话题只是“关于什么”，不是主旨。
  2. 2 · Turn + emphasis. Old view: loners. However → new evidence: some live in groups. The last sentence states the point. 转折后 + 末句 = 作者强调的重点。
  3. 3 · Main point (主旨). Although octopuses are usually seen as loners, some may be more social than expected. 模板：对象 + 核心判断 + 必要的限定（at least some）。
  4. 4 · Check size. Keep “some” (not “all”); color signals are only a detail. 保留限定词 some；变色只是细节。
Skill Builder

Literary texts: who, what situation, what feeling?

Mini passage 2 (literary)

Grandfather’s old radio no longer worked, but Ben would not let anyone throw it away. Every evening he dusted it carefully and set it back on the shelf by the window. Sometimes, when the house was quiet, he turned the dead knob and smiled, as if he could still hear the baseball games they used to share.

Q: Main idea? → Ben treasures the radio because it keeps his memories of his grandfather alive. 谁：Ben；处境：收音机坏了；情感：珍惜、怀念。

Words that carry the feeling · 情感线索

In the textWhat it shows
would not let anyone throw it awayvalues it 珍视
dusted it carefully every eveningcare, habit 用心
turned the dead knob and smiledfond memory 温暖的怀念
as if he could still hear…missing the past 怀旧

Traps: “Ben wants to fix the radio” (not in text) · “Ben loves baseball more than anything” (too narrow / distorted).

把描写动作的词“翻译”成情感，再把情感合成一句主旨；不能加原文没有的打算或原因。

Skill Builder

Five traps in main-idea choices (Mini passage 1)

Trap · 错误特征Wrong choiceWhy it is wrong
Too narrow
以偏概全		“Some octopuses change color to signal.”True, but only one detail. 细节属实，但不是全文核心。
Too broad
范围过宽		“All octopuses prefer to live in groups.”The text says some, in one bay. 从部分扩大到全部。
Distortion
信息歪曲		“Scientists have always known octopuses are social.”Reverses the old view (loners). 重复原词，却把方向弄反。
Unsupported addition
无据增添		“Octopuses form groups to protect their young.”The text gives no reason for the groups. 原文没说原因，选项自己加了。
Wrong focus
答非所问		“Divers in Australia often study sea animals.”About the divers, not the octopuses. 对象错了。

Turn words that mark the main point: however 然而 · but 但是 · yet 然而 · although 尽管 · in fact 事实上 · suggests 表明 · therefore 因此. 主旨常在这些词之后。正确答案常用 “Although X, Y” 结构。

Quick Check

Quick check · Topic, main idea, or trap?小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.“Honeybees.” Topic or main idea?
Topic only: no claim about bees. 只是话题。
2.“Although honeybees are small, their pollination is worth billions of dollars to farmers.” Topic or main idea?
Main idea: object + claim. 对象 + 判断 = 主旨。
3.Text: one school tried a four-day week and attendance rose. Choice: “Four-day weeks improve all schools.”
Too broad: one school → all schools. 范围过宽。
4.Text: the cat ignored its new toy and slept in the box it came in. Choice: “The cat preferred the box to the toy.”
Good main idea ✓ 正确概括。
5.Same text. Choice: “The cat was sick, so it slept all day.”
Unsupported addition: no illness in the text. 无据增添。
6.Text: critics hated the film at first, but today it is called a classic. Choice: “Critics hated the film.”
Too narrow: misses the turn and the point after but. 只对应转折前。

Worked Example 2

Form 4 · Module 1 · Question 10分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Main idea of a literary text. Ask: who (Mary), what situation (working in the hidden garden), what feeling? 谁？处境？情感？

2

Locate the key lines · 定位

“very much absorbed,” “only becoming more pleased with her work every hour instead of tiring of it,” “a fascinating sort of play.” 情感词：absorbed 专注、pleased 高兴、fascinating 着迷。

3

Predict · 预测

“Mary really enjoys working in the garden.” 预测：Mary 很享受照料花园。

4

Compare with the choices · 对照选项

D: “feels very satisfied when she’s taking care of the garden” = enjoys the work. C copies the word “play” but changes its meaning. D 是同义改写；C 借用原词 play 却歪曲了意思。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Chores are never mentioned; she is working hard in the garden, not hiding.
B ✗ Opposite: she becomes more pleased, “instead of tiring of it.”
C ✗ The work itself “seemed … like a fascinating sort of play”; she is not making a space to play in.

Answer: D. Mary is “very much absorbed,” becomes “more pleased with her work every hour instead of tiring of it,” and finds it “a fascinating sort of play.” → Mary feels very satisfied taking care of the garden. absorbed, pleased, fascinating → 很满足。

Worked Example 3

Form 4 · Module 1 · Question 12分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Main idea about Buck, a sled dog. How does he treat people? 主旨：Buck 怎样对待人？

2

Locate the key lines · 定位

“Thornton alone held [Buck]. The rest of mankind was as nothing.” He is cold to strangers and accepts Hans and Pete only after learning they are close to Thornton. 首两句是核心；后面都是例子。

3

Predict · 预测

“Buck cares about Thornton far more than anyone else.” 预测：Thornton 在他心中最重要。

4

Compare with the choices · 对照选项

D: “holds Thornton in higher regard than any other person” = cares about him most. B is too broad: Buck does not avoid all humans. D 对应；B 范围过宽。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ The text says nothing about how social Buck was before living with Thornton.
B ✗ Too strong: he does not avoid all humans; he loves Thornton and tolerates Hans and Pete.
C ✗ The text describes how Buck treats people, not how much they like him.

Answer: D. “Thornton alone held [Buck]. The rest of mankind was as nothing.” Buck is cold to strangers, and he accepts Thornton’s partners only because they are close to Thornton. → Buck holds Thornton in higher regard than anyone else. 只有 Thornton 在他心中有分量。

Worked Example 4

Form 1 · Module 2 · Question 10分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Main idea of a poem. The speaker talks to an absent friend (“thee”). What happens when he goes to bed? 诗：说话人上床后发生了什么？

2

Locate the key lines · 定位

Body: “Weary with toil, I [hurry] to my bed.” But then “begins a journey in my head”: thoughts make “a zealous pilgrimage to thee” and “keep my drooping eyelids open wide.” But then 是转折：身体累了，脑子却开始“旅行”。

3

Predict · 预测

“He is tired, but he can’t sleep because he keeps thinking of the friend.” The journey is only in his mind. 旅行只是比喻：在想朋友。

4

Compare with the choices · 对照选项

D: “thinking about the friend instead of immediately falling asleep.” A says he is asleep, but his eyes are “open wide.” D 正确；A 与“睁着眼”矛盾。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ He is not asleep: his eyelids are “open wide.” The journey is in his thoughts, not a dream.
B ✗ The journey is in his mind; no real trip is being planned.
C ✗ There is no discussion with the friend; the friend is far away.

Answer: D. He hurries to bed, “weary with toil,” but “then begins a journey in my head”: his thoughts make “a zealous pilgrimage to thee” and “keep my drooping eyelids open wide.” → He is thinking about the friend instead of falling asleep. 身体累了，脑子却在想朋友，睁着眼睡不着。

Summary

What to remember about Central Ideas中心思想要点

01 · 识别题干

Stem: Which choice best states the main idea of the text? 1–2 per test.

02 · 覆盖与重点

The main idea covers most of the details and includes what the author emphasizes.

03 · 转折与让步

After but / however / although comes the emphasis; good answers often keep both sides (“Although…, …”).

04 · 文学类文本

In literary texts: who, what situation, what feeling? Translate the feeling words into a plain statement.

05 · 排除干扰

Cut details, examples (after for instance), choices that are too strong, and choices that miss the turn.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
