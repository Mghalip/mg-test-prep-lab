Adaptive testing

Module 1 contains questions of mixed difficulty. How well you do on Module 1 decides whether Module 2 is harder or easier. Only the harder Module 2 can lead to a top score.

第一个模块的表现决定第二个模块的难度。想拿高分，第一个模块就要尽量少错。

About our practice forms

The examples in these lessons come from six official practice forms printed on paper. These paper forms are not adaptive, so each module has 33 questions instead of 27. The question types are exactly the same.

Question order

The order of questions in a module

Questions that test the same skill are grouped together, and the groups always come in the same order. Inside each group, questions go from easier to harder. Here is Module 1 of Form 4:

1–4 Words in Context5 Purpose6–7 Overall Structure8 Sentence Function9 Cross-Text 10 Central Idea11 Detail12 Central Idea13 Data14 Citing Evidence15 Data16 Quotation17 Data18 Inference 19–26 Grammar27–30 Transitions31–33 Notes

What this means for you

  • The reading questions come first in every module.
  • The last questions in each group are the hardest. If the last inference question feels hard, that is normal.
  • You can recognize most question types from their position and their stem before you read the text.

Anatomy

The parts of a reading question

1The introduction. Literary texts begin with a line giving the source and the situation. Always read it. 开头的介绍说明出处和人物，一定要读。
2The text. 25–150 words. Everything you need is here; no outside knowledge. 答案的依据全部在短文里，不需要背景知识。
3The question stem. It tells you the question type. 题干告诉你考的是哪种题型。
4Four choices. Exactly one is supported by the text. 只有一个选项有原文支持，其他三个都能找到错误原因。
Two kinds of texts

Literary texts and informational texts

Literary texts

Excerpts from novels, short stories, poems, plays, and memoirs, usually written between 1800 and 1950.

  • Who is speaking or being described?
  • What is happening, and where?
  • How does the character feel, or what does the speaker think?

文学类：小说、诗歌、戏剧选段。关注人物、情节、情感和态度。语言较旧，句子较长，先抓大意。

Informational texts

Short reports on research in science, social science, history, and the arts.

  • What question or problem did the researchers study?
  • What did they find or claim?
  • What evidence or reasoning supports the claim?

信息类：科学、社科、历史、艺术研究。关注研究问题、结论（claim）和证据（evidence）。

How to read

How to read one short text

1

Glance at the stem first

Know the question type before you read, so you know what to look for. 先看题干，知道题型，带着目的去读。

2

Read the whole text once, for its point

Ask: what is this text mainly saying? Mark signal words: however, but, although (contrast) · because, thus, therefore (cause and result) · for example (evidence) · researchers claim / hypothesize (the main claim). 通读全文抓主旨，注意转折、因果、举例和“观点句”。

3

Answer in your own words before you look at the choices

A prediction protects you from attractive wrong choices. 看选项前，先用自己的话预测答案。

4

Test every choice against the text

Keep a choice only if you can point to the words in the text that support it. 每个选项都要回原文找依据，找不到依据就排除。

Skill Builder

Read for three things: topic → claim → evidence

Read this short text

Mini passage · teacher-writtenMany people assume that city pigeons simply wander until they happen to find food.common view However, a team of biologists has argued that pigeons actually follow regular routes, much like commuters.CLAIM The researchers attached small GPS trackers to forty pigeons in one city. Most of the birds visited the same three or four feeding spots in the same order every day.evidence

Topic = what the text is about (a noun). Claim = what the author or researchers say about the topic. Evidence = the facts, data, or examples that support the claim.

话题 = 文章谈论的对象；观点 = 作者/研究者对它的看法；证据 = 支持观点的事实、数据、例子。

Think aloud · 边读边想

1 · Topic? Every sentence mentions pigeons and where they go. Topic: how city pigeons move around. 每句都在讲鸽子去哪儿 → 话题。

2 · Claim? Sentence 1 is what “many people assume.” However turns to the biologists’ view: pigeons follow regular routes. That is the claim. However 之后才是研究者的观点。

3 · Evidence? GPS trackers on 40 birds; same spots, same order, every day. This supports “regular routes.” GPS 数据支持观点。

4 · Say it in one sentence “People thought pigeons wander, but GPS data suggest they follow routes.” 用一句话复述全文：旧看法 → 但新观点 + 证据。

Wrong answers

How wrong choices are built

1 · Not in the text

Sounds reasonable, maybe even true in real life, but the text never says it.

合理但原文没说。SAT 只认原文。

2 · Opposite

Reverses the text, often by missing a not, however, or rather than.

与原文相反，常因漏看否定词或转折。

3 · Too strong or too narrow

Uses words like always, only, proves, all; or describes one detail when the question asks about the whole text.

说得太绝对，或只抓了一个细节。

4 · True but irrelevant

Accurately repeats the text or the data, but does not answer the question that was asked.

内容正确但不回答这道题，在证据题和图表题里最常见。

Rule: the correct answer is the one the text supports, not the one that sounds smartest. 正确答案一定有原文依据，不是“听起来最好”的那个。

Skill Builder

How to eliminate: give every wrong choice a reason

Text and question

Mini passage · teacher-writtenFor years, gardeners believed that planting a single kind of flower in large numbers would attract the most bees. A three-year survey of community gardens, however, found that gardens with ten or more flower species attracted more bee species than single-flower gardens did. The researchers suggest that variety, not quantity, may be what draws pollinators.

Which choice best states the main idea of the text?
A) Gardeners should stop growing single flowers because they harm bees.
B) A survey suggests that a variety of flowers may attract more kinds of bees than one kind of flower does.
C) Single-flower gardens attract more bee species than mixed gardens do.
D) The survey lasted three years and looked at community gardens.

Think aloud · cross out with a reason

Predict first: old belief → however → survey: more flower species, more bee species → “variety may matter.”

❌ A) “harm bees” is never said; “should stop” is advice the text does not give.Not in the text · 原文没说

✅ B) matches the prediction, and keeps the careful word “may.”Supported · 有原文依据

❌ C) reverses the survey result.Opposite · 与原文相反

❌ D) is true, but it is one detail, not the main idea.True but too narrow · 正确但只是细节

排除法：每划掉一个选项，都要说出它错在哪里（没说 / 相反 / 太绝对 / 只是细节）。说不出理由，就回原文再读。

Quick Check

Quick check · Signals, claims, and traps小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.The plan seemed simple. ___, it took the team three years to finish.
However or Therefore?
However: “simple” vs. “three years” is a contrast. 简单 vs. 花了三年 → 转折。
2.Because the soil was dry, the seeds did not sprout. Which part is the cause?
“the soil was dry” (after because). The result: the seeds did not sprout. 因 → 果。
3.Many birds eat insects; for example, swallows catch flies in midair. What does the second part do?
It gives an example that supports the general idea in the first part. 举例支持前面的总观点。
4.Critics claimed the bridge was unsafe. However, new tests show it is strong. Which is the author’s main point?
The part after However: new tests show the bridge is strong. 转折后才是作者的重点。
5.Text: “The drug may reduce pain in some patients.” Choice: “The drug proves to stop all pain.” Trap?
Too strong: may / some → proves / all. 原文“可能、一些”，选项“证明、全部”。
6.Text: “Rainfall decreased, so the river level dropped.” Choice: “The river level rose.” Trap?
Opposite of the text. 与原文相反。
7.A choice is true in real life, but the text never mentions it. Keep or cut?
Cut it: not in the text. SAT 只认原文。
8.Name the topic: “Honeybees dance to show other bees where food is. The angle of the dance points toward the food.”
Topic: honeybee dances (how bees share where food is). 话题是一个名词短语。

Summary

What to remember导论要点

01 · 考试形式

Reading and Writing: 2 modules × 27 questions × 32 minutes. One short text, one question. Module 1 decides the difficulty of Module 2.

02 · 阅读模块

Reading = Craft and Structure + Information and Ideas: eleven question types, each with a fixed stem.

03 · 题目顺序

Questions are grouped by skill in a fixed order, from easier to harder within each group.

04 · 解题步骤

Read the stem, read the whole text for its point, predict, then test every choice against the text.

05 · 错误选项

Wrong choices are usually: not in the text, opposite, too strong or too narrow, or true but irrelevant.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
