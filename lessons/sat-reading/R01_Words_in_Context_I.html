What is it?

What is a Words in Context question?

Definition

A short text has one blank. You choose the word or phrase that best completes it.

The College Board says these questions test whether you can “use context clues to find a replacement for the target word, and then confirm the choice by seeing if it fits.”

一段短文中有一个空格，选出最合适的词或短语。它考的是“根据上下文推断词义”，不是单纯背单词。

How often?

12–13 questions per test, about 18% of the section. It is the single most common reading question, and it always comes first in each module.

每套约 12–13 题，是阅读中最多的题型，每个模块的开头都是它。

“Logical and precise”

Logical: the word agrees with what the rest of the text says. Precise: the word says exactly that, not something close or vaguer.

logical = 和上下文意思一致；precise = 意思准确，不偏不泛。

What does it look like?

Two forms of the question两种问法

Almost every Words in Context question uses the first stem. The second form is rare; it is taught in Lesson 2.

4-1-1
Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?
The blank form: the text has a blank “_______”.
3-1-8
Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?
The answer can be a phrase, such as “paucity of” or “similarities with.”
5-1-1
As used in the text, what does the word “trace” most nearly mean?
The underlined-word form: no blank; a word in the text is underlined. See Lesson 2.
The key idea

The text always explains the blank

Test writers never choose a word you must simply know. Somewhere in the text, there are words that tell you what the blank must mean. We call them clues.

Many ancient sculptures of people’s heads are missing their noses. This is because the nose is the most part of a sculpture of a person’s head. It is delicate and sticks out from the rest of the sculpture, making it especially easy to break.

The clue

The next sentence explains the blank: delicate … especially easy to break.

下一句直接解释了空格：容易断。

Your prediction

“easy to break” → fragile (5-1-2, answer B).

先自己填“容易坏的”，再去选项里找同义词。

Four clues

Four kinds of context clues

1 · Definition or restatement 定义 / 解释

The text explains the blank right after it, often after a colon, a dash, or words like that is, in other words.

冒号、破折号后面常常就是对空格的解释。

2 · Synonym or repetition 同义复现

The same idea appears again elsewhere in the text, in different words: continuously → persistent.

同一个意思在别处换个说法再出现一次。

3 · Cause and result 因果

because, so, thus, as a result: the blank is the cause of a stated result, or the result of a stated cause.

空格是原因或结果，看 because / thus 连接的另一半。

4 · Examples and word association 举例 / 搭配

A list of examples describes the blank (including…), or the blank must fit the words around it (a web of relations, achieve … use).

后面列举的例子说明空格；或者空格必须和前后的词搭配。

The fifth kind, contrast (but, however, rather than, not), is so important that it has its own lesson: Lesson 2. 对比 / 转折线索放在第二课专门讲。

How to approach it

Four steps for Words in Context

1

Read the whole text, saying “blank” for the blank

Do not look at the choices yet. Understand what the text is about. 先读完整段，空格处读作“blank”，不要先看选项。

2

Find the clue

Ask: which words tell me about the blank? Look especially after the blank (colon, dash, next sentence) and at linking words (because, thus, including). 找线索：空格后面的解释、同义词、因果词、例子。

3

Predict a simple word of your own

It can be plain English or even Chinese: “easy to break,” “used by everyone,” “常见的.” Decide at least whether it is positive or negative. 用自己的简单词（中文也可以）填空，至少判断褒贬。

4

Match, then check by reading the sentence again

Choose the option closest to your prediction. Put it into the blank and read the full sentence: it must be logical and fit the words around it. 选出最接近的词，代回原句再读一遍。

Skill Builder

Predict the blank before you look at the choices

Read the text · say “blank”

Mini passage · teacher-writtenThe new bridge was designed to be extremely _______: engineers tested it against winds of 200 kilometers per hour, and it did not move at all.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word?
A) elegant   B) sturdy   C) temporary   D) fragile

Why predict first? If you read the choices first, every word “sounds possible.” A prediction gives you a target. 先看选项，四个词好像都能填；先预测，就有了明确目标，不容易被干扰项带走。

Think aloud · 边读边想

1 · What is the text about? A new bridge and how it was designed. 讲一座新桥的设计。

2 · Find the clue The colon means “here is the explanation”: it was tested against very strong winds and “did not move at all.” 冒号后就是对空格的解释：大风中纹丝不动。

3 · Predict in my own words “very strong, hard to move” · 结实的、稳固的。

4 · Match and check sturdy = strong and solid ✓. elegant (beautiful) is about looks; temporary (not lasting) and fragile (easy to break) go the wrong way. Read it back: “designed to be extremely sturdy” ✓ 代回原句检查。

Skill Builder

Two more clues: cause → result, and “this / these”

Cause → result · 因果线索

Mini passage · teacher-writtenBecause the museum guide was written in short sentences and everyday words, even young visitors found it _______.

1 · Clue Because gives the cause: short sentences, everyday words.

2 · Predict The result: easy to understand · 容易懂的。

3 · Match accessible ✓ (easy to reach or understand). Not obscure (hard to understand, the opposite), not lengthy (long), not costly. 原因是“句子短、用词简单”，结果自然是“容易理解”。

“this / these + noun” · 回指线索

Mini passage · teacher-writtenThe chef is known for her _______ approach to cooking: this careful attention to every detail shows in each dish she serves.

1 · Clue “this careful attention to every detail” points back to the blank. this 回指空格：空格 = 对细节的仔细关注。

2 · Predict “very careful about details” · 一丝不苟的。

3 · Match meticulous ✓. Not casual (relaxed, opposite), not creative (a real quality of chefs, but the text says “careful”), not rapid.

Skill Builder

Traps: the dictionary meaning is not always the meaning in context

Four common traps

Wrong meaning in context · 语境义错A familiar dictionary meaning that does not fit this sentence: “a novel idea” means a new idea, not a book.

Right feeling, wrong meaning · 色彩对、词义错Positive text, positive word, but the meaning is not the one the clue gives: creative for “careful.”

Too strong · 程度过强“somewhat unusual” → shocking; “helped” → guaranteed.

Wrong usage · 搭配不合The word does not fit the words around it: the physicists decide what is invisible ✗.

干扰项常常“意思对一半”：看清证据要求的具体意思，再检查程度和搭配。

Quick Check

Quick check · Predict the blank小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.The old map was nearly _______: the ink had faded so much that most place names could not be read.
Predict: impossible to read → illegible / unreadable. Clue: after the colon. 冒号后解释。
2.Because the trail was steep and rocky, only the most _______ hikers reached the top.
Predict: strong, tough → determined / experienced. Clue: cause → result. 路难走 → 坚持的人。
3.Her explanation was _______; in other words, it left out nothing important.
Predict: complete → thorough / comprehensive. Clue: “in other words.” 换句话说。
4.The town’s population grew _______: it doubled in just two years.
Predict: very fast → rapidly. Clue: “doubled in two years.”
5.The scientist’s results were _______, so other labs repeated the experiment and got the same numbers.
Predict: dependable → reliable / replicable. 其他实验室重复得到同样结果。
6.The senator promised to address the water shortage. “address” most nearly means:
deal with (处理), not “give a speech to” or “write a location on.” 熟词僻义。
7.The plant is _______ in the region: it grows in nearly every field and garden.
Predict: found everywhere → common / widespread / ubiquitous.
8.Text says the plan was “somewhat risky.” A choice says reckless. Good match?
No — too strong. “somewhat risky” ≠ reckless (鲁莽的). 程度过强。

Form 3 · Module 1 · Question 1分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

A blank to fill: “the most logical and precise word.” The blank describes the kind of use the script achieved. 词汇填空：空格描述文字被使用的情况。

2

Locate the key lines · 找线索

“easy to learn and thus quickly achieved ___ use: by 1830, over 90 percent of the Cherokee people could read and write it.” Two clues: thus (result) and the colon (explanation). thus 表结果，冒号后是解释。

3

Predict · 预测

Over 90 percent of people used it → “used by almost everyone.” 预测：几乎所有人都在用 → 广泛的。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

widespread = used in many places by many people ✓. Read it back: “quickly achieved widespread use” ✓ widespread 与预测一致，代回通顺。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ careful use describes how carefully people use something. The text is about how many people used it.
C ✗ unintended = not planned. People learned the script on purpose; nothing suggests the use was accidental.
D ✗ infrequent = rare. Opposite of “over 90 percent.”

Answer: A. Clue: cause and result + restatement. The script was accurate and easy to learn, thus it achieved ___ use; the colon explains: “over 90 percent of the Cherokee people could read and write it.” Use by almost everyone = widespread. 冒号后：90% 的人都会用 → 广泛的。

Form 4 · Module 2 · Question 3分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Fill the blank: what do the physicists spend their time doing to “what is invisible to the naked eye”? 空格是一个动作：物理学家对看不见的东西做什么。

2

Locate the key lines · 找线索

After the colon: “using sophisticated technology, they closely examine the behavior of subatomic particles.” The colon restates the blank. 冒号后 closely examine 就是对空格的解释。

3

Predict · 预测

“looking at very carefully” · 仔细观察、检查。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

inspecting = examining closely ✓. selecting, creating, deciding are not the same action as “closely examine.” 只有 inspecting 与 closely examine 同义。

Answer: B. Clue: restatement after a colon. “they closely examine the behavior of subatomic particles.” Spending time closely examining = inspecting. 冒号后的 closely examine 就是 inspecting。

Precise, not just possible

When two choices seem possible

Look for the most exact match

In 2-1-1, important fits better than a word like “popular”: the text proves importance (significant accomplishments), not popularity.

In 6-1-1, only similarities with fits both the meaning and the words around it: “share basic similarities with true hedgehogs.”

两个选项都“说得通”时，选和线索意思最贴、搭配最自然的那个。

Do not choose a word just because it is hard

The correct answer is often a simple word (collected, important, fragile). Difficult words are frequently traps: indecipherable, ornamental, obscure.

Never pick a word you cannot define. If you know three of the four words and they are all wrong, then the unknown word is the answer.

难词不一定对。认识的三个都错，才选不认识的那个。

Summary

What to remember about Words in Context语境词义要点

01 · 识别题干

Stem: Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase? About 18% of the section, always first.

02 · 寻找线索

The text always contains a clue. Look after the blank (colon, dash, next sentence) and at linking words.

03 · 四类线索

Four clues in this lesson: restatement, synonym, cause and result, examples / word association.

04 · 预测与代入

Predict a simple word first (Chinese is fine), then match, then read the sentence again with your choice.

05 · 精确匹配

Choose the most precise match, not the most difficult word.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
