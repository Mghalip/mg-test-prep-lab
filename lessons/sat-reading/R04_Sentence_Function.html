Warm-up · 热身

What job does the middle sentence do?中间这句话在“干什么”？

A sentence’s function = its relationship to the sentences around it. 句子功能 = 它和前后句的关系。

Text from a friend: “I can’t come to the party tonight. My grandmother is visiting from Shanghai. Let’s meet this weekend instead.”
What is the function of the underlined sentence?
What is it?

What is a Sentence Function question?

Definition

One sentence or portion of the text is underlined (or named: “the third sentence”). You choose what it does in the text: gives an example, explains a reason, states a problem, adds a detail…

原文中某一句被划线（或题干指明“第三句”），问它在全文中的作用。

How often?

1–3 questions per test (about 3%), near the beginning of a module.

The stems

Which choice best describes the function of the underlined sentence in the text as a whole?

…the function of the underlined portion in the text as a whole?

…the function of the third sentence in the overall structure of the text?

问法基本固定。注意划线可能只是一句的一部分。

The key idea

A sentence’s function = its relationship to the others

You cannot know what a sentence does by reading it alone. Its function is how it connects to the sentence before it, the sentence after it, and the main point of the text.

RelationshipAnswer choices often say…Look for
Example of an earlier ideaillustrates / exemplifies / elaborates on an idea introduced earlierFor example, a specific case after a general statement
General idea for a later examplepresents a generalization that is exemplified by…a broad statement followed by a story or case
Reasonexplains why…because, since; answers “why?”
Problem / questionidentifies the problem that … attempted to solveunclear, unknown, less confident, however
Methodexplains part of the methodologyusing data…, compared…, sampled…
Contrast / concessionnotes an exception / acknowledges / qualifiesbut, however, of course, although
How to approach it

Four steps for Sentence Function

1

Read the whole text and find its main point

The underlined sentence serves this main point somehow. 先读全文，抓主旨。

2

Read the sentences before and after the underlined part

Ask: does it support, explain, give an example of, introduce, or turn against its neighbors? 看前后句，判断关系：支持、解释、举例、引出、转折。

3

Say its job in a few words

“gives the method,” “a specific case of sentence 1,” “states the problem that the study tries to solve.” 用几个词概括它的作用。

4

Check both halves of each choice

Choices usually say what it does + what it relates to (“…an idea introduced in the previous sentence”). Both must be right. 选项通常有两半：作用 + 指向哪一句，两半都要对。

Skill Builder

Think-aloud: what does the underlined sentence do for its neighbors?

Read the text

Mini passage · teacher-written1Many desert plants survive long dry seasons by storing water in their thick leaves or stems. 2The aloe plant, for instance, can go for months without rain by drawing on the water held in its fleshy leaves. 3Such adaptations allow these plants to live where few others can.

Which choice best describes the function of the underlined sentence in the text as a whole?
A) It provides a specific example of the general claim made in the previous sentence.
B) It introduces a problem that the next sentence solves.
C) It presents a finding that contradicts the first sentence.
D) It describes the method used to study desert plants.

Think aloud · 四步

1 · Translate the underlined sentence Aloe can live months without rain using water in its leaves. 先用自己的话翻译划线句。

2 · Look before S1 is a general statement about “many desert plants.” 前一句：关于“很多沙漠植物”的概括。

3 · Look after S3 sums up: “Such adaptations…” 后一句：总结这种适应能力。

4 · Name the job, then test “for instance” + one plant → a specific example of S1. A ✓. B: no problem/solution. C: it agrees with S1. D: no study. 作用 = 内容 + 与前后句的关系。

Skill Builder

The same sentence can do different jobs

One sentence, three texts

Maya could not finish her project at the library. The library closed at 6 p.m.Reason: explains why she could not finish. 解释原因

The town library has strict rules. The library closed at 6 p.m. No food was allowed inside.Example: one of the “strict rules.” 举例说明

The guidebook said the library was open late. The library closed at 6 p.m.Contrast: what really happened vs. what was expected. 与预期相反

同一句话，放在不同的上下文里，作用完全不同。所以不能只读划线句，必须看前后句。

Science texts: common jobs

Job · 作用Typical words
Question / hypothesis 问题、假设wondered, unclear; hypothesized, predicted
Method 研究方法collected, compared, measured, surveyed, using data…
Finding / result 发现found, showed, revealed
Conclusion 结论suggests, therefore
Qualification 限定however, only, in some cases, may

Trap: “method” and “finding” are different jobs. They measured… = method; They found… = finding. “怎么研究”和“研究发现”是两种作用，别混淆。

Skill Builder

In stories and poems: what does the sentence add?

Read the text

Mini story · teacher-written1Leo told everyone he was not nervous about the piano recital. 2Yet as his name was called, his hands were cold and he could not stop tapping his foot. 3He walked to the stage slowly, as if each step weighed a hundred pounds.

1 · Translate His body shows he is nervous. 他的身体表现出紧张。

2 · Before / after S1: he says he is not nervous. S3: more signs of nervousness. 前：嘴上说不紧张；后：更多紧张表现。

3 · Job It contradicts what Leo claims by showing his true feelings (signal: Yet). 作用：用行为细节揭示真实情绪，与他说的话相矛盾。

Literary function words · 文学类功能词

establishes the setting 交代场景reveals a character’s feelings 揭示情感
introduces a character 引入人物shows a contrast 呈现反差
elaborates on 进一步说明illustrates 举例体现
marks a shift 标志转变foreshadows 预示

Check the direction: “elaborates on an idea in the previous sentence” vs. “an idea in the following sentence.” A right job pointing to the wrong sentence is wrong. 作用对、但指向的句子错，也是错。

Quick Check

Quick check · Name the function of sentence 2小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.S1: Some birds use tools. S2: Crows, for example, bend wires into hooks to catch food.
Example of the claim in S1. 举例。
2.S1: Scientists wondered whether noise affects sleep. S2: They recorded the sleep of 50 people in quiet and noisy rooms.
Method used to answer the question. 研究方法。
3.S1: They recorded 50 sleepers. S2: People in noisy rooms woke up twice as often.
Finding / result. 研究发现。
4.S1: The new drug lowered blood pressure. S2: However, it worked only in patients under 60.
Qualification: limits the claim in S1. 限定范围。
5.S1: The streets were empty. S2: A single lamp flickered at the corner. (start of a story)
Establishes the setting / adds detail to the scene. 交代场景。
6.S1: Ana smiled and said she loved the gift. S2: Later, she quietly put it in a drawer and never used it.
Contrasts her words with her real feelings. 言行反差。
7.Choice: “summarizes the results.” But the results come in the next sentence. Keep or cut?
Cut: describes a different sentence. 说的是别的句子的作用。
8.S1: Many thought the comet was dangerous. S2: Astronomers calculated that it would pass far from Earth.
Evidence against the common view in S1. 反驳前面的看法。

Worked Example 2

Form 1 · Module 1 · Question 8分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Function of the underlined sentence (sentence 2): what job does it do for the text? Translate it first: the landscape seemed to match and enlarge Lily’s mood; she found herself in its calm. 先翻译划线句：风景像是她心情的放大。

2

Locate before and after · 看前后句

Before (S1): Lily “could be keenly sensitive to a scene which was the fitting background of her own sensations.” After (S3): a description of maples, orchards, oaks. 前句：她对“与心情相配的景色”很敏感；后句：描写具体景物。

3

Predict · 预测

S1 states an idea about Lily; S2 shows that idea happening right now. → “an example of the idea in sentence 1.” 预测：划线句是前一句观点的具体体现。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

D “illustrates an idea that is introduced in the previous sentence” ✓ — right job, right direction (previous). 作用和指向（前一句）都正确。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ The detailed picture of the setting (sugar-maples, orchards) comes in the next sentence; the underlined one is about Lily’s mood.
B ✗ Lily feels calm and finds herself in the landscape; there is no conflict inside her.
C ✗ The next sentence describes the landscape; it does not expand on an assertion made in the underlined sentence. The underlined sentence itself expands on sentence 1.

Answer: D. Sentence 1: Lily is “keenly sensitive to a scene which was the fitting background of her own sensations.” The underlined sentence 2 shows exactly that: the landscape seemed “an enlargement of her present mood,” and she found herself in its calmness. → It illustrates an idea introduced in the previous sentence. 第一句说她对“与心情相配的景色”敏感，第二句正好是这一点的具体表现。

Worked Example 3

Form 1 · Module 1 · Question 9分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Function of the underlined sentence (S2) in the overall structure. Translate: using 1994–2010 data, the team compared 29,000 earnings forecasts with actual earnings. 翻译：团队用数据比较了预测和实际收益。

2

Locate before and after · 看前后句

S1: the study’s conclusion (sunshine → over-optimism). S3: “The team found that…” = the result. 第一句是结论，第三句 found 是研究结果。

3

Predict · 预测

S2 says what data and how they compared it → it explains how the study was done: the method. 预测：讲“怎么研究” = 研究方法。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

C “To explain part of the methodology used in the team’s study” ✓. methodology = 研究方法。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ The results are in the last sentence (“The team found that…”), not in the underlined one.
B ✗ It describes the data the whole study used, not one example that illustrates a finding.
D ✗ No difficulty or challenge is mentioned.

Answer: C. Sentence 2 tells how the study was done: using data from 1994–2010, the team “compared over 29,000 annual earnings forecasts to the actual earnings.” That is the methodology. The results come in sentence 3. 第二句讲“怎么研究”：用什么数据、怎么比较 → 研究方法。

Worked Example 4

Form 2 · Module 1 · Question 9分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Function of the underlined sentence. Translate: like people, trees have their own characters; some give their sap easily, others are “more reluctant.” 翻译：树像人一样有个性，有的爽快出汁，有的不太情愿。

2

Locate before and after · 看前后句

Before: the women strike each tree “to see if the sap would appear” — they are testing the trees. After: basins and chips are set to collect the sap. 前：妇女们测试树会不会出汁；后：开始收集树汁。

3

Predict · 预测

The sentence tells us more about the thing being tested: whether a tree is ready to give sap. → “adds detail about the trees the women test.” 预测：进一步说明妇女们测试的树的特点。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

D “elaborates on an aspect of the maple trees that the women evaluate” ✓. Note: the “characters” belong to the trees, not the women. “个性”说的是树，不是妇女。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ The “characters” described belong to the trees, not to the women.
B ✗ The sentence is about differences between trees, not about a relationship that benefits people and trees.
C ✗ Nothing shows nature changing how people behave; the sentence describes the trees.

Answer: D. The women strike each tree “to see if the sap would appear.” The underlined sentence adds that trees, “like people,” differ: some readily “yield up their life-blood” (sap), others are “more reluctant.” It elaborates on the quality the women are testing: whether a tree is ready to give sap. 妇女们在测试树是否出汁，划线句进一步说明树与树在“出汁”上的不同。

Wrong answers

How wrong function choices are built

Describes a different sentence

The job described is real, but it belongs to the sentence before or after (“summarizes the results” when the results come in the next sentence).

描述的是别的句子的作用。

Wrong relationship

“contrasts” when the sentence agrees; “questions” when it supports; “an exception” when it is an example.

关系判断反了。

Wrong reference

The job is right, but it points to the wrong place: “the previous sentence” vs. “the following sentence.”

作用对，但指向的句子错了。

Not in the text

“how it was received by others,” “a challenge the team faced,” “his travels.”

原文没有的内容。

Summary

What to remember about Sentence Function句子功能要点

01 · 识别题干

Stem: …the function of the underlined sentence / portion / third sentence… 1–3 per test.

02 · 句间关系

A sentence’s function is its relationship to the other sentences and to the main point.

03 · 阅读上下文

Always read the sentence before and the sentence after the underlined part.

04 · 常见功能

Common jobs: example, generalization, reason, method, problem, contrast or concession.

05 · 双重核对

Check both halves of the choice: the right job and the right sentence it refers to.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
