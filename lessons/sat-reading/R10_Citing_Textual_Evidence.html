Citing Textual Evidence

Decide which new finding would support or weaken a hypothesis
判断哪一个新发现能加强或削弱研究者的假设
FIND THE HYPOTHESISSeparate the facts the researchers found from the claim they make.
PREDICTIf the claim is true, what should we expect to see? What would prove it wrong?
TEST DIRECTLYThe right finding speaks to the exact claim, not just the topic.
What is it?

What is a Citing Textual Evidence question?

Definition

The text describes a study and a hypothesis or claim. The four choices are possible new findings. You decide which finding, if true, would most directly support it (or weaken / undermine it).

原文给出一个假设/观点，选项是“假如成立”的新发现，问哪个最能加强（或削弱）它。多见于科学和社科文章。

How often?

3–6 per test (about 6%), one of the most common reading types.

The stems

Which finding, if true, would most directly support the researchers’ hypothesis?

Which finding, if true, would most directly undermine / weaken Foster’s hypothesis?

Which finding from the researchers’ study, if true, would most strongly support their claim?

“if true” 表示选项不需要在原文中出现，假设它是真的就行。注意题目问加强还是削弱。

Facts → Claim → Evidence

What do we know—and what still needs support?事实、解释与证据

1 · Facts: what the text gives us

Dusky sharks eat cownose rays.
Cownose rays eat eastern oysters.

题干给出的背景：鲨鱼吃鳐鱼，鳐鱼吃牡蛎。
做题时先接受这些信息，不需要自己证明。

2 · Claim: the proposed explanation

Researchers hypothesize that fewer sharks caused the oyster population to decline.

研究者的解释：牡蛎减少，是鲨鱼减少造成的。
“谁吃谁”是背景；“这次减少由什么造成”仍需证据。

3 · If the claim is right, what should happen?

Fewer sharks → more rays → more oysters eaten → fewer oysters

鲨鱼少 → 鳐鱼被吃得少，可能增多 → 牡蛎被吃得更多 → 牡蛎减少。
这是假说预测的过程；题干的捕食关系本身，并不能证明每一步都实际发生了。

4 · Which new finding would strengthen the claim?　点击查看证据与解释

Cownose rays increased as dusky sharks decreased.
This supports the predicted middle link: fewer sharks → more rays.

这项发现补上了“鲨鱼少了，鳐鱼是否真的多了”这一环，因此加强假说。
只重复“鳐鱼吃牡蛎”没有增加新支持；加强也不等于已经完全证明因果。

读题提示：hypothesize / argue / suggest 常引出主张；found / observed 常引出发现。信号词只是线索，最终看题目要求支持或削弱哪句话。

Support and weaken

What supports and what weakens

Support 加强

  • Consistent result: the finding agrees with the claim.
  • Prediction comes true: what the claim predicts is observed (awe → more helping → the tree-watchers picked up more pens).
  • Cause-and-effect link confirmed: the middle step of the chain is shown.
  • Comparison: the effect appears with the cause and not without it (mosasaurs near the poles, other reptiles not).

Weaken 削弱

  • Counterexample: a case where the claim should hold but does not.
  • Opposite of the prediction: more otters → less healthy eelgrass.
  • Broken chain: the cause happens but the effect does not, or the effect comes first.
  • Exceeds the claim’s limit: planets with more iron than their star, when the claim says “equal or smaller.”
How to approach it

Four steps for support / weaken questions

1

Check the stem: support or weaken?

Circle the word. Many students choose a supporting finding for a “weaken” question. 先圈出“加强”还是“削弱”。

2

Find the claim and write it as a chain or comparison

“X causes Y because of Z.” Notice limits: equal or smaller, more than, even when. 找出假设，写成因果链或比较，注意限定词。

3

Predict what would prove or disprove it

If the claim is true, what would we see? 预测：如果假设成立，应该看到什么。

4

Choose the finding that tests the exact claim

Cut findings about another topic, another group, or a side detail, even if they sound scientific. 选直接检验该假设的选项；话题相关但不直接相关的排除。

Skill Builder

What “support”, “weaken” and “if true” really mean

Draw the chain 画逻辑链

Most claims here are cause → effect ideas (hypotheses, 假设). Write them as a chain with arrows:

fewer cats → more mice → more damaged crops

A finding supports the claim if it makes a link in the chain more believable. It weakens the claim if it breaks a link or shows the result happening without the cause.

“If true”: treat every choice as a fact, even if the passage never mentions it. Ask only: does this change how believable the claim is? “if true” 表示先把选项当成事实，不能因为原文没提到就排除；只问它是否让观点更可信或更不可信。

支持不等于证明，削弱也不等于推翻：只需要让观点“更可信 / 更不可信”。

Skill Builder

Think aloud: chain → prediction → test

Mini passage (teacher-written)

A gardener noticed that her tomato plants grew taller after she mixed used coffee grounds into the soil. She hypothesized that the coffee grounds add nitrogen to the soil and that this extra nitrogen makes the plants grow taller.

Which finding, if true, would most directly support her hypothesis?

A) Coffee grounds are cheap and easy to collect from cafés.

B) Tomato plants are the most popular plants in home gardens.

C) Soil mixed with coffee grounds had more nitrogen, and plants given the same amount of nitrogen without coffee grew just as tall.

D) Some of her tomato plants grew tall even before she used coffee grounds.

How a good reader thinks 思考过程

1 · Task. “Support” → I need a finding that makes the hypothesis more believable. 任务：支持。

2 · Chain. coffee grounds → more nitrogen → taller plants. The middle link (nitrogen) is the heart of her idea. 逻辑链：咖啡渣 → 氮增加 → 植物长高；中间环节最关键。

3 · Predict. Good support shows the grounds really add nitrogen, and nitrogen alone causes the growth. 预测：证明咖啡渣确实加氮，且氮本身让植物长高。

4 · Test. A, B: same topic, but they change nothing (relevant but ineffective). D: tall plants without coffee → weakens. C confirms both links → C. A、B 话题相关但无作用；D 反而削弱；C 证实两个环节。

Four traps in “which finding” questions

Trap · 陷阱What it looks like (coffee example)Why it is wrong
Relevant but ineffective
相关但无作用		“Coffee grounds are cheap and easy to collect.”Same topic, but it does not make the hypothesis more or less believable.
Wrong target
支持错对象		“Tomatoes need lots of sunlight.”True about tomatoes, but not about coffee → nitrogen → height.
Opposite effect
作用反向		“Plants with coffee grounds grew no taller than plants without.”This weakens. Read the task: support or weaken?
Missing link
缺少关键联系		“Coffee grounds contain many chemicals.”Never connects to nitrogen or growth; you must invent a step.

Final check: does this finding answer this claim, or is it just a true fact on the same topic? Circle support or weaken in the question before you read the choices. 最后检查：这个发现是否针对题目所问的观点？先圈出 support 还是 weaken，避免方向做反。

Quick Check

Quick check · Support, weaken, or neither?小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.Claim: a new bike lane reduced car accidents on Main St. Finding: accidents on Main St. fell after the lane opened, but not on nearby streets.
Support: the drop happened only where the lane was built. 只有建了车道的街道事故下降 → 支持。
2.Same claim. Finding: accidents fell by the same amount on every street in the city that year.
Weaken: something else (not the lane) probably caused the drop. 全城都下降 → 可能另有原因 → 削弱。
3.Same claim. Finding: the bike lane is painted bright green.
Neither: same topic, no effect on the claim. 话题相关但无作用。
4.Claim: bees visit purple flowers more because purple is easier for them to see. Finding: bees also visit purple flowers more in the dark, when they cannot see color.
Weaken: the result happens without the cause (seeing color). 看不见颜色时结果依旧 → 原因不成立 → 削弱。
5.Claim: students who sleep more get higher test scores. Finding: across 500 students, hours of sleep correlate positively with scores.
Support: more sleep goes with higher scores, as predicted. 正相关，符合预测 → 支持。
6.Claim: a fish species lost weight because the water got warmer. Finding: the fish lost weight before the water temperature began to rise.
Weaken: the effect came before the cause. 结果出现在原因之前 → 削弱。
Worked Example

Step 1 · Read the text and the question先读短文，再读题目在问什么

What is the question asking?

The question asks which finding would support the hypothesis that the plants depend on dissolving rock with acids, because this both creates channels for growth and releases phosphorus.

假设：植物“依赖”用酸溶解岩石（开路 + 获得磷）。

Worked Example

Step 2 · Find the evidence in the text回到短文，找到依据

1

Facts

The roots grow into quartzite; root hairs secrete malic and citric acids.

2

Claim

The plants depend on dissolving rock: it makes channels and releases phosphates (a vital nutrient).

3

Predict

If dissolving rock gives the plants something they need (like phosphorus), they should dissolve rock even when they do not need a new path, that is, even when easy cracks exist.

4

Match

Look for a finding that shows the plants actively choose to dissolve rock.

Worked Example

Step 3 · Test every choice逐个检验选项

A
CUT ✗
Other species in richer soil are a different situation; this says nothing about whether these plants depend on dissolving rock.
B
CUT ✗
The proportion of acids is a side detail; it does not test dependence on dissolving rock.
C
KEEP ✓
They carve new entry points even when cracks are available, so dissolving rock must give them something more than space, such as phosphorus.
D
CUT ✗
Weakens: if they thrive on rocks without phosphates, the phosphorus part of the claim is less important.

Answer: C. The best evidence rules out the easy explanation (just looking for space). D is the trap for students who forget the stem says support. C 排除了“只是找缝隙生长”的解释；D 实际是削弱。

Worked Example 2

Form 2 · Module 2 · Question 14分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Which finding, if true, would most directly support the researchers’ hypothesis?” → task = support; treat each choice as true. 任务：支持；把选项当作事实。

2

Locate & paraphrase · 找关键句

Hypothesis: fewer dusky sharks → fewer eastern oysters. Why? Sharks eat cownose rays, and rays are the main predators of oysters. Chain: fewer sharks → more rays → more oysters eaten → fewer oysters. 逻辑链：鲨鱼少 → 鳐鱼多 → 牡蛎被吃多 → 牡蛎少。

3

Predict · 预测

The missing middle link: when sharks went down, rays went up. A finding that shows this supports the whole chain. 预测：鲨鱼减少时，鳐鱼增加。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

D: “Cownose rays have increased … as dusky sharks have decreased.” This is exactly the middle link. D 正好证实中间环节。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ About why sharks declined (less of their other prey), not about how the shark decline affected oysters.
B ✗ Areas with both sharks and rays vs. only sharks: this does not show that losing sharks reduced oysters.
C ✗ If rays ate more oysters before sharks declined, the oyster loss could not have been caused by the shark decline: this weakens.

Answer: D. Chain: fewer dusky sharks → more cownose rays (sharks eat rays) → more oysters eaten (rays eat oysters) → fewer oysters. D confirms the middle link: rays increased as sharks decreased. 鲨鱼少 → 鳐鱼多 → 牡蛎被吃得多。D 证实了中间环节。

Worked Example 3

Form 1 · Module 1 · Question 13分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Which finding, if true, would most directly support the student’s claim?” → the claim belongs to the student, not to Chambi. 要支持的是学生的观点。

2

Locate & paraphrase · 找关键句

Student’s claim: Chambi’s photos have ethnographic value (民族志价值: they record the life and customs of a people), because he captured diverse elements of Peruvian society with dignity. 两部分：记录文化（民族志价值）+ 社会的多样性。

3

Predict · 预测

A good finding shows Chambi photographing many different groups and carefully recording their customs and places. 预测：拍摄多种人群，并认真记录其习俗与地点。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

A: wealthy Peruvians and hundreds of images “carefully documenting the peoples, sites, and customs of Indigenous communities.” Diverse ✓ + cultural record ✓. A 两部分都满足。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ Technical skill with light is not ethnographic value.
C ✗ Fame abroad does not show that the photos record Peruvian society.
D ✗ Other photographers chose the same subjects; this says nothing about the value of Chambi’s photos.

Answer: A. Claim: Chambi’s photos have ethnographic value (they record cultures) and capture diverse elements of Peruvian society. A: besides wealthy Peruvians, he “produced hundreds of images carefully documenting the peoples, sites, and customs of Indigenous communities.” Diverse subjects + careful cultural record. 既拍富人，也大量记录原住民的人物、地点和习俗 → 多样性 + 民族志价值。

Worked Example 4

Form 2 · Module 1 · Question 15分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Which finding, if true, would most directly undermine Foster’s hypothesis?” → task = weaken. Circle it so you do not pick a supporting finding. 任务是削弱，先圈出来。

2

Locate & paraphrase · 找关键句

Foster: otters damage roots → more sexual reproduction → more genetic diversity → healthier meadows. So otters, in the long run, are good for eelgrass. 假设：水獭破坏根 → 有性繁殖多 → 基因多样 → 草场更健康。

3

Predict · 预测

If Foster is right: more otters, present longer → healthier eelgrass. To weaken, find places where more/longer otters go with less healthy eelgrass. 预测：削弱 = 水獭越多越久，草场反而越差。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

C: meadow health “correlates negatively with the length of residence and size of otter populations.” Exactly the opposite of the prediction. C 呈负相关，与假设的预测相反 → 削弱。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Some meadows exist near small, new otter populations; this does not tell us whether otters make meadows healthier.
B ✗ Sites with otters but no eelgrass may simply have no eelgrass to begin with; this does not test the hypothesis.
D ✗ Plants unrelated to eelgrass are outside the hypothesis.

Answer: C. Hypothesis: root damage by otters → more sexual reproduction → more genetic diversity → healthier meadows. Prediction: more otters, longer present → healthier eelgrass. C shows the opposite: at other sites, health correlates negatively with otter population size and length of residence. 假设预测“水獭越多越久，草场越健康”；C 正好相反 → 削弱。

Wrong answers

How wrong findings are built

Right topic, not the claim

About the same subject but tests a different question: why sharks declined; how famous Chambi was; Morrison’s own novels.

话题相关，但不检验这个假设。

Wrong group

About other species, other planets, other stars, other plants: not the ones in the claim.

对象换了。

Wrong direction

A weakening finding for a “support” question, or the reverse. Always check the stem word.

加强/削弱方向反了。

Wrong order or link

The effect happens before the cause (rays ate more oysters before sharks declined), so the cause cannot explain it.

时间顺序或因果链不对。

Summary

What to remember about Citing Textual Evidence加强与削弱要点

01 · 识别题干

Stem: Which finding, if true, would most directly support / weaken…? 3–6 per test.

02 · 事实与主张

Separate facts (found, showed) from the claim (hypothesize, argue, conclude). Only the claim is tested.

03 · 梳理论证

Write the claim as a chain or comparison, and notice its limits (equal or smaller, even when).

04 · 加强与削弱

Support = consistent result, prediction comes true, missing link confirmed. Weaken = counterexample, opposite result, broken chain.

05 · 核对范围

Check the stem word every time, and cut findings about a different topic, group, or order of events.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
