Details

Find the information the text states, and recognize it in new words

What is it?

What is a Details question?

Definition

Detail questions ask about information that is stated in the text: a fact, a reason, a feeling, a description. Usually one or two sentences contain the answer.

问原文“直接写出来”的信息（直接信息题）。答案通常在一两句话里。

How often?

2–3 per test.

The stems

According to the text, what is true about Mother?

According to the text, why are ecologists worried about Pando?

Based on the text, how does the Lord Chancellor respond to the crowd?

Which choice best describes what is happening in the text?

According to / Based on the text 是标志。

Three steps

The three steps: question → locate → paraphrase

1 · 审题 Read the question

Underline the key words: the person or thing (Mother, Pando, the Lord Chancellor) and the question word (what is true, why, how).

圈出题干关键词：对象 + 问什么。

2 · 定位 Locate

Find the sentence where the key word appears, and read the sentence before and after it.

回原文找关键词所在句，读前后句。

3 · 改写 Paraphrase

Say the answer in your own words, then find the choice that means the same. The right answer rarely copies the text.

用自己的话说出答案，再找同义改写的选项。

The text saysThe correct choice says
Mars’s atmosphere is only one percent as dense as Earth’s… 2-1-11Mars and Earth have different atmospheric conditions
ecologists are worried that its growth is declining 3-2-9It isn’t growing at the same rate it used to.
though only nineteen, to be the counsellor of her mother 3-1-11Elinor is remarkably mature for her age.
One step further

When the stem says “would most likely agree” or “inferred”

The stems

Based on the text, Huang, Seager, and colleagues would most likely agree with which statement…?

It can most reasonably be inferred from the text that the finding … was important for which reason?

The answer is still built from the text’s details, but you must put two stated facts together. Take only one small, safe step.

仍然基于原文细节，但需要把两条信息结合起来，只能推一小步。

How to do it

  1. Collect the stated facts that are relevant (often two findings joined by But or crucially).
  2. Ask what they mean together.
  3. Choose the choice that follows directly; cut anything that goes further than the text.

这类题不是“补全最后一句”的推断题，而是基于细节的理解题。

Skill Builder

Find the exact sentence, then say it in your own words

Mini passage (teacher-written)

Honeybees usually collect pollen from many kinds of flowers. One spring, however, a beekeeper noticed that her bees were coming home with only bright orange pollen. She traced it to a nearby field of poppies that had bloomed early during an unusually warm March. Once other flowers opened in April, the bees’ pollen became mixed again.

Q: According to the text, why were the bees carrying only orange pollen?

Think aloud · 出声思考

  1. 1 · Focus the question. Object: the bees. Condition: only orange pollen. Question word: why → I need a reason. 圈出对象、条件（only）、问什么（why = 原因）。
  2. 2 · Locate and reread. “only bright orange pollen” → next sentence: “traced it to a field of poppies that had bloomed early.” 关键词所在句 + 后一句。
  3. 3 · Resolve references. “it” = the orange pollen. “Once other flowers opened…” tells me the poppies were almost the only flowers then. 还原指代：it = 橙色花粉。
  4. 4 · Paraphrase. The poppies bloomed before other flowers, so they were the main food source. 用自己的话：罂粟花开得早，当时几乎只有它可采。
Skill Builder

Small words, big difference: read them exactly

Word中文The text saysSo a choice that says … is wrong
only / alone只有Thornton alone mattered to Buck.“Buck loved Thornton and his friends equally.”
in part / partly部分地Growth is declining in part because of deer.“Deer are the only cause of the decline.”
not until直到…才The bridge did not open until 1910.“The bridge opened before 1910.”
rather than / instead of而不是She wrote rather than painted.“She mainly painted.”
may / suggests可能 / 表明The finding may explain the drop.“The finding proves the cause.”
more … than比较方向Crows used more tools than ravens.“Ravens used more tools than crows.”

Paraphrase, not copy: the right choice usually uses different words with the same meaning. “Growth is declining” → “isn’t growing at the same rate it used to.” 正确答案常是同义改写；照抄原词的选项反而要小心是否改了方向、程度或对象。

Skill Builder

Five traps in detail choices (bee passage)

Trap · 错误特征Wrong choiceWhy it is wrong
Distortion
信息歪曲		“The bees carried orange pollen all year.”Only in early spring; mixed again in April. 换了时间。
Unsupported addition
无据增添		“Bees prefer poppies to all other flowers.”The text says nothing about preference. 原文没说“更喜欢”。
Wrong focus
答非所问		“The beekeeper lived near a poppy field.”True, but it does not answer why. 属实但没回答问题。
Too strong
程度过强		“Warm weather always harms bees.”One spring, one hive; no harm is mentioned. 说得太绝对。
Opposite
方向相反		“The poppies bloomed later than usual.”They bloomed early. 与原文相反。

最后检查：选项的对象、时间、条件、程度是否都与原文一致？“眼熟”不等于正确。

Quick Check

Quick check · According to the text…小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.“Lena missed the bus, so she walked the two miles to school and still arrived before the bell.” Why did Lena walk?
She missed the bus. 原因在 so 前面。
2.Same text. Choice: “Lena was late for school.” True?
No: opposite, she arrived before the bell. 方向相反。
3.“The museum’s new wing, which opened in May, doubled the number of visitors.” What doubled?
The number of visitors (not the size of the museum). 看清对象。
4.“Unlike its cousins, the snow leopard cannot roar.” What is true about the snow leopard?
It cannot roar, though related cats can. unlike = 与…不同。
5.“The drug lowered pain in some patients.” Choice: “The drug cures pain.” OK?
No: too strong (some patients; lowered ≠ cured). 程度过强。
6.“Farmers planted clover between rows; this kept the soil from washing away.” What does “this” refer to?
Planting clover between rows. 还原指代：this = 在行间种三叶草。

Sentence 2 contains the reason: “Because Mars’s atmosphere is only one percent as dense as Earth’s, the air of Mars would not provide enough resistance to the rotating blades of a standard helicopter…”

The air on Mars is much thinner than on Earth → different atmospheres.

The last sentence says the Mars craft has longer, faster blades. So Earth blades are not “too large.”

1

Read the question · 读题

“According to the text, what is true about Mother?” A stated fact about Mother. 问原文直接写出的、关于母亲的事实。

2

Locate the key lines · 定位

“Besides this she used to write stories for them … and she always made up funny pieces of poetry for their birthdays.” Besides this 那一句最具体。

3

Predict · 预测

“She writes stories and poems for her children.” 预测：她为孩子写故事、编诗。

4

Compare with the choices · 对照选项

C: “creates stories and poems for her children”: write / made up → creates. B and D add “favorite,” which the text never says. C 同义改写；B、D 加了 favorite。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Opposite: she did not spend her time waiting for “dull ladies” to visit.
B ✗ Birthdays are one occasion for her poems; the text never says they are her favorite.
D ✗ She reads to them, but nothing says it is her favorite activity.

Answer: C. “She used to write stories for them … and she always made up funny pieces of poetry for their birthdays.” → She creates stories and poems for her children. 她给孩子写故事、编诗。

Worked Example 3

Form 3 · Module 2 · Question 9分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Why are ecologists worried about Pando?” Look for the reason after “worried.” 关键词 worried，找担心的内容。

2

Locate the key lines · 定位

“ecologists are worried that its growth is declining in part because of grazing by animals.” 担心的是：生长在下降。

3

Predict · 预测

“Pando is growing more slowly than before.” 预测：长得比以前慢。

4

Compare with the choices · 对照选项

A: “It isn’t growing at the same rate it used to” = growth is declining. B is too strong: deer eat young trees, so young trees still exist. A 同义改写；B 说得太绝对。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ Too strong: deer eat young trees, so Pando does still produce them.
C ✗ Fences are a proposed solution to protect young trees; they are not blocking growth now.
D ✗ Nothing is said about the root system being unable to support more trees.

Answer: A. “Ecologists are worried that its growth is declining in part because of grazing.” → It isn’t growing at the same rate it used to. 担心它的生长在减缓。

Worked Example 4

Form 3 · Module 1 · Question 11分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“What is true about Elinor?” A description of her character. 问 Elinor 的性格特点。

2

Locate the key lines · 定位

“strength of understanding, and coolness of judgment,” which qualified her, “though only nineteen, to be the counsellor of her mother.” She also knew how to govern her strong feelings. though only nineteen = 虽然只有十九岁。

3

Predict · 预测

“She is very wise and calm for someone so young.” 预测：年纪轻轻却很成熟稳重。

4

Compare with the choices · 对照选项

D: “remarkably mature for her age” = wise and calm though only nineteen. B is the opposite: she controls her feelings. D 正确；B 与原文相反。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ She advises her mother and often counteracts her impulses; the text never says she fails to change her mind.
B ✗ Opposite: she “knew how to govern” her strong feelings.
C ✗ Nothing says she judges her mother a bad role model.

Answer: D. Elinor has “strength of understanding, and coolness of judgment,” which qualified her, “though only nineteen, to be the counsellor of her mother.” She also controls her strong feelings, which her mother has “yet to learn.” → She is remarkably mature for her age. 年仅十九岁就能做母亲的顾问 → 少年老成。

Wrong answers

How wrong detail choices are built

Not in the text

Sounds reasonable or is true in real life (“the gravity of Mars”), but the text does not say it.

原文没说，即使常识上对。

Opposite

Reverses a detail: “too large” when the text says the blades had to be longer.

与原文相反。

Too strong

Adds favorite, anymore, always, all: “isn’t producing young trees anymore.”

加了绝对化的词。

Right words, wrong role

Uses a word from the text with the wrong meaning: fences are a solution, not the cause.

用原文的词，但角色或关系错了。

Summary

What to remember about Details细节理解要点

01 · 识别题干

Stems: According to the text…, Based on the text…, Which choice best describes… 2–3 per test.

02 · 定位步骤

Three steps: read the question (key words) → locate the sentence → paraphrase and match.

03 · 同义改写

The correct choice says the same thing in different words; it rarely copies the text.

04 · 有限推断

“Most likely agree / most reasonably inferred” versions: combine two stated facts and take only one small step.

05 · 排除干扰

Cut choices that are not in the text, opposite, too strong, or use the text’s words in the wrong role.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
