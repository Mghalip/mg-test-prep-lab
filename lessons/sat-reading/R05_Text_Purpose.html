What is it?

What is a Text Purpose question?

Definition

You choose the main rhetorical purpose of the text: what the author is trying to do with it. Every choice begins with “To …”: To describe…, To explain…, To argue…, To praise…

Which choice best states the main purpose of the text?

问作者写这段文字的主要目的。选项都以 To… 开头。1–3 题，常和结构题、功能题相邻。

The verb

Read the verb first

AttitudeVerbsUse when the text…
Neutraldescribe · depict · portray · explain · present · discuss · recount · illustrateshows a scene, a person, a process, or a finding without judging it
Positivepraise · celebrate · encourage · defend · express admirationclearly approves of something
Negativecriticize · warn · question · challenge · refute · mockclearly disapproves or argues against something
Argumentargue · assert · suggest · cast doubt on · call attention totakes a position and supports it

Step 1: the attitude

Is the author positive, negative, or neutral? A strong verb (refute, warn, praise) needs strong evidence in the text.

先判断态度：褒、贬、中性。强烈的动词需要原文有强烈的语气。

Step 2: the object

“To describe what?” The object must cover the whole text, not a detail from one sentence.

再看宾语：必须覆盖全文，不能只是一句话的细节。

Two kinds of texts

Purpose in informational and literary texts

Informational texts

Look at the last sentence and the turning point. Typical purposes:

  • to describe an experiment whose results cast doubt on a hypothesis
  • to explain that something thrived, and why
  • to discuss someone’s invention or discovery

信息类：注意转折和最后一句结论。

Literary texts

Ask: who is the main character, and what does the passage show about them? Typical purposes:

  • to portray a character’s feelings (often mixed or conflicted)
  • to depict a setting as characters wait for something
  • to praise someone, or to encourage someone

文学类：抓主要人物，作者想展示他/她的什么：性格、情感、处境。

How to approach it

Four steps for Text Purpose

1

Read the whole text; find the main point and the turn

What is the text mostly about, and where does it change direction (but, however, yet)? 读全文，找主旨和转折。

2

Decide the attitude

Positive, negative, neutral, or arguing a point? 判断作者态度。

3

Write “To + verb + object” in your own words

“To show that the shop owner both dislikes and is proud of the picture.” 自己先写一个 To… 的答案。

4

Check verb, object, and scope of each choice

Cut choices with the wrong verb (too strong), the wrong object, or only one detail. 逐项检查动词、宾语和范围。

Skill Builder

From “what it says” to “what it does”

Mini passage 1 (informational)

For years, gardeners in the northern United States welcomed earthworms, since worms help farm soil. However, many northern forests had no earthworms until people carried them in. There, the worms eat the thick layer of fallen leaves that young plants need. As a result, some native wildflowers are disappearing wherever the worms spread.

Q: Which choice best states the main purpose of the text?

Think aloud · 出声思考

  1. 1 · Content of each sentence. S1 old belief (worms are good) → S2 However: forests had no worms → S3 worms eat leaves → S4 result: wildflowers disappear. 先逐句说内容：旧看法 → 转折 → 原因 → 结果。
  2. 2 · Where is the weight? After However. The old belief is only the set-up. 重点在 However 之后，第一句只是铺垫。
  3. 3 · Attitude. Informative, a little worried. No one is blamed. 态度：说明为主，略带担忧，没有批评谁。
  4. 4 · Predict “To + verb + object”. To explain that earthworms, usually seen as helpful, can harm some forests. 自己先写：说明通常被认为有益的蚯蚓会伤害某些森林。
Skill Builder

Literary texts: what does the author show about the character?

Mini passage 2 (literary)

Lin had practiced for the recital all year. When her name was called, her hands turned cold, and she wished she could hide behind the curtain. Yet as the first notes filled the hall, she saw her teacher smiling in the front row, and her shoulders slowly relaxed.

Q: Main purpose? → To portray a character whose nervousness gives way to calm. 先找人物（Lin）和变化（紧张 → 放松），转折词 Yet 是关键。

Four traps in purpose choices (Mini passage 1)

Trap · 错误特征Wrong choiceWhy it is wrong
Content, not function
只翻译内容		“Earthworms eat fallen leaves.”A fact, not an action. A purpose answer starts with To + verb. 只说了内容，没说作者在做什么。
Too narrow
只覆盖局部		“To describe how earthworms help farm soil”Only sentence 1, the set-up. 只对应第一句铺垫。
Wrong action (too strong)
动作错误		“To criticize gardeners for spreading earthworms”The author explains; no one is blamed. 原文没有批评，动词太强。
Half right
半对半错		“To explain that earthworms harm forests and to recommend removing them”First half fits; the second half adds a recommendation. 前半对，后半凭空加了建议。

Action verbs: describe 描述 · explain 解释 · illustrate 举例说明 · emphasize 强调 · challenge 质疑 · qualify 限定 · portray 刻画 · praise 赞扬. Check the verb and its object. 动词对了还要核对对象：解释“什么”？

Quick Check

Quick check · Name the purpose小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.Many people think bats are blind. In fact, most bats see well and also use sound to find food. → To…?
To correct a common belief about bats. 纠正一个常见误解。
2.The old bridge looked weak, yet it carried trucks for ninety years without a crack. → To…?
To emphasize that the bridge was stronger than it looked. 强调桥比看起来更结实。
3.Maya smiled at the award, but her eyes kept returning to her friend, who had lost. → To…?
To portray Maya’s mixed feelings (happy, but sorry for her friend). 刻画矛盾的心情。
4.Your patience with every student, your careful notes—no teacher has given more. → To…?
To praise a teacher. 赞美。
5.The study found that the drug worked, but only in mice; human trials have not begun. → To…?
To qualify a finding (it is limited to mice). 限定：结论只适用于老鼠。
6.Choice: “To argue that all cities should ban cars.” Text: one city closed one street to cars and noise fell. Good choice?
No: too strong / too broad. The text only describes one result. 动词和范围都太大。

Form 4 · Module 1 · Question 5分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Main purpose” of the whole text. This is a literary text, so ask: what does the author show about the shop owner? 问全文目的；文学文本，看作者要展示店主的什么。

2

Locate the key lines · 定位

Before And yet: the picture “failed to fit in”; he fumes, “Don’t belong in here.” After: he “was secretly proud of his acquisition.” And yet 前后情感相反：嫌弃 vs. 暗自骄傲。

3

Predict · 预测

“To show that the owner both dislikes and is proud of the picture”: two opposite feelings at once. 自己预测：同时有两种相反的感情。

4

Compare with the choices · 对照选项

“Conflicted feelings about the new picture” = two opposite feelings. Choice A matches both sides of And yet. conflicted = 矛盾的，正好对应 And yet 前后。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ The young man who sold the picture is mentioned once; nothing shows resentment toward him.
C ✗ His other pictures (cats, dogs, landscapes) are listed only to show the new picture does not fit; nothing says he prizes them.
D ✗ The text says the picture does not fit, but it does not explain how it differs from the others. The focus is the owner’s feelings.

Answer: A. Before And yet: the picture “failed to fit in,” and he fumes, “Don’t belong in here.” After: he “was secretly proud of his acquisition.” He has two opposite feelings at once. → To reveal the shop owner’s conflicted feelings about the new picture. And yet 前：嫌它不合适；后：暗自骄傲 → 复杂矛盾的感情。

Worked Example 3

Form 5 · Module 1 · Question 7分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Main purpose” of an informational text. Watch the first sentence, the turn, and the last sentence. 信息类：看首句、转折、末句。

2

Locate the key lines · 定位

S1: newspapers “flourished in cities across Texas.” San Antonio had 11; “But El Paso surpassed all other cities” with 22. Last sentence: why El Paso (border, many Spanish speakers). 首句：全州繁荣；But 之后：El Paso 最多；末句：原因。

3

Predict · 预测

“To show that Spanish newspapers did well in Texas, and best of all in El Paso (and why).” 预测：得州整体繁荣，El Paso 尤其突出。

4

Compare with the choices · 对照选项

B: “thrived in Texas and especially in El Paso during the late 1800s” keeps both the whole state and the focus city. B 同时覆盖全州和重点城市。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Newspapers of today are never mentioned.
C ✗ The text compares the numbers in two cities; it never says El Paso papers influenced San Antonio papers.
D ✗ The text is about newspapers in Texas cities; nothing about their popularity in Mexico.

Answer: B. Sentence 1: Spanish-language newspapers “flourished” in Texas. San Antonio had 11, “But El Paso surpassed all other cities” with 22, and the text explains why (border location, many Spanish speakers). → To explain that these newspapers thrived in Texas, especially in El Paso. 得州西语报纸繁荣，尤其是 El Paso。

Form 1 · Module 1 · Question 11分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

Purpose of a poem. Who speaks to whom? The speaker addresses the poet Dunbar (“Thou”). 诗中说话人在对诗人 Dunbar 说话。

2

“Stroke of mighty pen” (admiring tone). He “read the hearts and souls of men” and “the language of the flowers … the little brook.” 语气赞美；他读懂了人心，也读懂了花和小溪。

B ✗ “Read” here is figurative: understanding hearts and flowers, not reading many books.

Answer: A. The speaker admires Dunbar (“stroke of mighty pen”). He “read the hearts and souls of men” (understands people) and “the language of the flowers … the little brook” (understands nature). → To praise a writer for being perceptive about people and nature. 语气赞美：他读懂了人心，也读懂了自然。

Wrong answers

How wrong purpose choices are built

Only a detail

True, but covers one sentence: “To call attention to von Ahn’s book-digitizing project.”

只是细节，不覆盖全文。

Wrong verb / attitude

“To warn” for an encouraging poem; “determination” for vague thoughts.

动词或语气不对，常常是说得太重。

Something not there

“today’s newspapers,” “competing hypotheses,” “an afternoon spent together.”

原文没有的内容。

Half the picture

Captures only one side of a contrast: the shop owner’s anger without his pride.

只说了转折的一边。

Summary

What to remember about Text Purpose写作目的要点

01 · 识别题干

Stem: Which choice best states the main purpose of the text? Every choice starts with “To + verb.”

02 · 目的与内容

Purpose = what the text does; it is one step more abstract than the main idea.

03 · 动词与对象

Check the verb (attitude: positive, negative, neutral, arguing) and the object (must cover the whole text).

04 · 文学类文本

In literary texts, focus on what the passage shows about the main character; watch for mixed feelings after but / yet.

05 · 常见陷阱

Common traps: a true detail, a verb that is too strong, something not in the text, only one side of a contrast.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
