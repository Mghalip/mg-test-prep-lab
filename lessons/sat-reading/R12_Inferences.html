LESSON 12

Inferences

Complete the last sentence with the conclusion the text logically leads to
用短文推出的结论补全最后一句
MAP THE ARGUMENTList the premises: what is believed, what was found, what is ruled out.
READ THE SIGNALTherefore / thus / suggesting that: what must follow from all the premises?
STAY SAFEChoose the conclusion that follows from every premise and goes no further.
What is it?

What is an Inference question?

Definition

The text ends with a blank, usually after therefore, thus, suggesting that, the team concluded that. You choose the conclusion that most logically completes the text.

Which choice most logically completes the text?

推断题只有这一种问法：补全最后一句（结论）。每套 3–5 题，排在阅读部分的最后，往往最难。

Inference vs. Details

Details (Lesson 8)Inference (this lesson)
Answerstated in the textnot stated: it is the missing conclusion
Your joblocate and paraphraseconnect the premises and decide what must follow
Forma questiona blank at the end

细节题答案在原文里；推断题答案是原文没写出的结论，要把前提连起来推出来。

The structure

Premises lead to a conclusion

The premises are the information given. The conclusion must follow from that information without adding an unsupported assumption.

Common patterns · 常见模式与小例子

Belief + counterevidenceQuestion or narrow the belief.
反例限制原观点。例：“只有夜间活动”遇到白天活动的记录 → 不能说只在夜间活动。
B occurs without AA is not required for B.
没有 A 也出现 B → A 不是必要条件。例：没有昆虫也完成授粉 → 授粉不一定依靠昆虫。
Two facts seem to conflictFind an explanation consistent with both.
解释两者为何能同时成立。例：读者变多、卖出的册数变少 → 可能有更多人借阅或共享。
Old assumption + new findingRevise the part the finding challenges.
修改被新发现挑战的部分。例：更早年代已存在同类工具 → 该技术的起源可能比原先认为的更早。
Study result + limited scopeKeep the conclusion within the study’s limits.
保留条件与范围。例：只测试一种植物 → 不能直接推广为对所有植物都有效。

The “missing piece”

What conclusion fits all the premises?

先列出已知信息，再问：哪一项既符合全部前提，又没有多走一步？

Check three things:
1. Uses the relevant facts.
2. Contradicts none of them.
3. Adds no unsupported certainty.

左侧例子展示推理方向；实际答案仍须由完整题干支持，不能只凭“听起来合理”。

Barring = excluding a possibility.
If both are correct = keep both facts.

排除已否定的可能；保留题干要求同时成立的事实。

How to approach it

Four steps for Inference questions

1

Read the whole text and number the premises

① what some people believe ② what was found ③ what is ruled out… 读全文，给前提编号。

2

Watch the turn and the signal before the blank

However usually brings the key premise. Therefore / thus / suggesting tells you a conclusion follows. 注意 However 带出的关键前提和空格前的结论信号。

3

Predict the conclusion in plain words

“So the crater cannot be linked to the cooling yet.” 先自己说出结论。

4

Choose the choice that matches and goes no further

Cut choices that ignore a premise, go beyond the evidence (eliminates, proves, always), or bring in new topics. 排除忽略前提、推得过头、引入新话题的选项。

Skill Builder

An inference connects the facts you are given

Fact 1 + Fact 2 → Conclusion

An inference (推断) is a conclusion the text does not say directly, but that must follow from what it does say. No outside knowledge is needed.

Fact 1: The school library closes at 5 p.m. on Saturdays.

Fact 2: Lena arrived at the library at 6 p.m. last Saturday.

✅ Conclusion: Lena could not borrow a book in person that day.

❌ Lena forgot the closing time.Unsupported: maybe she knew. The text never tells us why she came late. 文中没说原因。

推断 = 把已知事实连起来得出结论，不加入原文没有的信息。

Signal words 信号词

Word中文What it tells you
however / but / yet然而the next fact challenges the old view
therefore / thus / so因此the blank is the conclusion
if / assuming如果 / 假设the conclusion must keep this condition
despite / although尽管a surprising result follows
only / not yet / some只 / 尚未 / 一些limits: do not go beyond them
suggests / implies表明 / 暗示a careful conclusion, not proof

先看信号词判断句子之间的关系（转折、因果、条件），再预测结论。

Skill Builder

Think aloud: facts → link → predict → test

Mini passage (teacher-written)

A small songbird builds its nests only in deep holes in old trees. In one forest, almost all the old trees were cut down in the 1990s. The young trees that replaced them are healthy, but young trees rarely have holes deep enough for nesting. These facts suggest that ______

A) the songbird probably has fewer places to nest in that forest than it did before the 1990s.

B) the young trees in the forest are less healthy than the old trees were.

C) the songbird will soon disappear from every forest in the world.

D) the people who cut the trees wanted to harm the songbird.

How a good reader thinks 思考过程

1 · Facts. Bird nests only in old trees with deep holes. Old trees are gone. Young trees rarely have deep holes. 事实：只在老树洞筑巢；老树被砍；新树很少有深洞。

2 · Link. “only” + “rarely” = few trees left that the bird can use. “只”和“很少”连起来 → 可用的树很少。

3 · Predict. The bird has lost most of its nesting places in this forest. 预测：这片林中鸟的筑巢地点大大减少。

4 · Test. A = prediction ✓. B reverses a fact (young trees are healthy). C: one forest → every forest (scope shift). D adds a motive the text never gives. B 与原文相反；C 范围扩大；D 凭空加入动机。

Skill Builder

Six traps in inference choices (songbird example)

Trap · 陷阱Example choiceWhat went wrong
Unsupported leap 推理跳跃“The loggers wanted to harm the bird.”Adds a new fact or motive.
Scope shift 范围扩大“…will disappear from every forest.”One forest → all forests.
Too strong 程度升级“The bird cannot nest anywhere in the forest.”The text says rarely, not never.
Reversal 方向倒置“Young trees are less healthy.”Opposite of the text.
Concept shift 概念偷换“The bird prefers to eat insects in old trees.”Changes nesting to eating.
Doesn’t complete 未完成论证“Forests contain many kinds of trees.”True but says nothing new.

Do not just pick the softest word. “May” cannot save a choice with no support, and a strong word is fine when the facts are strong. The evidence decides. 不要机械地选语气最弱的选项：may 救不了没有依据的内容；证据决定结论的强弱。

Quick Check

Quick check · Predict the conclusion小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.All the cookies in the jar were chocolate. The jar is now empty, and Sam has chocolate on his fingers. So, ___
Sam probably ate some of the cookies. (Not “Sam ate all of them”: others may have eaten some too.) “可能”吃了，不能说全吃了。
2.Plant X grows only in salty soil. The soil in Field 7 contains no salt. So, ___
Plant X is unlikely to grow in Field 7. 只长在盐土中，此地无盐 → 不太可能生长。
3.A drug worked in tests on mice. It has not yet been tested on humans. So, ___
We cannot yet conclude it works in humans. 尚未做人体试验 → 还不能下结论。
4.Scholars dated a letter to 1820 because of its paper. However, that paper was also sold until 1850. So, ___
The paper does not prove the letter is from 1820; it could be as late as 1850. 纸张卖到 1850 年 → 不能证明是 1820 年。
5.If a fish can recognize itself in a mirror, it will try to remove a mark seen on its own body. The fish tried to remove the mark. So, ___
This result is consistent with the fish recognizing itself. 结果与“能认出自己”一致，但不是证明。
6.A survey asked only students at one art school about their favorite subject. Most chose painting. So, ___
The result may not apply to students in general (special sample). 样本特殊 → 推广要谨慎。
Worked Example

Step 1 · Read the text and the question先读短文，再读题目在问什么

What is the question asking?

The blank comes after “suggesting that.” We need the conclusion that follows from the text about sauropod size and carbon dioxide.

空格前是 suggesting that，需要补出结论。

Worked Example

Step 2 · Find the evidence in the text回到短文，找到依据

1

Premise 1

Some researchers say sauropods became giant because of more plants, caused by high CO₂.

2

Premise 2 (after However)

There is no evidence of CO₂ spikes at the key moments of sauropod growth: when the first large ones appeared, when lineages grew bigger, when they reached their maximum size.

3

Connect

The giant sizes appeared without CO₂ increases. So the size increase did not need high CO₂.

4

Predict

“Sauropods’ growth in size did not depend on CO₂.”

Worked Example

Step 3 · Test every choice逐个检验选项

A
CUT ✗
The text mentions no CO₂ fluctuations affecting different lineages; this adds a new idea.
B
KEEP ✓
Large sizes evolved without CO₂ spikes → the evolution did not depend on increased CO₂.
C
CUT ✗
Opposite of the premise: there were no significant CO₂ spikes at these times.
D
CUT ✗
Goes beyond the text: nothing suggests higher CO₂ would have prevented their growth.

Answer: B. The However sentence was the key premise: the effect (giant size) happened without the supposed cause (CO₂ spikes). 结果出现时，所谓的原因并不存在 → 二者没有依赖关系。

Worked Example 2

Form 6 · Module 2 · Question 17分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Which choice most logically completes the text?” → the blank comes after “These results, in turn, suggest that”: it is the conclusion. 空格是结论，前面的事实就是前提。

2

Find the facts · 找前提

Fact 1: logging is often thought to be bad for conservation. Fact 2 (after However): plots with limited logging may be more robust than plots with no logging. 事实 1：人们认为采伐有害；事实 2（转折后）：有限采伐的林地可能更健康。

3

Predict · 预测

Fact 1 + Fact 2 → logging is not always bad: careful, limited logging may actually help forests. Keep the word “limited.” 预测：有限的采伐可能有益；保留“有限”这个条件。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

A: “logging may be useful for maintaining healthy forests, provided it is limited.” Same idea, same limit. A 与预测一致，并保留了 limited 条件。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ Other management strategies are never discussed.
C ✗ Nothing suggests the benefits will become hard to judge over time.
D ✗ Opposite: unlogged plots were less robust than limited-logging plots.

Answer: A. Premises: logging is often thought to be bad for conservation; however, plots with limited logging may be more robust than unlogged plots. Conclusion: logging may help keep forests healthy, provided it is limited. 有限采伐的林地更健康 → 有限采伐可能有益。

Worked Example 3

Form 4 · Module 1 · Question 18分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Read the question · 读题

“Most logically completes the text” → the blank ends a sentence that starts with “The fact that a cupid is shown…, therefore, ___.” We need what follows from the facts about the cupid. therefore 提示空格是结论：丘比特这一事实说明了什么？

2

Find the facts · 找前提

Fact 1: some scholars say the figure is Venus because Venus is linked with cupids. Fact 2 (but): Brain says cupids may also be linked with fishing in general. 事实 1：因为维纳斯与丘比特有关，所以认为是维纳斯；事实 2：丘比特也可能与“钓鱼”有关。

3

Predict · 预测

The cupid could be there because of the fishing, not because of Venus. So the cupid is not enough to prove she is Venus (but she still might be). 预测：丘比特不足以证明是维纳斯，但也不排除。

4

Compare with the choices · 对比选项

A: “is not conclusive evidence that the figure is Venus.” Matches. C (“eliminates the possibility”) is too strong. A 与预测一致；C 说“排除可能”，程度过强。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ Nothing is said about how often Venus was shown fishing.
C ✗ Too strong: Brain gives another possibility; she does not prove the figure is not Venus.
D ✗ Opposite: Brain’s idea makes the cupid easy to explain even if the figure is not Venus.

Answer: A. Premises: scholars say the figure is Venus because a cupid is nearby (Venus is linked with cupids). Brain: cupids may also be linked with fishing in general. So a cupid near a woman who is fishing could be about fishing, not Venus. → The cupid is not conclusive evidence that the figure is Venus. 丘比特也可能与“钓鱼”有关 → 不能据此断定是维纳斯。

1

Read the question · 读题

Wrong answers

How wrong inference choices are built

Too strong

The text says “not conclusive”; the choice says “eliminates the possibility.” Inferences are usually cautious.

推得太过：原文说“不能确定”，选项说“完全排除”。

Ignores a premise

Fits one sentence but conflicts with another (“the plant arrived in the last 3,000 years” ignores the 100,000-year split).

只照顾了一个前提，与另一个矛盾。

Opposite

Reverses the evidence: “unlogged forests are best,” “flew farther.”

与证据方向相反。

New topic

Brings in something the text never discusses: other strategies, adult tortoises, other regions.

引入原文没有的话题。

Summary

What to remember about Inferences推断要点

01 · 识别题干

Stem: Which choice most logically completes the text? The blank is the conclusion. 3–5 per test, at the end of the reading questions.

02 · 列出前提

Number the premises; the sentence after However is often the key one.

03 · 连接所有前提

Ask what must be true for all the premises to fit together; barring or if both are correct points to the remaining explanation.

04 · 预测结论

Predict in plain words, then choose the conclusion that follows from every premise.

05 · 排除干扰

Cut choices that are too strong, ignore a premise, reverse the evidence, or add a new topic.

06 · 30 秒自检

What does the question ask? Which words support your answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

识别任务 → 指出原文依据 → 排除一个易错选项。
