Grammar You Need

How to read the bullet notes · 怎么读学生笔记

Sample notes (teacher-made)

• Karl von Frisch was an Austrian scientist. who
• He studied how honeybees communicate. topic
• He placed dishes of sugar water at different distances from a hive and watched marked bees. method
• He found that bees do a “waggle dance” that tells other bees where food is. finding
• He received the Nobel Prize in 1973. extra

The question then says: “The student wants to present the study and its findings.”

What to know about the notes 笔记的特点

  • Each bullet (•) = one fact. Every fact is true.
  • So “true” is never enough: all four choices are true. The right one does the goal.
  • You do not need every bullet. A good sentence uses only the bullets the goal asks for.
  • Give each bullet a quick label: who (person), topic, method (how), finding (result), extra (background).

每个圆点是一条事实，而且都是真的，所以四个选项都“没说错”。正确答案是完成“目标”的那一个。读笔记时给每条贴标签：人物、主题、方法、发现、背景，这样一看目标就知道该用哪几条。

Grammar You Need

Goal words: what must the sentence do? · 目标动词

Goal wordMeaning · 中文The sentence must…Mini example (bee notes)
present the study / findingsshow, report 呈现、陈述name the study AND state exactly what the goal names (its findings / its method)Von Frisch’s study found that bees dance to show where food is.
introduce Xtell a new reader what X is 介绍name X and say what X is (identity)Karl von Frisch, an Austrian scientist, studied honeybees.
describe Xtell what X is like 描述give features of X (look, parts, how it works)The waggle dance tells other bees where food is.
explain (why / how / an advantage)give the reason or process 解释原因/过程use “because / so / by …” to show the reasonBees dance because they need to share where food is.
emphasize Xmake X the main point 强调put X in the main clause, not in a side detailIn 1973, von Frisch received the Nobel Prize for his work on bees.
identify / provide an examplename it exactly 指出、举例give exactly that one piece of informationOne example of animal communication is the bee dance.

目标动词决定句子“必须包含什么”。present 呈现；introduce 介绍（是什么/是谁）；describe 描述特征；explain 解释原因或过程；emphasize 强调（放在句子主干）；identify 指出。

Grammar You Need

Research words: study, method, findings · 研究类词汇

Words you will see 常见词

study / research研究（一项研究）
aim / goal / research question研究目的：想知道什么
method / methodology研究方法：怎么做的
findings / results研究发现：发现了什么
conclusion结论：研究者由此得出的判断
hypothesis假设：研究前的猜测

“present the study and its findings” 要写发现；“present the study and its methodology” 要写方法。二者常被互换做陷阱。

Goal: present the study and its findings

❌ Von Frisch studied honeybees by watching marked bees at sugar-water dishes.method only — no finding.

❌ Karl von Frisch, an Austrian scientist, won the Nobel Prize in 1973.background only — no study, no finding.

✅ Studying honeybees, Karl von Frisch found that bees do a “waggle dance” to tell other bees where food is.study ✓ + finding ✓

If the goal were “the study and its methodology,” the first sentence would be the best start.

目标里每个 and 都是一项“必须有”的内容。缺一项就错，哪怕句子很长、信息很多。

Grammar You Need

Familiar or unfamiliar audience? · 熟悉还是不熟悉的读者

Unfamiliar → identify X 必须介绍身份

An unfamiliar reader has never heard of X. Add a short label: who the person is or what the thing is.

Two common ways: a label before the name, or an appositive (同位语: a noun phrase between commas that renames X).

✅ Iraqi-British architect Zaha Hadid designed the Guangzhou Opera House.label before the name

✅ Zaha Hadid, an Iraqi-British architect, designed the Guangzhou Opera House.appositive

❌ Zaha Hadid designed the Guangzhou Opera House.An unfamiliar reader asks: “Who is Zaha Hadid?”

读者不熟悉 X → 句子里要简短交代 X 的身份（国籍、职业、是什么）。

Familiar → don’t explain X 不要再解释

A familiar reader already knows X. Use the words for the new information.

Goal: tell an audience familiar with Mo Yan about his 2012 award.

✅ Mo Yan won the 2012 Nobel Prize in Literature.

❌ Mo Yan, a Chinese novelist who wrote Red Sorghum, won the 2012 Nobel Prize in Literature.Explaining who Mo Yan is wastes words for this reader.

The best answer is not the longest. It does every part of the goal and nothing that works against it. 正确答案不是信息最多的，而是刚好完成目标的。

Quick Check

Quick check · Does it meet the goal? (bee notes)只看目标，逐项核对

1.Goal: introduce von Frisch to an unfamiliar audience.
“Von Frisch studied how honeybees communicate.”
✗ It never says who he is. Add: “Austrian scientist Karl von Frisch…”
2.Goal: introduce von Frisch to an unfamiliar audience.
“Austrian scientist Karl von Frisch studied how honeybees communicate.”
✓ name + identity + what he did.
3.Goal: present the study’s methodology.
“Von Frisch found that bees use a waggle dance.”
✗ That is a finding, not the method.
4.Goal: present the study’s methodology.
“Von Frisch watched marked bees visit sugar-water dishes placed at different distances.”
✓ It says how the study was done.
5.Goal: emphasize the award.
“Von Frisch, who won a Nobel Prize in 1973, studied bees.”
✗ The award is hidden in a side clause; the main point is “studied bees.”
6.Goal: emphasize the award.
“In 1973, von Frisch received the Nobel Prize for his work on bee communication.”
✓ The award is the main clause.
7.Goal: describe the waggle dance.
“The waggle dance was studied by von Frisch.”
✗ It does not say what the dance is like or does.
8.Goal: present the study and its findings to an audience familiar with von Frisch.
“Von Frisch found that bees dance to show where food is.”
✓ Finding stated; no need to explain who he is.

The audience

Familiar or unfamiliar?

Teacher-made notes: Maya Chen is a biologist. She developed a new microscope in 2024.

Unfamiliar audience · 需要介绍身份

Goal: introduce Chen’s invention to readers unfamiliar with her.

✓ Biologist Maya Chen developed a new microscope in 2024.
✓ Maya Chen, a biologist, developed a new microscope in 2024.
✗ Maya Chen developed a new microscope in 2024.

前两句交代人物身份；最后一句没有说明 Chen 是谁。

Familiar audience · 聚焦新信息

Goal: tell readers familiar with Chen when she developed the microscope.

✓ Chen developed the microscope in 2024.
✗ Maya Chen is a biologist.

读者已熟悉她，重点是时间。只介绍身份的句子没有完成目标。

The right sentence meets the stated goal. Extra identity information is not automatically wrong, but it cannot replace the information the goal requires.

按目标判断信息是否必要；不能仅因为出现身份介绍，就判选项错误。

How to approach it

Four steps

1

Skip the notes; read the goal

Underline the goal verb and every “and”: present the study and its findings. 先读目标，不读笔记。

2

Turn the goal into a checklist

Study ✓ findings ✓ · Name the work ✓ identify the artist ✓ 把目标拆成清单。

3

Test each choice against the checklist

Cross out any choice that misses an item, focuses on the wrong subject, or explains what the audience already knows. 逐项核对，缺项即排除。

4

Use the notes only to settle a tie

You rarely need to read all the bullets. 笔记只在需要时查看。

Worked Example 2

Form 1 · Module 1 · Question 32分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the question · 看目标

Skip the notes. Goal: “present the study and its findings.” Two items on the checklist: ① the 2021 study ② what it found. 先看目标：研究 + 研究发现，两项都要有。

2

Label the notes · 给笔记贴标签

Bullet 1 = background (what pterosaurs are). Bullet 2 = the study. Bullet 3 = her early doubt. Bullet 4 = method (microscope, growth lines). Bullet 5 = finding/conclusion: the bones belonged to juveniles. 第 4 条是方法，第 5 条是结论（幼年翼龙）。

3

State the rule · 规则

Every “and” in the goal is a must-have. A sentence that gives only the study, only the method, or only the background fails, even though it is true. 目标里的每个 and 都必须完成；只写研究或方法都不算。

4

Apply it · 逐项核对

A: study + early doubt (no finding). B: study only. C: study + method + background. D: study ✓ + growth lines → “belonged to juveniles” ✓. → D. 只有 D 写出了研究结论。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Gives the study but only her initial uncertainty, not the finding.
B ✗ Mentions the study but gives no finding.
C ✗ Describes the method, not the finding.

Answer: D. Checklist: ① the 2021 study ② its finding (the bones had few growth lines → belonged to juveniles). Only D states the conclusion. 必须写出研究结论：骨头属于幼年翼龙。

Summary

What to remember about Rhetorical Synthesis I修辞综合 I 要点

01 · 先读目标

Read the goal first; the notes come second.

02 · 拆解目标

Turn the goal into a checklist: every “and” is a separate item (study and findings; study and methodology).

03 · 判断受众

Unfamiliar audience → briefly identify the person or thing. Familiar audience → do not explain it.

04 · 错误选项

Every choice is accurate. Wrong choices miss part of the goal or focus on the wrong subject.

05 · 最终标准

The answer is not the one with the most facts; it is the one that does exactly the job.
