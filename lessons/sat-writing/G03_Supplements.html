Warm-up · 热身

Extra or essential?插入成分怎么加标点？

Extra (nonessential) information is set off on both sides; essential information gets no punctuation.

Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
______ won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1993.

Grammar You Need

A supplement = extra information inside a sentence

A supplement 补充成分 / 插入语 is a word group that adds extra detail. If you remove it, the core (subject + verb + object) is still a complete, correct sentence.

Kind · 种类Example (supplement crossed out)
Appositive 同位语
a noun that renames the noun before it		My brother, a civil engineer, lives in Boston.
which / who clause 非限定定语从句The library, which opened in 1902, was renovated.
-ed / -ing phrase 分词短语The book, written by my mom, became a bestseller.
however-type word 连接副词This theory, however, is difficult to prove.
dates, translations, notesLouise Bennett (1919–2006) was a Jamaican poet.

Cross-out test: cross out the part. Is what is left still a complete sentence that means the same thing? Yes → it is a supplement and needs punctuation on both sides. 划掉测试：删去这部分后，剩下的句子仍然完整、意思不变 → 它是插入语，两边都要加标点。

Grammar You Need

Essential (限定) or nonessential (非限定)?

Essential → NO commas

It tells us which one. Without it, we do not know who or what the sentence means. Pattern: big group → smaller group.

✅ The students who studied passed the test.only the students who studied, not all students

✅ The books that were damaged were replaced.

❌ The students, who studied, passed the test.now it says ALL the students studied: meaning changes

限定信息：说明“哪一个/哪一些”，删掉后指代不清或意思改变，不加逗号。大范围 → 小范围。

Nonessential → a PAIR of marks

The noun is already clear (a name, “my mother”, “the Eiffel Tower”). The part just adds a fact.

✅ Dr. Li, who studies coastal ecosystems, will speak tonight.

✅ Jack Chen, a Beijing-born artist, won the prize.

❌ Dr. Li who studies coastal ecosystems will speak tonight.Dr. Li is already one specific person: the clause is extra

非限定信息：名词已经很明确（人名、地名），后面只是补充，两边用成对的逗号。

that vs which: that = essential, never with commas. which after a comma = nonessential. ❌ “The book, that I borrowed, was useful.” ✅ “The book, which I borrowed yesterday, was useful.” that 引导的从句前面永远不加逗号；非限定从句用 which / who。

Grammar You Need

Supplements come in pairs: open + close · 成对出现

Three ways to set off extra info

✅ The library, established in 1902, is historic.commas: normal

✅ The library—established in 1902—is historic.dashes: stronger, more emphasis

✅ The library (established in 1902) is historic.parentheses: weaker, a side note

All three mean the same. The SAT tests only that the two marks match.

逗号、破折号、括号都可以夹住插入语，作用相同；关键是“开头用什么，结尾就用什么”。

Three common mistakes

❌ The library, established in 1902—is historic.mixed marks: comma … dash

❌ The library, established in 1902 is historic.only one comma: the supplement never closes

❌ The library, established, in 1902, is historic.the closing mark is in the wrong place: it must come right where the extra part ends

How to fix: find where the extra part starts (look at the mark already there), find where it ends (just before the main verb returns), and use the same mark.

找插入语的开头（看已有的标点），再找结尾（主干动词回来之前），结尾用同样的标点。

Grammar You Need

Title + name: comma or no comma? · 头衔 + 人名

No commas: the name tells WHICH one

A job title or general noun without “the/a” right before a name works like one long name.

✅ Biologist Maya Chen showed that light affects plant growth.

✅ Her novel Gingerbread offers a surprising twist.which novel? Gingerbread → essential

✅ the chemical aluminum oxide is hard.

❌ Biologist, Maya Chen showed that light affects plant growth.one comma splits a single noun

❌ Biologist, Maya Chen, showed that light affects plant growth.cross out the name → “biologist showed…” is broken (no the/a)

头衔（前面没有 the/a）+ 人名、作品类型 + 作品名：名字说明“是哪一个”，整体当作一个名词，不加逗号。

Commas: name first, then a description

When the name comes first, the description after it (often starting with a / an / the) is an appositive: extra information.

✅ Maya Chen, a plant cell biologist, showed that light affects plant growth.

✅ Louise Bennett, an influential Jamaican poet, wrote in Creole.

❌ Louise Bennett, an influential Jamaican poet wrote in Creole.the second comma is missing

人名在前、描述在后（常以 a / an / the 开头）：描述是同位语补充，两边加逗号。

Grammar You Need

Inside the core: no punctuation · 主干内部不加标点

Never split these pairs

❌ The students in the back row, laughed.subject | verb — even when the subject is long

❌ She likes, pizza.verb | object

❌ She put the book on, the table.preposition | its object

❌ He said, that he was tired.verb | that-clause

❌ The novel explores themes of: healing and memory.
✓ The novel explores themes of healing and memory.colon after an incomplete part

主语和谓语之间、动词和宾语之间、介词和宾语之间、that 前后，都不能加标点（除非是成对插入语）。

Introductory phrase: one comma at its END

A phrase or clause before the subject is followed by one comma, right where the main clause begins.

✅ After the lecture, we left.

✅ Powered by solar energy during the day, the lights glow at night.

❌ After, the lecture we left.
✓ After the lecture, we left.Keep the prepositional phrase together; put the comma at its end.

句首短语/从句：只在它结束、主句开始的地方加一个逗号，不要在短语内部加。

Quick check · Pair, none, or one?小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.Novelist, Toni Morrison won the Nobel Prize in 1993.
✗ title + name = one noun. Fix: Novelist Toni Morrison won…
2.Toni Morrison, a novelist from Ohio, won the Nobel Prize.
✓ name first, then an appositive in a pair of commas.
3.The city—founded in 1820, grew quickly.
✗ mixed marks. Fix: 1820—grew
4.Students who arrive late must sign in.
✓ essential: tells which students. No commas.
5.The Amazon River, which flows through Brazil, is very long.
✓ the river is already named: the which-clause is extra.
6.The report, that the team wrote, was long.
✗ “that” never takes commas. Fix: The report that the team wrote was long.
7.The results of the long study, surprised everyone.
✗ comma between subject (results) and verb (surprised).

✗ comma after the preposition “of”. Fix: themes of loss and hope.
Two kinds of information

Essential and nonessential elements

Essential (restrictive 限定) · no punctuation

Identifies which one we mean. Removing it changes the meaning or leaves the sentence unclear.

Books that have won awards are often more popular.

Plant cell biologist Maya Chen showed that light affects plant growth.

限定信息：说明“是哪一个”，不能删，不加标点。

Nonessential (非限定) · set off on both sides

Adds extra information about something already identified. The sentence still works without it.

Our town’s library, established in the 1900s, is a historic building.

Our town’s library — established in the 1900s — is a historic building.

Our town’s library (established in the 1900s) is a historic building.

非限定信息：可以删掉，两边用成对的逗号、破折号或括号。

Test: cover the part. Does the sentence still say the same thing about the same person or thing? Yes → nonessential, set it off. No → essential, no punctuation. Never mix marks: comma … dash ✗. 遮住测试；成对标点不能混用。

Titles and names

Titles and names: the most common SAT pattern

PatternPunctuationExample
title (no the / a) + namenone
Biologist Maya Chen studies plants.
Portrait artist Leo Park painted the mayor.
the + general noun + specific namenone when the name identifies which one
The novel River Light won a prize.
The city Riverton lies beside a river.
name, + description,commas (the description is extra)
Maya Chen, a plant biologist, led the study.
Leo Park, a portrait artist, painted the mayor.

Title + name stays together

Biologist Maya Chen studies plants. The title and name form one noun phrase; do not separate them with a comma.

头衔 + 人名构成一个名词短语，中间不加逗号。

An internal supplement needs a pair

Maya Chen, a plant biologist, studies plants. Remove “a plant biologist”: the main sentence still works.

句中插入描述要成对隔开；删掉后仍是 Maya Chen studies plants.

No punctuation zones

Where punctuation never goes

Between…Wrong ✗Right ✓
subject and verbThe students in the back row, laughed.The students in the back row laughed.
verb and objectHer poems popularized; a local dialect.Her poems popularized a local dialect.
preposition and its objectThe novel explores themes of, loss and hope.The novel explores themes of loss and hope.
noun and an essential clauseThe book, that I borrowed is useful.The book that I borrowed is useful.
verb and an integrated quotationThe critic called the film, “a quiet masterpiece.”The critic called the film “a quiet masterpiece.”

Do not insert a lone comma or semicolon between tightly connected sentence parts. Paired supplements are different: The students, tired after practice, laughed.

不要用单个标点切断主干。成对插入语另当别论：主干仍是 The students laughed.

How to approach it

Four steps for supplements

1

Find the core: subject, verb, object

Cross out what might be extra. 先找主干。

2

Is the blank inside the core?

Between subject and verb, verb and object, preposition and object, title and name → no punctuation. 空格在主干内部 → 不加标点。

3

Is there an extra element?

Find where it starts. It must end with the same mark: comma…comma, dash…dash. 有插入成分 → 找它的开头，结尾用同样的标点。

4

Introductory phrase?

A phrase before the main clause (“Powered with energy … during the day”) ends with one comma, at its end, not inside it. 句首短语：只在结尾加一个逗号。

Worked Example 2

Form 6 · Module 1 · Question 20分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“popularized; / popularized, / popularized / popularized:”: the word is the same; only the mark after it changes. 只差动词后的标点。

2

Find the core · 找主干

“Her innovative poems popularized the use of Jamaican Creole…” Subject = poems, verb = popularized, object = the use. 主语 poems，谓语 popularized，宾语 the use。

3

Where is the blank? · 空格位置

The blank sits between the verb (popularized) and its object (the use). There is no extra part here to set off. 空格在动词和宾语之间，这里没有插入语。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Verb | object is a no-punctuation zone. A colon also needs a complete sentence before it, and “Her poems popularized” is not complete → no punctuation. 动宾之间不加任何标点。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ A semicolon between verb and object splits one clause in two.
B ✗ A comma separates the verb from its object.
D ✗ A colon must follow a complete clause; “Her innovative poems popularized” is not complete without its object.

Answer: C. “Her innovative poems (S) popularized (V) the use of Jamaican Creole (O).” The blank is between the verb and its object → no punctuation. 动宾之间不加标点。

Worked Example 3

Form 4 · Module 2 · Question 20分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“of / of, / of— / of:”: what, if anything, goes after the word of? 考 of 后面加不加标点。

2

Find the core · 找主干

In her two major series…, “painter Howardena Pindell explored themes of healing, self-discovery, and memory…” 主干：Pindell explored themes。

3

Where is the blank? · 空格位置

of is a preposition 介词; its object is the list “healing, self-discovery, and memory”. The blank is between the preposition and its object. 空格在介词 of 和它的宾语之间。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Never put a comma, dash, or colon right after a preposition. “explored themes of” is not a complete sentence, so a colon is also impossible → no punctuation. 介词后不加标点。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ A comma after a preposition separates it from its object.
C ✗ A dash after a preposition breaks the phrase.
D ✗ A colon needs a complete clause before it; “explored themes of” is not complete.

Answer: A. “explored themes of healing, self-discovery, and memory”: of is a preposition and “healing, self-discovery, and memory” is its object. → no punctuation after “of.” 介词和宾语之间不加标点。

Form 2 · Module 1 · Question 28分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“Gingerbread— / Gingerbread, / Gingerbread / Gingerbread:”: which mark, if any, comes after the title? 考书名后的标点。

2

Find the core · 找主干

“while her 2019 novel Gingerbread offers a delicious twist…” The subject is her 2019 novel Gingerbread; the verb is offers. 主语 novel Gingerbread，谓语 offers。

3

Essential or extra? · 限定还是补充

“her 2019 novel” + the title: the title tells which novel, so it is essential, like title + name. And there is no opening comma or dash before “Gingerbread” to pair with. 书名说明“哪一本”，是限定信息；前面也没有逗号/破折号需要配对。

The blank is between the subject and its verb → no punctuation. One comma or one dash would split subject from verb. 主谓之间不加标点。

A ✗ A single dash separates the subject from its verb.
B ✗ A single comma separates the subject from its verb.
D ✗ A colon cannot follow an incomplete clause.

Answer: C. Subject: “her 2019 novel Gingerbread” (title + name = one unit). Verb: “offers.” Nothing may come between the subject and its verb → no punctuation. 主谓之间不加标点。

Summary

What to remember about supplements插入成分要点

01 · 必要与非必要

Essential information (which one?) → no punctuation. Nonessential information → set off on both sides.

02 · 成对标点

Use matching marks: two commas, two dashes, or two parentheses. Never one comma alone; never mix.

03 · 头衔与姓名

Title (no article) + name = one unit → no commas: critic Stina Chyn.

04 · 不可断开的位置

No punctuation between subject–verb, verb–object, preposition–object, or before an essential that clause.

05 · 句首短语

An introductory phrase gets one comma, at its end.
