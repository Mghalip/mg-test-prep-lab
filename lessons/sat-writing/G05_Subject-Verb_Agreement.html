LESSON G05

Subject-Verb Agreement

Find the real subject and make the verb agree with it
找到真正的主语，让谓语动词与它的单复数一致
SPOT ITThe choices are forms of one verb: is / are, allows / allow, has / have.
FIND THE SUBJECTCross out prepositional phrases, descriptive phrases, and clauses between subject and verb.
MATCHSingular subject → verb with -s (is, has); plural subject → verb without -s (are, have).
Grammar You Need

Singular, plural, and the strange “-s” rule

Nouns: one or more than one? 名词的单复数

Singular = one thing. Plural = two or more things. Most nouns become plural by adding -s.

Singular 单数Plural 复数
a student, a box, a citystudents, boxes, cities
a child, a person, a mousechildren, people, mice (irregular 不规则)
he, she, it, this, onethey, we, these, those

单数 = 一个；复数 = 两个或以上。名词复数一般加 -s，但 children、people、mice 等是不规则复数，没有 -s 也是复数。

Verbs: the -s goes the other way 动词正好相反

In the present tense, a singular subject takes a verb with -s; a plural subject takes a verb without -s.

✅ The dog barks.one dog → barks (-s on the verb)

✅ The dogs bark.many dogs (-s on the noun) → bark

❌ The dogs barks.plural subject + singular verb

❌ My sister study biology.one sister → studies

记住：名词加 -s 变复数，动词加 -s 却表示单数。一个句子里通常只有一个 -s：The dog barks / The dogs bark。

Most SAT agreement questions use exactly these pairs: is / are, was / were, has / have, does / do, or a verb with / without -s.

SAT 主谓一致题的选项几乎都是这几组：is/are、was/were、has/have、does/do，或者动词加不加 -s。

Examples 例句

✅ The result is surprising. / The results are surprising.

✅ The cable was finished in 1866. / The cables were finished.

✅ The museum has grown. / The museums have grown.

❌ Her writing have been honored.writing is singular → has been

Good news: a simple past verb like walked, found, used has only one form, so it can never be wrong for agreement.

好消息：一般过去时（walked、used）单复数同形，不存在主谓一致问题；只有 was/were 要区分。

Grammar You Need

Cross out the extras, then find the true subject · 找真正主语

Examples 例句

✅ The collection of old photographs is valuable.What is valuable? the collection (one) → is

✅ The triangle representing the mountain is small.-ing phrase crossed out

✅ The results, published in two journals, were clear.part between commas crossed out

✅ The captain, as well as the players, is anxious.“as well as” does not add a subject

❌ The box of letters are heavy.“letters” is not the subject → is

What to cross out 要划掉什么

  • of + noun and other prepositional phrases (in, on, with, from…)
  • -ing / -ed phrases that describe a noun
  • anything between two commas or two dashes
  • as well as / along with / together with / including / in addition to + noun

Never match the verb to the nearest noun. Ask: “Who or what is / does this?”

划掉介词短语（尤其 of + 名词）、分词短语、两个逗号之间的插入语，以及 as well as / along with 等短语。它们不改变主语的单复数。动词不能跟最近的名词一致，要问“谁/什么 + 动词”。

Grammar You Need

Clauses with who / which / that: two verbs, two subjects

What is a relative clause? 定语从句

A relative clause starts with who, which, or that and describes the noun just before it. It has its own verb.

✅ The students who study regularly are confident.who = students (plural) → study; main subject students → are

✅ The man who helped us is my uncle.cross out the who-clause → The man is

定语从句由 who / which / that 开头，修饰前面的名词，从句里有自己的动词。找主句的主语时，把整个从句划掉。

“that” copies the noun it refers to

Inside the clause, who / which / that is the subject. It is singular or plural like the noun it refers to.

✅ a technique that creates emphasisthat = technique (one) → creates

✅ two techniques that create emphasisthat = techniques → create

❌ a document that outline our rightsdocument is one → outlines

✅ one of the songs that are popularthat = songs (the popular ones)

that/which/who 本身没有单复数，要看它指代的名词：指 technique 用 creates，指 techniques 用 create。

Grammar You Need

When the subject comes after the verb · 倒装与 there be

Inverted order 倒装

Pattern: place phrase + verb + real subject. Put the sentence back into normal order first.

✅ On the top shelf sits a dusty box.normal order: A dusty box sits on the top shelf.

❌ Near the entrance stand a statue of the founder.a statue (one) → stands

✅ Among the club’s leaders was Josephine Ruffin.Ruffin was among the leaders.

倒装句：地点短语 + 动词 + 真正主语。先还原成正常语序（主语 + 动词 + 地点），再判断单复数。句首地点短语不是主语。

There is / There are

There is never the subject. The real subject comes after the verb.

✅ There are two options.

✅ There is a reason for the change.

❌ There is several reasons for the change.reasons is plural → There are

❌ There were a storm last night.a storm → There was

there is / there are 中 there 不是主语，看动词后面的名词：单数用 is/was，复数用 are/were。

2.The students in the back row ___ tired. (is / are)
are — subject = students.
3.Each of the paintings ___ a different city. (show / shows)
shows — subject = each (singular).
4.A number of birds ___ nesting here. (is / are)
are — “a number of” = many → plural.
5.The number of visitors ___ growing. (is / are)
is — subject = number (singular).
6.Collecting fossils ___ patience. (require / requires)
requires — gerund subject = one activity.
7.The coach, along with the players, ___ arrived. (has / have)
has — “along with the players” is crossed out.
8.Lions and tigers ___ big cats. (is / are)
are — A and B → plural.

The rule

Singular and plural verbs

Present tense

SubjectVerbExample
singular (it, he, she, one thing)-s: allows, is, hasThe technique creates emphasis.
plural (they, two or more)no -s: allow, are, haveThe toxins increase its attractiveness.

注意：名词加 -s 是复数，动词加 -s 是单数。

Always singular

  • a gerund (verb + -ing used as a noun) subject: Landing on one of the good spaces allows…
  • a title of a book or work: A Sheaf Gleaned in French Fields has enhanced…
  • each, every, either, neither; the number of (but a number of = plural)
  • collective nouns (one group made of many members): group, team, government, school

Plural: two subjects joined by and: The Monarch and the Red Admiral are…

动名词、书名、each/every、the number of、集体名词 → 单数；A and B → 复数。

Distractors

How the SAT hides the subject

TrickExampleReal subject
Prepositional phraselanding on one of the good spaces ___ a player to skip aheadlanding → allows
Descriptive (-ing / -ed) phrasethe triangle representing the mountain itself ___ among the few…triangle → is
Appositive or clause between commasSuch iconic references, cited in rapid … procession by the musician, who is seated impassively at a dinner table, ___ key momentsreferences → represent
Relative clause (that / which / who)a document that ___ the basic freedomsdocument (what “that” refers to) → outlines

介词短语、分词短语、插入语都要划掉；that/which 的单复数看它指代的名词；倒装句主语在动词后面。

How to approach it

Four steps for agreement

1

See that the choices are one verb in different forms

Usually one singular and three plural (or the reverse). 选项是同一个动词的不同形式。

2

Ask “who or what does this action?”

Work backward from the blank, crossing out phrases and clauses. Do not take the nearest noun. 问“谁做这个动作”，不要直接取最近的名词。

3

Decide singular or plural

Watch gerunds, titles, and “and.” 判断单复数。

4

Choose the matching verb, then check tense

If two choices are singular, use the tense of the other verbs in the text. 若有两个单数选项，再看时态。

Worked Example 2

Form 4 · Module 1 · Question 22分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“are / have been / were / is”: all forms of be. Three are plural (are, have been, were); one is singular (is). → Subject–verb agreement: find the subject. 选项是 be 的不同形式，三复一单 → 考主谓一致。

2

Find the true subject · 找主语

“the triangle representing the mountain itself ___ among the few defined figures.” Cross out the -ing phrase. What is among the figures? The triangle. 划掉 representing… 分词短语，主语是 triangle。

3

Singular or plural? · 单复数

“the triangle” = one triangle → singular. “mountain” is also singular, but it is not the subject; “paintings” and “figures” are plural traps. triangle 是一个 → 单数。paintings、figures 是干扰项。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Singular subject → singular verb → is. Present tense also fits: it describes the painting, which still exists. 单数主语 → is。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ “are” is plural.
B ✗ “have been” is plural.
C ✗ “were” is plural (and past).

Answer: D. Subject: “the triangle representing the mountain itself” → singular → is. 主语 triangle，单数。

Worked Example 3

Form 1 · Module 2 · Question 19分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“were / have been / has been / are”: forms of be in different numbers and tenses. → Agreement first, then tense. 先看单复数，再看时态。

2

Find the true subject · 找主语

“but her writing ___ honored before.” What has been honored? Her writing, not Harris and not the novel. 被表彰的是 her writing。

3

Singular or plural? · 单复数

“writing” is one thing (an uncountable noun, no -s) → singular. That removes were, have been, are. writing 单数 → 排除 were、have been、are。

4

Check the tense · 时态

“before” = at some time up to now → present perfect has been. “以前被表彰过（到现在）”用现在完成时 has been。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ “were” is plural.
B ✗ “have been” is plural.
D ✗ “are” is plural, and present tense does not fit “before.”

Answer: C. Subject: “her writing” → singular. The time is “before” now → present perfect: has been. 主语 writing，单数；“以前就被表彰过”用现在完成时。

Worked Example 4

Form 6 · Module 1 · Question 21分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“create / are creating / have created / creates”: one singular (creates), three plural. → Find the subject. 一单三复 → 考主谓一致。

2

Find the structure · 分析结构

“…antimetabole, a writing technique that ___ emphasis.” The blank is inside a that-clause. Its subject is that. 空格在 that 引导的定语从句里，从句主语是 that。

What does “that” refer to? · 指代

“that” refers to the noun right before it: a writing technique → one technique → singular. that 指代 a writing technique，单数。

Singular subject → creates. Simple present also fits: it states a general fact about how the technique works. 单数 → creates；一般现在时描述普遍事实。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ “create” is plural.
B ✗ “are creating” is plural.
C ✗ “have created” is plural.

Answer: D. “that” refers to “a writing technique” → singular → creates. that 指代 technique，单数。

Summary

What to remember about subject-verb agreement主谓一致要点

01 · 单复数对应

Singular subject → verb with -s (is, has, allows). Plural subject → verb without -s (are, have, allow).

02 · 寻找真正主语

Ask “who or what does this?” and cross out prepositional phrases, -ing / -ed phrases, and anything between commas.

03 · 就近名词陷阱

The nearest noun is often a trap.

04 · 常见单复数主语

Gerunds, titles, each / every, the number of, collective nouns → singular; A and B → plural.

05 · 关系代词与倒装

“that / which / who” takes the number of the noun it refers to. In inverted sentences, the subject comes after the verb.
