Why this matters

On the SAT, the four choices are often four forms of the same verb: used / to use / using / to have used. You must decide which form the sentence needs.

中文说明

SAT 动词题的四个选项常常是同一个动词的不同形式。先认识这些形式：原形、三单、过去式、过去分词、-ing、to do。规则动词过去式和过去分词都加 -ed；不规则动词要记（write – wrote – written）。

Grammar You Need

Finite (main) verbs vs nonfinite forms · 谓语和非谓语

Finite verb = the main verb 谓语动词

A finite verb shows time (past, present, future) and goes with a subject. It can be one word or a group: uses, used, will use, is using, has used, was used.

✅ Land used his technology.past time → a real main verb

✅ The fish enter a dormant state.

✅ The bridge was built in 1890.

谓语动词（finite verb）= 能表示时间、跟主语搭配的动词，可以是一个词，也可以是一组词（has used、was built）。

Nonfinite form = cannot be the main verb 非谓语

A nonfinite form shows no time: -ing, to + verb, and -ed/-en without is / was / has. Alone, it cannot make a sentence.

✗ Maya using a camera.
✓ Maya used a camera.

✗ The fish to enter a dormant state.
✓ The fish enter a dormant state.

✗ The bridge built in 1890.
✓ The bridge was built in 1890.

非谓语 = doing / to do / done，没有时间，不能单独做谓语，只能修饰或补充。看起来像动词，其实不是“主动词”。

Grammar You Need

Find the finite verb in each clause · 找谓语

Rule 规则

Every independent clause needs a finite predicate. A relative clause also needs its own finite verb.

  1. Find the subject and its finite verb.
  2. If the clause has no finite verb, supply one.
  3. If the added words describe a noun or an action, use a suitable nonfinite form.

Coordinated verbs can share a subject: The birds land and rest.

先找主谓。额外的修饰动作可用非谓语；并列谓语可以通过 and 等连接，不能简单理解为“每句只能有一个动词”。

Examples 例句

The birds fly south every winter.fly is the finite verb; every winter signals a habit.

✅ The birds fly south, traveling thousands of miles.“fly” is the main verb → the extra action is -ing

❌ The birds fly south, travel thousands of miles.two main verbs with only a comma

✅ The handbook, which provided guidance, helped the team.which-clause gets its own finite verb

❌ The handbook, which providing guidance, helped the team.the which-clause has no verb

“which providing” 错：which 从句缺谓语。

Grammar You Need

Which nonfinite form? -ing, -ed, or to + verb

FormMeaning · 含义✅ Examples
-ing (present participle 现在分词)active: the noun does the action 主动、正在
The girl sitting by the window smiled.
Snow covered the food, forcing the monkeys to hunt.
-ed / -en (past participle 过去分词)passive: the noun receives the action 被动、已完成
A letter written in 1850 is on display.
Built in 1890, the bridge is still in use.
to + verb (infinitive 不定式)purpose: in order to 目的
Maya used a camera to photograph the birds.
To finish the report, Leo stayed up late.

Test: do or receive?

❌ A letter writing in 1850 is on display.
✓ A letter written in 1850 is on display.a letter cannot write → written

❌ The girl sat by the window smiled.
✓ The girl sitting by the window smiled.two finite verbs → sitting

中文说明

问自己：这个名词是“做”这个动作（用 -ing），还是“被”做（用 -ed）？如果表示“为了……”，用 to do。信不能自己写，所以是 a letter written。

Grammar You Need

Tense = time + shape · 时态表

Past 过去Present 现在Future 将来
Simple 一般She studied yesterday.She studies every day.She will study tomorrow.
Progressive 进行 (be + -ing)She was studying at eight last night.She is studying now.She will be studying at eight tonight.
Perfect 完成 (have + -ed)She had studied before the test began.She has studied three chapters so far.She will have studied all chapters by Friday.
Perfect progressive 完成进行She had been studying for two hours when I called.She has been studying since noon.By six, she will have been studying for two hours.

Most tested on the SAT

simple present (facts, habits, describing a book), simple past (finished past event), present perfect (past → now), past perfect (earlier past).

中文说明

时态 = 时间（过去/现在/将来）+ 形态（一般/进行/完成/完成进行），共 12 种。SAT 最常考：一般现在、一般过去、现在完成、过去完成。根据时间线选，不要凭语感。

❌ The museum has opened in 1920.has + done does not go with “in 1920”

Also: keep the tense consistent with the other verbs in the text.

had done = “过去的过去”，必须有另一个过去时间点作参照；has done = 从过去延续/影响到现在，不能和 in 1920 这种明确过去时间连用。

Grammar You Need

Voice: does the subject do the action or receive it? · 语态

Active 主动 vs Passive 被动

Active voice: the subject does the action. Passive voice: the subject receives the action. Passive = be + past participle.

✅ The committee reviewed the proposal.active: the committee did it

✅ The proposal was reviewed by the committee.passive: the proposal received it

❌ The experiment conducted the scientists.an experiment cannot conduct anyone

Test: Can the subject perform this action?

主动：主语做动作；被动：主语承受动作（be + done）。检验：主语能不能自己做这个动作？

Passive in every tense 被动的时态

TensePassive form
presentNew bridges are built every year.
pastThe bridge was built in 1990.
present perfectTwo bridges have been built so far.
past perfectThe bridge had been built before the road opened.
futureThe bridge will be built next year.
after can / mustThe bridge can be built here.

被动的时态体现在 be 的形式上，后面的过去分词不变。行动者未知或不重要时，被动语态是正确、自然的。

Quick Check

Quick check 1 · Main verb or not?小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.Every spring, the river ___ its banks. (flooding / floods)
floods — the clause has no main verb yet.
2.The river floods every spring, ___ the nearby farms. (covers / covering)
covering — “floods” is already the main verb; the extra action is -ing.
3.She saved money ___ a new bike. (to buy / buys)
to buy — purpose: in order to buy.
4.The book, ___ in 1925, is still popular. (written / writing)
written — the book was written (passive).
5.The scientist ___ the results last year. (to publish / published)
published — no main verb yet; “last year” → past.
6.a tool that ___ farmers (helps / helping)
helps — a that-clause needs its own finite verb.
7.The girl ___ next to me is my cousin. (sitting / sits)
sitting — “is” is the main verb; she sits → active -ing.
8.___ in the desert, the plant needs little water. (Growing / Grows)
Growing — an opening phrase; the main verb is “needs”.

Quick check 2 · Tense and voice先找时间线索，再问主语是做还是被做

landed — “In 1969” = finished past time.
2.Since 2015, the town ___ two new schools. (built / has built)
has built — since → up to now.

had burned — before another past event.

Finite and nonfinite

Finite and nonfinite verbs

Finite verb · 谓语

Some historians claim that the event changed trade.
The handbook, which provided guidance, helped the team.

Each clause has a subject and a finite verb: historians → claim; which → provided; handbook → helped.

主句和定语从句分别有自己的主语、谓语。

Nonfinite form · 非谓语

✗ Some historians claiming that the event changed trade.
✓ Some historians claiming that the event changed trade cite new evidence.
The team read the handbook to learn the procedure.

claiming 不能独立做谓语；补上 cite 后整句完整。to learn 表示目的。

Find the finite verb for each clause. A descriptive participle or infinitive does not replace that verb.

先找每个分句的谓语，再辨认分词或不定式的修饰作用。

Which nonfinite form?

Choosing among -ing, -ed, and to + verb

FormMeaningComplete example · 自编
-ing (present participle)the noun does the action; often a result or added actionSnow covers the food sources, forcing the monkeys to hunt elsewhere.
-ed (past participle)the noun receives the action (passive)Built by local workers, the bridge connects two villages.
to + verb (infinitive)purpose: in order toThe researchers designed a tool to cut DNA precisely.

Ask: does the noun do the action or receive it? Is the second action a result, or a purpose? 主动用 -ing，被动用 -ed，目的用 to do。

How to approach it

Four steps for verb forms

1

Look at the choices

Different forms (to / -ing / -ed / finite)? → finite or not. Different tenses? → tense. 选项是形式不同，还是时态不同？

2

Find the subject and count the finite verbs in the clause

No finite verb yet → the blank needs one. Already has one → nonfinite. 找主语，数谓语。

3

For nonfinite: active, passive, or purpose?

-ing, -ed, or to + verb. 主动、被动还是目的？

4

For tense: find the time clue

Dates, “today,” “by…,” and the tense of nearby verbs. 找时间线索。

Worked Example 2

Form 2 · Module 1 · Question 20分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“enter / to enter / having entered / entering”: one finite verb and three nonfinite forms. → Does the sentence still need its main verb? 一个谓语、三个非谓语 → 考谓语还是非谓语。

2

Find the subject · 找主语

“To survive when water is scarce, embryos inside African turquoise killifish eggs ___ a dormant state.” The opening to-phrase is purpose. Subject = embryos. 句首 to survive 表目的，划掉；主语是 embryos。

3

Count the verbs · 数谓语

“is scarce” belongs to the when-clause. The main clause “embryos ___ a dormant state” has no finite verb yet. is 属于 when 从句；主句还没有谓语。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

No main verb → the blank needs a finite verb: enter (plural embryos, present like “is paused”). 主句缺谓语 → enter。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ “to enter” is nonfinite: no main verb.
C ✗ “having entered” is nonfinite.
D ✗ “entering” is nonfinite.

Answer: A. “To survive when water is scarce” is an introductory phrase. Main clause: “embryos inside … eggs ___ a dormant state.” No finite verb yet → enter (present, plural). 主句还没有谓语，填 enter。

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“used / to have used / to use / using”: one finite, three nonfinite. 一个谓语、三个非谓语。

2

“A decade later, Land ___ his technology to invent the world’s first instant camera.” Subject = Land. 主语是 Land。

3

“to invent” is nonfinite (purpose). So the clause has no finite verb yet; the blank must supply it. to invent 是非谓语（目的），句子还缺谓语。

B ✗ “to have used” is nonfinite.
C ✗ “to use” is nonfinite.
D ✗ “using” is nonfinite.

Answer: A. Subject: Land; no finite verb yet. Time: “A decade later” than 1929 → past → used. “to invent” is already the purpose. 主句缺谓语，过去时间 → used。

Worked Example 4

Form 3 · Module 1 · Question 20分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“provided / having provided / to provide / providing”: one finite, three nonfinite. 一个谓语、三个非谓语。

2

Find the structure · 分析结构

Main clause: “Mary Golda Ross is renowned for…” — it already has its verb. But the blank is inside a new clause: “…Handbook, which ___ detailed guidance.” 主句已有谓语 is，但空格在 which 从句里。

3

Count the verbs · 数谓语

A which-clause needs its own finite verb. “which ___ guidance” has none yet. which 从句需要自己的谓语，现在还没有。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Finite verb → provided — the only finite choice. Simple past fits: the handbook gave this guidance in the past.. 从句缺谓语 → provided。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ “having provided” is nonfinite: the which-clause would have no verb.
C ✗ “to provide” is nonfinite.
D ✗ “providing” is nonfinite.

Answer: A. “…NASA’s Planetary Flight Handbook, which ___ detailed mathematical guidance.” A clause starting with which needs its own finite verb → provided. which 引导的从句也需要谓语。

Summary

What to remember about verb forms动词形式要点

01 · 限定动词

Every clause (including which / that clauses) needs one finite verb. No finite verb yet → the blank needs one.

02 · 非限定动词

Clause already has its verb → the extra action is nonfinite: -ing (active / result), -ed (passive), to + verb (purpose).

03 · 主动与被动

Introductory participle: does the subject do the action (-ing) or receive it (-ed)?

04 · 时态线索

Tense: general facts and literary works → present; “Today” → present; past dates → past; “By [past time]” → past perfect.

05 · 时态一致

Make the tense consistent with the other verbs in the text.
