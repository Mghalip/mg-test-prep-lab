Linking Clauses II

Dependent clauses, phrases, and words like however: where the punctuation goes
从句、短语和 however 一类连接副词：标点该放在哪里
DEPENDENT CLAUSESA because / while / when clause cannot stand alone: no period or semicolon before or after it.
PHRASESA phrase (positing…, producing…, the stitching…) attaches to a main clause with a comma.
HOWEVER-TYPE WORDShowever, rather, though are not conjunctions: find the sentence boundary, then place the semicolon or colon.
Grammar You Need

Recap: three kinds of word groups

Independent clause 独立句

subject + verb with tense + complete thought. It can be a sentence alone.

✅ The rain stopped.

✅ Others look to the past.

独立句（主句）：有主语、有时态的谓语，意思完整，可以单独成句。

Dependent clause 从句

Has a subject + verb, but a linking word in front makes it “hang”.

❌ When the rain stopped.When… what happened? Unfinished.

✅ When the rain stopped, we went out.

从句：有主谓，但开头有 when、because 等词，必须挂在主句上。

Phrase 短语

No subject + verb pair. Often starts with -ing, -ed, to, or a preposition.

❌ Hoping for sunshine.no subject, no real verb

✅ Hoping for sunshine, we waited.

短语：没有“主语 + 谓语”，永远不能单独成句。

Today’s question: when a sentence has a main clause plus a dependent clause or a phrase, what punctuation goes between them? 本课核心：主句 + 从句 / 短语之间用什么标点？（G01 讲的是两个独立句怎么连。）

Grammar You Need

Subordinating conjunctions: words that make a clause “depend”

A subordinating conjunction 从属连词 is a joining word that turns a complete sentence into a dependent clause. Add one to the front, and the sentence can no longer stand alone.

MeaningWords · 中文Example (dependent clause underlined)
time 时间when 当…时, whenever 每当, while 在…期间, before, after, until 直到, as soon asWhenever it rains, the road floods.
reason 原因because 因为, since 因为/自从, as 因为She stayed home because she was sick.
contrast 让步/对比although / though / even though 虽然, while / whereas 而Although the test was hard, she passed.
condition 条件if 如果, unless 除非, once 一旦If you study, you will improve.

Watch the change

✅ The test was hard.a complete sentence

❌ Although the test was hard.now a dependent clause: fragment

中文说明

从属连词放在一个完整句前面，这个句子就变成了从句，不能再单独成句。注意 while 有两个意思：“当……时”和“而（对比）”。SAT 中 while 常表示对比。

Grammar You Need

Dependent clause first or last? · 从句在前还是在后

Dependent clause FIRST → comma

Pattern: Because S V, S V. The comma marks where the main clause starts.

✅ When the bell rang, students left.

✅ While many creators want lifelike graphics, others look to the past.

❌ When the bell rang. Students left.period → “When the bell rang.” is a fragment

❌ When the bell rang; students left.semicolon needs complete sentences on both sides

从句在前：从句结束处用逗号。不能用句号或分号。

Dependent clause LAST → usually nothing

Pattern: S V because S V. Time, reason, and condition words: no comma.

✅ Students left when the bell rang.

✅ A leap second is added whenever time lags.

Contrast words (while, whereas, although) after the main clause usually take a comma:

✅ Some rockfish live ten years, while others live two centuries.

❌ Students left. When the bell rang.fragment

从句在后：时间、原因、条件从句一般不加逗号；表示对比的 while / whereas / although 前面常加逗号。

Grammar You Need

Phrases hang on the main clause with a comma

Kind of phrase✅ Attached with a comma❌ Cut off with . or ;
-ing phrase 现在分词短语
adds a result or what else happened		The storm hit the coast, destroying many homes.The storm hit the coast. Destroying many homes.
-ed phrase 过去分词短语
describes a noun, passive meaning		Built in 1890, the bridge is still in use.Built in 1890; the bridge is still in use.
noun phrase 名词短语 (renames)She met Ada Lovelace, a pioneer of computing.She met Ada Lovelace. A pioneer of computing.
noun + -ing/-ed (“absolute phrase” 独立主格)The quilts look like paintings, the stitching barely visible.…like paintings; the stitching barely visible.

Rule: a phrase has no subject + verb pair, so it can never sit alone after a period or semicolon. Join it to the main clause with a comma (or no mark if it is short and closely tied). 短语没有主谓，不能放在句号或分号后面单独成句；用逗号把它挂在主句上。独立主格 = 名词 + doing/done，看起来像句子，但没有真正的谓语（visible 只是形容词，没有 is）。

Grammar You Need

However-type words are adverbs, not conjunctions

What they are 连接副词

These words show the logic between two ideas, but they cannot join two sentences. Grammatically they are adverbs, so they can move around.

however 然而therefore / thus 因此
moreover 此外consequently 结果
instead 相反/取而代之rather 而是
nevertheless 尽管如此meanwhile 与此同时
for example 例如in fact 事实上

连接副词只表示逻辑关系（转折、因果、递进），不是连词，不能用逗号连接两个句子。

Three patterns 三种用法

✅ I studied all night; however, I failed the test.between two sentences: ; + word + ,

✅ I studied all night. However, I failed the test.or period + word + ,

✅ The researchers, however, continued.inside ONE sentence: commas on both sides

❌ I studied all night, however, I failed the test.comma splice: two sentences + only commas

✅ I studied all night, but I failed the test.compare: but IS a conjunction (FANBOYS)

两个句子之间：分号/句号 + however + 逗号。只在一个句子里插入：两边逗号。

Grammar You Need

“Though” has two jobs · though 的两种用法

Conjunction ≈ although 连词

Starts a dependent clause. Followed by a subject + verb.

✅ Though I was tired, I finished the homework.

✅ I finished the homework, though I was tired.

❌ Though I was tired. I finished the homework.fragment

连词 though = although（虽然），引导从句，后面跟主谓。

Adverb ≈ however 副词

Stands alone between commas, often at the end of a sentence. Nothing follows it in its clause.

✅ I was tired. I finished the homework, though.

✅ The plan was risky; it worked, though.

❌ The plan was risky, it worked, though.two sentences joined by commas only

副词 though = however（不过），常放句末，用逗号隔开；它不能连接两个句子。

Quick Check

Quick check · Clause, phrase, or adverb?小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.Because the museum was crowded. We left early.
✗ “Because the museum was crowded” is a dependent clause. Fix: crowded, we left early.
2.We left early because the museum was crowded.
✓ main clause + reason clause at the end: no comma.
3.The volcano erupted; sending ash across the sky.
✗ “sending ash…” is a phrase. Fix: erupted, sending ash…
4.The volcano erupted, however, no one was hurt.
✗ comma splice. Fix: erupted; however, no one…
5.Some birds migrate thousands of miles, whereas others never leave home.
✓ main clause + contrast clause (whereas): comma.
6.The scientist, however, disagreed.
✓ one sentence; “however” interrupts it, so commas on both sides.
7.Though the data were limited; the results were useful.
✗ “Though…” is a dependent clause. Fix: limited, the results…
8.If the plan works, the city will save money.
✓ dependent clause first → comma.
Review

Only an independent clause can stand alone

Independent clause

others look to the past

Can be a sentence by itself.

独立句：可以单独成句。

Dependent clause

While many video game creators strive to make their graphics lifelike

Has a subject and verb but starts with a subordinating word: because, while, when, whenever, although, if, since, as.

从句：有主谓，但由从属连词引导，不能单独成句。

Phrase

positing that all life’s virtues derived from this absence

No subject + real (tensed) verb pair: -ing / -ed phrases, noun phrases, prepositional phrases.

短语：没有主谓结构。

Key consequence: a period or semicolon must have a complete sentence on both sides. If one side is a dependent clause or a phrase, a period or semicolon creates a fragment. 句号、分号两边都必须是完整句，否则会产生残句（fragment）。

Rules

Dependent clauses and phrases: the rules

PatternPunctuationExample
Dependent clause, Main clausecommaWhile many creators strive to make their graphics ever more lifelike, others look to the past. (2-2-19)
Main clause + dependent clauseusually nothing; a comma before contrast words (while, whereas, although)an extra leap second is added whenever time … lags (3-1-23) · …a little over a decade, while the rougheye rockfish… (3-2-21)
Main clause + -ing / -ed phrasecommaEpicurus defined pleasure as “…the soul,” positing that… (1-1-20)

Any of these with ; or . or : between✗ fragment…the soul.” Positing that… → “Positing…” is not a sentence.
However-type words

however, rather, though, therefore: conjunctive adverbs

They are not conjunctions

however, rather, though, therefore, thus, instead, moreover, consequently can move around inside a sentence. They cannot join two sentences with a comma.

✗ Okinaka doesn’t make such decisions single-handedly, however, all designations must be approved…

连接副词不是连词，“, however,” 夹在两个句子之间仍是逗号粘连。

How to place the punctuation

  1. Find the two clauses.
  2. Decide which clause the adverb belongs to by meaning: does it react to the sentence before (“doesn’t decide single-handedly, however”) or start the next one (“; rather, the spider seems to wait…”)?
  3. Put the semicolon / period / colon at the boundary, and a comma on the other side of the adverb.

…single-handedly, however; all designations…
…to capture its prey; rather, the spider seems to wait…

先找句子边界放分号/冒号，副词另一侧用逗号。副词属于哪一句看意思。

3

Main + phrase or main + dependent → comma (or nothing). Two main clauses → period / semicolon / comma + FANBOYS. 按规则选择标点。

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

2

Label the left side · 左边

3

Apply the rule · 规则

Dependent clause first, main clause second → one comma between them, and no extra conjunction. Adding “but” would leave the sentence with no main clause. 从句在前 + 主句 → 逗号；不能再加 but。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ “lifelike but others…”: “but” turns the second part into another dependent piece, so the sentence has no main clause.
B ✗ No comma between an introductory dependent clause and the main clause.
D ✗ “, but” also leaves the sentence without a main clause: While… but… .

Answer: C. “While many video game creators strive to make their graphics ever more lifelike” = dependent clause. “others look to the past” = main clause. Dependent clause first → comma. While 从句在前，主句在后，中间用逗号。

“decade: while / decade. While / decade; while / decade, while”: the word while stays; only the mark before it changes. while 不变，只换标点。

2

Label the left side · 左边

Apply the rule · 规则

Period, semicolon, and colon all need a complete sentence after them (or make a fragment). Main clause + contrast clause (while) → comma. 主句 + while 对比从句 → 逗号。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ A colon cannot introduce a dependent clause in this way.
B ✗ A period leaves “While the rougheye rockfish … two centuries.” as a fragment.
C ✗ A semicolon needs a complete sentence after it; the while-clause is dependent.

Answer: D. Main clause: “the colorful calico rockfish … can survive for a little over a decade.” Then “while the rougheye rockfish … boasts a maximum life span of about two centuries” = dependent clause of contrast. Main + contrasting dependent clause → comma. 主句 + while 对比从句，用逗号。

C ✗ A period makes “Whenever time … clocks.” a fragment.

Summary

What to remember about dependent clauses, phrases, and however-type words从句、短语与连接副词要点

01 · 避免残句

Period and semicolon need complete sentences on both sides; otherwise you create a fragment.

02 · 从句顺序

Dependent clause first → comma. Main clause first → usually nothing (comma before while / whereas / although).

03 · 附加短语

Main clause + -ing / -ed phrase or absolute phrase → comma.

04 · 连接副词

however, rather, though, therefore are adverbs, not conjunctions: a comma alone cannot join two clauses with them.

05 · 定位句子边界

Find the clause boundary by meaning; put ; or : (or .) there and a comma on the other side of the adverb.
