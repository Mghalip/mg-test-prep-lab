Warm-up · 热身

Colon, semicolon, or question mark?用哪个标点？

A colon needs a complete sentence before it. A question mark needs a real (direct) question.

The recipe needs three ______ flour, eggs, and sugar.
Which choice conforms to the conventions of Standard English?
Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
Grammar You Need

The colon ( : ) = “here is what I mean” · 冒号

The two-part rule

1. Before the colon: a complete sentence (subject + verb + complete thought).

2. After the colon: something that explains, names, or lists what came before. It can be a full sentence, a phrase, a single word, or a list.

✅ He had one goal: to win the game.after = a phrase that names the goal

✅ She brought everything: a pen, a notebook, and a ruler.after = a list

✅ The research is clear: sleep improves memory.after = a full sentence that explains

Test: read the colon as “namely / 也就是 / 具体来说”. If that makes sense, the colon works.

冒号前必须是完整句；冒号后解释、说明或列举前面的内容，可以是句子、短语、单词或列表。

Wrong colons ❌

❌ He bought: a pen and a notebook.“He bought” is not complete: bought what?

✅ He bought two things: a pen and a notebook.now the left side is complete

❌ The team members include: Ana, Ben, and Li.never right after a verb

❌ We spoke about: history and art.never right after a preposition

❌ She likes fruits such as: apples and pears.never after “such as / including”

❌ I was late: however, the class waited.the second part does not explain the first; it contrasts → use “;”

冒号不能直接放在动词、介词、such as、including 后面；后半句要“解释”前半句，不是转折。

中文说明

分号：连接两个关系紧密的完整句；此时可换成句号。

The rain stopped; we went outside.

冒号：左边必须完整，右边解释或列举，未必是完整句。

We packed three items: bread, fruit, and water.

单个破折号：也可引出补充解释或强调的信息。

She needed only one thing—more time.

逗号：不能单独连接两个完整句；可加并列连词。

✓ It rained, so we stayed inside.

Grammar You Need

Lists: commas for simple items, semicolons for complex items

Simple list → commas

Items are single words or short phrases: A, B, and C.

✅ She bought apples, bananas, and oranges.

✅ We visited Paris, Rome, and Madrid.

❌ We visited Paris; Rome; and Madrid.no commas inside the items, so no semicolons needed

普通列举：A, B, and C，用逗号隔开。

Complex list → semicolons

When an item already has a comma inside it (e.g., a name + description, a city + country), commas between items would be confusing. So use semicolons between items, commas inside items.

✅ We visited Paris, France; Rome, Italy; and Madrid, Spain.

✅ The guests were Amy, my friend; John, her brother; and Lisa, their cousin.

❌ The guests were Amy, my friend, John, her brother, and Lisa, their cousin.how many guests? 3 or 6? unclear

❌ We visited Paris, France; Rome, Italy, and Madrid, Spain.once you use ; between items, use it between ALL items

复杂列举：项内部有逗号时，项与项之间用分号（最后一项前写 “; and”）。一旦用了分号，所有项之间都要用分号。

Grammar You Need

Question order vs statement order · 疑问语序与陈述语序

Direct question 直接疑问句 → ?

The sentence itself asks. Word order: helping verb (do, does, did, is, are, can, could, will, have) comes before the subject.

✅ Is she ready?

✅ Why did the experiment fail?

✅ Could the blueberries thrive in salty soil?

❌ Why the experiment failed?statement order + ? → mismatch

直接疑问句：助动词/be 动词放在主语前面，句末用问号。

Indirect question 间接疑问句 → .

A statement that reports a question, usually after verbs like ask, wonder, investigate, explore, examine, question + how / why / what / whether / if. Normal order: subject before verb.

✅ I wonder whether she is ready.

✅ Scientists asked why the experiment failed.

❌ Scientists asked why did the experiment fail.question order inside a statement

❌ Scientists asked why the experiment failed?the whole sentence is a statement → period

间接疑问句：问题被放进陈述句里，用陈述语序（主语在前），句末用句号。

✓ complete sentence + list that names the “three things”.
2.The recipe needs: flour, eggs, and sugar.
✗ “The recipe needs” is not complete. Remove the colon.
3.The town had one problem: water.
✓ the single word explains “one problem”.
4.She was tired; because she had worked all night.
✗ after “;” you need a complete sentence. Fix: tired because…
5.The team included Ana, a chemist; Ben, a physicist; and Li, a biologist.
✓ complex list: items contain commas, so semicolons go between them.

✗ indirect question → period.

✓ direct question: helping verb “do” before the subject “bees”.

✗ question order inside a statement. Fix: whether sleep habits affect grades.
Colons

The colon: complete sentence + explanation

The rule

What comes before a colon must be a complete sentence. What comes after explains, specifies, or lists what came before. (After it may be a complete sentence or not.)

There are three types of muscle in the body: cardiac, smooth, and skeletal.

The research is conclusive: climate change is a reality.

冒号前必须是完整句；冒号后是解释、具体说明或列举。

Wrong colons

✗ On our trip, we visited: museums, palaces, and monuments.

“we visited” is not complete without its object. → ✓ On our trip, we visited these attractions: museums, palaces, and monuments.

A colon may also introduce a quotation after a complete sentence: Thoreau ends his essay with a metaphor: “Time is but the stream I go a-fishing in.”

动词或介词后面不能直接接冒号。

Colon vs. semicolon: a semicolon simply joins two sentences; a colon promises that the second part explains the first. A dash can do the same job as a colon. 分号只是连接；冒号表示“下面解释前面”。破折号也可以起冒号的作用。

List semicolons

Semicolons in complex lists

Use semicolons between list items when the items already contain commas.

The panel included Ana, a chemist; Ben, a physicist; and Li, a biologist.
StepSee the pattern in the complete sentence
1 · Find the complete sentence and the list.The panel included Ana, a chemist; Ben, a physicist; and Li, a biologist.
2 · Mark the three complete items.The panel included [Ana, a chemist]; [Ben, a physicist]; and [Li, a biologist].
3 · Separate items with semicolons.The panel included Ana, a chemist; Ben, a physicist; and Li, a biologist.

Another example: We visited Paris, France; Rome, Italy; and Madrid, Spain.

每一项内部已有逗号，项与项之间用分号。看到分号后，还要确认它是在分隔列举项，还是连接完整分句。

Check both the word order and the whole sentence. A direct question uses “How do people perceive kindness?” A reported question uses “Researchers asked how people perceive kindness.”直接问句用疑问语序和问号；陈述句中的间接问句用陈述语序，句末用句号。
How to approach it

Steps for these questions

1

Colon or something else?

Is the part before the blank a complete sentence? Does the part after it explain or list? If both yes → colon. 前面完整、后面解释 → 冒号。

2

Look ahead for semicolons

A semicolon later in the sentence (often “; and”) signals a complex list. 后文有分号 → 复杂列举。

3

Question choices: check order and mark together

Is the sentence asking, or reporting a question (investigated how…, wondered whether…)? 直接问句还是间接问句？

4

Eliminate

Colon after an incomplete clause ✗; comma splice ✗; mixed list marks ✗; mismatched order and mark ✗. 逐项排除。

Worked Example 2

Form 2 · Module 2 · Question 25分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“varied: / varied, / varied, while / varied while”: colon, comma, or a “while” clause? → What is on each side of the blank? 比较冒号、逗号、while。

2

Test the left side · 左边

“The number of jams available for tasting varied” → subject + verb, a complete sentence. 左边 The number … varied，完整句。

3

Test the right side · 右边

“some shoppers had twenty-four different options, others only six” → also a complete sentence, and it tells how the number varied (24 vs 6). 右边是完整句，并且具体说明“怎么不同”。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Complete sentence + an explanation of it → colon (read it as “namely”). A comma alone would be a comma splice. 完整句 + 解释 → 冒号；只用逗号是逗号粘连。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ Comma splice: two complete sentences joined by a comma.
C ✗ “, while” makes the explanation a contrast and the structure unclear.
D ✗ “varied while” runs the ideas together without showing the explanation.

Answer: A. “The number of jams available for tasting varied” is complete. “some shoppers had twenty-four different options, others only six” explains how it varied. → colon. 后半句具体说明如何不同，用冒号。

Worked Example 3

Form 4 · Module 2 · Question 26分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“continents geological / continents: geological / continents; geological / continents. Geological”: nothing, colon, semicolon, or period. 四种标点。

2

Test the left side · 左边

“A study published by … Ming Tang in 2019 offers a new explanation for the origin of Earth’s continents” → complete sentence. 左边主谓宾完整。

3

Test the right side · 右边

“geological structures called arcs, towering ridges that form when…” → no main verb for “structures”: it is a long noun phrase, not a sentence. It names the new explanation: continents come from arcs. 右边是名词短语，没有谓语；它说明“新解释”是什么。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Complete sentence + a phrase that explains → colon. A semicolon or period needs a full sentence after it; no mark runs the nouns together. 冒号后可以是短语，只要它解释前文。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ No punctuation runs “continents geological structures” together.
C ✗ A semicolon needs a complete sentence after it; “geological structures called arcs…” is a phrase.
D ✗ A period makes “Geological structures called arcs…” a fragment.

Answer: B. “A study … offers a new explanation for the origin of Earth’s continents” is complete. “geological structures called arcs, towering ridges that form when…” is a phrase that explains the new explanation (what the origin is). → colon. 冒号后可以是短语，只要它解释前文。

Worked Example 4

Form 5 · Module 2 · Question 22分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

Two marks change: one before “in part due to its sheer size” and one after it. 两处标点：短语前、短语后。

2

Find the extra phrase · 找补充短语

“The vessel took six days to dislodge, in part due to its sheer size”. The phrase gives a reason and can be removed, so set it off with a comma before it. Do not split “in part due to” itself. in part due to its sheer size 是补充原因的短语，前面用逗号；短语内部不加逗号。

3

Test what comes after · 后面

“it’s as heavy as two thousand blue whales when fully loaded” → subject it + verb ’s (is): a complete sentence. And it explains how big “its sheer size” is. it’s… 是完整句，解释“体积有多大”。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Complete sentence + a complete sentence that explains → colon after “size”. A comma there would be a comma splice. → “dislodge, in part due to its sheer size:” 短语后接解释性完整句 → 冒号。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ No comma before the phrase and a comma after it: “size, it’s…” is a comma splice.
C ✗ Comma splice before “it’s as heavy…”.
D ✗ Run-on before “it’s,” and the extra commas around “in part” break the phrase.

Answer: B. “The vessel took six days to dislodge” + extra phrase “in part due to its sheer size” (set off by a comma). Then “it’s as heavy as two thousand blue whales” explains its sheer size. → comma before the phrase, colon after it. 逗号隔开补充短语，冒号引出解释。

Summary

What to remember about colons, list semicolons, and question marks冒号、分号与问号要点

01 · 冒号前后

Colon: complete sentence before; explanation, example, list, or quotation after.

02 · 冒号禁区

No colon right after a verb or preposition (“we visited:” ✗).

03 · 复杂列表

If the list items contain commas, separate items with semicolons. Look ahead for “; and”.

04 · 直接与间接问句

Direct question → question word order + ?. Indirect question → statement order + .

05 · 破折号与分号

A dash can do a colon’s job; a semicolon only joins two complete sentences.
