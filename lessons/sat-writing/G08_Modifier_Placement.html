LESSON G08

Subject-Modifier Placement

An opening description must be followed immediately by the noun it describes
句首修饰语后面必须紧跟它所修饰的名词
READ THE MODIFIERWho or what is described by the opening phrase?
ASK “WHO?”Who recovered? Who is one of the explorers? What was woven?

Grammar You Need

What is a modifier? · 什么是修饰语

Core vs. modifier 主干 vs. 修饰语

Every sentence has a core: S (subject 主语, who/what) + V (verb 谓语) + often O (object 宾语). A modifier is any word or group of words that describes something. It adds detail, but it is not the core.

The girl smiled.core only: S + V

Tired from work, the girl smiled.“Tired from work” describes the girl.

She read a letter written in French.“written in French” describes the letter.

修饰语 = 用来“描述”某个人或物的词或短语，只是补充信息，不是句子主干（主语 + 谓语 + 宾语）。

The one rule of this lesson 本课唯一规则

A modifier must sit right next to the thing it describes. If it sits next to the wrong noun, the sentence says something silly.

✅ Barking loudly, the dog chased the cat.The dog is barking. ✓

❌ Barking loudly, the cat was chased by the dog.Now the cat is barking!

Pattern to remember: Modifier, SVO. The S right after the comma is the one the modifier describes.

修饰语必须紧挨着它所修饰的对象。句型“修饰语, SVO.”中，逗号后的主语 S 就是修饰语描述的那个人/物。

Grammar You Need

Six kinds of opening modifiers · 句首修饰语的六种形式

Pattern 句型Meaning 含义Example (the S after the comma does it)
Doing, SVO.S is doing this (active) 主语主动在做Seeing the results, she felt relieved.
Done, SVO.S had this done to it / is in this state 被动或状态Tired from work, he fell asleep. · Built in 1890, the bridge is still used.
To do, SVO.S’s purpose 主语的目的To finish the report, she stayed up all night.
By / After / Upon / Despite + doing, SVO.S does the action 主语做这个动作After eating dinner, they went for a walk.
Noun phrase, SVO.renames S 说明主语身份A teacher by profession, she understands students well.
Like / As + noun, SVO.comparison or role 比较或身份As a leader, he must make tough decisions.

All six have no subject of their own, so they “borrow” the subject after the comma. 这些句首短语自己没有主语，只能“借用”逗号后面的主语。所以逗号后第一个名词必须是做这个动作、处于这个状态的人/物。

Grammar You Need

Dangling modifiers and the “Who is doing it?” test

Dangling modifier 悬垂修饰语 ✗

A dangling modifier is an opening phrase whose “doer” is not the subject after the comma. It “dangles” (hangs) with nothing to hold on to.

❌ Walking down the street, the trees looked beautiful.Trees cannot walk.

✅ Walking down the street, I noticed the beautiful trees.

❌ After reading the article, the main argument seemed unclear.An argument cannot read.

✅ After reading the article, the students found the argument unclear.

悬垂修饰语：句首短语的动作执行者不是逗号后的主语，意思变得荒谬。

The test 检验三步

  1. Read the opening phrase. Ask: “Who is doing / being / trying to do this?”
  2. Answer from the meaning: a person? a machine? a book?
  3. The first noun after the comma must be that answer.

✅ Born in 1907, Frida Kahlo painted self-portraits.Who was born? Kahlo ✓

❌ Born in 1907, self-portraits made Kahlo famous.Portraits were not born in 1907.

✅ To win the race, the runner trained daily.Who wants to win? the runner ✓

问：“谁在做这个动作？”逗号前是谁做事，逗号后的主语就必须是谁。

Grammar You Need

Four ways a wrong choice hides the real subject

1 · Possessive 所有格

❌ Upon arriving, Maya’s phone rang.The subject is “phone”; “Maya’s” only describes it.

✅ Upon arriving, Maya heard her phone ring.

Maya’s phone 的主语是 phone，不是 Maya。

2 · Passive voice 被动语态

❌ Using a microscope, the cells were studied by Lin.The subject is “cells”; Lin is pushed to the end.

✅ Using a microscope, Lin studied the cells.

被动语态把真正做事的人放到 by 后面，主语变成了物。

3 · “there” / “it” 空主语

❌ Despite being tired, there was a reason for Ben to keep running.“there” sits after the comma.

✅ Despite being tired, Ben kept running.

there be / it was … who 让 there、it 紧跟逗号，错误。

4 · Abstract noun 抽象名词

❌ By studying every night, her improvement was fast.An “improvement” cannot study.

✅ By studying every night, she improved fast.

improvement、focus、mitigation 这类抽象名词不会“做动作”。

Grammar You Need

Modifiers after the main clause · 句末修饰语

With a comma: SVO, doing / done.

After a comma, the phrase describes the subject or the whole action.

✅ Jack walked into the room, carrying a heavy bag.Jack is carrying the bag.

✅ He entered the hall, surrounded by reporters.He is surrounded.

有逗号：后面的 doing / done 一般修饰前面句子的主语或整个动作。

No comma: SVO doing / done.

Without a comma, the phrase describes the nearest noun.

✅ They found a letter written in French.The letter is written in French.

I saw a dog wearing sunglasses.The dog wears sunglasses (funny, but that is what it says).

She served cake to the children on paper plates.Better: She served the children cake on paper plates.

无逗号：修饰最近的名词。修饰语放错位置，意思就变了。

Quick Check

Quick check · Dangling or correct?先问“谁在做这个动作？”，再看逗号后的主语

1.Running to catch the bus, my backpack fell open.
✗ Dangling — a backpack cannot run. Fix: …, I felt my backpack fall open.
2.Running to catch the bus, I dropped my backpack.
✓ I am the one running.
3.Painted in 1503, the Mona Lisa hangs in the Louvre.
✓ The painting was painted (done → passive meaning).
4.After finishing the experiment, the data were sent to the lab.
✗ Data cannot finish an experiment. Fix: …, the scientists sent the data to the lab.
5.A skilled pianist, Mei’s concert sold out quickly.
✗ The subject is “concert,” not Mei. Fix: …, Mei filled every seat at her concert.
6.To save money, the family cooked at home.
✓ The family wants to save money.
7.By planting trees, the city reduced summer heat.
✓ The city planted the trees.
8.Known for his speed, it was Bolt who won the race.
✗ “it” comes after the comma. Fix: …, Bolt won the race.
The rule

Modifiers sit next to what they modify

The rule

When a sentence begins with a descriptive phrase that has no subject of its own, the noun it describes must come immediately after the comma.

✗ Born in Mexico City in 1907, self-portraits were what Frida Kahlo was best known for.

Who was born in Mexico City? Frida Kahlo, not the self-portraits.

✓ Born in Mexico City in 1907, Frida Kahlo was best known for her self-portraits.

句首修饰语没有自己的主语时，逗号后第一个名词必须是它修饰的对象，否则就是“悬垂修饰语 (dangling modifier)”。

Opening modifiers on the SAT

  • -ing phrases: Upon recovering two years later, … · By reducing the runoff, … · In assessing the films, …
  • -ed phrases: Woven from recycled yarn, … · Named in 1999 as one of the greatest achievements, …
  • noun phrases: One of the few African American global explorers, …
  • “Despite being cheap…,” “since uncovering fragments…”

常见：-ing 短语、-ed 短语、名词短语、despite / since + doing。

Traps

Wrong choices that look acceptable

Wrong patternWhy it fails
Possessive: Henry’s reign resumedThe subject is “reign,” not Henry. “Henry’s” is only a describer.
Passive: several treks … were made by Matthew HensonThe subject is “treks.” The person is pushed to the end.
Empty subject: there are…, it was Henry who…“there” / “it” comes right after the comma.
Abstract noun: the focus of many critics, the argument…, the mitigation…An abstraction cannot recover, assess, uncover, or reduce.

Shortcut: the right answer usually begins with the person or thing itself and uses an active verb. Read the modifier, ask “who?”, and look for the choice that begins with that answer. 正确选项通常以那个人/物开头，并用主动语态。所有格、被动语态、there be、抽象名词开头都要警惕。

How to approach it

Three steps

1

Notice the pattern

The sentence starts with a phrase and a comma, and the choices are whole clauses with different first words. 句首短语 + 逗号，选项是不同开头的分句。

2

Ask who or what the opening phrase describes

Who recovered? Who assessed the films? What was named one of the greatest achievements? 问：修饰语描述的是谁？

3

Choose the choice that starts with that noun

Not its possessive, not “there / it,” not a passive subject, not an abstraction. Then check that the sentence is complete. 选以该名词开头的选项，并检查整句完整。

Worked Example 2

Form 1 · Module 1 · Question 28分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

Each choice begins with a different first noun: Henry / the reign of Henry / Henry’s reign / it. → A modifier question: which noun must come right after the comma? 选项开头的名词各不相同 → 考修饰语位置。

2

Find the pattern · 找句型

“Upon recovering two years later, ____ …” = Upon + doing, SVO. The opening phrase has no subject of its own, so it borrows the subject after the comma. 句首是 Upon + doing 短语，自己没有主语，要借用逗号后的主语。

3

Ask “Who is doing it?” · 谁在康复？

Who recovered? The text says King Henry VI “became unfit to rule after falling gravely ill.” So the person who recovered is Henry. 生病后康复的是亨利六世。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

The first noun after the comma must be Henry himself — not “the reign of Henry,” not the possessive “Henry’s reign,” not “it.” Only A: “Henry resumed his reign.” 逗号后必须直接是 Henry，所有格和 it 都不行。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ “the reign of Henry” makes the reign the one who recovered.
C ✗ “Henry’s reign” makes the reign the subject; “Henry’s” only describes it.
D ✗ “it was Henry who…” puts “it” right after the modifier.

Answer: A. “Upon recovering two years later”: the person who had fallen ill, Henry VI. → “Henry resumed his reign.” 康复的是 Henry，逗号后应是 Henry。

Worked Example 3

Form 1 · Module 2 · Question 26分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

The choices start with there / two problems / commercial plastics’ … problems / commercial plastics. → Modifier question. 开头的主语不同 → 考修饰语。

2

Find the pattern · 找句型

“Despite being cheap, versatile, and easy to produce, ____” = Despite + doing, SVO. The commas inside the list are part of the modifier; the last comma ends it. Despite being… 是句首修饰语，里面的逗号是列举。

3

Ask “Who is doing it?” · 什么便宜、易生产？

What is cheap, versatile, and easy to produce? Not “problems” and not “there.” It is commercial plastics. 便宜、多用途、易生产的是塑料本身，不是“问题”。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

The subject after the comma must be commercial plastics, not the possessive “commercial plastics’ problems.” → D: “commercial plastics have two associated problems:” 逗号后主语必须是 commercial plastics，所有格形式不算。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ “there are two problems” puts “there” after the modifier, as if the problems were cheap.
B ✗ “two problems are…” says the problems are cheap and easy to produce.
C ✗ “commercial plastics’ two associated problems” makes the problems the subject; the possessive only describes them.

Answer: D. “Despite being cheap, versatile, and easy to produce” describes commercial plastics. → “commercial plastics have two associated problems:” 便宜、易生产的是塑料本身。

Worked Example 4

Form 4 · Module 2 · Question 25分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

Four whole clauses with different subjects: the mitigation / the bioswales / the bioswales’ mitigation / both street flooding and … pollution. → Modifier question. 四个分句主语不同 → 考修饰语。

2

Find the pattern · 找句型

“By reducing the runoff flowing into city sewers, ____” = By + doing, SVO. 句首是 By + doing 短语。

3

Ask “Who is doing it?” · 谁减少了径流？

What reduces the runoff? The bioswales (vegetated channels that absorb stormwater). So the subject must be the bioswales. 吸收雨水、减少径流的是 bioswales（植被浅沟）。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Choose the clause that starts with the bioswales and uses an active verb: B “the bioswales have mitigated…”. A and D are passive; C uses a possessive + abstract noun. 主语 bioswales + 主动语态；被动、所有格、抽象名词都错。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ “the mitigation … has been achieved by bioswales”: the mitigation becomes the subject.
C ✗ “the bioswales’ mitigation…” makes “mitigation” the subject.
D ✗ Passive: flooding and pollution become the subject, as if they reduced the runoff.

Answer: B. “By reducing the runoff flowing into city sewers” describes what the bioswales do. → “the bioswales have mitigated both street flooding and…” (active). 减少径流的是 bioswales，用主动语态。

Summary

What to remember about modifier placement修饰语位置要点

01 · 修饰对象

A sentence-opening phrase with no subject of its own describes the first noun after the comma.

02 · 寻找动作主体

Ask who or what the phrase describes: who recovered, what was woven, what was named an achievement.

03 · 匹配句子主语

Choose the clause that begins with that noun as its subject.

04 · 常见干扰

Traps: possessives (Henry’s reign), passive voice, there / it, and abstract nouns (the focus, the argument).

05 · 代回检查

Read the opening phrase together with the subject of your chosen answer. Check that the subject is the person or thing the phrase describes.
