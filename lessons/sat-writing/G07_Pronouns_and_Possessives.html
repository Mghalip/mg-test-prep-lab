Pronouns and Possessives

Pronouns that agree with their nouns, and apostrophes that show ownership

Grammar You Need

What is a pronoun? What is an antecedent? · 代词与先行词

A pronoun is a short word that replaces a noun so we do not repeat it. The noun it replaces is its antecedent (先行词 = the word that “goes before”).

Maria lost her keys, so she called her brother.her, she → Maria (the antecedent)

The forks were new, and people distrusted them.them → the forks

Job · 作用Singular 单数Plural 复数Example
subject 主格I, you, he, she, itwe, you, theyThey won the prize.
object 宾格me, you, him, her, itus, you, themThe bishop condemned them.
possessive 所有格my, your, his, her, itsour, your, theirTheir findings were new.
reflexive 反身myself, himself, itselfourselves, themselvesBarnacles attach themselves.

代词用来代替名词，被代替的那个名词叫“先行词”。代词有四种形式：主格（做主语）、宾格（做宾语）、所有格（“……的”）、反身（自己）。SAT 最常考 it / its / itself 与 they / them / their / themselves 的选择。

Grammar You Need

Pronoun agreement: one thing → it / its; many → they / their

Match the number 单复数一致

✅ The company revised its policy.one company → its

❌ The company revised their policy.a company / team / group is one thing → its

✅ The bees returned to their hive.many bees → their

❌ The utensils were new, so he called it a luxury.utensils (plural) → them

✅ Watson and Crick published their paper.A and B → plural

先行词是一个（包括 company、team、organization 这种集体名词）→ it / its；两个或以上 → they / them / their。

Find the antecedent first 先找先行词

  1. Ask: what does the pronoun stand for?
  2. Look back — sometimes to the previous sentence.
  3. Cross out “of + noun”: the color of the leaves → antecedent = color → it / its.

this / that = one thing or one whole idea; these / those = several things.

✅ Minh became leader in 2013, making this the first time…“this” = the whole event (one)

Unclear pronoun ✗: Ana told Mia that she won. (who won?)

先找代词指代谁，可能在前一句；this 可以指前面整件事（单数）。如果两个名词都可能被指代，代词就不清楚，应换成名词。

Grammar You Need

Relative pronouns: who, whom, which, that, whose

Which word for which noun? 用哪个词

WordUse · 用法Example
whoa person, subject (does the action)the scientist who found the fossil
whoma person, object (receives the action)the mentor whom the students thanked
whicha thing; often after a commathe book, which was new,
thata thing or person; no comma before itthe book that I borrowed
whosepossession (person or thing) “……的”a writer whose novel won

who 指人做主语；whom 指人做宾语；which 指物；that 指人或物、前面不加逗号；whose 表示“谁的/它的”。

The he / him test for who / whom

Answer the clause with he or him: he → who; him → whom.

✅ the player who scoredhe scored → who

✅ the player whom the coach chosethe coach chose him → whom

❌ the player whom scoredhim scored ✗ → who

❌ Rachel Carson, which wrote Silent Springa person → who

❌ The book, that I borrowed, was useful.never “, that” → which

用 he/him 替换：能说 he 就用 who，能说 him 就用 whom（m 对 m）。人不用 which；逗号后不用 that。

Grammar You Need

Apostrophes show ownership · 撇号表示所属

The rules 规则

OwnerAddExample
one owner (singular)’sthe student’s book (1 student)
many owners, plural ending in -s’ onlythe students’ books (many students)
irregular plural (no -s)’sthe children’s toys · people’s stories
shared (A and B own one thing)’s on the last nameJack and Jane’s book
separate (each owns one)’s on each nameJack’s and Jane’s books

一个所有者加 ’s；以 s 结尾的复数只加 ’；children、people 这种不规则复数加 ’s。共同拥有只在最后一个名字加 ’s。

Owner first, then the thing owned

A possessive word must be followed by the noun it owns: the city’s museums.

✅ the scientist’s discoveryone scientist owns the discovery

✅ the scientists’ discoveryseveral scientists share it

❌ Two scientist’s made a discovery.no owning here → plain plural: scientists

❌ the childrens’ books“childrens” is not a word → children’s

撇号单词后面必须紧跟它拥有的名词。只是“很多个”而不表示所属时，就是普通复数，不加撇号。

Grammar You Need

Plural, possessive, or contraction? · 复数、所有格还是缩写

Three look-alikes for nouns

WordMeansExample
planetsplural: more than oneEight planets orbit the Sun.
planet’sone planet ownsthe planet’s surface
planets’many planets ownthe planets’ orbits

Two questions: ① Does it own the next noun? (no → no apostrophe) ② One owner or many? (’s or s’)

两个问题：① 它是否“拥有”后面的名词？不拥有就不加撇号；② 拥有者是一个还是多个？一个用 ’s，多个用 s’。

Pronouns: possessive has NO apostrophe

Possessive 所有格Contraction 缩写
its shellit’s = it is / it has
their findingsthey’re = they are · there = a place
whose workwho’s = who is / who has
your ideayou’re = you are

❌ The bird spread it’s wings.“it is wings” makes no sense → its

Test: read it’s / they’re / who’s as “it is / they are / who is”. Nonsense → use the possessive. its’ never exists.

代词所有格（its、their、whose）永远不加撇号；带撇号的是缩写。把缩写展开读一遍，不通就选所有格。

Quick Check

Quick check 1 · Which pronoun?小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.The team celebrated ___ victory. (its / their)
its — a team is one group.
2.The penguins protect ___ eggs. (its / their)
their — penguins is plural.
3.The museum, along with its gardens, opens ___ doors at 9. (its / their)
its — the antecedent is the museum (cross out “along with its gardens”).
4.The volunteers ___ cleaned the beach were thanked. (who / which)
who — people, subject of the clause (they cleaned).
5.The author ___ we interviewed was kind. (who / whom)
whom — we interviewed him → whom.
6.The forest, ___ covers the hills, is old. (that / which)
which — after a comma, never “that”.
7.An artist ___ paintings sold out (whose / who’s)
whose — possession: the artist’s paintings.
8.Turtles lay eggs and then leave ___ . (them / themselves)
them — the eggs, not the turtles. (They attach themselves = to their own bodies.)
Quick Check

Quick check 2 · Apostrophe or not?问两个问题：是否所属？一个还是多个？

1.The ___ tail was long. (one dog) (dogs / dog’s / dogs’)
dog’s — one owner + ’s.
2.The ___ owners met. (many dogs) (dog’s / dogs’ / dogs)
dogs’ — many owners ending in -s → ’ only.
3.Three ___ ran in the park. (dog’s / dogs / dogs’)
dogs — just plural; nothing owned.
4.The ___ library opened. (childrens’ / children’s)
children’s — irregular plural + ’s.
5.___ going to rain today. (Its / It’s)
It’s — it is going to rain.
6.The plant turns ___ leaves to the sun. (it’s / its)
its — possession; “it is leaves” makes no sense.
Pronoun agreement

Pronouns must match the noun they replace

The rule

AntecedentPronouns
singular thingit, its, itself, this, that
plural (things or people)they, them, their, themselves, these, those

The antecedent may be in an earlier sentence, and it may be a whole idea: Minh became secretary-general in 2013, making this the first time…

代词的单复数与它所指的名词一致；所指对象可能在前一句，也可能是前面整件事。

Avoid these

  • you or one to mean a specific group (customers) ✗
  • an unclear pronoun when two nouns could match
  • them where the subject acts on itself → themselves: barnacles attach themselves

不要用 you / one 指代具体人群；主语作用于自身时用反身代词。

Possessives

Apostrophes, plurals, and possessives

FormMeaningExample
screwsplural: more than one screwtwo screws
screw’ssingular possessive: belongs to one screwthe screw’s threads
screws’plural possessive: belongs to several screwsthe screws’ threads
people’s / children’spossessive of an irregular pluralother people’s stories

Two questions for every noun

  1. Does it own the next noun? → apostrophe. If not → no apostrophe.
  2. Is it one or more than one? → s’ or ’s.

An apostrophe word must be followed by the noun it owns (the screw’s threads). Choices with two apostrophe words are rarely correct.

撇号词后面必须紧跟它“拥有”的名词。

Pronouns never take apostrophes for possession

itspossessive (its shell)
it’sit is / it has
theirpossessive
they’rethey are
its’does not exist
How to approach it

Steps

1

Pronoun choices: find the antecedent

Read back (sometimes to the previous sentence). Singular or plural? Thing or idea? 找代词指代的对象。

2

Its / it’s / their / they’re: expand the contraction

Read “it is” or “they are” in place of it’s / they’re. If it makes no sense, use the possessive. 把缩写展开读一遍。

3

Apostrophe choices: handle one noun at a time

Does it own the next noun? How many are there? 逐个名词判断：是否所属？单数还是复数？

4

Use context for number

“the Racetrack Playa” = one playa; “162 rocks” = many rocks. 用上下文判断单复数。

Worked Example 2

Form 5 · Module 2 · Question 20分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“them / this / that / it”: four pronouns. One is plural (them); three are singular. → Pronoun agreement: find the antecedent. 选项是代词，一复三单 → 找先行词。

2

Find the antecedent · 找先行词

“The Bishop … condemned the eating utensils because he considered ___ dangerous and unnecessary luxury items.” What did he consider dangerous? The utensils (the forks). 他认为什么危险？餐具 utensils。

3

Singular or plural? · 单复数

“utensils” ends in -s → plural. The rest of the sentence agrees: “luxury items” is also plural. utensils 复数，后面的 items 也是复数，互相印证。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Plural antecedent + object position (after “considered”) → them. 复数 + 宾格 → them。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ “this” is singular.
C ✗ “that” is singular.
D ✗ “it” is singular.

Answer: A. He condemned “the eating utensils” because he considered ___ dangerous luxury items. Antecedent: utensils (the forks) → plural → them. 指代 utensils，复数。

Worked Example 3

Form 5 · Module 1 · Question 20分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“these / those / this / some”: pointing words. These / those are plural; this is singular. → What does the pronoun point to? 指示代词：these/those 复数，this 单数。

2

Find the structure · 分析结构

“Minh became the thirteenth secretary-general … in January 2013, making ___ the first time the organization would appoint a Vietnamese leader.” The blank is the object of making. 空格是 making 的宾语。

Find the antecedent · 找先行词

What was “the first time”? Not one noun — the whole event: Minh becoming secretary-general. One event → singular. 指代前面整件事：一件事 → 单数。

Apply the rule · 规则

One event / idea → this. (“some” does not point to a specific event.) 指代整件事用 this。

A ✗ “these” is plural.
B ✗ “those” is plural.
D ✗ “some” does not refer to a specific event.

Answer: C. The pronoun refers to the whole event: Minh becoming secretary-general in 2013 → one event → singular → this. 指代前面整件事，单数 this。

Worked Example 4

Form 1 · Module 1 · Question 21分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“They’re / It’s / Their / Its”: two contractions and two possessives, one plural pair and one singular pair. → Possessive or contraction? Singular or plural? 两个缩写、两个所有格 → 先判断是否所属，再判断单复数。

2

Expand the contractions · 展开缩写

“They are findings were based…” and “It is findings were based…” make no sense. The blank must own “findings” → possessive. 展开后不通 → 需要所有格。

3

Find the owner · 找所有者

Whose findings? The previous sentence: “Watson and Crick discovered the double helix.” Two people → plural. 发现属于 Watson 和 Crick，两个人 → 复数。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Plural possessive pronoun → Their findings. 复数所有格 → Their。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ “They’re” = they are: “They are findings were based…” makes no sense.
B ✗ “It’s” = it is: not possessive, and singular.
D ✗ “Its” is singular; the findings belong to two people.

Answer: C. The findings belong to Watson and Crick → plural possessive → Their findings. Watson 和 Crick 的发现 → Their。

Summary

What to remember about pronouns and possessives代词与所有格要点

01 · 指代一致

A pronoun matches the number of its antecedent; the antecedent may be in an earlier sentence or may be a whole idea (this).

02 · 人称与反身代词

Do not use you / one for a specific group; use reflexive pronouns when the subject acts on itself.

03 · 所有格与缩写

its, their = possessive; it’s, they’re = it is, they are. its’ does not exist.

04 · 单复数所有格

For each noun: does it own the next noun? (apostrophe or not) How many? (’s or s’)

05 · 不规则复数

Irregular plurals: people’s, children’s. A possessive must be followed by the noun it owns.

What is tested? Which rule decides the answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

指出考点 → 说明规则 → 解释一个易错选项。
