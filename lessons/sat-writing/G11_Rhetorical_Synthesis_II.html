LESSON G11

Rhetorical Synthesis II: Compare, Contrast, Emphasize, Generalize

Goals about two things: the sentence must show the relationship the goal names
涉及两个对象的目标：句子必须体现目标要求的逻辑关系
SIMILARITYBoth A and B … · Neither A nor B …
DIFFERENCEA …, whereas / while / but B … · Unlike A, B …

Grammar You Need

Goals about two things: compare, contrast, similarity, difference

Sample notes (teacher-made)

• Giant pandas live in the mountains of central China. A
• Red pandas live in the Himalayas and southwestern China. B
• Both animals eat mostly bamboo. A+B
• The giant panda is a bear and weighs about 100 kg. A
• The red panda is not a bear and weighs about 5 kg. B

compare A and B比较：把 A 和 B 的同一特征并排放
contrast A and B对比：说出 A 和 B 的不同
emphasize a similarity强调相同点：A 和 B 都…
emphasize a difference强调不同点：A…，而 B…

Same notes, different goals

Goal: emphasize a similarity

✅ Both giant pandas and red pandas eat mostly bamboo.both names + what is true of both

❌ Giant pandas live in central China, and red pandas live in the Himalayas.two places = a difference, not a similarity

Goal: contrast the two animals’ size

✅ The giant panda weighs about 100 kg, whereas the red panda weighs only about 5 kg.same feature (weight) + contrast word

❌ The giant panda weighs about 100 kg, whereas the red panda is not a bear.two different features: weight vs. family

两个对象的目标：①两个名字都要出现；②说的是同一个特征；③用对关系词（相同 both，不同 whereas）。

Grammar You Need

Signal words that show the relationship · 关系信号词

Similarity 相同 = both are…

both A and B 两者都 · neither A nor B 两者都不 · like A, B… 像 A 一样 · also 也 · similarly / likewise 同样 · the same 相同

✅ Like the giant panda, the red panda eats mostly bamboo.

✅ Neither animal eats much meat.neither = not A and not B

❌ Unlike the giant panda, the red panda eats bamboo.“Unlike” says different, but both eat bamboo.

表示相同：both…and、neither…nor、like、also、similarly。

Difference 不同 = A is…, but B is…

whereas / while 而 · but 但是 · unlike A, B… 与 A 不同 · in contrast 相比之下 · -er than 比…更 · only 只有

✅ The giant panda is a bear, while the red panda is not.

✅ The giant panda is much heavier than the red panda.comparison with -er than

Grammar trap · compare like with like 同类比较:

❌ Unlike the giant panda, the red panda’s weight is small.compares an animal with a weight

✅ Unlike the giant panda, the red panda weighs only about 5 kg.animal vs. animal

表示不同：whereas、while、but、unlike、in contrast、比较级。用 unlike 时，比较的两边必须是同一类东西。

Grammar You Need

Emphasize and generalize · 强调与概括

Emphasize X 强调 X

To emphasize X means X is the main point of the sentence: put X in the main clause (the core S + V), not in a side detail.

Goal: emphasize the red panda’s small size.

✅ The red panda, which lives in the Himalayas, weighs only about 5 kg.main clause = “weighs only about 5 kg”

❌ The red panda, which weighs about 5 kg, lives in the Himalayas.Size is hidden in the which-clause; the main point is where it lives.

强调 X：让 X 出现在句子主干里（主语 + 谓语），而不是藏在 which 从句或插入语里。

Generalize 概括

To generalize (make a generalization) means to say something about a whole group, not just one member. Look for many, most, some, generally, a variety of.

Notes: camels go weeks without drinking · fennec foxes get water from their food · kangaroo rats get water from the seeds they eat.

✅ Many desert animals have special ways to survive with little water.about the whole group

❌ Kangaroo rats get water from the seeds they eat.one example, not a general statement

概括：对整类事物作总体陈述（many、most、generally），而不是只说其中一个例子。

Quick Check

Quick check · Does it match the goal? (panda notes)两个对象都出现了吗？关系对吗？

1.Goal: similarity.
“Giant pandas and red pandas both eat mostly bamboo.”
✓ both names + “both” + shared fact.
2.Goal: similarity.
“The giant panda weighs about 100 kg, and the red panda weighs about 5 kg.”
✗ Two different weights = a difference.
3.Goal: contrast where they live.
“Giant pandas live in central China, whereas red pandas live in the Himalayas and southwestern China.”
✓ same feature (home) + “whereas.”
4.Goal: contrast the two animals.
“The two animals differ in several ways.”
✗ Says they differ but not how.
5.Goal: contrast the two animals.
“The red panda is not a bear.”
✗ Only one animal is mentioned.
6.Goal: emphasize that the giant panda is a bear.
“The giant panda, a bear, lives in central China.”
✗ “a bear” is only an appositive; the main point is where it lives.
7.Goal: generalize about pandas’ diet.
“Both kinds of panda rely mostly on bamboo.”
✓ a statement about the whole group.
8.Goal: compare their weights.
“The giant panda is about twenty times heavier than the red panda.”
✓ same feature, both animals (100 kg vs. 5 kg).
Signal words

Match the logic of the goal

GoalThe right sentence…Signal words to look for
emphasize a similaritysays what is true of bothboth … and, neither … nor, like, also, the same
emphasize a difference / contrast A and Bstates a fact about A and the opposing fact about Bwhereas, while, but, unlike, in contrast, only
compare A and B (lengths, contributions…)puts the same feature of both side by side-er than, slightly shorter, while
emphasize Xmakes X the main pointX is the subject or the main clause

相似 → both / neither；差异 → whereas / unlike；比较 → 同一属性并列；强调 → 让 X 成为主干；概括 → 对整体的总体陈述。

Wrong-answer patterns

Why tempting choices fail

Common traps

  • Only one side: gives a fact about A but nothing about B.
  • Two facts, no relationship: “A was published in 2008, and B in 2018.” Different facts, but not the difference the goal asks for, or not stated as a contrast.
  • Wrong relationship: a difference when the goal asks for a similarity.
  • Right topic, wrong focus: background definitions instead of the point.

只说一方；两个事实没有关系；关系方向错；只讲背景。

A quick test

Cover the notes. Read the choice alone. Could a reader say, “So the difference is …” (or “the similarity is …”)?

If the reader would have to work it out from separate facts, the choice does not emphasize it.

只读选项本身：读者能否直接说出“区别/相同点是…”？

How to approach it

Steps

1

Read the goal: what relationship, between what?

“a difference between baking soda and baking powder” 关系类型 + 两个对象。

2

Cut choices that mention only one of the two

For compare / contrast / similarity, both names must appear. 只提一个对象的先排除。

3

Check the relationship word

Similarity → both / neither. Difference → whereas / unlike / but. 看连接词的逻辑方向。

4

Check the content against the notes

Is it the specific similarity or difference the goal names? 内容是否与目标一致。

Worked Example 2

Form 1 · Module 1 · Question 33分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the question · 看目标

Goal: “compare the two women’s contributions to the March on Washington.” Checklist: ① Hedgeman ② Bates ③ the same feature — what each did at the march. 比较两位女性对游行的贡献：两人都要出现，而且都要写“对游行做了什么”。

2

Label the notes · 给笔记贴标签

Careers (background): Hedgeman = political adviser; Bates = journalist. Contributions to the march: Hedgeman worked behind the scenes to get a woman speaker included; Bates was the only woman to speak. 职业只是背景；对游行的贡献：一个幕后争取，一个上台演讲。

3

State the rule · 规则

A comparison needs both people and the same feature the goal names. Comparing their jobs is a different feature; describing only one person is half a comparison. 比较 = 两个对象 + 同一特征。

4

Apply it · 逐项核对

A: only Bates’s contribution. B: careers, not the march. D: says “only one spoke” but not what either did. C: Hedgeman’s behind-the-scenes work + Bates’s speech, joined by “Although.” → C. 只有 C 把二人对游行的贡献并列。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Says they contributed differently but describes only Bates’s contribution.
B ✗ Compares their careers, not their contributions to the march.
D ✗ Does not say what either woman did at the march.

Answer: C. Contributions to the march: Hedgeman worked behind the scenes to get a woman speaker included; Bates was the woman who spoke. C sets the two side by side with “Although.” 比较二人对游行的贡献：一个幕后，一个上台。

Worked Example 3

Form 3 · Module 2 · Question 32分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the question · 看目标

Goal: “emphasize a similarity between the two books.” Checklist: ① both book titles ② something true of both. 强调相同点：两本书 + 两者共有的特点。

2

Label the notes · 给笔记贴标签

Different: 2008 vs. 2018; fifteen vs. twenty-five stories; suburbs vs. city. Same: both describe surreal events in otherwise ordinary settings. 年份、篇数、地点都不同；相同的是都写平凡环境中的超现实事件。

3

State the rule · 规则

Similarity → look for “both,” “also,” “like.” A choice with “unlike,” “fewer than,” or two different dates states a difference and fails. 相同点用 both / also / like；unlike、比较级、不同年份都是“不同点”。

4

Apply it · 逐项核对

A: fewer stories (difference). B: two dates (difference). C: “Unlike” (difference). D: names both books and says both describe surreal events in ordinary places. → D. 只有 D 说的是相同点。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Gives a difference (number of stories).
B ✗ Gives two different dates; a difference, not a similarity.
C ✗ “Unlike” signals a difference.

Answer: D. Both books describe surreal events in otherwise ordinary places. D names both books and says “both.” 相同点：都写平凡环境中的超现实事件。

Worked Example 4

Form 5 · Module 2 · Question 32分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the question · 看目标

Goal: “contrast first-class levers and second-class levers.” Checklist: ① first-class ② second-class ③ how they differ. 对比第一类和第二类杠杆：两类都要出现，并说出具体区别。

2

Label the notes · 给笔记贴标签

Bullets 1–4 = definitions for all levers (beam, fulcrum, effort, load). Bullet 5: first-class → fulcrum between effort and load. Bullet 6: second-class → load between effort and fulcrum. 前 4 条是所有杠杆的定义（背景）；第 5、6 条才是两类的区别。

3

State the rule · 规则

A contrast must name both things and say how they differ, with a contrast word (but, whereas, while). “They are different” is not enough; a shared fact is a similarity. 对比要说清楚“怎么不同”，只说“不同”不够。

4

Apply it · 逐项核对

A: contrasts effort vs. load, not the two classes. B: “in different locations” — but how? C: a similarity. D: first-class (fulcrum in the middle) but second-class (load in the middle). → D. D 说出了具体区别。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Contrasts effort and load, not the two classes of levers.
B ✗ Says they differ but not how.
C ✗ States a similarity.

Answer: D. First-class: fulcrum between effort and load. Second-class: load between effort and fulcrum. D states both with “but.” 对比两类杠杆中支点与负载的位置。

Summary

What to remember about Rhetorical Synthesis II修辞综合 II 要点

01 · 关系与对象

Identify the relationship (similarity, difference, comparison) and the two objects.

02 · 比较信号词

Similarity → both / neither; difference → whereas / while / unlike / but.

03 · 覆盖两个对象

Cut any choice that mentions only one of the two objects.

04 · 比较同一特征

Two separate facts are not a comparison unless they describe the same feature the goal names.

05 · 强调与概括

“Emphasize X” → X is the main point. “Generalize” → a statement about the whole group.

指出考点 → 说明规则 → 解释一个易错选项。
