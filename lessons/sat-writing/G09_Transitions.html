Grammar You Need

What is a transition? · 什么是过渡词（逻辑连接词）

A transition = a signpost 路标

A transition is a word or phrase at the start of a sentence (or between commas) that tells the reader how this sentence is related to the one before it: opposite? result? example? one more point?

I studied all night. However, I failed the test.opposite of what we expect 转折

I studied all night. As a result, I passed the test.result 结果

❌ I studied all night. For example, I passed the test.Passing is not an example of studying.

过渡词像“路标”，告诉读者后一句和前一句是什么关系。同样两句话，换一个过渡词，逻辑就完全不同。

Say the relationship in your own words first 先用自己的话说关系

Before you look at the choices, cover them and fill in this frame:

“Sentence 1 says ___. Sentence 2 says ___. So sentence 2 is the opposite / a result / an example / one more point / the next step of sentence 1.”

S1: Wolves cannot see at two weeks. S2: Dogs can see at two weeks.My words: “wolves can’t, but dogs can” → opposite → by contrast

先遮住选项，用自己的话（中文也可以）说出两句之间的关系，比如“但是”“所以”“比如”“而且”，再去选项中找同类的词。

Family 关系Words · 中文意思Example
Contrast 对比/转折however 然而, but, by contrast / in contrast 相比之下, on the other hand 另一方面, conversely 相反地Mia likes cities. In contrast, Leo prefers villages.
Concession 让步（出乎意料）nevertheless / nonetheless 尽管如此, still 仍然, even so 即便如此, though 不过The plan was risky. Nevertheless, the team tried it.
Addition 补充/递进moreover 此外, furthermore 而且, in addition 另外, also, besidesShe is smart. Moreover, she is kind.
Cause → effect 因果therefore / thus 因此, consequently 结果, as a result 所以, accordingly 相应地, henceIt rained heavily; therefore, the game was canceled.

Contrast vs. concession: contrast = two different things side by side; concession = “X is true, but the result is surprising.” 对比：两件事不一样（A 喜欢城市，B 喜欢乡村）。让步：按理说不会这样，但事实就是这样（计划有风险，但仍然去做）。

Grammar reminder: these words are adverbs, not conjunctions. Use “. However,” or “; however,” — never “, however,” between two complete sentences (G02). however、therefore 等是副词，不能用逗号连接两个完整句，前面用句号或分号。

Grammar You Need

Five tricky transitions: in fact, after all, instead, to this end, though

in fact · after all · instead

In fact 事实上 = the next sentence makes the first one stronger or more specific.

✅ The museum is popular. In fact, a million people visited last year.

After all 毕竟 = the next sentence gives the reason for the first one.

✅ Don’t be too hard on him. After all, he is only five.

Instead 而是/取而代之 = replaces something not done. Look for a “not” before it.

✅ The bird did not fly south. Instead, it stayed all winter.

❌ The bird flew south. Instead, it arrived in Mexico.Nothing was rejected, so nothing is replaced.

in fact：加强前句；after all：给前句理由；instead：前面通常有否定，后句说“实际做了什么”。

to this end · though

To this end 为此 = “to reach this goal.” The first sentence states a goal; the next states an action taken for it.

✅ The city wants cleaner air. To this end, it banned old cars.

❌ The city has clean air. To this end, it is popular.No goal is stated.

Though has two uses:

✅ I was tired. I finished the homework, though.adverb ≈ however (end or middle of a sentence)

✅ Though I was tired, I finished the homework.conjunction ≈ although

❌ Though she studied, but she failed.Never use though + but together.

to this end：前句是目标，后句是为此采取的行动。though 作副词≈however，作连词≈although；不能和 but 连用。

Grammar You Need

Easily confused pairs · 易混淆的关系

PairHow to tell them apartMini example
for example vs. in additionIs S2 one case of S1 (example), or a new, separate point (addition)? 举例 vs. 补充Many birds migrate. For example, swallows fly south. / Birds migrate. In addition, some change color.
therefore vs. after allDirection! therefore: S1 is the cause → S2 the result. after all: S2 is the cause of S1. 方向相反It rained. Therefore, we stayed in. / We stayed in. After all, it was raining.
however vs. neverthelessBoth turn. nevertheless stresses “even so, against expectation.” 让步更强调“出乎意料”The price was high. Nevertheless, many bought it.
as a result vs. nextas a result needs a cause; next only needs time order. 因果 vs. 顺序First, boil water. Next, add tea.
similarly vs. in contrastSame pattern in a new place → similarly; opposite → in contrast. 相同 vs. 相反Ants live in colonies. Similarly, bees do.

SAT 的错误选项常常是“相邻类别”：举例和补充、因果和顺序、对比和相似。判断方向和类别，而不是凭感觉。

What is it?

What is a Transitions question?

Definition

A blank sits at the start of a sentence (or inside it, between commas). The four choices are transition words. You choose the one that shows the logical relationship between this sentence and the previous one.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

每套 4–6 题。考的是逻辑关系，不是语法。

One idea is enough

Usually only one choice belongs to the right category. Once you name the relationship correctly, the other three are clearly wrong.

一般只有一个选项属于正确的逻辑类别。关键是先判断关系。

Read the sentence before

The transition connects the blank sentence to the previous sentence (sometimes to an earlier one). Always read both.

Categories

Transition categories

RelationshipTransitionsQuestion to ask
Contrast 转折对比however, but, yet, still, nevertheless, nonetheless, by contrast, in contrast, on the other hand, conversely, thoughDoes the new sentence go against the previous one or against what we would expect?
Cause → result 因果therefore, thus, so, as a result, consequently, accordingly, hence, for this reason, in turnIs the new sentence caused by the previous one?
Example 举例for example, for instance, specifically, to illustrateIs it one case of a general statement?
Addition 递进in addition, furthermore, moreover, additionally, also, besides, in fact (stronger)Is it one more point of the same kind?
Similarity 相似similarly, likewise, in the same wayDoes it show the same thing happening elsewhere?
Time / sequence 顺序first, second, next, then, finally, later, meanwhile, previously, today, currentlyIs it the next step or a different time?
Restatement 换言之in other words, that isDoes it say the same thing again more simply?
Replacement / alternative 替代alternatively, insteadIs it another option?
How to approach it

Four steps for Transitions

1

Cover the choices; read the previous sentence and the blank sentence

Understand what each sentence says. 先不看选项，读前一句和空格所在句。

2

Summarize each in a few words

“wolves: can’t see or hear at two weeks” / “dogs: can see, hear, smell at two weeks.” 各用几个词概括。

3

Name the relationship

Same or different? Cause or result? General or example? Earlier or later? 判断关系：相同/相反/因果/举例/顺序。

4

Pick the choice in that category

Then read the sentence with it to confirm. 选对应类别的词，代入确认。

Worked Example 2

Form 3 · Module 1 · Question 27分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

for instance / nevertheless / meanwhile / in addition → all transitions from different families. This is a logic question, not grammar. 四个选项是不同类别的过渡词 → 考逻辑关系。

2

Summarize sentence 1 · 概括前句

Cover the choices. The sentence before says: “Not all conifer species are evergreen.” = a general statement. 前一句：并非所有针叶树都常绿（一个总的说法）。

3

Summarize sentence 2 · 概括空格句

“Larch trees ___ lose their needles every fall.” = one particular conifer that is not evergreen. 空格句：落叶松每年秋天掉针叶——一种不常绿的针叶树。

4

Name the relationship · 判断关系

In my own words: “Not all conifers are evergreen — for example, larches.” S2 is one case of S1 → Example family → A for instance. 后句是前句的一个具体例子 → 举例。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

B ✗ “nevertheless” signals contrast, but larches support the previous statement.
C ✗ “meanwhile” signals time, not an example.
D ✗ “in addition” signals a new, separate point, not an example of the one just made.

Answer: A. General statement: “not all conifer species are evergreen.” Then: “Larch trees … lose their needles every fall” = one case of that statement → for instance. 落叶松是“并非所有针叶树都常绿”的一个例子。

Worked Example 3

Form 1 · Module 1 · Question 30分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

in other words / for instance / by contrast / accordingly → restatement, example, contrast, result. 重述、举例、对比、因果四类。

2

Summarize sentence 1 · 概括前句

Wolves at two weeks: can smell, but cannot see or hear. 两周大的狼：能闻，但看不见、听不见。

3

Summarize sentence 2 · 概括空格句

Dogs at two weeks: can see, hear, and smell. Same age, different animal, opposite abilities. 两周大的狗：能看、能听、能闻。

4

Name the relationship · 判断关系

My words: “wolves can’t, but dogs can.” Two different things side by side → Contrast family → C by contrast. 两种动物同一时间点情况相反 → 对比。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ “in other words” would mean the dogs’ sentence restates the wolves’ sentence.
B ✗ “for instance” would make dogs an example of wolves’ limits.
D ✗ “accordingly” would mean the dogs’ abilities result from the wolves’ limits.

Answer: C. Wolves at two weeks: can smell but cannot see or hear. Dogs at two weeks: can see, hear, and smell. Opposite facts about two animals → by contrast. 狼和狗在同一时间点的感官相反 → 对比。

Form 5 · Module 2 · Question 26分步引导：先思考，再点击显示下一步

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

For example / Next / Similarly / In contrast → example, sequence, similarity, contrast. 举例、顺序、相似、对比四类。

2

Find the signal words · 找信号词

Read before and after the blank: “it first scraped off the top layer … ___ it sprayed water … Lastly, it smoothed over the ice.” 前后有 first … lastly，说明在讲步骤。

3

Summarize the blank sentence · 概括空格句

Spraying water is the second step of the machine’s work, between “first” and “lastly.” 喷水是机器工作的第二步。

4

Name the relationship · 判断关系

Steps in time order → Sequence family → B Next. (first → next → lastly) 时间顺序 → 选 Next。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ “For example” does not continue a sequence of steps.
C ✗ “Similarly” suggests the same action again, not the next step.
D ✗ “In contrast” signals opposition; spraying water is the next step, not an opposite.

Answer: B. The text lists steps: the machine “first scraped off the top layer,” “___ it sprayed water,” “Lastly…”. The middle step → Next. first … next … lastly：顺序。

Wrong answers

Common traps in Transitions questions

Unexpected contrast

Still, nevertheless, however can contrast with what we would expect, not only with a stated fact (Sher-Gil in Paris).

转折可以针对“预期”，不一定针对字面的事实。

Example vs. addition

Is the new sentence one case of the previous general statement (for example), or a new point (in addition)?

举例和递进容易混。

Result vs. sequence

“As a result” needs a cause; “next / finally” only needs time order.

因果与时间顺序要区分。

Read beyond the blank sentence

Sometimes the relationship is with an earlier sentence, or the following sentence helps (e.g., “first … next … lastly”).

有时要看更前或更后的句子。

Watch for contrast with an expectation (still), example vs. addition, and result vs. time order.
