Why these points are worth it

Grammar questions test a limited set of rules. Once you know them, these are the most reliable points on the test.

语法考点有限，学会规则就能稳定拿分。

The stems

What the questions look like

TypeStemWhat changes in the choices
BoundariesWhich choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?the same words, different punctuation
Form, Structure, and Sensethe same stemdifferent forms of a word (is / are; to use / using; its / it’s) or different word order
TransitionsWhich choice completes the text with the most logical transition?different linking words (However, Therefore, For example)
Rhetorical SynthesisThe student wants to … Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?different sentences built from the notes

The first two types share one stem. To know what is tested, look at the choices: if only punctuation changes, it is Boundaries; if word forms change, it is Form, Structure, and Sense. 前两类问法相同，看选项的差异判断考点。

Grammar Foundations 1

Parts of speech · 词性

Part of speech · 词性What it does · 作用ExamplesWhy it matters on the SAT
Noun 名词names a person, place, thing, or ideaLucas, ocean, cable, ideasubjects and objects are nouns
Pronoun 代词replaces a nounhe, it, they, this, which, whoG07: must match the noun it replaces
Verb 动词an action or a stateenjoys, was completed, is, seemevery sentence needs one (G05, G06)
Adjective 形容词describes a noundeep, ancient, translucentpart of the description, not the core
Adverb 副词describes a verb, adjective, or whole sentencequickly, very, however, thereforehowever is an adverb, not a conjunction (G02)
Preposition 介词links a noun to the rest of the sentencein, of, with, by, from, about“of + noun” is never the subject (G05)
Conjunction 连词joins words or clausesand, but, so · because, althoughdecides the punctuation (G01, G02)

不用背定义，但要认得出：名词/代词做主语，动词做谓语，介词后面跟名词组成介词短语，连词负责把句子连起来。

Grammar Foundations 2

Every sentence needs a subject and a verb

Subject 主语 + Verb 谓语

The subject is who or what the sentence is about. The verb tells what the subject does or is.

She likes pizza.SVO

The cable was completed in 1866.

Many deep-sea fish have unusual proteins.

The results were surprising.

主语：句子在说“谁/什么”；谓语：主语“做什么/是什么”。is、are、was、were 也是动词（系动词）。

How to find them 怎么找

  1. Find the verb first: the word that shows the action or state and has a tense (past, present, future).
  2. Ask “Who or what + verb?” The answer is the subject.

Last year, the museum opened a new hall.Who opened? → the museum (not “last year”).

In the deep ocean, pressure can affect proteins.What can affect? → pressure.

先找有时态的动词，再问“谁/什么 + 这个动词？”，答案就是主语。句首的时间、地点短语不是主语。

Grammar Foundations 2

S–V–O: five basic sentence patterns · 五种基本句型

S = subject 主语 · V = verb 谓语 · O = object 宾语 (receives the action) · C = complement 补语 (says what someone/something is or is like)

PatternMeaningExampleHow to see it · 怎么看
SVThe action needs no object.I woke up.I 是主语，woke up 是谓语，后面不需要宾语。
SVOThe subject does something to an object.I love you.you 是 love 的宾语。
SVCThe complement describes the subject.I am a student.a student 说明 I 的身份（系动词 am/is/seem/become）。
SVOOTwo objects: a person + a thing.I gave you a book.you 是间接宾语，a book 是直接宾语。
SVOCThe complement describes the object.The news made me happy.happy 说明宾语 me 的状态。

How we use this on the SAT: you do not need to name the pattern. In a long sentence, find S + V first; then decide whether the next part is an object, a complement, or an extra part you can cross out. Not every sentence has an object: “The audience left.” is a complete S + V sentence. 不必背句型名称。看到长句先找 S + V，再判断后面是宾语、补语，还是可以划掉的修饰成分。不是每句都有宾语。

Grammar Foundations 3

Which verb forms can be the main verb?

Can be the main verb ✓ 可以做谓语

A form with a tense:

✅ Scientists study / studied / will study the ocean.

✅ Scientists are studying / have studied the ocean.

✅ The bridge was built in 1890.

有时态的动词形式才能做谓语：一般现在/过去/将来、进行时（be + doing）、完成时（have + done）、被动（be + done）。

Cannot work alone ✗ 非谓语，不能单独做谓语

-ing, to + verb, and -ed/-en (without be or have):

❌ Scientists studying the ocean.No real verb: this is not a sentence. 没有谓语。

❌ The bridge, built in 1890.“built in 1890” only describes the bridge.

✅ Scientists studying the ocean have found new species.The real verb is “have found”; “studying the ocean” describes scientists.

doing、to do、done 单独出现时只是修饰或补充，不是谓语。SAT 常在这里设陷阱：看起来像动词，其实不是。

Grammar Foundations 4

Phrases: groups of words without a subject + verb

Kind of phrase · 短语ExampleWhat it does
Prepositional 介词短语in the deep ocean, of the proteins, with the help of digital imagingadds where / when / which one
-ing phrase 现在分词短语distorting the proteins’ shape, ensuring that…describes a noun or adds a result
-ed phrase 过去分词短语built into the rocky outcrops, named in 1999describes a noun (passive meaning)
to phrase 不定式短语to protect itself from predatorspurpose (in order to)
Appositive 同位语a physicist at Berkeley, the author of Paradiserenames the noun next to it

Rule: a phrase is never a sentence by itself. It must be attached to an independent clause. 短语没有“主语 + 谓语”，永远不能单独成句，必须挂在一个完整句上。

Grammar Foundations 5

Clauses: independent and dependent

Independent clause 独立句（主句）

subject + verb + a complete thought. It can stand alone as a sentence.

✅ Lucas enjoys hiking.

✅ TMAO is found in deep-sea fish.

Test: put a period after it and read it aloud. If it sounds finished, it is independent.

独立句 = 主语 + 谓语 + 意思完整，能单独成句。检验方法：后面加句号读一遍，听起来“说完了”就是独立句。

Dependent clause 从句

subject + verb, but it begins with a word that makes it depend on another clause.

❌ because Lucas enjoys hikingBecause… what? The thought is unfinished.

❌ which is found in deep-sea fish“which” needs a noun to describe.

Starting words: because, although, though, when, while, if, since, after, before, unless, whereas · who, which, that, whose, where

从句也有主谓，但开头有 because、although、when、which、who 等词，意思“悬着”，必须依附一个独立句。

Grammar Foundations 6

Complete sentence or fragment? · 完整句还是残句

Fragment 残句 ✗What is missingFixed ✓
By doing homework.only a phrase; no main clauseBy doing homework, she improved a lot.
Because she was sad.dependent clause aloneBecause she was sad, she stayed home.
Although I studied hard.dependent clause aloneAlthough I studied hard, I didn’t pass the test.
Sitting at the desk.-ing phrase; who is sitting?Sitting at the desk, she started writing.
Went to the store.no subjectI went to the store.
The scientist, a professor at Yale.no verb (only a noun + appositive)The scientist, a professor at Yale, published the study.

A fragment “feels unfinished”: you keep waiting for the rest. On the SAT, a choice that leaves a fragment is always wrong. 读出来觉得“话没说完”就是残句。SAT 中造成残句的选项一定错。

Grammar Foundations 7

Three kinds of connecting words · 三类连接词

KindWordsCorrect patternLesson
Coordinating conjunctions (FANBOYS) 并列连词for, and, nor, but, or, yet, soS V, and S V.G01
Subordinating conjunctions 从属连词because, although, when, if, since, while, whereas…Because S V, S V. / S V because S V.G02
Transition words (conjunctive adverbs) 连接副词however, therefore, moreover, thus, instead, for exampleS V; however, S V. / S V. However, S V.G02, G09

The big difference

✅ The test was hard, but she passed.

❌ The test was hard, however, she passed.however cannot join two sentences with a comma.

✅ The test was hard; however, she passed.

中文说明

FANBOYS 和从属连词是真正的“连词”，可以把两个句子连起来；however、therefore 等是副词，只表示逻辑关系，不能连接句子，所以前面要用句号或分号。

Find the core

Finding the core of a long sentence

SAT sentences are long. Cross out what is extra to find the subject and the verb.

The earliest transatlantic telegraph cable linking the North American and European continents was completed in 1866.

Cross out

  • prepositional phrases (of…, in…, with…)
  • -ing / -ed descriptive phrases
  • clauses with which, who, that
  • anything between commas, dashes, or parentheses

What remains

The subject (cable) and its verb (was completed). Now you can check agreement, punctuation, and form.

中文提示

划掉介词短语、分词短语、定语从句和插入语，剩下的就是句子主干（SVO）。几乎每道语法题都从这一步开始。

Grammar Foundations 9

Cross out the extras, keep the core · 去掉修饰，找主干

The girl (sitting by my side) smiled.subject = the girl

The book (on the table) is mine.prepositional phrase crossed out

The apple (eaten by my mom) was sweet.-ed phrase crossed out

The man (who helped us yesterday) is my uncle.who-clause crossed out

The earliest transatlantic telegraph cable (linking the North American and European continents) was completed in 1866.SAT-length sentence

blue = subject 主语red = verb 谓语gray = cross out 划掉

划掉：介词短语、doing/done 短语、who/which/that 从句、两个逗号或破折号之间的插入语。剩下的主语和谓语就是句子主干。

Quick Check

Quick check 1 · Sentence or fragment?小练习：先自己判断，再点开看答案

1.The scientists who studied the coral reef.
Fragment ✗ — “who studied the coral reef” describes the scientists; there is no main verb for scientists. Fix: …reef published their results.
2.Although the experiment failed, the team learned a lot.
Sentence ✓ — dependent clause + independent clause (the team learned).
3.Built in 1890, the bridge is still in use.
Sentence ✓ — “Built in 1890” is a phrase; the core is the bridge is.
4.Hoping to find water on Mars.
Fragment ✗ — an -ing phrase only. Who is hoping?
5.The results surprised everyone.
Sentence ✓ — subject results + verb surprised.
6.Because the ocean is so deep.
Fragment ✗ — dependent clause alone.
7.The novel, a story about colonial East Africa.
Fragment ✗ — no verb; “a story about…” only renames the novel.
8.The novel tells a story about colonial East Africa.
Sentence ✓ — novel + tells.
Quick Check

Quick check 2 · Find the subject and the verb先找有时态的动词，再问“谁/什么”做了这个动作

1.The proteins in the cells of deep-sea fish keep their shape.
S = proteins, V = keep. (“in the cells of deep-sea fish” is a prepositional phrase.)
2.Each of the paintings shows a different city.
S = Each, V = shows. (“of the paintings” is extra.)
3.Last summer, the students working in the lab discovered a new species.
S = students, V = discovered. (“working in the lab” describes students.)
4.The theory, developed by a physicist and his son, explained the extinction.
S = theory, V = explained. (“developed by…” is between commas.)
5.Among the club’s leaders was Josephine Ruffin.
S = Josephine Ruffin, V = was (inverted order 倒装).
6.To finish the project on time was difficult.
S = To finish the project on time, V = was.
Anatomy

A Boundaries question

1The blank. The text is one or two sentences with a blank. You choose what goes in it.
2The choices. All four have the same words (out … but); only the punctuation and the conjunction change. → a Boundaries question.
3The question to ask. Where does one clause end and the next begin? What can legally join them? 问自己：前后各是什么结构？用什么连接才合法？
The method

Four steps for every grammar question

1

Compare the four choices

What is the same? What changes? The difference tells you the rule being tested. 比较四个选项，找差异，确定考点。

2

Read the whole sentence, and the one before it

Many answers depend on words far from the blank: the real subject, a tense earlier in the text, an earlier dash. 读完整句，必要时读前一句。

3

Analyze the structure

Find subjects and verbs; mark clauses and phrases; cross out extra parts. 分析句子结构：主谓、分句、短语。

4

Apply the rule and eliminate

Cross out choices that break the rule. Two choices that are grammatically identical (a period and a semicolon between the same clauses) must both be wrong. 按规则排除；语法作用相同的两个选项一定都错。
