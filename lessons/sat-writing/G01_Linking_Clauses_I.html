Linking Clauses I

How to join or separate two complete sentences correctly
两个完整句之间该用什么连接或分隔

KNOW THE LEGAL JOINSPeriod, semicolon, or comma + FANBOYS; never a comma alone, never nothing.

Grammar You Need

First: what is an independent clause?

Independent clause = a complete sentence

It has three things: a subject, a verb with tense, and a complete thought.

✅ Lucas enjoys hiking.

✅ Clear glass (, which is classified as transparent,) allows all light to pass through.

❌ because Lucas enjoys hikingdependent clause: starts with “because”.

❌ enjoying a new mountain every summerphrase: no subject, no real verb.

独立句 = 主语 + 有时态的谓语 + 意思完整。可以单独成句。

The period test 句号测试

Cover everything after the blank. Put a period where the blank is. Read the left part alone. Does it sound finished? Then do the same for the right part.

✅ Plastic-bag consumption decreased by ninety percent.finished → independent

✅ Such taxes are subject to the rebound effect.finished → independent

If both sides pass the test, you have two independent clauses meeting at the blank. This lesson is about how to join them.

把空格当成句号，分别读左边和右边：都“说完了”，就是两个独立句相遇，本课就是解决它们怎么连接。

Grammar You Need

Two mistakes: run-on and comma splice

Run-on sentence 流水句 ✗

Two independent clauses with nothing between them.

❌ I woke up I brushed my teeth.

❌ Lucas enjoys hiking he climbs a mountain every summer.

两个完整句之间什么都没有 = run-on，错误。

Comma splice 逗号粘连 ✗

Two independent clauses joined by only a comma.

❌ I woke up, I brushed my teeth.

❌ Lucas enjoys hiking, he climbs a mountain every summer.

A comma is too weak to hold two sentences together by itself.

只用一个逗号连接两个完整句 = comma splice，SAT 最常考的错误之一。逗号“力气不够”。

Grammar You Need

FANBOYS: the seven coordinating conjunctions

WordMeaning · 意思Example (comma + FANBOYS)
forbecause 因为She stayed home, for she was sick.
andadds 并且I woke up, and I brushed my teeth.
norand not 也不He did not call, nor did he write.
butcontrast 但是The test was hard, but she passed.
orchoice 或者We can walk, or we can take the bus.
yetcontrast 然而The plan was simple, yet it worked.
soresult 所以It started raining, so we canceled the picnic.

Watch out: use the comma only when FANBOYS joins two complete sentences. One subject with two verbs needs no comma: “I woke up and brushed my teeth.” (no second subject) 只有连接两个完整句时才用“逗号 + FANBOYS”；同一个主语、两个动词时不加逗号。

The rule

Two independent clauses: four legal joins

Legal ✓

PeriodLucas enjoys hiking. He climbs a new mountain every summer.
SemicolonLucas enjoys hiking; he climbs a new mountain every summer.
Comma + FANBOYSLucas enjoys hiking, so he climbs a new mountain every summer.
Make one dependentSince Lucas enjoys hiking, he climbs a new mountain every summer.

FANBOYS = for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so（并列连词）。

Illegal ✗

Comma splice 逗号粘连Lucas enjoys hiking, he climbs a new mountain every summer.
Run-on 无标点Lucas enjoys hiking he climbs a new mountain every summer.
FANBOYS, no comma (long clauses)…die out but it left unexplored the question… (SAT expects “, but”)

两个独立句之间：只有逗号不行，什么都不加也不行。

Semicolon = period. Both need a complete sentence on each side. 分号 = 句号，两边都必须是完整句。

What it looks like

How these questions look

The four choices contain the same words with different punctuation. Usually: a comma, a period, a semicolon, nothing, or a FANBOYS word.

Form · QChoicesAnswer
6-2-22through, / through / through; / through andsemicolon
3-1-22percent, such / percent and such / percent. Such / percent suchperiod
3-2-23(Ru) / (Ru) but / (Ru), / (Ru), butcomma + but

Same job, same answer. Between two complete sentences, a period and a semicolon do exactly the same job. So if a question offered both “percent. Such” and “percent; such”, they would be equally correct, and neither could be the one answer. Use this to eliminate: two choices that do the same grammatical job are both wrong. 句号和分号在两个完整句之间作用完全相同。如果两个选项只差在句号和分号，它们要么都对、要么都错，不可能只有一个是答案，可以同时排除。

How to approach it

Four steps for linking clauses

1

Read to the end of the sentence

The second clause often starts right after the blank. 读到句子结束。

2

Test the left side

Can the words before the blank stand alone as a sentence? (subject + verb + complete thought) 空格左边能否独立成句？

3

Test the right side

Can the words after the blank stand alone? Phrases that begin the next sentence (Prior to…, With the help of…, Built into…) belong to the right-hand sentence. 空格右边能否独立成句？开头的短语属于后一句。

4

Both complete? Choose a legal join

Period, semicolon, or comma + FANBOYS. Cut a comma alone and no punctuation. 两边都完整：选句号、分号或“逗号+并列连词”。

1

Look at the choices · 看选项

“through, / through / through; / through and”: the words are the same; only the punctuation changes. → A boundary question: what kind of structure is on each side of the blank? 选项只差标点 → 考句子连接。

2

Test the left side · 左边

Cross out the extra part: “Clear glass , which is classified as transparent, allows all … light to pass through.” Subject = Clear glass, verb = allows. Period test: finished → independent. 去掉 which 插入语，主语 glass + 谓语 allows，完整。

3

Test the right side · 右边

“wax paper , which is classified as translucent, allows only some light to pass through.” Subject = wax paper, verb = allows → independent. 右边主语 wax paper + 谓语 allows，也完整。

4

Apply the rule · 规则

Two complete sentences meet at the blank → period, semicolon, or comma + FANBOYS. Only the semicolon is offered. 两个完整句 → 句号 / 分号 / 逗号 + FANBOYS。

5

Eliminate the other choices · 排除错误选项

A ✗ Comma splice: a comma alone cannot join two complete sentences.
B ✗ Run-on: no punctuation between two complete sentences.
D ✗ “and” without a comma between two long main clauses; the SAT expects “, and.”

Answer: C. Left: “Clear glass … allows all … light to pass through”: complete. Right: “wax paper … allows only some light to pass through”: complete. Two independent clauses → semicolon. (The “which is classified…” parts are extra.) 两边都是完整句，用分号。

Traps

Common traps in linking-clause questions

Words that look like conjunctions

later, then, however, also, thus are not FANBOYS. “, later,” or “, however,” between two sentences is still a comma splice. (More in G02.)

later/then/however 不是并列连词，不能和逗号一起连接两个句子。

Long introductory phrases

“Prior to VisiCalc’s release,” “With the help of digital imaging,” “Built into the rocky outcrops”: these begin the next sentence. Do not let them hide the boundary.

开头的长短语属于后一句，不要被它掩盖句子边界。

Extra pieces inside a clause

“which is classified as transparent,” “ensuring that proteins…”: cross them out to see the clause clearly.

划掉插入成分再判断。

Comma + FANBOYS

Between two long main clauses, the SAT always uses both: comma and FANBOYS.

两个长独立句：逗号和并列连词都要有。

Summary

What to remember about linking independent clauses独立句连接要点

01 · 正确连接方式

Two independent clauses can be joined by a period, a semicolon, or a comma + FANBOYS.

02 · 常见连接错误

A comma alone = comma splice ✗. No punctuation = run-on ✗.

03 · 分号规则

A semicolon works exactly like a period: complete sentences on both sides.

04 · 判断独立句

Test each side: subject + finite verb + complete thought. Cross out extra phrases first.

05 · 识别连接词

later, then, however are not conjunctions; introductory phrases belong to the next sentence.

06 · 30 秒自检

What is tested? Which rule decides the answer? Why does one tempting choice fail?

指出考点 → 说明规则 → 解释一个易错选项。
