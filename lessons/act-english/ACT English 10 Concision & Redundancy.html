Warm-up · 热身

Say it once只说一遍

If two choices mean the same, the shorter correct one wins. 意思相同，选短的。

Which sentence is the most concise and correct?
What these questions look like

Recognizing a concision question

Typical choices

F. NO CHANGE (The first inaugural Tour started on July 1, 1903.)
G. Beginning on July 1, 1903, the first Tour commenced.
H. On July 1, 1903, the initial Tour began its first run.
J. The inaugural Tour began on July 1, 1903.

All four choices are grammatical and say roughly the same thing, but they have different lengths. Often one choice is “DELETE the underlined portion.”

选项意思相近、长短不同,常有 DELETE → 简洁题。

Where ACT puts them

ACT lists this under Knowledge of Language: “eliminate wordiness and redundancy.”

Important: shorter is not automatically better. The shortest choice must still be grammatical and keep all the meaning the sentence needs.

不是越短越好:必须语法正确、意思完整。

Types of redundancy

The same meaning twice

Redundancy · 重复Original sentence · 原句Clear revision · 修改
Same meaning twiceWe returned back to the village.We returned to the village.returned 已包含“返回”。
Repeated timeIn 2020, Mia moved here that year.In 2020, Mia moved here.
Wordy causeWe stayed home due to the fact that it rained.We stayed home because it rained.
Unneeded modifierThe final outcome surprised us.The outcome surprised us.
Keep needed meaningMia left before the storm arrived.Keep the time clause if the sequence matters.不能为缩短句子而删掉题目需要的时间信息。

简洁建立在语法正确、意思完整、符合题干要求的基础上；不是看到最短选项就选。

Shorter, but complete

When the shortest choice is not right

Check meaning

A shorter choice is wrong if it drops important information — for example, a time, a cause, or a detail the next sentence depends on.

Also check that DELETE does not leave the sentence ungrammatical.

删得太多、丢了必要信息或破坏语法,也是错的。

A simple test

  1. Find the shortest choice (often DELETE).
  2. Read it in the sentence. Is it grammatical?
  3. Is any needed meaning lost?
  4. If both answers are fine, choose it. If not, try the next shortest.

Clue: when three choices are long and one is short (or DELETE), think concision first. But a choice can also be too short — “telegraphic” and missing information.

从最短的选项开始验证。

How to approach it

Steps for concision questions

1

Notice the pattern

Same meaning, different lengths, maybe a DELETE choice. 意思相同、长短不同。

2

Look for repeated meaning

Check the words around the underline too. The repetition may be outside the underlined part. 重复的内容可能在划线外。

3

Start with the shortest choice

Test it for grammar and meaning. 从最短开始。

4

Choose the shortest choice that is complete

Clear, grammatical, and saying everything the sentence needs. 选最短且完整的。

Summary

What to remember about concision简洁要点

Remove repeated meanings

Look for two words with one meaning: first inaugural, futilely … in vain, hidden unseen.

删除重复意思，保留必要区别。

Read outside the underline

Check the words outside the underline — the repetition may be there.

划线外也可能已表达同样的信息。

Replace wordy phrases

Replace wordy phrases: due to the fact that → because.

用更直接的表达替换冗长短语。

Test the shortest choice

Start with the shortest choice, but make sure it is grammatical and complete.

最短选项仍须语法正确、意思完整。

Keep useful information

Extra facts that do not serve the sentence’s point are also “wordy.”

时间、因果、限定等必要信息不能为简洁而丢掉。
