Warm-up · 热身

Put it in order排顺序

Time words and “this / these / then” show what must come first. 找时间词和指代词。

[1] Then she poured the batter into a pan. [2] First, Mia mixed the flour and eggs. [3] Finally, she baked the cake for thirty minutes.
What is the most logical order?
What these questions look like

Four kinds of organization questions

1 · Sentence order

For the sake of the logic and cohesion of this paragraph, Sentence 2 should be placed… or Which sequence of sentences makes this paragraph most logical?

句子顺序:句子前有 [1][2][3] 编号。

2 · Placement (Point A–D)

The writer is considering adding the following sentence… It would most logically be placed at: Point A / B / C / D.

插入位置:文中标出 A–D 四个点。

3 · Introductions and transitions

Which statement most effectively introduces this paragraph? / …provides the best transition to the next sentence?

段首句 / 过渡句。

4 · Conclusions and paragraph breaks

Which choice would most effectively conclude the paragraph and the essay? / Where should the new paragraph begin?

结尾句 / 分段位置。

Clues that show order

Look for words that point backward

A short ordering example · 排序示例

[1] This discovery surprised the team.
[2] The team found seeds beneath the ice.
[3] The seeds later grew in the laboratory.

Order: 2 → 1 → 3. “This discovery” needs the discovery first.

先交代发现种子，再用 This discovery 指代；later 推进时间。

Connect both sides · 前后都要接上

Mia bought a camera. She used it to photograph birds. Her first exhibition featured those photographs.

She → Mia; it → camera; those photographs → photographs of birds.

插入句既承接前面的 camera，又为后面的 photographs 提供来源。只读前一句不够。

Introductions and conclusions

Openings look forward; endings look back

Introducing a paragraph

A good opening sentence states the main idea of the paragraph that follows — not the one before. Read the whole paragraph first, then choose the sentence that summarizes it.

段首句概括本段,而不是上一段。

Concluding a paragraph or essay

A good ending restates the essay’s main point or brings the paragraph to a close.

  1. Read the question closely: is there a specific requirement?
  2. Skim the paragraph (or essay) and summarize it yourself.
  3. Eliminate choices that do not match your summary and the requirement.

Wrong choices often add a new detail, are too broad, or focus on a minor point.

三步:读题干要求 → 自己概括 → 对照排除。

How to approach it

Steps for organization questions

1

Read the whole paragraph

These questions cannot be answered from one sentence. Save them until you have read the full paragraph (or essay). 读完整段(或全文)再做。

2

Find the clues

Pronouns, “the/this,” time words, and contrast words show what must come before. 找代词、时间词、转折词。

3

Test the order

Read the sentences in the new order. Every “the pair” or “then” should have a clear reference. 按新顺序读一遍。

4

Match openings and endings to the main idea

Intro → what follows. Conclusion → the essay’s main point. 开头看后文,结尾看主旨。

Summary

What to remember about organization组织结构要点

Read the full paragraph

Read the whole paragraph before answering an order or placement question.

读完相应段落或全文再判断组织结构。

Find the referent

Words like the pair, this, then, also, but must come after what they refer to.

this / they 等必须找到明确前文对象。

Connect both sides

For Point A–D, the new sentence must connect to the sentence before and after.

插句与前一句、后一句都要连接。

Introductions and conclusions

An introduction summarizes what follows; a conclusion returns to the essay’s main point.

开头看能否引出下文；结尾看能否收束重点。

Keep endings supported

Reject endings that add new details or make claims the essay never made.

不加入原文未支持的新主张。
