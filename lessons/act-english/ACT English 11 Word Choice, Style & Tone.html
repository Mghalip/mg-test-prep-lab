What these questions look like

Recognizing a word-choice question

Typical choices

F. NO CHANGE (a big to-do)
G. all the more notable
H. super noteworthy
J. that much cooler

The choices are similar in meaning but differ in precision or formality. Sometimes the question says: Which choice best maintains the word pattern…? or …most precisely conveys…?

选项意思相近,差别在精确度或正式程度。

Where ACT puts them

ACT lists these under Knowledge of Language: “precision and concision in word choice” and “consistency in style and tone.”

The meaning of the sentence and the voice of the whole essay decide the answer — not which word “sounds smarter.”

不是选最“高级”的词,而是最贴切、语气最一致的词。

Precision

Near-synonyms are not the same

Word · 单词Meaning · 中文Complete example · 完整例句
inaccessible无法到达／进入The trail was inaccessible after the flood.
impermeable不透水等物质The coat has an impermeable outer layer.
insurmountable难以克服的The team faced an insurmountable obstacle.
validated验证了有效性／真实性The experiment validated the prediction.
approved / encouraged批准／鼓励The council approved the plan.
The teacher encouraged us to try.

词义相近也不能互换。先说清句子需要“无法到达”还是“不透水”，再选对应词；正式不等于堆砌难词。

Tone and style

Match the voice of the essay

Formality

Most ACT essays use a clear, neutral, somewhat formal voice. Choices that are too casual (super noteworthy, flip way out, amp up, a big to-do) or too dramatic (momentous fearfulness, insidious terror) do not fit.

A personal essay can be relaxed, but it should still be consistent and clear: have a scare.

既不过于口语,也不过于夸张。

Following a pattern

If the question mentions a pattern, look at the earlier examples in the sentence and copy their structure.

egrets dive into still water, minks run through tall grasses, and frogs sit on waxy lily pads — subject + verb + place.

题目提到 pattern 时,照前面的结构写。

Four kinds of word questions

Four kinds of word questions

1 · Redundancy · 用语重复

Two words with the same meaning (see Lesson 10).

2 · Confused words · 形近词

affect / effectverb / noun
than / thencomparison / time
site / cite / sightplace / quote / vision
past / passedtime, beyond / verb
accept / exceptreceive / not including
lie / layrest / put

3 · Precision · 用词准确

Near-synonyms: choose the exact meaning the context needs (inaccessible vs. impermeable).

4 · Style & tone · 语言风格

Formal vs. informal: keep the essay’s voice. A pattern question asks you to copy a structure.

The core skill is vocabulary. Reading the context helps with precision; sensitivity to repetition helps with redundancy; knowing formal and informal registers helps with tone.

四类:用语重复、形近词、用词准确、语言风格。核心是词汇量。

How to approach it

Steps for word-choice questions

1

Say the meaning in simple words

What does the sentence need this word to mean? 用简单的话说出所需意思。

2

Check each choice’s exact meaning

Cut words that mean something slightly different. 排除意思有偏差的词。

3

Check the tone

Cut slang, overly dramatic words, and vague words. 排除口语、夸张和模糊的词。

4

If a pattern is mentioned, copy it

Match the structure of the earlier examples. 按照已有结构。

Summary

What to remember about word choice用词与语气要点

State the exact meaning

Say the needed meaning in simple words first, then find the choice that means exactly that.

先用简单话说明句子需要的确切意思。

Distinguish near-synonyms

Near-synonyms differ: inaccessible ≠ impermeable, validated ≠ approved.

近义词不完全等价，逐个检查语义。

Choose precise words

Avoid vague words (stuff) when a precise word is available.

有明确对象时避免含糊的 stuff / things。

Match the tone

Keep the essay’s tone: no slang, no exaggerated drama.

正式程度与全文一致，不一律排斥个人化语言。

Follow the pattern

If a question mentions a pattern, copy the structure of the earlier examples.

题干要求 pattern 时，核对前文的结构模式。

30-second check · 30 秒自检

Name the rule or writer’s goal, apply it in context, and reject one wrong choice.

说出本课规则／方法，指出依据，并解释一个错误选项为什么错。
