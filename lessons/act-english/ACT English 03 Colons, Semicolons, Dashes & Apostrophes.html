Warm-up · 热身

Pick the mark选对标点

Colon and semicolon both need a complete sentence before them. 冒号、分号前面都要完整句。

She had one goal ______ to win the championship.
Which choice is correct?
The two ______ tails wagged at the same time.
Which choice is correct? (two dogs)
The semicolon

Two uses of the semicolon

Between clauses · 连接完整句

The rain stopped; we went outside.

Both sides can stand alone. A period can replace this semicolon.

✗ The rain stopped; allowing us to leave.
✓ The rain stopped, allowing us to leave.

连接分句时，分号两边都必须完整；allowing 是非谓语，不能单独放在分号后。

Inside a complex list · 复杂列举

We visited Lima, Peru; Quito, Ecuador; and Bogotá, Colombia.
Lima, Peru第 1 项
→
Quito, Ecuador第 2 项
→
Bogotá, Colombia第 3 项

每个“城市＋国家”是一项；项内用逗号，项与项之间用分号。此用法不要求每项是完整句。

The colon

A colon must follow a complete sentence

Use · 用法Complete example · 完整例句Check · 检查
List列举We packed three things: pens, maps, and water.“We packed three things” is complete.冒号前能单独成句。
Explanation解释We stayed indoors: the storm had closed every road.The second part explains the first.后半句解释为什么留在室内。
Quotation引语Mia gave one instruction: “Wait here.”The quotation explains the instruction.
Wrong boundary不能切断动宾✗ We packed: pens, maps, and water.
✓ We packed pens, maps, and water.		“We packed” does not complete the intended statement here.不要在动词和所需宾语之间加冒号。

冒号前完整；冒号后可以是完整句、短语或列举，但必须解释、展开前文。

The dash

Dashes: one or two

One dash · 单个破折号

Mia had one goal—to finish before sunset.

The words after the dash expand on the complete statement before it.

此处用于强调补充说明，类似冒号；例句前半部分 Mia had one goal 已完整。

Two dashes · 成对破折号

Mia—the youngest runner on the team—won the race.

Remove the insertion: Mia won the race.

✗ Mia—the youngest runner on the team, won the race.

句中插入语用同一种标点成对围住；不能破折号开头、逗号结束。

The apostrophe

Possessives, plurals, and contractions

Form · 形式Meaning · 含义Complete example · 完整例句
colony’s / colonies’one owner / plural owners单数／复数所有格The colony’s queen survived.
The two colonies’ queens survived.
children’sirregular plural + ’s不以 s 结尾的复数The children’s game lasted an hour.
its / it’sits = possession; it’s = it is / it hasThe bird spread its wings.
It’s ready to fly.
whose / who’swhose = possession; who’s = who is / who hasThe writer whose book won is here.
The writer who’s won the prize is here.
their / they’re / therepossession / they are / placeTheir bags are over there.
They’re ready to leave.

先判断“复数”还是“所属”；再把缩写展开检验。its’ 不是标准形式。

Quotation marks

Punctuation with quotation marks (American style)

Mark · 标点Rule · 规则Complete example · 完整例句
Comma / periodInside the closing quotation mark in American style.美式英语中逗号、句号放引号内。She called it “remarkable.”
He said “yes,” then smiled.
Colon / semicolonOutside the closing quotation mark.She called it “remarkable”; I agreed.
Question markPut it where the question belongs.She asked, “Are you ready?”
Did she really say “I agree”?
Introducing a quotationUse the sentence structure, not quotation marks alone.Mia said, “Wait here.”
Mia gave one instruction: “Wait here.”
Mia called the result “a success.”

引语融入句法时不自动加逗号：Mia called the result “a success.” 中引语是宾语补足语。

How to approach it

Steps for these punctuation questions

1

Read what comes before the mark

Semicolon, colon, and single dash all need a complete sentence before them. 先判断是连接分句、复杂列举还是补充说明，再检查完整性。

2

Read what comes after the mark

Semicolon: another complete sentence. Colon / dash: an explanation, list, or example. 再看后面是什么。

3

For apostrophes, ask two questions

Is it plural? Does it own something? For it’s / who’s / they’re, expand the contraction and read it. 撇号:复数?所有格?缩写展开读。

4

Check pairs, then meaning

Two dashes, two commas, or two parentheses — never one of each. If more than one choice is grammatical, ask which one gives the meaning the writer intends. 先判断语法是否正确,再比较不同标点带来的意思差别。

Summary

What to remember冒号/分号/破折号/撇号要点

Semicolon: two clauses

Semicolon = period: complete sentence on both sides.

分号可连接完整句，也可分隔复杂列举项。

Colon: complete lead-in

Colon: complete sentence before; explanation, list, or example after. Never between a verb and its object or after such as.

冒号前完整，后面解释或列举。

Single and paired dashes

Single dash works like a colon; two dashes work like two commas. Do not mix dashes and commas.

句中插入语用同一种标点成对围住。

Mark possession

Apostrophes: ’s for one owner, s’ for several; its, whose, their have no apostrophe.

先判断所有者单复数；不规则复数用 ’s。

Expand contractions

Expand every contraction (it’s → it is) and read it in the sentence.

it’s 可展开为 it is / it has；its 是所有格。
