What these questions look like

Recognizing a transition question

Typical choices

F. NO CHANGE (therefore,)
G. furthermore,
H. however,
J. indeed,

The underlined part is a connecting word or phrase, usually at the start of a sentence or clause. The choices have different meanings, so grammar alone will not decide.

选项是不同意思的连接词 → 逻辑题,不是语法题。

Where ACT puts them

ACT lists transitions under Production of Writing (organization, unity, cohesion). You must understand the meaning of the sentences before and after the transition.

Key idea: cover the underlined word, read the two sentences, and decide their relationship yourself before looking at the choices.

先遮住选项,自己判断两句关系。

Relationships and signal words

Common relationships

Relationship · 关系Signals · 常见词Complete example · 完整例句
Contrast转折however · neverthelessIt was raining. However, the match continued.
Result结果therefore · consequentlyThe road flooded. Therefore, the bus changed its route.
Example举例for example · for instanceSome birds cannot fly. For example, penguins swim instead.
Addition补充also · furthermoreThe park is free. Furthermore, it is close to school.
Sequence顺序first · then · finallyMia mixed the batter. Then, she poured it into a pan.
Restatement换言之in other words · that isThe fee is nonrefundable. In other words, you cannot get the money back.

because 引出原因，therefore 引出结果，方向不同；although 是从属连词，however 不能只用逗号连接两个完整句。

No transition at all

Sometimes the best transition is none

No transition · 自然接续

Mia has published three books. She also runs a writing club.

Adding “Instead” would falsely suggest that running the club replaces publishing books.

没有转折或替代关系，就不要硬加 Instead / Rather。自然接续也可能是最佳答案。

Meaning + grammar · 意思与结构

It rained; however, we continued.
Although it rained, we continued.

✗ It rained, however, we continued.

however 是连接副词，不能代替 and 连接两个分句；although 把一部分变成从句。

不能只凭“两个词意思相近”就直接排除；还要检查语法、标点、位置和语境。如果两选项在这里作用完全相同，才考虑单一正确答案的限制。

How to approach it

Steps for transition questions

1

Cover the transition

Do not read the choices yet. 先不看选项。

2

Read the two sentences being connected

Wherever the transition sits (start, middle, or end of a sentence), it usually links its own sentence and the sentence before. For a long sentence, focus on the main clause. 无论过渡词在句首、句中还是句末,考的都是本句与前一句的关系;长句先抓主干。

3

Name the relationship

Contrast, cause, example, addition, time — or none? 说出关系。

4

Match a choice to your relationship

Eliminate choices from the wrong category; then check grammar. 对号入座,再查语法。

Summary

What to remember about transitions过渡词要点

Predict the relationship

Cover the choices and decide the relationship yourself first.

遮住原连接词，先自己判断关系。

Name the logical link

Five relationships: contrast, cause, example, addition, time.

分清转折、原因、结果、举例、补充与顺序。

Consider no transition

If the sentence simply continues the same idea, no transition may be best.

没有特殊逻辑关系时，自然接续可能最好。

Compare equivalent choices

Compare meaning, placement, and grammar before treating two choices as equivalent.

意思相近还要核对标点、位置和语法。

Read the preceding context

Read at least one full sentence before the transition — sometimes two.

读本句与前后必要语境，不只看一个信号词。

30-second check · 30 秒自检

Name the rule or writer’s goal, apply it in context, and reject one wrong choice.

说出本课规则／方法，指出依据，并解释一个错误选项为什么错。
