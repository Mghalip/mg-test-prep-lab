Warm-up · 热身

Comma or no comma?加不加逗号？

Every comma needs a reason. No reason → no comma. 每个逗号都要有理由。

My brother ______ is visiting next week.
Which choice is correct? (I have one brother.)
The tall, old tree, fell during the storm.
Which comma is wrong?
Why commas matter

Commas are the most common punctuation topic

What ACT tests

ACT lists punctuation under Conventions of Standard English. On every form, several questions are only about where commas go. The choices often look almost the same, differing by a single comma.

逗号题选项往往只差一个逗号,要逐个检查。

A useful attitude

A comma is not a “pause.” Do not add a comma just because you would breathe there when reading aloud. Every comma needs a grammatical reason. If you cannot name the reason, the comma is probably wrong.

逗号不是“停顿”,每一个逗号都要说得出语法理由。

In recent ACT tests, too many commas is a more common error than too few. 多余的逗号比漏掉的逗号更常见。

Jobs a comma can do

Five correct uses of the comma

Use · 用法Pattern · 结构Complete example · 完整例句
Join clauses连接两个完整分句I, and I.The rain stopped, and we went outside.
Introductory element前置从句／修饰语D, I.Because the rain stopped, we went outside.
Extra information非必要补充Noun, extra, verb.Mia, our team captain, welcomed the visitors.
List列举A, B, and CWe packed pens, notebooks, and maps.
Coordinate adjectives并列形容词adjective, adjective + nounIt was a long, difficult journey.可以说 long and difficult；不是所有相邻形容词都要加逗号。

I = independent clause（独立分句），D = dependent clause（从句）。逗号必须有明确作用，不能按朗读时的停顿随意添加。

Essential vs. non-essential

The two-comma test for extra information

Extra information · 补充信息

Mia, our team captain, welcomed the visitors.

Remove the extra words: Mia welcomed the visitors. The same person is still identified.

句中补充信息用成对逗号；若补充在句末，则由句末标点结束。

Essential information · 限定信息

The students who finished early left the room.
Biologist Maya Chen led the study.

who finished early 限定“哪些学生”；头衔后的人名说明“哪位生物学家”。去掉后主干仍完整，不代表信息就一定非必要。

两步判断：①去掉后语法是否完整？②去掉后指代范围是否改变？只有检查语法完整性是不够的。

Commas that do not belong

Where a comma should NOT go

Protect the link · 保护结构Incorrect · 错误Correct · 正确
Subject → verb主语—谓语The results of the study, were surprising.The results of the study were surprising.
Verb → object动词—宾语Mia carried, a heavy bag.Mia carried a heavy bag.
Preposition → object介词—宾语We talked about, the project.We talked about the project.
Essential clause限定性从句The book, that I borrowed is useful.The book that I borrowed is useful.
Shared subject一个主语、两个动作Mia opened the door, and waved.Mia opened the door and waved.这里 and 连接两个谓语，不是两个独立分句。

不要用“介词前后永远不能有逗号”等绝对口诀。例：In winter, we stay indoors. 中逗号结束的是前置短语。要判断逗号切断了什么结构。

How to approach it

Steps for comma questions

1

Find the core sentence

Locate the subject and main verb. Nothing should separate them except a pair of commas around extra information. 先找主干:主语和谓语。

2

Name the reason for every comma

For each comma in a choice, say which of the five jobs it does. No job → the choice is wrong. 每个逗号都要说出它的作用。

3

Check pairs

If a comma opens extra information, there must be a matching comma (or the end of the sentence) closing it. 检查是否成对。

4

Use structure, not comma counts

Choose the punctuation required by the sentence structure and meaning; do not count commas. 按结构与意义检查，不用逗号数量猜答案。

1

Find the core

Subject: Bartoshuk and other scientists. Verb: continue. Object: to research potential health benefits.

2

Is “and other scientists” extra?

No. It is part of a compound subject — that is why the verb is plural continue. It should not be set off by commas or parentheses.

3

Look at the comma after “continue”

“continue, to research” puts a comma between a verb and its object. There is no reason for it.

🙋 Ask · 说规则每个逗号说出它的“工作”；没有工作就删掉。👂 听什么：五种用法
4

Choose

“Bartoshuk and other scientists continue to research…” — no commas needed.

Parentheses make and other scientists look optional, and the comma splits the verb from its object.

Summary

What to remember about commas逗号要点

Give each comma a reason

Every comma needs a reason: FANBOYS between clauses, after an intro, around extra info, in a list, between coordinate adjectives.

每个逗号都要说明具体作用。

Protect the sentence core

Never use one comma between subject and verb or verb and object.

保护主谓、动宾等紧密结构。

Essential or extra?

Extra (non-essential) information takes a pair of commas; essential information takes none.

限定信息不用逗号；补充信息通常成对隔开。

Lists and adjectives

In a list: A, B, and C. No comma between the last adjective and its noun.

并列形容词可用 and 检验；最后一个形容词与名词不隔开。

Avoid unnecessary commas

Check each comma’s grammatical role; fewer commas is not a rule.

按结构选择，不按“逗号越少越好”猜。
