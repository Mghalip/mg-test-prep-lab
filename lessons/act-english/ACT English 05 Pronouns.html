The antecedent

A pronoun replaces a noun called its antecedent. Every pronoun question starts with one question: What noun does this pronoun refer to?

The mosses grow in soil that they generate for themselves.

先找代词指代的名词(先行词)。

Three rules

Agreement, clarity, and case

Agreement · 数与人称

The camera is new. It takes clear pictures.
The cameras are new. They take clear pictures.

这里 it 指单数物品，they 指复数物品。指人时还要结合语境；单数 they 是标准用法，不能机械排除。

Clarity · 指代清楚

Unclear: Mia told Nora that she had won.
Clear: Mia told Nora that Nora had won.

两个人都可能是 she，必须用上下文确定；若仍有歧义，选明确的名词。

Case · 句中作用

The artist who painted this is here.
The artist whom we invited is here.

who 在从句里作主语；whom 作 invited 的宾语。测试：he painted；we invited him。

its / their / whose 表示所属；it’s / they’re / who’s 是缩写。每个选项都放回完整句子。

Other pronoun points

Relative pronouns and finding the antecedent

Point · 要点Complete example · 完整例句中文说明
who / whomThe student who called is here.
The student whom we called is here.		看代词在从句中的作用，不看它离主句动词多近。
which / thatThe book that I borrowed is useful.
This book, which I borrowed yesterday, is useful.		限定信息不用逗号；补充信息成对加逗号。that 也可指限定从句中的人。
Look backMia painted three portraits. They are on display.They 指 portraits，不是 Mia。先行词可能在上一句。
Keep perspectiveWhen we revise, we read aloud.没有语义需要时，不随意从 we 换成 you。
ContractionsIt’s a new museum. Its roof is glass.It’s = it is；its 表示所属，没有撇号。
How to approach it

Steps for pronoun questions

1

Find the antecedent

Read back one or two sentences to find the noun the pronoun replaces. 往前读,找到被指代的名词。

2

Match number

Singular or plural? Cut choices that do not match. 单复数一致。

3

Check the role

Is the pronoun a subject, an object, or showing possession? Expand contractions. 主格 / 宾格 / 所有格。

4

Ask: is it clear?

If a reader could not tell who “they” or “it” is, choose the specific noun. 指代不清就选名词。

Summary

What to remember about pronouns代词要点

Find the antecedent

Find the antecedent before choosing a pronoun.

先确定代词指向谁或什么。

Match number

Singular → it, its, itself; plural → they, their, themselves.

核对数与语境；指人时不要机械排除单数 they。

Make reference clear

If the reference is unclear, choose a specific noun.

指代不清就换成明确名词。

Subject or object?

who = he (subject), whom = him (object); which never refers to people.

who / whom 看其在从句中作主语还是宾语。

Possession or contraction?

its, their, whose show possession; it’s, they’re, who’s are contractions.

所有格与缩写分别检验；把缩写展开。
