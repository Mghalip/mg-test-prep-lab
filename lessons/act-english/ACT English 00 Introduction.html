What you will see

  • Each essay is printed on the left; questions are on the right.
  • Each question has an explicit stem. Underlining identifies the words being tested.
  • Other questions ask about the essay as a whole, or about a numbered box or bracketed sentence.

左边文章,右边题目;划线部分带题号。

What it is really testing

  • Not memorized grammar terms, but whether you can edit a piece of writing.
  • Every choice is read in the sentence and in the essay.
  • No penalty for wrong answers: never leave a question blank.

考的是“修改文章”的能力;不扣分,不留空。

Reading the choices

Read the explicit question stem

1 · Grammar and punctuation

Which choice makes the sentence most grammatically acceptable?

Locate the underlined words. Read the complete sentence and compare all four choices.

NO CHANGE keeps the underlined wording. Choose it only when the original fits the question.

语法与标点题：明确考查部分，把选项放回完整句子判断。

2 · Meaning, organization, and style

A question explains the goal: Which choice most clearly…? Should the writer make this addition? Sentence 2 should be placed…

Read the stem carefully. The goal in the stem decides the answer — not grammar alone.

DELETE the underlined portion is sometimes a choice. It is right when the words add nothing.

有题干:按题干要求选;DELETE 也可能正确。

What it measures

What the English section measures

Production of Writing

38–43%

Topic development: purpose, adding or deleting, relevance. Organization, unity, and cohesion: order, transitions, introductions and conclusions.

写作产出:目的、增删、结构、过渡、开头结尾。

Knowledge of Language

18–23%

Precise and concise word choice; consistent style and tone.

语言知识:简洁、精准、语气一致。

Conventions of Standard English

38–43%

Sentence structure and formation; punctuation; usage (verbs, pronouns, modifiers, agreement).

标准英语规范:句子结构、标点、用法。

Read the task word in every stem. If a question asks for an exception or the least acceptable choice, reverse the selection task while checking each choice by the same rules.

A single question can test more than one point (for example, a verb’s number and its tense). Eliminate every choice that breaks any rule; what remains is the answer. 先看选项差别判断考点;一题可能考多个点,要把所有错误选项都排除。

A general process

A process for every question

1

Read the whole sentence, not just the underline

Errors often depend on words outside the underline: the subject, a pair of commas, a time word. 读完整句,而不只是划线部分。

2

Compare the choices: what changes?

Punctuation? Verb form? Pronoun? Length? Meaning? The difference tells you the rule being tested. 比较选项,找出差别 → 判断考点。

3

Apply the rule

Grammar questions have one correct rule. Rhetorical questions depend on the stem and the paragraph’s focus. 用规则判断;修辞题看题干和段落焦点。

4

Read your answer back in the sentence

Of the choices that are correct, prefer the clearest and most concise. 代回原句;都对时选更简洁清楚的。

Pacing

Pacing

About 6–7 minutes per essay

  • Read the essay and answer questions as you go.
  • Grammar questions are usually quick; spend the time you save on stem questions.
  • Questions about the whole essay or paragraph order: answer them after you finish the paragraph or the essay.

边读边做;排序和全文题读完再做。

When you are stuck

  • Eliminate choices that clearly break a rule.
  • If two remain, recheck the rule and the writer’s goal. Prefer concision only when the needed meaning is preserved.
  • Never leave a question blank.

先排除明显错误;二选一时重新核对规则、语义与题干要求;不留空。
