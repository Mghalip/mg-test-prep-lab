Subject–verb agreement

Find the real subject

Subject · 主语Complete sentence · 完整例句Why · 判断
Singular coreThe box of old photographs is heavy.主语核心 box 为单数；of photographs 不决定谓语。
Plural coreThe photographs in the box are old.主语核心 photographs 为复数。
Inserted informationThe scientist, along with two assistants, is ready.along with 不是 and，不把主语变复数。
Relative clauseThe books that are on the desk belong to Mia.从句和主句各找自己的主语与谓语。

先划出主语核心，再排除插入成分干扰。不能只让动词与离它最近的名词一致。

Main verb or not?

Main verbs and non-main verbs (谓语与非谓语)

Finite verb · 限定动词

A finite verb carries tense or agreement; a verb phrase can include auxiliaries.

The plants grow quickly.
The plants are growing quickly.
The plants have grown quickly.

grow / are / have 让谓语具备时态或一致关系；一个谓语可以由多个词组成。

Nonfinite form · 非谓语

An -ing form, an infinitive, or a participle alone does not supply a finite predicate.

✗ The plants growing quickly.
✓ The plants growing by the window need water.
✓ The plants are growing quickly.

第二句 growing by the window 修饰 plants；真正的谓语是 need。

Tense and form

Keep time consistent; use the right form

Time / form · 时态与形式Complete example · 完整例句中文说明
Past → earlier pastMia had left before Nora arrived.过去的 arrived 之前已经离开，用 had left。
Present perfectMia has lived here since 2020.从过去延续到现在；has 后用过去分词。
lie / layYesterday, Mia lay on the sofa.
She laid the book on the table.		lie（躺）过去式 lay；lay（放置）过去式 laid，后接宾语。
begin / leadThe lesson began at nine.
Mia has led the team for two years.		begin → began → begun；lead → led → led。

同一篇文章可以有多个时态，只要时间关系合理。不要把“时态一致”理解为所有动词都必须长得一样。

How to approach it

Steps for verb questions

1

Find the subject

Ignore the words closest to the verb; they are often a trap. Ask who or what does the action. 不要被离动词最近的名词误导。

2

Decide singular or plural

Cut choices that do not agree. This often removes two choices at once. 先用单复数排除。

3

Check the time frame

Read the verbs in the sentence before and after. Pick the tense that matches. 再用时态排除。

4

Check the required verb form

Choose the form that matches the time relationship and sentence structure. 按时间关系和句法选形式，不按长短选时态。

Summary

What to remember about verbs动词要点

Find a finite verb

Every clause needs one main verb; -ing, to + verb, having done cannot be the main verb alone.

限定谓语可以含助动词；-ing 单独不构成谓语。

Find the real subject

Find the real subject: cross out phrases in commas and phrases starting with of, such as, who, that.

找主语核心，排除插入语和介词短语干扰。

Compound subjects

Two distinct subjects joined by and normally take a plural verb.

两个不同主体用 and 连接通常用复数谓语。

Inverted sentences

In inverted sentences, the subject comes after the verb.

倒装仍按真正主语判断单复数。

Match the time frame

Keep the tense consistent with the surrounding sentences unless the passage signals a time change.

由时间关系选时态，不比较选项长短。

Irregular verb forms

Know irregular forms: lie / lay / lain, lay / laid, begin / began / begun, lead / led.

has / had 后用过去分词；注意不规则形式。
