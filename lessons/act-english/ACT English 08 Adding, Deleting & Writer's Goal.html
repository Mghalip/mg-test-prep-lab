Warm-up · 热身

Keep it on topic紧扣段落主题

Judge every addition against the focus of the paragraph. 增删看段落焦点。

A paragraph explains how bees turn nectar into honey. The writer is considering adding: “My cousin is allergic to bee stings.”
Should the writer add this sentence?
What these questions look like

Four kinds of “writer’s purpose” questions

1 · Should the writer add this?

At this point, the writer is considering adding the following sentence… Should the writer make this addition here?

是否应该加入这句话?

2 · What would be lost?

If the writer were to delete the underlined portion, the paragraph would primarily lose…

删掉后会失去什么?

3 · Which choice best accomplishes…?

Given that all the choices are accurate, which one makes clearest that…?

哪个选项最能实现题干的目的?

4 · Did the essay fulfill a goal?

Suppose the writer’s primary purpose had been to… Would this essay accomplish that purpose?

文章是否完成了某个写作目的?

These are Production of Writing questions. Grammar does not decide them — every choice is usually grammatical. Meaning and focus decide them. 这类题看的是意思和段落焦点,不是语法。

Adding or deleting

Yes/No + a reason: judge the reason too

Focus first · 先定焦点

Paragraph focus: how bees make honey.

Possible addition: “Bees store nectar in the hive before turning it into honey.”

This explains a step in the process.

新句提供与制蜜过程直接相关的信息，可以支持本段焦点。

Judge the reason · 再核对理由

Possible addition: “My cousin is allergic to bee stings.”

It mentions bees, but it does not explain how honey is made.

都提到 bees 不等于切题。Yes/No 与理由必须同时正确；不能理由不对但结论对就选。

Sentence function

What a sentence does depends on where it is

PositionPossible functionsAsk
First sentence of a paragraphintroduction · main idea (topic sentence) · transition from the previous paragraphDoes it set up what the paragraph will discuss?
Inside the paragraphtransition from the sentence before to the sentence after · supporting detailDoes it connect or support the point being made?
Last sentence of a paragraphconclusion · transition to the next paragraph · supporting detailDoes it close the point or lead into what follows?

Order of work: read the paragraph and answer its other questions first. Then come back to the add/delete question: if the sentence has one of the functions above, keep it — and then check which choice gives the right reason. 句子的作用取决于位置。先做完本段其他题,再回来判断这句话是否承担了引入、主旨、过渡、支持或总结的作用;有作用就保留,再核对理由。

“Which choice best…?”

Do exactly what the question asks

Read the question stem carefully

The stem tells you the goal, for example:

  • “makes clearest that the race was designed to include a large part of France”
  • “most specifically describes a particular negative effect”

Underline the key words. Every choice is true, so “true” is not enough — it must match those key words.

题干就是标准:圈出关键词,逐个对照。

“Specific” beats “vague”

When the stem asks for something specific, vague choices like can produce problems or creates an unpleasant outcome are wrong even though they are true.

“Primary purpose” questions: compare the stated purpose with what the essay mostly does. The reason must match the essay’s actual content.

要求“具体”时,笼统的选项都错。

How to approach it

Steps for writer’s-goal questions

1

Read the question stem first

Know what you are judging: an addition, a deletion, a specific goal, or the whole essay. 先读题干,明确任务。

2

Summarize the paragraph (or essay)

One short phrase for its focus. 一句话概括焦点。

3

Answer in your own words

“No, it’s off-topic.” / “It would lose the reason the dancers refused.” 先自己回答。

4

Match a choice — and check its reason

The reason must be accurate about both the sentence and the passage. 核对理由是否准确。

Summary

What to remember增删与写作目的要点

Meaning and focus

These questions test meaning and focus, not grammar.

修辞题看意义与焦点，不能只检查语法。

Summarize the paragraph

Summarize the paragraph’s focus before deciding to add or delete.

先概括整段，再评估新增或删去的贡献。

Evaluate the reason

In Yes/No questions, the reason must be accurate about the sentence and the passage.

Yes/No 与理由必须同时正确。

Match the stated goal

For “which choice best…,” underline the stem’s key words; when the stem says all choices are true, match the goal exactly.

题干要求什么，就核对什么。

Specific or vague?

When asked for something specific, reject vague choices.

要求具体效果时，笼统评价不足以完成任务。

30-second check · 30 秒自检

Name the rule or writer’s goal, apply it in context, and reject one wrong choice.

说出本课规则／方法，指出依据，并解释一个错误选项为什么错。
