Misplaced modifiers

An opening modifier must match the subject

The rule

An introductory participial phrase describes the subject of the main clause. The subject must be the person or thing doing or experiencing what the phrase describes.

While studying a canyon in Italy, a mystery caught his attention.
(The mystery was not studying the canyon.)

While studying a canyon in Italy, geologist Walter Alvarez stumbled onto a mystery. ✓

Born in Mexico City in 1907, self-portraits were what Frida Kahlo was best known for. → Born in Mexico City in 1907, Frida Kahlo was best known for her self-portraits.

句首分词修饰语应与主句主语对应；不能只机械地找第一个名词。

How to check

Ask who is doing or experiencing the opening action. Then identify the main-clause subject.

-ing: Carrying a heavy bag, Mia walked slowly.

-ed: Placed near the window, the plant received more light.

Description: Only five centimeters long, the fish fit in Mia’s palm.

三个例子分别对应 Mia、the plant、the fish，修饰对象必须合理。

Parallel structure

Items in a series must match

Match the structure · 结构平行

✗ Mia enjoys reading, to swim, and hiking.
✓ Mia enjoys reading, swimming, and hiking.

三项同为 enjoys 的宾语，都用 -ing；重点是语法作用和结构平行。

Share a frame · 共用结构

Mia plans to read, to swim, and to hike.
Mia plans to read, swim, and hike.

to 可以每项重复，也可以共用；不能把 to read、swimming、hike 随意混在一起。

Signal words & logical comparison

Signal words for pairs, and comparing like with like

Pattern · 结构Complete example · 完整例句中文说明
both A and BBoth Mia and Nora joined the club.both 配 and，A 与 B 在句中作用相同。
not only A but also BThe course teaches not only reading but also writing.reading 与 writing 平行。
either A or BWe can either walk or take the bus.walk 与 take 都是 can 后的动词原形。
Compare like with like✗ Mia’s essay is longer than Nora.
✓ Mia’s essay is longer than Nora’s.		比较文章与文章，不是文章与人。
that / thoseThe roads here are wider than those in my hometown.those 代替复数 roads；that 代替单数名词。

先补全比较对象，再判断形式；不要只凭 than 或 as 的出现选答案。

Comparisons, word forms & idioms

Small words that must be exactly right

Point · 要点Complete example · 完整例句中文说明
fewer / lessWe need fewer chairs and less furniture.可数复数 chairs 用 fewer；不可数 furniture 用 less。
than / thenMia is taller than Nora.
We ate dinner; then we left.		than 表比较；then 表时间顺序。
affect / effectRain can affect travel.
The storm had a serious effect on traffic.		常见用法：affect 是动词“影响”，effect 是名词“影响／结果”。
Fixed prepositionMia took part in the race.
The delay resulted from heavy rain.		固定搭配要结合完整句意。result from 表原因；result in 表结果。

这里列的是常见用法，不是说每个词只有一种词性。精确用词时同时核对语义、词性和搭配。

Summary

What to remember修饰/平行/比较/搭配要点

Attach modifiers clearly

An opening descriptive phrase must be followed by the noun it describes.

前置修饰语的动作执行者应与主句主语一致。

Keep parallel structure

Items in a list or pair take the same form: to mark, to visit, to dance.

并列成分的语法结构与作用要匹配。

Comparison and quantity

Use -er/-est for short adjectives; fewer for count nouns, less / little for non-count.

比较同类事物；可数复数与不可数名词区分。

Distinguish confused words

Know common confused words: affect/effect, than/then, a part/apart, past/passed, site/cite.

同音、近形词要放回完整句判断。

Check fixed expressions

Idioms are fixed — read the phrase in the full sentence and trust standard usage.

固定搭配结合语义，不能只凭读起来顺口。
