Each lesson: key facts → steps (with Desmos) → a three-step worked example → seven practice questions → summary → after-class practice. Multiple-choice questions use A–D buttons; student-produced responses have an answer box (fractions or decimals). Use ← → to move between slides and M to open a lesson’s contents. Questions come from six official practice forms (Form 1–6).
每课结构：知识要点 → 解题步骤（含 Desmos）→ 分步示范 → 七道练习 → 总结 → 课后练习。选择题点 A–D，填空题在框内输入答案。翻页用左右方向键，M 键打开本课目录。
