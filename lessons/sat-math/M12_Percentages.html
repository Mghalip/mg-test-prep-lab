Concept

What is a percent? · 什么是百分数

Percent = per 100 百分之

25% means 25 out of every 100: 25% = 25100 = 0.25. To change a percent to a decimal, move the decimal point 2 places left.

Percent50%5%120%0.4%
Decimal0.50.051.20.004

Three questions, same numbers (“of” → ×, “is” → =):

  1. What is 20% of 80? → 0.20 × 80 = 16.
  2. 16 is what percent of 80? → 1680 = 0.20 = 20%.
  3. 16 is 20% of what number? → 16 = 0.20x → x = 80.

百分数就是“每 100 份中占几份”。三类基本题：求部分（乘）、求百分比（部分 ÷ 整体）、求整体（部分 ÷ 百分比）。“of”后面的量是整体（基准）。

Concept

Percent change and multipliers · 百分比变化与倍数

Step by step 分步

A $80 jacket. Price goes up 25%.

  1. Keep 100%, add 25% → multiplier 1 + 0.25 = 1.25.
  2. New price 80 × 1.25 = 100 dollars.

The $80 jacket is on sale for 25% off.

  1. Keep 100% − 25% = 75% → multiplier 0.75.
  2. Sale price 80 × 0.75 = 60 dollars.

Percent change from 40 to 50:

new − oldold = 50 − 4040 = 0.25 → a 25% increase.

增加 p% → 乘 (1 + p%)；减少 p% → 乘 (1 − p%)。百分比变化 = 变化量 ÷ 原值（原值是基准）。

Common mistakes 常见错误

❌ Increase by 7% → multiply by 0.070.07 is only the increase. New total: × 1.07. 只乘 0.07 得到的是增加量。

❌ From 50 to 40 is also a 25% changeBase is the old value 50: 1050 = 20% decrease. 基准是原值。

❌ 20% off, then 20% up → back to the start100 → 80 → 96: 0.8 × 1.2 = 0.96. Percents multiply, not add. 连续变化要相乘。

❌ After a 25% increase the price is $100. Original = 100 × 0.75 = 75Work backward by dividing: 1001.25 = 80. 倒推原值用除法。

Key facts

Percent tools百分数工具

p% of N
求某数的百分之几
p100 × N: 10% of 470 = 47
先把百分数除以 100。例：20% of 80 = 0.2 × 80 = 16。
What percent is a of b?
部分占整体的比例
ab × 100%: 75 of 300 = 25%
例：75 占 300 的比例 = 75 ÷ 300 = 0.25 = 25%。
p% more than x
增加百分之几
x(1 + p100): 30% more → 1.30x
例：100 增加 30% → 100 × 1.3 = 130。
p% less than x
减少百分之几
x(1 − p100): 80% less → 0.20x
例：100 减少 80% → 100 × 0.2 = 20。
Percent change
变化率
new − oldold × 100%
分母用原值。例：80 → 100，增加 (100 − 80)/80 = 25%。
Two changes in a row
连续变化
+14% then +4% → 1.14 × 1.04 = 1.1856 (not 1.18)
例：先涨 20% 再降 20%，100 → 120 → 96，并未回到 100。
Increase by 400%
增加与变为不同
new = x + 4x = 5x
增加 400% 是变成 5 倍；变为原来的 400% 才是 4 倍。
Work backward
倒推原值
new = 1.30 × old → old = new1.30
例：涨价 30% 后为 130，原价 = 130 ÷ 1.3 = 100。

“The sale price is 30% greater than the cost” → the cost is the base (the 100%). The base is the number after “than” or “of”. “比……”或“……的”后面的量是基准（100%）。

How to approach it

Steps

1

Find the base

The quantity after “of” or “than” is 100%. 找基准量。

2

Write a multiplier equation

new = (multiplier) × base. 30% greater → 1.30; 80% less → 0.20. 写成“新 = 倍数 × 基准”。

3

Solve, often backward

If the new value is known and the base is not, divide by the multiplier. 已知新值求基准时，用除法。

Common trap: percent changes do not add. A 20% decrease followed by a 20% increase gives 0.8 × 1.2 = 0.96, a 4% loss, not 0%. 连续百分比变化不能直接相加，要相乘。

  3. Work backward: c = 2.341.30 = 1.80.

Summary

What to remember about percents百分数要点

01 · 部分与整体

p% of N = p100N. What percent: part ÷ whole × 100%.

02 · 增减倍率

p% more → × (1 + p/100); p% less → × (1 − p/100).

03 · 确定基数

The base is the quantity after “of” or “than”.

04 · 连续变化

Several changes: multiply the multipliers; never add the percents.

05 · 反求原值

Unknown base: divide the new value by the multiplier.
