The test

The Math section

Format

  • Two modules. On the digital test the second module adapts to how you did on the first.
  • About 75% multiple choice (A–D) and 25% student-produced response (you type the answer).
  • A calculator is allowed for every question; Desmos is built in.
  • A reference sheet gives area, volume, and special-right-triangle formulas.
  • The practice forms in these lessons have 27 questions per module.

两个模块；约四分之三选择题、四分之一填空题；全程可用计算器（内置 Desmos）；有公式参考表。

Question order

Within each module, questions get harder from beginning to end. Easy questions early are worth exactly as much as hard ones late: do not rush them.

Each question is labeled in these lessons as Form · Math Module · Question, e.g. “Form 4 · Math Module 2 · Question 16”.

每个模块内题目由易到难；前面的简单题同样重要。课件中题目按“套题 · 模块 · 题号”标注。

Student-produced responses

How to enter your own answer

Rules

  • Enter a number, a fraction like 72 (type 7/2), or a decimal.
  • No mixed numbers: 312 must be 7/2 or 3.5.
  • A long decimal may be cut off or rounded at the last place that fits: 293 → 9.666 or 9.667.
  • If there are several correct answers, enter one.
  • Do not type units, %, or commas.

填空题：可填整数、分数或小数；不能用带分数；多解只填一个；不要带单位或百分号。

In these lessons

Type your answer in the box and press Check (or Enter). Fractions and decimals are both accepted, as on the test.

Concept

How to read a math word problem · 怎么读应用题

Four questions to ask 读题四问

  1. What is asked? Underline the last sentence: “How many …?”, “What is the value of x − 7?”
  2. What is unknown? Give it a letter and write what it means: d = number of days.
  3. What numbers are given, and their units? $11 per day, $10 onetime.
  4. Which numbers change and which stay fixed? “per / each / every” → multiply; “onetime / initial / fee” → add once.

先看最后一句问什么；给未知量设字母并写清含义；圈出每个数字和单位；“per / each”要乘，“一次性费用”只加一次。

Mini example 例子

A taxi charges a $3 fee plus $2 per mile. Ana pays $15. How many miles did she ride?

  1. Asked: number of miles → let m = miles.
  2. Fixed: $3 (once). Changing: $2 per mile → 2m.
  3. Equation: 3 + 2m = 15.
  4. Solve: 2m = 12 → m = 6 miles. Check: 3 + 2(6) = 15 ✓

固定费用 3 只加一次，每英里 2 元要乘英里数，列出 3 + 2m = 15，解得 6 英里。

Desmos basics · 图形计算器入门

Typing 输入

  • Powers: x^2; fractions: / (e.g. 3/4)
  • Square root: type sqrt; π: type pi
  • Multiply: 2x or 2*x
  • Each line is one expression; use a new line for a new equation.

平方用 ^，分数用 /，根号输入 sqrt，π 输入 pi。每行输入一个式子。

Solving an equation 用 Desmos 解方程

Solve 3 + 2m = 15.

  1. Type y=3+2x (Desmos uses x).
  2. Type y=15.
  3. Click where the two lines cross: (6, 15).
  4. The solution is the x-coordinate: x = 6.

把方程两边分别画成 y = 左边、y = 右边，交点的横坐标就是解。

General strategies

Strategies from your Math Mastery notes

1

Read the question carefully

Underline what is asked: x, x − 7, the smaller number, the total, the difference, the units. 看清问什么：是 x、x − 7、较小的数，还是总数、差、单位。

2

Take the right first step

Translate words into an equation; draw and label a figure; write the formula. 第一步：列式、画图、写公式。

3

Plug in the answers / pick numbers

With numerical choices, test them in the original. With variables, pick a value like x = 2 and compare. 代入选项，或给字母取值。

4

Check

Substitute back, estimate the size, or graph in Desmos. 代回检验、估算、Desmos 验证。

Example 1

Form 5 · Math Module 1 · Question 2

Form 5 · Math Module 1 · Question 2

先自己想 1–2 分钟，再点开完整解析。

Idea · 思路

Count the days carefully: 4 days after the first day means 4 increases of 10.

“第一天之后 4 天” = 增加 4 次。

Solution · 解析

  1. Model: members = 90 + 10d, where d = days after the first day.
  2. Substitute: d = 4: 90 + 10(4) = 130.
  3. Read carefully: The question asks for the total, not the number of new members (40).

Why not the others · 错误选项

A ✗ 400 = 4 × 100: multiplies instead of adding.
C ✗ 94 = 90 + 4: adds 4 members, not 4 days of 10 members.
D ✗ 90 is the number on the first day.

Answer: B. 130 members. Takeaway. Most mistakes are reading mistakes. Name the variable, write the model, and answer exactly what is asked. 多数错误是读题错误：设变量、列模型、答所问。
