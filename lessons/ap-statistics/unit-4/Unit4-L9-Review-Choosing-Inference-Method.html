📝 Warm-up · 例题★★★★Warm-up

What kind of data? 什么类型的数据？

For each question, is the response categorical or quantitative? How many groups?

aWhat proportion of students at our school own a pet?
a. Categorical (yes/no), one group → one-proportion procedure.
bDo boys and girls at our school differ in mean sleep time?
b. Quantitative (hours), two independent groups → two-sample t procedure.
cIs favorite subject associated with grade level?
c. Two categorical variables, one sample → chi-square test for independence.
dDo students score higher on a retest than on the first test?
d. Quantitative, same students twice → paired t procedure.
🔑 Step 1 of every inference problem: type of variable.

📝 Example 2 · 例题★★★★Skill 2.C

Name that procedure 说出方法

Name the procedure, the parameter, and the hypotheses (if a test).

aA random sample of 200 voters: is there convincing evidence that more than half support a new park?
a. One-sample z test for p. H₀: p = 0.5, Hₐ: p > 0.5 (p = true proportion of all voters who support the park).
bEstimate the difference in mean commute time between workers in two cities (random samples of 40 and 50).
b. Two-sample t interval for μ₁ − μ₂ (difference in true mean commute times, city 1 − city 2).
c30 patients take drug A and drug B in random order; is there a difference in mean pain score?
c. Paired t test. H₀: μ_{d} = 0, Hₐ: μ_{d} ≠ 0 (μ_{d} = true mean difference A − B in pain score for patients like these).
dRandom samples of students from three schools: do the distributions of favorite lunch differ?
d. Chi-square test for homogeneity. H₀: no difference in the distribution of favorite lunch among the three schools; Hₐ: there is a difference.
🔑 Variable type → groups → interval or test.

📝 Example 3 · 例题★★★★AP-style FRQ

FRQ: phone use and sleep FRQ：手机使用与睡眠

A researcher randomly assigned 60 student volunteers to two groups. One group (n = 30) stopped using phones 1 hour before bed for two weeks; the other (n = 30) kept their usual routine. Average nightly sleep (hours): no-phone x̄ = 7.4, s = 0.9; usual x̄ = 6.9, s = 1.1.

aIdentify the appropriate procedure and state the hypotheses for testing whether stopping phone use increases mean sleep.
Two-sample t test for μ₁ − μ₂. H₀: μ_{N} − μ_{U} = 0, Hₐ: μ_{N} − μ_{U} > 0, where μ_{N}, μ_{U} = true mean nightly sleep for students like these with no phone / usual routine.
bCheck the conditions.
Random assignment ✓ (no 10% check — experiment). Both n = 30 ≥ 30 ✓.
cCalculate the test statistic and p-value (technology: df ≈ 55.8).
t = (7.4 − 6.9)/√(0.9²/30 + 1.1²/30) = 0.5/0.2595 ≈ 1.93; df ≈ 55.8; p-value ≈ 0.030.
dState a conclusion in context at α = 0.05, including the scope of inference.
0.030 < 0.05 → reject H₀. Convincing evidence that stopping phone use 1 hour before bed increases mean nightly sleep. Random assignment → cause and effect, but only for volunteers like these (no random sample).
🔑 Full four-step answer.

📝 AP Practice 4 · AP 选择题★★★★3.14.B

Which procedure? (1)

A random sample of 500 adults is asked whether they exercise regularly, and their age group (18–34, 35–54, 55+) is recorded. To test whether exercise and age group are related, use a
  • (A) two-sample z test
  • (B) chi-square test for homogeneity
  • (C) chi-square test for independence
  • (D) two-sample t test
  • (E) paired t test

One random sample classified by two categorical variables → (C).

Which procedure? (2)

Twenty pairs of identical twins: one twin in each pair is randomly assigned to a new reading program, the other to the standard program. Reading scores are compared. The appropriate procedure is
  • (A) two-sample t test
  • (B) paired t test
  • (C) two-sample z test for proportions

📝 AP Practice 7 · AP 选择题★★★★3.14.D.1.iii

Conditions

Which procedure requires every expected cell count in a two-way table to exceed 5?
  • (A) Large Counts for a one-proportion test
  • (B) The 10% condition
  • (C) Chi-square tests: all expected counts > 5
  • (D) Sample data condition for a t test
  • (E) Random condition

Chi-square tests require all expected counts > 5 → (C). (The one-proportion test uses np₀ ≥ 10, which is also an expected count, but the 5 rule is for χ².)

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

Name the procedure for each.

1Estimate the mean amount of caffeine in a brand’s energy drinks using 25 randomly selected cans.
One-sample t interval for μ.
2Is there convincing evidence that the proportion of left-handed students differs between two schools? (random samples)
Two-sample z test for p₁ − p₂ (Hₐ: p₁ − p₂ ≠ 0).
3Students take a pre-test and a post-test; did scores improve on average?
Paired t test (Hₐ: μ_{d} > 0, d = post − pre).
