Longleaf pines 长叶松

In the Wade Tract forest, researchers took independent random samples of 30 longleaf pines from the northern and southern halves and measured diameter at breast height (DBH, cm).

Halfnx̄ (cm)s (cm)
North3023.7017.50
South3034.5314.26
Q1Compare the two samples.
Southern trees are larger on average (34.53 vs. 23.70 cm); northern trees vary more (s = 17.50 vs. 14.26). The northern sample is right-skewed.
Q2Is the difference in sample means (−10.83 cm) large compared to how much it could vary by chance?
SE = √(17.50²/30 + 14.26²/30) ≈ 4.12 cm. −10.83 is about 2.6 SEs from 0 — likely a real difference. Today we turn this into an interval.

The Two-Sample t Interval 两样本 t 区间

Procedure方法
Two independent samples (or two groups in a randomized experiment) → two-sample t interval for μ₁ − μ₂. Define the parameter with the order, the response variable, and both populations.
两个独立样本（或随机化实验的两组）→ 两样本 t 区间。定义参数时写明顺序、响应变量和两个总体。
Formula公式
(x̄₁ − x̄₂) ± t*√(s₁²/n₁ + s₂²/n₂). SE = √(s₁²/n₁ + s₂²/n₂); ME = t*·SE.
(x̄₁ − x̄₂) ± t*√(s₁²/n₁ + s₂²/n₂)；SE、ME 同理。
Degrees of freedom自由度
Use technology (2-SampTInt, not pooled). df falls between the smaller of n₁ − 1 and n₂ − 1 (conservative) and n₁ + n₂ − 2.
用计算器（2-SampTInt，不合并）。df 在 min(n₁−1, n₂−1)（保守）与 n₁ + n₂ − 2 之间。

Conditions for Two-Sample t Procedures 两样本 t 方法的条件

Random随机
Two independent random samples or a randomized experiment.
两个独立随机样本或随机化实验。
10%10% 条件
n₁ ≤ 10% N₁ and n₂ ≤ 10% N₂ (not needed for experiments).
n₁ ≤ 10%N₁，n₂ ≤ 10%N₂（实验不需要）。
Sample data样本数据
Both n ≥ 30, or both populations approximately normal. If either n < 30, both sample distributions must be free of strong skewness and outliers.
两组都 n ≥ 30，或两个总体都近似正态。若任一组 n < 30，两组样本都需无明显偏斜和离群值。

📝 Example 2 · 例题★★★★EK 4.7 · 4.8.A

Pines: the interval 长叶松：区间

Use the Wade Tract summary statistics.

Halfnx̄ (cm)s (cm)
North3023.7017.50
South3034.5314.26
Construct and interpret a 90% confidence interval for μ_{N} − μ_{S}, the difference in mean DBH of all longleaf pines in the northern and southern halves.
Step 1. State: 90% CI for μ_{N} − μ_{S} = true difference in mean DBH (north − south) of all longleaf pines in the Wade Tract.
Step 2. Plan: two-sample t interval. Independent random samples ✓; 30 < 10% of the trees in each half ✓; n₁ = n₂ = 30 ≥ 30 ✓.
Step 3. Do: SE = √(17.50²/30 + 14.26²/30) ≈ 4.12. Technology: df ≈ 55.7, t* ≈ 1.673 → −10.83 ± 1.673(4.12) → (−17.72, −3.94) cm. (Conservative df = 29, t* = 1.699: (−17.83, −3.83).)
Step 4. Conclude: We are 90% confident that the interval from −17.72 to −3.94 cm captures the true difference (north − south) in mean DBH. The whole interval is negative → southern trees are larger on average.
🔑 State → Plan → Do → Conclude.

Interpreting and Using the Interval 解释与使用区间

Interval区间
“We are C% confident that the interval from a to b captures the true difference in mean (variable) (group 1 − group 2) for (populations).”
“我们有 C% 的信心，(a, b) 包含（两总体）（变量）均值的真实差值（组 1 − 组 2）。”
Confidence level置信水平
In repeated random sampling with the same sample sizes, about C% of such intervals capture μ₁ − μ₂.
重复抽样时约 C% 的区间包含 μ₁ − μ₂。
Is 0 in it?含 0 吗？
0 in the interval → not convincing evidence of a difference. 0 not in the interval → convincing evidence of a difference (the sign shows which mean is larger).
含 0 → 没有充分证据存在差异；不含 0 → 有充分证据（符号说明哪个更大）。

📝 Example 3 · 例题★★★★EK 4.7 · 4.8 · experiment

Does tutoring help? 辅导有帮助吗？

29 volunteers were randomly assigned: 15 to an online tutoring program, 14 to regular homework. Quiz scores (out of 20): tutoring x̄ = 16.0, s = 3.0; homework x̄ = 13.2, s = 3.5. Dotplots of both groups show no strong skewness or outliers.

aConstruct a 95% confidence interval for μ_{T} − μ_{H}.
Conditions: random assignment ✓; both n < 30 but no strong skew/outliers ✓. SE = √(3.0²/15 + 3.5²/14) ≈ 1.214; df ≈ 25.7, t* ≈ 2.057 → 2.8 ± 2.50 → (0.30, 5.30) points.
bDoes the interval give convincing evidence that tutoring helps? Can we conclude causation?
Yes — the whole interval is above 0 → convincing evidence that tutoring raises the mean score. Because treatments were randomly assigned, we can conclude tutoring causes the increase for volunteers like these.
🔑 Randomized experiment → no 10% condition.

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★EK 4.8.B

Two schools, one exam 两所学校，一场考试

Independent random samples of students from two large schools: School 1 (n = 25) x̄ = 71.2, s = 8.1; School 2 (n = 22) x̄ = 68.4, s = 9.3. A 95% CI for μ₁ − μ₂ is (−2.37, 7.97).

aInterpret the interval.
We are 95% confident that the interval from −2.37 to 7.97 points captures the true difference (School 1 − School 2) in mean exam score.
bA newspaper says “School 1 students score higher.” Is the claim supported?
Not supported: 0 is in the interval, so “no difference” is plausible — even though x̄₁ > x̄₂ in these samples.
🔑 0 in the interval → plausible no difference.

Independent random samples: n₁ = 25, s₁ = 10; n₂ = 16, s₂ = 8. The standard error of x̄₁ − x̄₂ is

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

Random samples of 40 juniors and 35 seniors at a large school: weekly hours of homework — juniors x̄ = 9.5, s = 3.2; seniors x̄ = 8.1, s = 3.8.

1Check the conditions and construct a 95% CI for μ_{J} − μ_{S} (technology: df ≈ 66.8, t* ≈ 1.996).
Independent random samples ✓; 40 and 35 < 10% of juniors/seniors ✓; both n ≥ 30 ✓. SE ≈ 0.818; 1.4 ± 1.996(0.818) → (−0.23, 3.03) hours.

Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
3 min · derivation optional

Why variances add for a difference两组差值为什么不是相减标准差

For independent sample means, covariance is zero. The minus sign disappears when its coefficient is squared.

\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_1-\bar X_2)=\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_1)+(-1)^2\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_2)
=\frac{\sigma_1^2}{n_1}+\frac{\sigma_2^2}{n_2}
\operatorname{SE}=\sqrt{\frac{s_1^2}{n_1}+\frac{s_2^2}{n_2}}

If each mean has SE 2, their difference has SE √(4 + 4) ≈ 2.83. Subtracting two uncertain estimates does not cancel independent noise.

均值相减，独立误差的方差仍相加。配对数据有协方差项，应先算差值。

Derivation optional / 推导不作 AP 必背要求
Optional Extension · 选学拓展（超出 AP 要求）

Why Welch df can be a decimal两份方差估计的不确定性

Each sample contributes an estimated variance to the squared SE. Let v₁ = s₁²/n₁ and v₂ = s₂²/n₂. Welch’s approximation balances the uncertainty in these two estimates.

\mathrm{df}\approx\frac{(v_1+v_2)^2}{\frac{v_1^2}{n_1-1}+\frac{v_2^2}{n_2-1}}

Example: n₁ = 10, s₁ = 6; n₂ = 20, s₂ = 4. Then v₁ = 3.6 and v₂ = 0.8.

\mathrm{df}\approx\frac{4.4^2}{\frac{3.6^2}{9}+\frac{0.8^2}{19}}\approx13.14

This is an effective df for an approximate t distribution. Use technology with Pooled: No; do not automatically use n₁ + n₂ − 2.

公式用于解释“小数自由度”从何而来，课堂无需手算或背诵。
