Tomato BINGO 番茄宾果

Tomato weights on a farm are normal with μ = 100 g and σ = 20 g. A farmer takes an SRS of only n = 4 tomatoes and builds a 99% interval for μ in three ways: 三种方法。

M1x̄ ± 2.576 · σ/√n (σ known)
M2x̄ ± 2.576 · s/√n (plug in s)
M3x̄ ± 5.841 · s/√n (use t*)
Which methods capture μ = 100 about 99% of the time? Try the simulator.
Step 1. M1 captures about 99% — but in practice we never know σ.
Step 2. M2 captures noticeably fewer than 99% (only about 92% in the long run): with n = 4, s is very variable and often too small, so intervals are too short.
Step 3. M3 uses a larger critical value t* = 5.841 from a t distribution with df = 3 → captures about 99% again.
🔑 Using s adds variability → need a bigger critical value.
💡 When σ is unknown, use s and a t* critical value.

The t Distributions t 分布

Standardizing with s用 s 标准化
When σ is unknown, we use the standard error s/√n. The statistic t = (x̄ − μ)/(s/√n) has a t distribution with df = n − 1 (degrees of freedom).
σ 未知时用标准误 s/√n。统计量 t = (x̄ − μ)/(s/√n) 服从自由度 df = n − 1 的 t 分布。
Shape形状
A family of symmetric, bell-shaped curves centered at 0 with wider (fatter) tails than the standard normal. As df increases, the t distribution gets closer to the standard normal.
一族对称、钟形、中心为 0 的曲线，尾部比标准正态更厚。df 越大越接近标准正态。

Finding t* 查找临界值 t*

What critical value t* should be used?

aA 95% confidence interval based on an SRS of size n = 12.
df = 11; 95% column → t* = 2.201. Calculator: invT(0.975, 11) = 2.201.
bA 90% confidence interval from a random sample of 48 observations.
df = 47 is not in Table B → use df = 40 (more conservative): t* = 1.684. Calculator: invT(0.95, 47) ≈ 1.678.
🔑 df = n − 1; Table B: if your df is not listed, use the next smaller df.

One-Sample t Interval for μ 单样本 t 区间

Procedure方法
Use a one-sample t interval for μ (σ unknown). Define μ in context: “μ = the true mean (variable) of all (population).”
用单样本 t 区间估计 μ（σ 未知），并在语境中定义 μ。
Formula公式
x̄ ± t* · s/√n, df = n − 1. SE = s/√n (standard error of x̄). ME = t* · s/√n.
x̄ ± t*·s/√n，df = n − 1；SE = s/√n；ME = t*·s/√n。
\bar x\pm t^*\frac{s}{\sqrt n}\qquad\mathrm{df}=n-1
df = n − 1

Conditions for a t Interval t 区间的条件

Random随机
Data come from a random sample or a randomized experiment.
数据来自随机样本或随机化实验。
10%10% 条件
When sampling without replacement: n ≤ 10% of N.
不放回抽样：n ≤ 10%N。
Sample data (Normal/Large Sample)样本数据条件
The population is approximately normal, or n ≥ 30, or (if n < 30) a graph of the sample data shows no strong skewness and no outliers.
总体近似正态，或 n ≥ 30，或（n < 30 时）样本数据的图形没有明显偏斜和离群值。

📝 Example 5 · 例题★★★★EK 4.2 · 4.3.A

How much do students sleep? 学生睡多久？

A random sample of 25 students at a large school recorded last night’s sleep: x̄ = 6.8 hours, s = 1.2 hours. A dotplot of the data is roughly symmetric with no outliers.

aFind the standard error and the margin of error for a 95% confidence interval.
SE = 1.2/√25 = 0.24 hours; df = 24, t* = 2.064; ME = 2.064(0.24) ≈ 0.495 hours.
bConstruct and interpret the interval.
6.8 ± 0.495 → (6.305, 7.295). We are 95% confident that the interval from 6.31 to 7.30 hours captures the true mean sleep time last night for all students at this school.
🔑 SE = s/√n; ME = t*·SE.

t vs. standard normal

Which statement about t distributions is FALSE?
  • (A) They are symmetric about 0.
  • (B) They have more area in the tails than the standard normal.
  • (C) As df increases, they approach the standard normal.
  • (D) For the same confidence level, t* is smaller than z*.

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

A company claims its chips bags contain 28 g. A random sample of 15 bags gives x̄ = 28.4 g and s = 0.9 g; a dotplot shows no strong skewness or outliers.

1Construct a 99% confidence interval for the true mean weight.
Conditions ✓ (random, 15 < 10% of bags, no strong skew/outliers). 28.4 ± 2.977(0.9/√15) = 28.4 ± 0.692 → (27.71, 29.09) g.
2Does the interval give convincing evidence against the company’s claim?
No — 28 g is inside the interval, so 28 g is a plausible value for the true mean weight.

Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
3 min · derivation optional

Why t instead of z?估计 σ 带来的额外不确定性

If σ were known, we would standardize with σ/√n. In practice, s changes from sample to sample, so the denominator is also uncertain.

Z=\frac{\bar X-\mu}{\sigma/\sqrt n}\qquad T=\frac{\bar X-\mu}{S/\sqrt n}

For independent observations from a normal population, T has exactly a t distribution with n − 1 degrees of freedom. For other populations, the t procedure is an approximation under suitable conditions.

A small S can make |T| unusually large. The t distribution allows more tail probability. As n grows, S estimates σ more stably and t approaches the standard normal.

t 分布的厚尾反映分母也在波动。n−1 来自估计样本方差时的一个均值约束。

Check your understanding · Why is t* larger for a smaller sample?

More uncertainty about σ requires a wider interval at the same confidence level.

Degrees of freedom: four settings不要把所有 df 都写成 n−1

SettingDegrees of freedomConstraint / reason
One-sample tn − 1Estimate one mean
Paired tnumber of pairs − 1Analyze one list of differences
Two-way χ² table(rows − 1)(columns − 1)Fix row and column totals
Welch two-sample tTechnology, possibly nonintegerApproximation using two estimated variances

“Information remaining after constraints” is a useful intuition. Welch df is an effective approximation, not a literal count of free data values.

配对的 n 是“对数”；Welch 的 df 可以是小数。不能把所有方法都套成“样本量减1”。
