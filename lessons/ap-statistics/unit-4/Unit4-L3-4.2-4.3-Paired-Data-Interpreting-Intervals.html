Paired Data 配对数据

Paired data配对数据
Two measurements of the same quantitative variable that belong together: the same individual measured twice (before/after, two treatments), or two very similar individuals (twins, matched subjects). The two samples are dependent.
同一量化变量的两次测量成对出现：同一个体测两次（前后、两种处理），或两个非常相似的个体（双胞胎、配对受试者）。两个样本不独立。
Analyze the differences分析差值
Compute d = (value 1) − (value 2) for each pair → one sample of differences. Parameter: μ_{d} = the true mean difference. State the order of subtraction in context.
对每一对算差值 d → 得到一个差值样本。参数 μ_{d} = 真实平均差值。必须说明相减的顺序。

📝 Example 2 · 例题★★★★EK 4.2.B · Skill 4.C

Keyboard differences 键盘差值

Differences (B − A) in typing speed for the 10 students: 3, 3, 1, 4, 1, 4, 4, 3, 1, 4.

aMake a dotplot of the differences and describe it.
All 10 differences are positive (1 to 4 wpm); no outliers; somewhat left-skewed with peaks at 1, 3 and 4.
bCalculate x̄_{d} and s_{d}. Interpret x̄_{d}.
x̄_{d} = 2.8 wpm, s_{d} ≈ 1.32 wpm. On average, these students typed 2.8 words per minute faster on keyboard B than on keyboard A.
cDefine the parameter μ_{d}.
μ_{d} = the true mean difference (B − A) in typing speed for students like these.
🔑 One sample of differences.

Paired t Interval for μ_{d} 配对 t 区间

Procedure方法
A one-sample t interval for a population mean difference (paired t interval): x̄_{d} ± t*·s_{d}/√n_{d}, df = n_{d} − 1.
单样本 t 区间（配对 t 区间）：x̄_{d} ± t*·s_{d}/√n_{d}，df = n_{d} − 1。
Conditions条件
Random: random sample or randomized experiment. 10%: n_{d} ≤ 10% of the population (sampling only). Sample data: n_{d} ≥ 30, or the differences show no strong skewness or outliers.
随机；10%（仅抽样时）；样本数据：n_{d} ≥ 30，或差值没有明显偏斜和离群值。
\bar x_d\pm t^*\frac{s_d}{\sqrt{n_d}}\qquad\mathrm{df}=n_d-1
df = n_{d} − 1

📝 Example 3 · 例题★★★★EK 4.2.C–D · 4.3.A

Keyboard B: how much faster? B 键盘快多少？

Use the 10 differences (B − A): x̄_{d} = 2.8, s_{d} = 1.317. Treatment order was randomly assigned.

aConstruct and interpret a 90% confidence interval for μ_{d}.
Step 1. Plan: paired t interval; random assignment of order ✓; n_{d} = 10 < 30, dotplot of differences has no strong skewness or outliers ✓ (no 10% check — experiment).
Step 2. Do: df = 9, t* = 1.833: 2.8 ± 1.833(1.317/√10) = 2.8 ± 0.763 → (2.04, 3.56) wpm.
Step 3. Conclude: We are 90% confident that the interval from 2.04 to 3.56 wpm captures the true mean difference (B − A) in typing speed for students like these.
bA student wrongly treats the data as two independent samples. Compare the standard errors.
Paired SE = 1.317/√10 ≈ 0.42. Two-sample SE = √(7.52²/10 + 8.15²/10) ≈ 3.51 — more than 8 times larger, because student-to-student variation was not removed.
🔑 Paired → use the differences.

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★EK 4.2 · Skill 3.E

New running shoes 新跑鞋

A coach randomly selected 12 runners from a large club. Each ran a mile in old shoes and in new shoes (random order). Differences (old − new) in seconds:

4 7 -2 5 3 9 1 6 2 -1 8 4

Construct and interpret a 95% confidence interval for the true mean difference. Use the four-step process.
Step 1. State: 95% CI for μ_{d} = the true mean difference (old − new) in mile time for runners in this club.
Step 2. Plan: paired t interval. Random sample of runners and random order ✓; 12 < 10% of club members ✓; n_{d} = 12 < 30, dotplot of differences: no strong skew or outliers ✓.
Step 3. Do: x̄_{d} = 3.83, s_{d} = 3.43, df = 11, t* = 2.201: 3.83 ± 2.201(3.43/√12) = 3.83 ± 2.18 → (1.65, 6.01) seconds.
Step 4. Conclude: We are 95% confident that the interval from 1.65 to 6.01 seconds captures the true mean difference (old − new) in mile time for runners in this club.
🔑 Four steps with the differences.

Difficulty 难度 ★★★★

Interpreting Intervals for μ and μ_{d} 解释 μ 与 μ_{d} 的区间

Interval区间
“We are C% confident that the interval from a to b captures the true mean (difference, with order) of (variable) for (population).” The interval may or may not contain the true value.
“我们有 C% 的信心，区间 (a, b) 包含（总体）（变量）的真实均值（或带顺序的平均差值）。”区间可能包含也可能不包含真值。
Confidence level置信水平
In repeated random sampling with the same n from the same population, about C% of the intervals would capture μ (or μ_{d}).
重复抽样（相同 n、相同总体）时，约 C% 的区间会包含 μ（或 μ_{d}）。
Justify a claim支持结论
Values in the interval are plausible. For μ_{d}: interval entirely > 0 or < 0 → convincing evidence of a difference; interval contains 0 → not convincing.
区间内的值都是合理的。对 μ_{d}：区间全部 > 0 或 < 0 → 有充分证据存在差异；包含 0 → 证据不足。

📝 Example 5 · 例题★★★★EK 4.3.A–B

Do new shoes help? 新跑鞋有用吗？

The 95% CI for μ_{d} (old − new mile time) was (1.65, 6.01) seconds.

aInterpret the confidence level.
If we repeated this study many times with random samples of 12 runners, about 95% of the resulting intervals would capture the true mean difference (old − new).
bThe shoe company claims runners are “at least 5 seconds faster” in the new shoes. Does the interval support this claim?
Not convincingly: the interval includes values from 1.65 to 5, so the true mean improvement could be less than 5 seconds. “At least 5” is not supported.
cIs there convincing evidence that the new shoes help? Explain.
Yes — the entire interval is above 0, so the new shoes appear to reduce mile times, on average, for runners like these.
🔑 Plausible values = values in the interval.

Width, Confidence Level, and Sample Size 区间宽度、置信水平与样本量

Confidence level ↑置信水平提高
For a given sample, increasing C makes the critical value t*, the margin of error, and the width all increase.
样本不变时，C 提高 → t*、误差范围、区间宽度都增大。
Sample size ↑样本量增大
Increasing n decreases the standard error, so the interval tends to be narrower. Width ∝ 1/√n: 4 times the sample size → about half the width.
n 增大 → 标准误减小 → 区间变窄。宽度 ∝ 1/√n：样本量 4 倍，宽度约减半。

📝 Example 6 · 例题★★★★EK 4.3.C · Skill 2.D

What happens to the width? 宽度会怎样？

A 95% t interval for μ from a sample of n = 25 has width 8.

aSame data, 99% confidence: wider or narrower?
Wider — t* increases (2.064 → 2.797), so the ME and width increase.
b95% confidence with a new sample of n = 100 (similar s): about how wide?
Width ∝ 1/√n: √(100/25) = 2 → about 4 units wide.
cWhy can’t we get 100% confidence with a narrow interval?
Higher confidence always costs width; 100% confidence would need an infinitely wide interval. To be both confident and precise, increase n.
🔑 C ↑ → wider; n × 4 → width ÷ 2.

Interpreting the confidence level

A 95% confidence interval for the mean commute time of a city’s workers is (24.1, 29.7) minutes. Which is the correct interpretation of “95% confident”?
  • (A) 95% of workers commute between 24.1 and 29.7 minutes.
  • (B) There is a 95% probability that μ is between 24.1 and 29.7.
  • (C) In repeated random samples, about 95% of intervals built this way would capture the true mean commute time.
  • (D) 95% of sample means are between 24.1 and 29.7.
  • (E) The sample mean is within 95% of the true mean.

C% refers to the long-run capture rate of the method → (C).

(A) talks about individuals; (B) gives a probability for a fixed parameter.

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

A teacher gives a random sample of 25 of her students a pre-test and a post-test. A 95% CI for μ_{d} = (post − pre) is (3.2, 8.6) points.

1Interpret the interval.
We are 95% confident that the interval from 3.2 to 8.6 points captures the true mean difference (post − pre) in test score for students of this teacher.
2Would a 90% interval from the same data be wider or narrower? Why?
Narrower — a smaller confidence level uses a smaller t*, so the margin of error is smaller.
3Does the interval give convincing evidence that scores improved?
Yes — the whole interval is above 0, so there is convincing evidence that scores improved, on average.
🔑 Order of subtraction: post − pre.

Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
3 min · derivation optional

Why pairing can improve precision相关性如何帮助比较

For each pair define D = A − B. The variance identity is:

\operatorname{Var}(D)=\operatorname{Var}(A)+\operatorname{Var}(B)-2\operatorname{Cov}(A,B)

If A and B are positively correlated, the covariance term reduces the variance of the difference. Stable person-to-person differences can cancel.

\begin{aligned}\operatorname{SD}(A)&=\operatorname{SD}(B)=10,\quad\rho_{AB}=0.8\\\operatorname{Var}(D)&=100+100-2(0.8)(10)(10)=40\end{aligned}
\operatorname{SD}(D)=\sqrt{40}\approx6.32\qquad\operatorname{SE}(\bar D)=\frac{s_D}{\sqrt n}

同一个人前后比较，可以消掉部分个体差异。是否配对由研究设计决定，不能为了更小的 p 值随意配对。

Check your understanding · What if the within-pair correlation is zero?

The covariance term vanishes. Pairing does not guarantee a precision gain.
