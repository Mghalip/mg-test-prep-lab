Graphs for a Quantitative Variable 定量变量的图形

Key idea核心
Histograms, stem-and-leaf plots, and dotplots show the distribution of a quantitative variable: the frequency or relative frequency of values (or intervals of values), keeping the natural order from smallest to largest.
直方图、茎叶图、点图展示定量变量的分布：各数值（或区间）的频数或相对频数，并保持从小到大的自然顺序。
Dotplot点图
A dotplot represents each value by a dot above (or beside) its location on a number line; nearly identical values are stacked.
点图：每个数据画一个点，放在数轴对应位置的上方；相同（或几乎相同）的值叠起来。
How to make a dotplot: ① Draw a number line and label it with the variable. ② Scale it from the minimum to the maximum. ③ Mark a dot above each value (stack repeats). 画数轴并标名称 → 标刻度 → 每个数据画一个点。

Stem-and-Leaf Plot (Stemplot) 茎叶图

Definition定义
A stem-and-leaf plot splits each value into a stem (all but the final digit) and a leaf (the final digit). Stems and leaves are ordered from smallest to largest. Always include a key.
茎叶图：把每个数分成茎（除最后一位以外的数字）和叶（最后一位）。茎和叶都从小到大排列，一定要写图例（key）。
How to make a stemplot: ① Separate each value into stem + leaf. ② Write stems in a column (smallest on top), draw a vertical line. ③ Write each leaf beside its stem. ④ Arrange leaves in increasing order. ⑤ Add a key. 分茎叶 → 写茎 → 写叶 → 叶排序 → 加 key。

Splitting Stems & Back-to-Back Stemplots 分茎与背靠背茎叶图

Split stems分茎
When values are bunched up, split stems: each stem appears twice — leaves 0–4 on the first line, 5–9 on the second.
数据挤在一起时可以分茎：每个茎写两行，第一行放叶 0–4，第二行放叶 5–9。
Back-to-back stemplot背靠背茎叶图
Two distributions of the same variable can be compared with a back-to-back stemplot using common stems; leaves for one group go to the left.
比较两组同一变量时，用公共的茎，一组的叶写在左边、一组写在右边。

Histogram 直方图

Definition定义
A histogram places values into ordered intervals (bins) on the horizontal axis. Each bar is a bin; its height is the frequency or relative frequency of observations in that bin. Bars touch (no gaps between bins). Changing the bin width can change the appearance. (Bars can also be horizontal.)
直方图：把数据分到有序的区间（组）中；每根柱子代表一个组，高度是频数或相对频数。柱子之间没有间隙。改变组距会改变图形的样子。
How to make a histogram: ① Choose equal-width intervals that span the data. ② Make a frequency (or relative frequency) table. ③ Draw and label the axes. ④ Scale the axes. ⑤ Draw bars. 选等宽区间 → 频数表 → 画坐标轴并标注 → 标刻度 → 画柱。

Histograms: cautions 直方图注意事项

⚠️ 1

Histogram ≠ bar graph

Histograms: quantitative data, ordered bins, bars touch. Bar graphs: categorical data, bars separated, order can change.

直方图用于定量数据，柱子相连。

⚠️ 2

Use percents to compare

Use relative frequencies when comparing distributions with different numbers of observations.

样本大小不同时用百分比。

⚠️ 3

Nice ≠ meaningful

Just because a graph looks nice doesn’t make it a meaningful display of data.

好看不等于有意义。

Describing a Distribution 描述分布

Key idea核心
A description of the distribution of one quantitative variable includes shape, center, and variability (spread), as well as any unusual features such as outliers, gaps, or clusters — all in context.
描述一个定量变量的分布要包括形状、中心、变异（离散程度）以及异常特征（离群值、间隙、簇），并且都要结合语境。
🔑 Remember SOCS: Shape · Outliers (unusual features) · Center · Spread (variability). AP® tip: use the variable name in context, not just the units. 要用变量名称，不只是单位。

Shape 形状

Symmetric · skewed对称 · 偏态
Skewed right (positively skewed): the right tail (toward larger values) is longer. Skewed left (negatively skewed): the left tail is longer. Approximately symmetric: the left half is about a mirror image of the right half.
右偏（正偏）：右侧尾巴（较大值方向）更长。左偏（负偏）：左侧尾巴更长。近似对称：左右大致镜像。
Peaks峰
Unimodal: one main peak. Bimodal: two prominent peaks. Approximately uniform: all frequencies about the same, no prominent peaks.
单峰：一个主峰。双峰：两个明显的峰。近似均匀：各频数差不多，没有明显的峰。
⚠️ The direction of skewness is toward the long tail, not where most observations are. 偏的方向 = 长尾方向。

Unusual Features 异常特征

Outlier · gap · cluster离群值 · 间隙 · 簇
Outliers are values that are unusually small or large relative to the rest of the data. A gap is a region between two values with no observed data. Clusters are concentrations of values, usually separated by gaps.
离群值：与其他数据相比异常小或异常大的值。间隙：两个值之间没有数据的区域。簇：数据集中的区域，通常被间隙隔开。

Describe the distribution 描述进球数的分布

Describe the distribution of goals scored in 20 games by the 2016 U.S. women’s soccer team.

Use SOCS and include context.
Step 1. Shape: the distribution of goals scored is skewed to the right, with a single peak at 1 goal. There is a gap between 5 and 9 goals.
Step 2. Outliers: the games when the team scored 9 and 10 goals appear to be outliers.
Step 3. Center: the median is 2 goals scored.
Step 4. Variability: the number of goals scored varies from 1 to 10 goals.
🔑 Goals scored in 20 games.
💡 Did we include context? YES — “goals scored”, “games”, not just numbers.

Justify a Claim with a Graph 用图形论证结论

Key idea核心
Graphical representations of a quantitative variable may reveal information that can be used to justify claims about the variable in context — cite specific features (counts, center, spread, outliers).
定量变量的图形可以用来结合语境论证结论——要引用具体的特征（个数、中心、变异、离群值）。

Is the claim supported? 结论有依据吗？

Use the dotplot of goals scored (20 games).

a“The team usually scored at least 2 goals.”
Supported — 14 of 20 games (70%) had 2 or more goals (pink).
b“The team never scored between 6 and 8 goals.”
Supported for these 20 games — there is a gap from 6 to 8 goals.

Which way is it skewed?

Most students in a class finished a quiz in 8 to 12 minutes, but a few took 20, 25, and 30 minutes. The distribution of finishing times is most likely
  • (A) skewed to the left
  • (B) skewed to the right
  • (C) roughly symmetric
  • (D) uniform
  • (E) bimodal

The long tail is toward larger values (20, 25, 30) → skewed right → (B).

Remember: skew points to the long tail, not where most data are.
