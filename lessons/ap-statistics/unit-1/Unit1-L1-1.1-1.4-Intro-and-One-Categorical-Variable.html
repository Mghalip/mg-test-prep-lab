Statistics 统计学

Definition定义
Statistics is the science and art of collecting, analyzing, and drawing conclusions from data. Data are numbers (or other information) with a context.
统计学是收集、分析数据并从数据中得出结论的科学与艺术。数据是有背景（语境）的数字或信息。
Just a number

911

Data (with context)

有了语境，数字才有意义

Statistical Study, Population & Sample 统计研究、总体与样本

Statistical study统计研究
A statistical study collects data from a sample to answer an investigative question about a larger population. Studies are needed when the population is too large or it is too difficult to collect data from everyone.
统计研究：从样本收集数据，回答关于更大总体的研究问题。当总体太大或很难从每个个体收集数据时，就需要统计研究。
Population · sample总体 · 样本
The population is all items or individuals of interest; its size is N. A sample is a subset of the population from which data are obtained; its size is n.
总体：所有感兴趣的个体，大小记作 N。样本：总体中实际收集数据的子集，大小记作 n。
A datum (singular of data) is one piece of information about an item or individual; a collection of data is a data set. Relating each result to the real-world situation = answering “in context”. datum 是单个数据；data set 是数据集；“in context” = 结合实际情境作答。

📝 Example 2 · 例题★★★★EK 1.1.A.1–6

How much do students sleep? 学生睡多久？

A principal wants to know how many hours the 1,200 students at her school sleep on school nights. She asks 80 randomly chosen students. One student, Li Wei, reports 6.5 hours.

aIdentify the population and N.
Population: all 1,200 students at the school → N = 1200.
bIdentify the sample and n.
Sample: the 80 randomly chosen students → n = 80.
cWhat is “6.5 hours” called?
A datum — one piece of information about one individual. All 80 answers form the data set.
dWhy not just ask all 1,200 students?
Asking everyone takes a lot of time and effort; a well-chosen sample can give a good estimate. 问所有人太费时费力。
🔑 1,200 students · 80 surveyed · Li Wei: 6.5 hours.
💡 Population (N) → sample (n) → data → conclusion about the population.

Lucky guesser? 只是运气好吗？

Joy classified 11 of 12 shirts correctly.

aIf she were just guessing, about how many would you expect her to get right?

Investigative Question 研究问题

Requirements要求
An investigative question for a study should have a defined purpose and should not be changed based on the data analysis or results. It should be posed so that the required data can be collected and analyzed.
研究问题要有明确目的，不能根据数据分析结果再修改；并且要能实际收集和分析所需数据。

Observational Units & Variables 观测单位与变量

Observational unit观测单位
An observational unit is an item or individual from which a datum is collected. (Older name: individual.)
观测单位：从中收集数据的个体或物品（旧称 individual）。
Variable变量
A variable is a characteristic that may change from one observational unit to another. Data on numerical and categorical variables — including photographs, sounds, videos, and text — can convey meaningful information.
变量：在不同观测单位之间可能变化的特征。数值、类别，甚至照片、声音、视频、文字都可以是有意义的数据。

Parameter vs. Statistic 参数与统计量

Parameter参数
A parameter is a numerical summary of the variable of interest for a population.
参数：描述总体中某变量的数值概括。
Statistic统计量
A statistic is a numerical summary for a sample. It is often not equal to the unknown parameter, but it can be the basis for making inferences about the parameter.
统计量：描述样本的数值概括。它通常不等于未知的参数，但可以作为推断参数的依据。
🔑 Parameter ↔ Population · Statistic ↔ Sample

Types of Variables 变量的类型

Categorical vs. quantitative分类变量 vs 定量变量
A categorical (qualitative) variable takes values that are category names or group labels. A quantitative (numerical) variable takes numerical values for a measured or counted quantity and generally has units.
分类变量：取值是类别名称或组别标签。定量变量：取值是测量或计数得到的数，通常有单位。
Discrete vs. continuous离散 vs 连续
A discrete quantitative variable takes a countable number of values. A continuous quantitative variable can take any value in an interval — measurable but not countable.
离散型定量变量：取值可数。连续型：可以取某区间内的任意值，可测量但不可数。
⚠️ Numbers aren’t always quantitative: zip codes, jersey numbers, phone numbers are categorical — averaging them makes no sense. 能否求平均是好方法判断。

Frequency & Relative Frequency Tables 频数表与相对频数表

Frequency table频数表
A frequency table shows the number (count) of observational units in each category of a categorical variable.
频数表：列出分类变量每个类别中观测单位的个数。
Relative frequency table相对频数表
A relative frequency table shows the proportion of observational units in each category. Relative frequency = count ÷ total.
相对频数表：列出每个类别所占的比例。相对频数 = 频数 ÷ 总数。

Describing Tables & Justifying Claims 描述表格、论证结论

Key idea核心
Percentages, relative frequencies, and ratios all provide the same information as proportions. Counts and relative frequencies reveal information that can be used to justify claims about the variable in context.
百分比、相对频数、比率与比例提供的是相同的信息。频数和相对频数可以用来结合语境论证关于变量的结论。

Bar Charts & Pie Charts 条形图与饼图

Bar chart条形图
A bar chart (bar graph) displays frequencies or relative frequencies for the categories of one categorical variable. Each bar is a category; its height (or length) is the frequency or relative frequency.
条形图：每根柱子代表一个类别，柱子的高度表示频数或相对频数。
Pie chart饼图
A pie chart shows each category as a slice; the area of each slice, as a fraction of the whole, is the relative frequency. All slices together = 1 (100%).
饼图：每个扇形代表一个类别，扇形面积占整体的比例 = 相对频数，所有扇形加起来 = 100%。

Graphs: good and bad 好图与坏图

Bar graphs are a bit dull. It is tempting to replace the bars with pictures or add 3-D effects to make them “more interesting.” Don’t do it! 不要用图片或 3D 效果代替柱子！

⚠️ Caution 1 · Beware the pictograph当心象形图
If a picture is made taller and wider, its area grows much faster than the count.
图片同时变高变宽 → 面积被夸大。
⚠️ Caution 2 · Watch those scales注意坐标轴
A vertical axis that doesn’t start at 0 exaggerates differences between bars.
纵轴不从 0 开始 → 差异被夸大。

Comparing Data Sets & Justifying Claims 比较数据集与论证

Key idea核心
Graphs of a categorical variable reveal information that can be used to justify claims in context. Frequency and relative frequency tables, bar charts, and pie charts can be used to compare two or more data sets on the same categorical variable — use relative frequencies when the data sets have different sizes.
分类变量的图可以用来结合语境论证结论。比较两个或多个数据集时，若样本大小不同，要用相对频数。

Population and sample

To estimate the percent of the 3,000 residents of a town who support a new park, a city worker surveys 150 randomly selected residents; 96 support it. Which statement is correct?

  • (B) N = 150 and n = 3,000.
  • (C) % is a parameter.

64% comes from the sample → a statistic, used to estimate the unknown parameter → (D).

  • (D) The bars should be ordered alphabetically.
  • (E) There is no problem.

Bar heights above 84: 2 vs. 6 → looks 3 times as big, but 90 is only about 1.05 times 86 → (B).

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

A teacher asks a random sample of 40 of the school’s 900 students how they get to school: Walk 12, Bus 18, Car 6, Bike 4.

1Identify the population, N, the sample, and n.
Population: all 900 students (N = 900) · Sample: the 40 surveyed (n = 40).
2Is “how they get to school” categorical or quantitative?
Categorical — the values are labels.
3Make a relative frequency table. Is “most students take the bus” supported?
Walk 30% · Bus 45% · Car 15% · Bike 10%. Bus is the most common (45%), but not “most” (not over 50%).
🔑 n = 40 of N = 900 students.
