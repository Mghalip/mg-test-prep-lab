Conditional Probability 条件概率

Definition定义
The probability that event A will occur given that event B has occurred is called a conditional probability, written P(A | B). It is defined as P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B).
在事件 B 已经发生的条件下，事件 A 发生的概率叫作条件概率，记作 P(A | B)，竖线读作 “given that（已知）”。P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) ÷ P(B)。
🔑 The condition (after the bar) goes in the denominator — exactly like Lesson 1’s “among…” questions. 竖线后面的“条件”就是分母。
Unit 2 · Lesson 4
Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
3 min · derivation optional

Why Divide by the Probability of B? 把 B 当作新的范围

Once B is known to occur, restrict attention to outcomes in B. The favorable outcomes are those in both A and B.

In a group of 100 students, 40 are in B and 12 are in both A and B.

The original total cancels. Dividing by P(B) rescales the probability inside B so its total becomes 1.

已知 B 后，分母改为 B 内的人数。这里是 12/40，不是 12/100；不需要假设 A、B 独立。

Using the formula 只给概率时用公式

aP(A) = 0.5, P(A ∩ B) = 0.2. Find P(B | A).
0.2 / 0.5 = 0.4
b40% of students play a sport; 12% play a sport and an instrument. Among athletes, what proportion play an instrument?

2.6 · Key ConceptEK 2.6.A.2
Difficulty 难度 ★★★★

General Multiplication Rule 一般乘法法则

Rule法则
The probability that events A and B both occur equals the probability that A occurs times the conditional probability that B occurs given that A has occurred: P(A ∩ B) = P(A) · P(B | A).
A 和 B 都发生的概率 = A 发生的概率 × 在 A 已发生的条件下 B 发生的概率。
It is just the conditional probability formula rearranged: P(B | A) = P(A ∩ B)/P(A) → multiply both sides by P(A). 先 A 发生，然后在此基础上 B 发生。

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★EK 2.6.A.2

Two hearts 两张红心（无放回）

Draw 2 cards from a standard deck without replacement.

aFind P(both hearts).
P(♥ ∩ ♥) = 13/52 · 12/51 ≈ 0.0588
bFind P(exactly one heart).
P(♥, not) + P(not, ♥) = 13/52 · 39/51 + 39/52 · 13/51 ≈ 0.1912 + 0.1912 = 0.382
cFind P(the second card is a heart).
P(2nd ♥) = 13/52 · 12/51 + 39/52 · 13/51 = 0.25 — the same as the first card!
🔑 2 cards without replacement: ♥ or not ♥.
💡 Multiply along branches, add across the ends you need.

Tree Diagrams 树状图

Tool工具
A tree diagram shows the sample space of a chance process with several stages. Each branch is labeled with a (conditional) probability. Multiply along a path to get P(that path); add the paths you need.
树状图用于多步骤随机过程：每条分支标上（条件）概率；沿路径相乘得到该路径的概率，再把需要的路径相加。

📝 Example 6 · 例题★★★★EK 2.6.A.1 · 2.6.A.2

Given a positive test… 检测阳性，真的患病吗？

A disease affects 2% of a population. A test is positive for 95% of people who have it, and for 6% of people who don’t. (illustrative)

aFind P(test positive).
Step 1. Tree: P(D ∩ +) = 0.02 × 0.95 = 0.019 · P(Dᶜ ∩ +) = 0.98 × 0.06 = 0.0588.
Step 2. P(+) = 0.019 + 0.0588 = 0.0778 (add the two pink ends).
bA person tests positive. Find P(disease | +).
P(D | +) = 0.019 / 0.0778 ≈ 0.244 — far below 95%!
cPredict first: is it close to 95%?
Why? Most positives come from the large group without the disease. 大部分阳性来自没病的人群。
🔑 P(D) = 0.02 · P(+ | D) = 0.95 · P(+ | Dᶜ) = 0.06.
💡 “Reverse” conditional: P(D | +) = P(D ∩ +) / P(+).

Independent Events 独立事件

Definition定义
Events A and B are independent if and only if their joint probability equals the product of their probabilities:
Equivalent conditional-probability checks, when the conditioning event has positive probability:
独立：一个事件的发生不改变另一个事件发生的概率。三个等式是分别使用的判定条件，不是连乘式。条件概率要求所给事件的概率大于 0；乘积判定式也适用于零概率事件。

Multiplication Rule for Independent Events 独立事件的乘法法则

Rule法则
If A and B are independent, then P(A ∩ B) = P(A) · P(B). (In the general rule, P(B | A) = P(B).)
若 A、B 独立，则 P(A ∩ B) = P(A) · P(B)（一般乘法法则中 P(B | A) = P(B)）。

📝 Example 9 · 例题★★★★EK 2.5.A.2 · 2.7.A.1

Can disjoint events be independent? 互斥事件能独立吗？

Roll one die. A = “roll a 1”, B = “roll a 6”. So P(A) = P(B) = 1/6.

aAre A and B mutually exclusive?
Yes — one roll can’t be both 1 and 6: P(A ∩ B) = 0.
bFind P(B | A). Are A and B independent?
If A happened, B can’t happen: P(B | A) = 0 ≠ 1/6 = P(B) → not independent.
cIn general: if P(A) > 0 and P(B) > 0, can disjoint events be independent?
No! Disjoint events (with positive probabilities) are always dependent — knowing one happened tells you the other didn’t. 互斥事件（概率都大于 0）一定不独立。
🔑 A = 1, B = 6 on one die.

Union of Two Events (review) 事件的并（复习）

Definition & rule定义与法则
The probability that event A or event B (or both) will occur is the probability of A union B, written P(A ∪ B): P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B).
A 或 B（或两者都）发生的概率叫作 A 与 B 的并的概率，记作 P(A ∪ B)。
Unit 2 · Lesson 4
Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
3 min · derivation optional

Why Subtract the Overlap? 并集公式的去重

Adding P(A) and P(B) counts every outcome in the overlap twice. Subtract one copy so each outcome in the union is counted once.

Of 100 students, 40 are in A, 30 are in B, and 10 are in both.

A 加 B 时交集被数了两遍，所以减去一次。互斥时交集为 0；独立时交集等于两个概率的乘积。

2.6–2.7 · AP Exam Tips

Common mistakes 常见错误

✗ Mistake 1

Flipping the condition

P(A | B) and P(B | A) are usually different.

把条件和事件对调。

✗ Mistake 2

Multiplying without independence

P(A)·P(B) works for "and" only if A and B are independent. Otherwise use P(A)·P(B | A).

不独立时不能直接相乘。

✗ Mistake 3

Disjoint = independent?

No! Disjoint events with positive probabilities are always dependent.

互斥 ≠ 独立。

✗ Mistake 4

"At least one" by adding

Use the complement: P(at least one) = 1 − P(none).

“至少一个”用补集。

📝 AP Practice 12 · AP 选择题★★★★EK 2.7.A.1 · 2.7.A.3

Union of independent events

Events A and B are independent, with P(A) = 0.3 and P(B) = 0.5. What is P(A ∪ B)?
  • (A) 0.15
  • (B) 0.50
  • (C) 0.65
  • (D) 0.80
  • (E) 0.85

Independent → P(A ∩ B) = 0.3 × 0.5 = 0.15.

P(A ∪ B) = 0.3 + 0.5 − 0.15 = 0.65 → (C). (D) assumes disjoint; (A) is P(A ∩ B).

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

P(A) = 0.6, P(B) = 0.3, P(A ∩ B) = 0.18.

1Find P(B | A).
0.18 / 0.6 = 0.3
2Are A and B independent? Justify.

3Find P(A ∪ B).
0.6 + 0.3 − 0.18 = 0.72
