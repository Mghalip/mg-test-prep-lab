Simulation 模拟

Definition定义
Simulation is a way to model random events so that simulated outcomes closely match real-world outcomes. All possible outcomes are associated with a value to be determined by chance. Record the counts of simulated outcomes and the count total.
模拟：用一个能准确反映真实情况的模型来模仿随机事件。把每个可能结果对应到一个由机会决定的值（如随机数字），记录各结果出现的次数和总次数。

② Plan

③ Do

Perform many trials.

重复很多次

Use the results to answer the question.

得出结论

Myths about randomness 关于随机性的误区

Myth 1 · Short-run regularity短期规律
Randomness is predictable in the long run, but probability does not let us make short-run predictions.
概率只在长期有规律，不能预测短期。
Myth 2 · "Law of averages"平均律
Random behavior evens out in the long run, but future outcomes are not affected by past outcomes.
过去的结果不会影响将来的结果。
🎲 Casinos rely on the long run; gamblers who believe a number is "due" rely on a myth. 赌场靠长期规律赚钱；“该轮到我了”是误区。

Sample Space & Probability Model 样本空间与概率模型

Sample space样本空间
The sample space S of a random process is the set of all possible nonoverlapping outcomes. The probability of the sample space is 1: P(S) = 1.
随机过程的样本空间 S 是所有可能的、互不重叠的结果组成的集合。样本空间的概率为 1。
Probability model概率模型
A probability model describes a random process with two parts: a sample space S and a probability for each outcome.
概率模型由两部分组成：样本空间，以及每个结果的概率。

Equally Likely Outcomes 等可能结果

Theoretical probability理论概率
If all outcomes in the sample space are equally likely, the probability that event E occurs is P(E) = (number of outcomes in E) ÷ (total number of outcomes in S).
如果样本空间中所有结果等可能，事件 E 的概率 = E 包含的结果数 ÷ 样本空间的结果总数。
⚠️ Only when outcomes are equally likely! The sums 2, 3, …, 12 of two dice are not equally likely, so P(sum = 7) ≠ 1/11. “和”不是等可能的！

Complement 补集（对立事件）

Complement rule补集法则
The complement of event E is "not E," written E′, Ē, or Eᶜ. Its probability is P(Eᶜ) = 1 − P(E).
事件 E 的补集（对立事件）是“E 不发生”，记作 E′、Ē 或 Eᶜ。它的概率 = 1 − P(E)。
P(E) + P(Eᶜ) = P(S) = 1
“至少一个”“不是”类问题，先想补集！

2.4 · AP Exam Tips

Common mistakes 常见错误

✗ Mistake 1

"Two outcomes → 50-50"

Thumbtacks, sums of dice, basketball shots… outcomes aren't always equally likely.

两种结果不代表各占一半。

✗ Mistake 2

Short-run predictions

P = 0.8 does not mean "exactly 8 out of every 10."

概率不能预测短期结果。

✗ Mistake 3

Vague simulation steps

Forgetting to say how to label, what to skip, when a trial stops, or what to record.

模拟步骤写得不清楚。

✗ Mistake 4

Ignoring repeats

When choosing different people, a repeated label must be skipped.

抽不同的人时，重复的编号要跳过。

Not an A or a B

A student is chosen at random. The probability model for their grade on a test is shown. What is the probability the student did not get an A or a B?
GradeABCDF
Probability0.270.350.220.10?
  • (A) 0.06
  • (B) 0.32
  • (C) 0.35
  • (D) 0.38
  • (E) 0.62

P(F) = 1 − (0.27 + 0.35 + 0.22 + 0.10) = 0.06.

P(not A or B) = 1 − (0.27 + 0.35) = 0.38 → (D). Check: 0.22 + 0.10 + 0.06 = 0.38 ✓

(E) 0.62 is P(A or B), not its complement.

Valid probability model? 是合法的概率模型吗？

A valid model needs: every probability between 0 and 1, and a total of exactly 1. Which are valid?

1Spinner — Red 0.25 · Blue 0.25 · Green 0.30 · Yellow 0.20
Spinner: total = 1.00, all between 0 and 1 → valid.
2Pizza topping — Cheese 0.45 · Pepperoni 0.40 · Veggie 0.25
Pizza: total = 1.10 > 1 → not valid.
3Number of pets — 0: 0.30 · 1: 0.45 · 2: 0.35 · 3+: −0.10
Pets: total = 1.00 but −0.10 < 0 → not valid.
4Coin toss — H 0.6 · T 0.4
Coin: total = 1 → valid — a model can be valid even if the coin is not fair!
🔑 Check each model: 0 ≤ P ≤ 1 and total = 1.

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

A fair 8-sided die (faces 1–8) is rolled.

1Write the sample space. Find P(roll is at least 7).
S = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}; P(≥ 7) = 2/8 = 0.25
2Use the complement rule to find P(roll is less than 7).
P(< 7) = 1 − 0.25 = 0.75
3Lily rolled four 1s in a row. She says, “An 8 is due next.” Is she right? Explain.
No — rolls are independent; past results don’t change future probabilities. P(8) is still 1/8.
🔑 Fair 8-sided die.
