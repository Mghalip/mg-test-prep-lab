Continuous Random Variables & Density Curves 连续随机变量与密度曲线

Continuous random variable连续随机变量
A variable that can take on any value within a specified domain. Every interval within the domain has a probability associated with it.
可以在某个范围内取任意值的随机变量；每个区间都对应一个概率。
Density curve密度曲线
A curve that is always on or above the horizontal axis and has area exactly 1 underneath it. The area under the curve above an interval = the proportion (probability) in that interval.
始终在横轴上方、曲线下总面积为 1 的曲线。某区间上方的面积 = 落在该区间的比例（概率）。

📝 Example 1 · 例题★★★★EK 2.11.A.1 · TPS 6.1

Waiting for the train 等火车（均匀分布）

A train arrives at a random time; your wait X is uniform from 0 to 20 minutes (the density curve is a flat rectangle).

aWhat is the height of the density curve? Why?
Total area must be 1: 20 × height = 1 → height = 1/20.
bFind P(5 < X < 12).
Area = (12 − 5) × 1/20 = 0.35
cFind P(X > 15).
Area = (20 − 15) × 1/20 = 0.25
dFind P(X = 10).
A single value has no width → area 0. For continuous variables, P(X = a) = 0.
🔑 X uniform on [0, 20].

Density curve vs. histogram 密度曲线 vs 直方图

The histogram shows the vocabulary scores of all 947 seventh-grade students in Gary, Indiana, on the Iowa Test of Basic Skills (ITBS). 287 of the scores are below 6.0. The smooth curve is a normal density curve with μ = 6.84 and σ = 1.55.

aWhat proportion of the 947 scores are below 6.0?
287 / 947 ≈ 0.303 (the pink bars).

Normal Distribution 正态分布

Definition定义
A normal distribution is a continuous, unimodal, bell-shaped, and symmetric curve, identified by two parameters: the mean μ and standard deviation σ, written N(μ, σ). The smaller σ, the taller and more concentrated the curve around μ; the larger σ, the shorter and less concentrated. σ is the distance from μ to the inflection points.
正态分布是连续、单峰、钟形、对称的曲线，由均值 μ（位置）和标准差 σ（胖瘦）确定，记作 N(μ, σ)。σ 越小越高、越集中；σ 越大越矮、越分散。σ = 中心到拐点的距离。

The Empirical Rule (68–95–99.7) 经验法则

Rule法则
For a normal distribution, approximately 68% of observations are within 1σ of μ, 95% within 2σ, and 99.7% within 3σ.
正态分布中，约 68% 的数据在均值 ±1σ 内，95% 在 ±2σ 内，99.7% 在 ±3σ 内。

📝 Example 3 · 例题★★★★EK 2.11.C.1

Using the empirical rule 经验法则练习

Heights of young women are approximately N(64, 2.7) inches.

aAbout what percent are between 58.6 and 69.4 in.?
58.6 to 69.4 = μ ± 2σ → about 95%
bAbout what percent are taller than 66.7 in.?
66.7 = μ + 1σ → (100% − 68%)/2 = 16%
cAbout what percent are between 61.3 and 72.1 in.?
61.3 = μ − 1σ, 72.1 = μ + 3σ → 34 + 34 + 13.5 + 2.35 ≈ 83.85%
🔑 N(64, 2.7): 56, 58.6, 61.3, 64, 66.7, 69.4, 72.1

The Standard Normal Distribution 标准正态分布

Definition定义
A standard normal distribution is a normal distribution with mean μ = 0 and standard deviation σ = 1: N(0, 1).
标准正态分布：均值为 0、标准差为 1 的正态分布 N(0, 1)。
Standardizing (z-score) · review标准化（z 分数）
If X is N(μ, σ), then z = (x − μ) / σ has the standard normal distribution. A z-score tells how many standard deviations a value is from the mean, and in which direction.
z 分数：一个值距离均值有几个标准差；正数在均值以上，负数在均值以下。

Area Under the Curve = Probability 面积 = 概率

Key idea核心
If a random variable is approximately normal, the probability that it takes a value in an interval = the area under the normal curve over that interval. The total area is 1. For continuous variables, P(X = a) = 0, so P(X < a) = P(X ≤ a).
某区间内取值的概率 = 正态曲线在该区间上方的面积，总面积为 1。连续型：单点概率为 0，“<”和“≤”相同。

📝 Example 6 · 例题★★★★EK 2.11.D.1

Tiger on the range 老虎伍兹练球

When Tiger Woods hits his driver, the distance follows a normal distribution with μ = 304 yards and σ = 8 yards.

aWhat percent of his drives travel at least 290 yards?
z = (290 − 304)/8 = −1.75 → P(Z ≥ −1.75) = 1 − 0.0401 = 0.9599. About 96% of Tiger’s drives travel at least 290 yards.
bWhat percent travel between 305 and 325 yards?
z = (305 − 304)/8 = 0.125 and (325 − 304)/8 = 2.625 → 0.9957 − 0.5497 ≈ 0.446. About 45% of his drives.
🔑 X = drive distance ~ N(304, 8).

📝 Example 7 · 例题★★★★EK 2.11.D.1

Heights between 68 and 70 inches 身高在 68 到 70 英寸之间

Heights Y of young women are approximately N(64, 2.7) inches.

aFind P(68 ≤ Y ≤ 70).
z = (68 − 64)/2.7 ≈ 1.48 and (70 − 64)/2.7 ≈ 2.22 → normalcdf(lower: 68, upper: 70, μ: 64, σ: 2.7) ≈ 0.0561
bInterpret the result in context.
About 5.6% of young women are between 68 and 70 inches tall.
cWould P(68 < Y < 70) be different? Why?
No — for a continuous variable, P(Y = 68) = P(Y = 70) = 0, so “<” and “≤” give the same answer.

From an Area to a Boundary 由面积求边界值

Key idea核心
Sometimes we know the area (probability) and want the boundary. Use invNorm (or Table A backwards): x = μ + z·σ. Four cases: lowest p%, middle p%, highest p%, most extreme p% (split equally between the tails).
已知面积求边界：用 invNorm（或反查表），x = μ + zσ。四种情况：最低 p%、中间 p%、最高 p%、两端最极端 p%（两侧各一半）。
MeaningInequality中文
Lowest p% lie left of x_{a}P(X < x_{a}) = p/100最低的 p%
p% lie between x_{a} and x_{b}P(x_{a} < X < x_{b}) = p/100中间的 p%
Highest p% lie right of x_{b}P(X > x_{b}) = p/100最高的 p%
Most extreme p%, split equallyP(X < x_{a}) = P(X > x_{b}) = ½(p/100)两端最极端的 p%

Comparing Relative Positions 比较相对位置

Key idea核心
To compare values from different distributions, compare their z-scores or percentiles. The p^{th} percentile is the value with p% of the distribution at or below it.
比较来自不同分布的值时，比较它们的 z 分数或百分位数。第 p 百分位数：有 p% 的数据小于或等于它。

2.11 · AP Exam Tips

Common mistakes 常见错误

✗ Mistake 1

Calculator-speak only

Write "normalcdf(lower: 68, upper: 70, μ: 64, σ: 2.7)" with labels — or show the z-scores.

计算器输入要标注每个参数。

✗ Mistake 2

Left vs. right area

Table A gives area to the left. For "greater than," subtract from 1.

表格给的是左侧面积。

✗ Mistake 3

Variance for σ

N(μ, σ) uses the standard deviation, not the variance.

N(μ, σ) 里是标准差。

✗ Mistake 4

No picture

Always sketch the curve and shade — it catches most errors.

先画图再计算。

Battery life 电池寿命（mini-FRQ）

The life of a certain battery is approximately normal with mean 500 hours and standard deviation 50 hours. (illustrative)

z = 2 → P(Z > 2) ≈ 0.0228 (empirical rule: about 2.5%)

z = −1 and 1.5 → 0.9332 − 0.1587 = 0.7745
cThe company wants to label the top 5% as “extra long life.” What is the cutoff?
P(X > x_{b}) = 0.05 → z ≈ 1.645 → x_{b} = 500 + 1.645(50) ≈ 582.2 hours

📝 AP Practice 13 · AP 选择题★★★★EK 2.11.E.1

Find the mean from two percentiles

The heights of 3-year-old boys are approximately normal. Duncan is 32.0 in. tall, at the 32nd percentile. Shane is 34.0 in. tall, at the 62nd percentile. Which is closest to the mean height?
  • (A) 32.5 in.
  • (B) 33.0 in.
  • (C) 33.2 in.
  • (D) 33.5 in.
  • (E) 34.0 in.

invNorm(0.32) ≈ −0.468 and invNorm(0.62) ≈ 0.305.

32 = μ − 0.468σ and 34 = μ + 0.305σ → subtract: 2 = 0.773σ → σ ≈ 2.59.

μ = 32 + 0.468(2.59) ≈ 33.2 in. → (C).

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

Scores on a test are approximately N(100, 15).

1About what percent score between 85 and 115? (empirical rule)
μ ± 1σ → about 68%
2Find P(score > 130).
z = 2 → 0.0228
3What score is the 80th percentile?
invNorm(0.80) ≈ 0.842 → 100 + 0.842(15) ≈ 112.6
🔑 Scores ~ N(100, 15).
