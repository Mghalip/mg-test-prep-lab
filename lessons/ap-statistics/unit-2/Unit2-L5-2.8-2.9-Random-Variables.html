Random Variable 随机变量

Definition定义
A random variable is a variable whose values are numerical outcomes that result from a random phenomenon. Random variables are named with capital letters (X, Y, C…).
随机变量：取值为随机现象所产生的数值结果的变量，通常用大写字母表示。
Discrete离散型
Takes a countable set of values — e.g., number of heads.
取值可数（有限或可列出）。
Continuous · Lesson 7连续型
Takes any value in an interval — e.g., height, time.
可取某区间内任意值（第 7 课）。

Discrete or continuous?

Probability Distribution 概率分布

Definition定义
A probability distribution for a discrete random variable shows the probability associated with every possible value of the random variable. The sum of the probabilities over all possible values is 1.
离散随机变量的概率分布给出它每一个可能取值对应的概率；所有概率之和为 1。
Valuex₁x₂x₃…
Probabilityp₁p₂p₃…
Every pᵢ is between 0 and 1, and p₁ + p₂ + p₃ + … = 1. 与第 2 课的概率模型一致。

Three ways to show a distribution 三种表示方式

A discrete probability distribution can be represented as a table, a graph, or a function. 可以用表格、图形或函数表示。

Table 表格
x0123
P(x)1/83/83/81/8
Graph 图形 (probability histogram)
Function 函数

P(X = x) = C(3, x) / 8

for x = 0, 1, 2, 3. C(3, x) counts the ways to get x heads. （第 6 课二项分布会用到）

Apgar scores of newborns 新生儿 Apgar 评分

Doctors rate a newborn’s health on a 0–10 scale. Let X = Apgar score of a randomly selected newborn.

x012345678910
P(x)0.0010.0060.0070.0080.0120.0200.0380.0990.3190.4370.053
aShow that this is a valid probability distribution.
Every probability is between 0 and 1, and they add to 1 ✓
bFind and interpret P(X > 7).
P(X > 7) = 0.319 + 0.437 + 0.053 = 0.809. About 81% of newborns have Apgar scores greater than 7.
cFind P(X ≥ 7). dDescribe the shape.
Step 1. P(X ≥ 7) = 0.099 + 0.809 = 0.908 — “≥” includes 7!
Step 2. Skewed to the left, single peak at 9.
🔑 X = Apgar score of a randomly selected newborn.
💡 Discrete: P(X > 7) ≠ P(X ≥ 7). List the values!

Cumulative Probability Distribution 累积概率分布

Definition定义
A cumulative probability distribution can be represented as a table or function and shows the probability of being less than or equal to each value: P(X ≤ x).
累积概率分布给出随机变量小于或等于每个值的概率 P(X ≤ x)，可以用表格或函数表示。

Cumulative distribution & median 累积分布与中位数

Use the Apgar distribution.

x012345678910

Parameter 参数

Definition定义
A numerical value measuring a characteristic of a probability distribution of a random variable, or of a population, is a parameter. The value of a parameter is a single, fixed value.
描述随机变量的概率分布（或总体）某一特征的数值叫作参数。参数是一个固定的值。
Today’s two parameters: the mean μ_{X} (center) and the standard deviation σ_{X} (variability). Greek letters → parameters. 希腊字母表示参数。
Unit 2 · Lesson 5
2.9 · Key ConceptEK 2.9.A.2
Difficulty 难度 ★★★★

Expected Value (Mean) 期望（均值）

Definition定义
The expected value (or mean) of a discrete random variable, written E(X) or μ_{X}, is μ_{X} = Σ xᵢ · P(xᵢ). It is a weighted average of the possible values, each weighted by its probability, and is interpreted as the long-run average outcome.
期望 E(X) 或 μ_{X} = 每个取值 × 其概率，再求和。它是以概率为权重的加权平均，可解释为大量重复后的长期平均结果。
The mean is the balance point of the distribution. 均值是分布的平衡点。

Pete's expected earnings 计算期望

Passengers23456
C$300$450$600$750$900
P(C)0.150.250.350.200.05

Standard Deviation 标准差

Definition定义
The standard deviation of a discrete random variable, SD(X) or σ_{X}, is σ_{X} = √[ Σ (xᵢ − μ_{X})² · P(xᵢ) ]. It is the typical deviation of the values from the mean over the long run. Its square is the variance, V(X) or σ_{X}².
标准差 σ_{X}：长期来看，随机变量的取值与均值之间的典型偏离。标准差的平方叫方差，记作 V(X) 或 σ_{X}²。
Unit 2 · Lesson 5
📝 Example 7 · 例题★★★★EK 2.9.A.3

Pete's standard deviation 计算标准差

Passengers23456
C$300$450$600$750$900
P(C)0.150.250.350.200.05
aUsing μ_{C} = $562.50, calculate the variance and the standard deviation σ_{C}.
Step 1. (300 − 562.5)²(0.15) + (450 − 562.5)²(0.25) + (600 − 562.5)²(0.35) + (750 − 562.5)²(0.20) + (900 − 562.5)²(0.05)
Step 2. = 10,335.9 + 3,164.1 + 492.2 + 7,031.3 + 5,695.3 = 26,718.75 (variance)
Step 3. σ_{C} = √26,718.75 ≈ $163.46 — don’t forget the square root!
bApgar: μ = 8.128 and σ ≈ 1.437. Which distribution is more spread out relative to its scale?
Pete: σ is about 29% of μ; Apgar: about 18% of μ → Pete’s amounts vary more relative to their size.
🔑 σ² = Σ (c − μ)² · P(c), μ = 562.5.
💡 Variance → √ → standard deviation (same units as X).

Interpreting μ and σ in context 结合语境解释

Sentence frames 句型

Mean: If we [repeat the random process] many, many times, the average [variable in context] will be about μ.
SD: The [variable in context] typically varies from the mean of μ by about σ.
⚠️ The expected value doesn’t have to be a possible value. 期望不一定是可能取到的值。

📝 Example 8 · 例题★★★★EK 2.9.B.1

Interpret in context 结合语境解释

Pete’s Jeep Tours: μ_{C} = $562.50, σ_{C} ≈ $163.46. Apgar: μ_{X} = 8.128, σ_{X} ≈ 1.437.

aInterpret μ_{C}.
If we randomly select many, many jeep tours, Pete will collect about $562.50 per trip, on average.
bInterpret σ_{C}.
The amount Pete collects on randomly selected trips typically varies from the mean ($562.50) by about $163.46.
cInterpret σ_{X} for the Apgar scores.
The Apgar scores of randomly selected newborns typically vary from the mean (8.128) by about 1.44 points.
🔑 Mean = long-run average · SD = typical distance from the mean.

2.8–2.9 · AP Exam Tips

Common mistakes 常见错误

✗ Mistake 1

Unweighted average

The mean is not (x₁ + x₂ + …)/n — weight each value by its probability.

期望要用概率加权。

✗ Mistake 2

Forgetting the √

Σ(x − μ)²P(x) is the variance; take the square root for σ.

方差开根号才是标准差。

✗ Mistake 3

"≤" vs "<"

For discrete variables, P(X < 3) ≠ P(X ≤ 3). List the values!

离散型要注意是否包含端点。

✗ Mistake 4

No context

"The mean is 8.128" earns nothing — say what it means for the newborns in the long run.

解释必须结合语境。

Cars per household 每户汽车数量（mini-FRQ）

Choose a household at random in a city. Let X = the number of cars it owns. (illustrative)

x01234
P(x)0.080.300.380.18?
aFind P(X = 4).   bFind P(X ≥ 2).
Step 1. 1 − (0.08 + 0.30 + 0.38 + 0.18) = 0.06
Step 2. 0.38 + 0.18 + 0.06 = 0.62
cCalculate and interpret μ_{X}.   dCalculate σ_{X}.

Step 2. Σ(x − 1.84)²P(x) ≈ 1.014 → σ ≈ 1.007 cars
🔑 X = number of cars in a randomly selected household.

Raffle ticket

A raffle ticket has expected winnings of $1.50. Which is the best interpretation?
  • (A) Every ticket wins $1.50.
  • (B) Most tickets win $1.50.
  • (C) If you bought many, many tickets, you would win about $1.50 per ticket, on average.
  • (D) There is a 1.5% chance of winning.
  • (E) The median prize is $1.50.

Expected value = long-run average outcome → (C).

Most tickets may win $0 — a few big prizes pull the mean up.
