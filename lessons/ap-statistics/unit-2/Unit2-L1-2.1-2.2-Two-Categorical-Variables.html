Two-Way Table 双向表 / 列联表

Definition定义
A two-way table, also called a contingency table, summarizes and compares data for two categorical variables. Cell entries can be frequencies (counts) or relative frequencies (proportions).
双向表（列联表）用于汇总和比较两个分类变量的数据；格子里可以是频数，也可以是相对频率。
Column variable 列变量
Level ALevel BTotal
Row level 1cellcellrow total
Row level 2cellcellrow total
Totalcolumn totalcolumn totalgrand total
  • Cell 单元格: one level of each variable
  • Margins 边际: row & column totals
  • Grand total 总计: all individuals

2.1 · Key ConceptEK 2.1.A.3
Difficulty 难度 ★★★★

Association 关联

Definition定义
There is an association between two variables if knowing the value of one variable helps us predict the value of the other. If the conditional distributions are exactly the same for every group, there is no association.
如果知道一个变量的取值能帮助预测另一个变量，就说两者存在关联；如果各组条件分布完全相同，就没有关联。
No association (hypothetical) 无关联
Association (real data) 有关联
⚠️ Association does not necessarily imply causation!
Joining the club doesn't necessarily cause less snowmobile use.
关联不一定意味着因果。

Joint Relative Frequency 联合相对频率

Definition定义
A joint relative frequency is a cell frequency divided by the total for the entire table. It answers questions about both variables ("and").
联合相对频率 = 某一单元格频数 ÷ 全表总数，涉及两个变量（关键词 “and”）。
\text{Joint RF}=\frac{\text{cell count}}{\text{grand total}}
Unit 2 · Lesson 1
2.2 · Concept ②EK 2.2.A.2
Difficulty 难度 ★★★★

Marginal Relative Frequency 边际相对频率

Definition定义
A marginal relative frequency is a row total or a column total divided by the total for the entire table. It describes only one variable. All of them together form the marginal distribution.
边际相对频率 = 行总数或列总数 ÷ 全表总数，只描述一个变量；合起来叫边际分布。
\text{Marginal RF}=\frac{\text{row or column total}}{\text{grand total}}

Conditional Relative Frequency 条件相对频率

Definition定义
A conditional relative frequency is computed by restricting to one level (category) of interest — the condition. It is a cell frequency divided by its row total or its column total.
条件相对频率：限定在某一类别（条件）里再算 = 单元格 ÷ 所在行或列的总数。
\text{Conditional RF}=\frac{\text{cell count}}{\text{total of the conditioning group}}

🔑 Signal words → condition = denominator: among · of those who · given that · what percent of ___ are… “在……之中”后面就是分母。

Difficulty 难度 ★★★★

Conditional Distribution 条件分布

Definition定义
The conditional distribution of a variable describes its values among individuals who have a specific value of the other variable (the condition). Its percents add to 100%.
在另一个变量取某一特定值（条件）的个体中，该变量的分布叫条件分布，各百分比之和为 100%。
To look for association: compare conditional distributions across groups. Different → association. 比较各组的条件分布：不同 → 有关联。

2.2 · Justify a ClaimEK 2.2.C.1
Difficulty 难度 ★★★★

How to justify an association claim 如何论证“有关联”

Sentence frame 句型

Yes / No, there is / is not an association between [var 1] and [var 2] for [individuals].
[x]% of [group A] … compared with [y]% of [group B].
Because these conditional percentages are (not) very different, knowing [var 1] does (not) help predict [var 2].

Checklist ✔

  • A clear conclusion (yes / no)
  • Conditional percents from both groups
  • Context (the variables and individuals)
  • No causation language
Say "a greater percentage of club members…", not "more club members…". 要说“更高的百分比”。

2.2 · AP Exam Tips

Common mistakes 常见错误

✗ Mistake 1

Comparing counts

"More non-members own snowmobiles (279 vs 16)." Groups differ in size!

用频数比较大小不同的组。

✗ Mistake 2

Sloppy wording

Say "a greater percentage of…", not "more…".

措辞不严谨。

✗ Mistake 3

Wrong denominator

Grand total used when the question says "among club members".

分母用错。

✗ Mistake 4

Claiming causation

Survey data show association only.

把关联当成因果。

A survey of 500 students: 200 juniors and 300 seniors. Of these, 60 juniors and 150 seniors drive to school. Of the students who drive to school, what proportion are juniors?
  • (A) 0.12
  • (B) 0.286
  • (C) 0.30
  • (D) 0.42
  • (E) 0.50

Condition = "students who drive" → denominator = 60 + 150 = 210.

60 / 210 ≈ 0.286 → (B).

Every wrong choice uses a wrong denominator: A = 60/500, C = 60/200, D = 210/500, E = 150/300.

Complete the two-way table 补全双向表

120 students were asked which phone they use. Fill in the blanks, then decide: is there an association between grade and phone type?

PhoneGrade 10Grade 11Total
iPhone4580
Android25
Total60120
Step 1. Fill in: Grade 11 iPhone = 80 − 45 = 35; Grade 10 Android = 60 − 45 = 15; Android total = 15 + 25 = 40; Grade 11 total = 35 + 25 = 60.
Step 2. iPhone users: 45/60 = 75% of Grade 10 vs. 35/60 ≈ 58.3% of Grade 11.
Step 3. These differ noticeably → some association: Grade 10 students in this sample were more likely to use an iPhone.

Which statement is best supported by the data?
  • (A) Because 72 > 66, age group and daily use are not associated.
  • (B) 90% of adults under 30 use social media daily, compared with 55% of adults 30 and over, suggesting an association.
  • (C) 36% of the sample are under 30 and use daily, so there is an association.
  • (D) Being under 30 causes daily social media use.
  • (E) There is no association because 69% of all adults use social media daily.

Association needs conditional relative frequencies: 72/80 = 90% vs. 66/120 = 55% → (B).

(A) uses counts · (C) is a joint RF · (E) is a marginal RF · (D) claims causation.
