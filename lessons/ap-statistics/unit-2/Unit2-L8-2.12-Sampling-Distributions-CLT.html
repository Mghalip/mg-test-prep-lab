Do you believe in ghosts? 👻 热身：你相信有鬼吗？

The Gallup Poll asked 515 randomly selected U.S. adults whether they believe in ghosts. 160 said “Yes.”

Q1What proportion of the sample said yes?
160/515 ≈ 0.311
Q2Is that exactly the proportion of all U.S. adults who believe in ghosts?
Probably not exactly — it is an estimate from one sample.
Q3If Gallup took another random sample of 515, would they get the same proportion?
Almost certainly not! Each new sample gives a different p̂ (dots →). How much do they vary? That is today’s question.
🔑 Gallup: 160 of 515 said yes.
💡 Different samples → different statistics: sampling variability.

Parameter vs. Statistic 参数与统计量

Parameter参数
A number that describes some characteristic of the population. Usually unknown; a single fixed value.
描述总体某一特征的数，通常未知，是固定值。
Statistic统计量
A number that describes some characteristic of a sample. Its value varies from sample to sample. Also called a point estimator of the parameter.
描述样本某一特征的数，随样本变化；也叫参数的点估计量。
MeanProportionSD
Parameter (population)μpσ
Statistic (sample)x̄p̂s_{x}
🔑 statistics ← samples
parameters ← populations

Sampling Distribution 抽样分布

Sampling variability抽样变异性
Different random samples of the same size from the same population produce different values of a statistic.
从同一总体中抽取相同大小的不同随机样本，统计量的值会不同。
Definition定义
A sampling distribution of a statistic is the distribution of values of the statistic for all possible samples of a given size from a given population.
某个统计量的抽样分布：从给定总体中抽取给定大小的所有可能样本，这个统计量所有取值的分布。

John and Carol's four sons 四个儿子的身高

John and Carol have four grown sons. Their heights (inches) are 71, 75, 72, 68. Population mean μ = 71.5; population range = 7.

aList all 6 possible samples of size 2.
(71, 75) (71, 72) (71, 68) (75, 72) (75, 68) (72, 68)
bFind x̄ and the range for each sample.
x̄: 73, 71.5, 69.5, 73.5, 71.5, 70 · ranges: 4, 1, 3, 3, 7, 4
cMake a dotplot of the sampling distribution of x̄ and of the sample range.
Dotplots → x̄ centers at 71.5 = μ; the ranges center at 3.67, well below 7.
🔑 All samples of size 2 from {71, 75, 72, 68}.

Simulating a Sampling Distribution 用模拟得到抽样分布

Method方法
Repeatedly generate a large number of random samples from the population assuming known value(s) of the parameter(s). Record the statistic for each sample. The resulting distribution approximates the sampling distribution.
假设参数已知，反复生成大量随机样本，记录每个样本的统计量；这些值的分布近似于抽样分布。

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★EK 2.12.A.2 · TPS 7.1

Mrs. Lin's chips Lin 老师的筹码

A student selects an SRS of 20 chips from a large bag; the number of red chips decides homework time. She gets only 7 red.

aDescribe a simulation assuming half the chips are red (p = 0.5).
Each chip: random digit, even = red (p = 0.5). Read 20 digits = one SRS; record # red. Repeat 1000 times.
bIn the simulation, what does each dot represent?
Each dot = the number of red chips in one simulated SRS of 20, assuming p = 0.5.
cIs 7 red convincing evidence that fewer than half the chips are red?
7 or fewer red happened in about 13% of simulated SRSs — not unusual → not convincing evidence.
🔑 Assume p = 0.5; SRS of n = 20.

Randomization Distribution 随机化分布

Definition定义
A randomization distribution is the distribution of a statistic generated by simulation from repeatedly randomly reallocating (reassigning) the response values to treatment groups. It approximates the sampling distribution of the statistic.
随机化分布：把实验中的响应值反复随机重新分配到各处理组，每次记录统计量，得到的分布。它近似于该统计量的抽样分布。

📝 Example 5 · 例题★★★★EK 2.12.A.3

Does caffeine raise your pulse? 咖啡因会提高心率吗？

10 volunteers were randomly assigned: 5 drink caffeine, 5 a placebo. Increase in pulse (bpm): (illustrative)

Caffeine: 12 15 9 14 11  ·  Placebo: 6 9 4 8 10

aFind the observed difference x̄_{caffeine} − x̄_{placebo}.
12.2 − 7.4 = 4.8 bpm
bIf caffeine had no effect, how could we simulate the difference?
If caffeine has no effect, the 10 responses would be the same regardless of group → shuffle the 10 values into two groups of 5, record the difference, repeat.
cOnly 3 of the 252 possible reassignments give a difference ≥ the observed one. Conclusion?
3/252 ≈ 0.012 — very rare by chance alone → convincing evidence caffeine increases pulse.
🔑 Observed difference vs. randomization distribution.

The Central Limit Theorem (CLT) 中心极限定理

Statement定理
The central limit theorem states that the sampling distribution of a mean of a random sample has a shape that can be approximated by a normal distribution. The larger the sample, the better the approximation.
中心极限定理：随机样本的均值的抽样分布，其形状可以用正态分布近似；样本越大，近似越好（即使总体本身不是正态！）。
🔗 Galton board (Lesson 7): many small random effects add up → bell shape. A sample mean adds up n random values → bell shape too.
⚠️ The CLT is about the sampling distribution of x̄, not the population or a single sample.

📝 Example 6 · 例题★★★★EK 2.12.A.4

Predict the shape 预测形状

A population is strongly skewed to the right with mean μ = 1.

aDescribe the sampling distribution of x̄ for samples of size n = 2.
n = 2: still skewed right (a little less than the population), centered at 1.
b…for n = 30?
n = 30: approximately normal by the CLT, centered at 1, much narrower.
cDoes a larger n change the population?
No — the population stays skewed. Only the distribution of x̄ changes.
🔑 Skewed-right population, μ = 1.

Unbiased Estimator (preview of 3.1) 无偏估计量（预告 3.1）

Definition定义
A statistic used to estimate a parameter is an unbiased estimator if the mean of its sampling distribution equals the value of the parameter being estimated.
如果一个统计量的抽样分布的均值等于它所估计的参数，就称它为无偏估计量。

📝 Example 7 · 例题★★★★TPS 7.1

Biased or unbiased? 有偏还是无偏？

Use the six samples of size 2 from the four sons (μ = 71.5, population range = 7).

aIs the sample mean an unbiased estimator of μ?
Mean of the 6 sample means = 429/6 = 71.5 = μ → unbiased.
bIs the sample range an unbiased estimator of the population range?
Mean of the 6 sample ranges = 22/6 ≈ 3.67 ≠ 7 → biased (it consistently underestimates).

Bias and variability 偏差与变异性

Bias偏差
Sample statistics do not center on the parameter → not accurate.
统计量不以参数为中心 → 不准确。
High variability高变异性
Repeated samples give very different results → not precise. Larger samples → less variability.
不同样本结果差别大 → 不精确。样本越大，变异性越小。

2.12 · AP Exam Tips

Common mistakes 常见错误

✗ Mistake 1

Mixing up the three distributions

Population (individuals) ≠ one sample (individuals in it) ≠ sampling distribution (a statistic from many samples).

三种分布不要混淆。

✗ Mistake 2

"CLT makes the population normal"

No — larger samples make the sampling distribution of x̄ approximately normal.

CLT 不会改变总体分布。

✗ Mistake 3

"What does a dot represent?"

In a simulated sampling distribution, each dot is the statistic from one sample.

每个点 = 一个样本的统计量。

✗ Mistake 4

Bias from small n

Small samples cause variability, not bias.

样本小导致变异性大，不是偏差。

Blue candies 蓝色糖果（mini-FRQ）

A candy company claims 20% of its candies are blue. A student takes a random sample of 50 candies and finds only 5 blue (p̂ = 0.10). (illustrative)

aIdentify the parameter and the statistic.
Parameter: p = proportion of all the company’s candies that are blue (claimed 0.20). Statistic: p̂ = 0.10.
bIn a simulation assuming p = 0.20, what does each dot represent?
The number of blue candies in one simulated random sample of 50 candies, assuming p = 0.20.
cIs there convincing evidence the claim is wrong?
Only about 5% of simulated samples had 5 or fewer blue — fairly unusual → some evidence that fewer than 20% are blue.
🔑 Claim p = 0.20; sample of 50 had 5 blue.

Skewed incomes, n = 100

The distribution of household incomes in a city is strongly skewed to the right. Random samples of size n = 100 are taken and x̄ is computed for each. Which best describes the sampling distribution of x̄?

📝 AP Practice 11 · AP 选择题★★★★TPS 7.1

Sample size and variability

A pollster increases the sample size from 400 to 1600 (using the same random sampling method). Which statement is true?
  • (A) The bias of p̂ decreases.
  • (B) The bias of p̂ increases.
  • (C) The variability of p̂ increases.
  • (D) The variability of p̂ decreases.
  • (E) Neither bias nor variability changes.

Larger random samples → less variability → (D).

Sample size doesn’t fix (or cause) bias.
