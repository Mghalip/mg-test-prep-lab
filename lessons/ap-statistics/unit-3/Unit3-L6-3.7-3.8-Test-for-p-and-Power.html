The shooter, by formula 用公式检验罚球手

Last lesson: a player claims he makes 80% of free throws, but he made only 32 of 50 (p̂ = 0.64). Simulation gave a p-value ≈ 0.0075. 上节课用模拟得到 p 值。

Q1If p = 0.80, what are the mean and standard deviation of the sampling distribution of p̂ (n = 50)?
μ_{p̂} = 0.80, σ_{p̂} = √(0.80 × 0.20 / 50) ≈ 0.0566.
Q2How many standard deviations below 0.80 is 0.64? Use the normal model to estimate P(p̂ ≤ 0.64).
Step 1. z = (0.64 − 0.80) / 0.0566 ≈ −2.83 → 0.64 is 2.83 SDs below the claim.
Step 2. P(Z ≤ −2.83) ≈ 0.0023. That is a p-value from the normal model — very small → convincing evidence that p < 0.80.
🔑 Assume H₀: p = 0.80 is true.
💡 This standardized value is the test statistic z. Today we make this the standard procedure.

Conditions for a Test About p 比例检验的条件

Random随机
The data come from a random sample (or randomized experiment).
数据来自随机样本（或随机化实验）。
10%10% 条件
When sampling without replacement: n ≤ 0.10N.
不放回抽样时：n ≤ 0.10N。
Large Counts大计数条件
np₀ ≥ 10 and n(1 − p₀) ≥ 10 — use the null value p₀, because we assume H₀ is true.
np₀ ≥ 10 且 n(1 − p₀) ≥ 10——用零假设值 p₀，因为检验时假设 H₀ 为真。
⚠️ Interval: check x ≥ 10 and n − x ≥ 10 (we don’t know p). Test: check np₀ ≥ 10 and n(1 − p₀) ≥ 10 (we assume p = p₀). 区间用观测成功/失败数；检验用 np₀、n(1−p₀)。

Check the conditions 检验条件

Are the Large Counts and 10% conditions met for a test of H₀: p = p₀?

aPotatoes: random sample of n = 500 from a truckload of 10,000 potatoes, p₀ = 0.08.
a. 500(0.08) = 40 ≥ 10 and 500(0.92) = 460 ≥ 10 ✓; 500 ≤ 0.10 × 10,000 = 1000 ✓.

The z Statistic and the p-Value z 统计量与 p 值

Test statistic检验统计量
z = (p̂ − p₀) / √(p₀(1 − p₀)/n) — statistic minus parameter, divided by the standard deviation of the statistic (computed with p₀). If H₀ is true and conditions hold, z has approximately a standard normal distribution (the null distribution).
z = (p̂ − p₀)/√(p₀(1 − p₀)/n)：（统计量 − 参数）/ 统计量的标准差（用 p₀ 计算）。H₀ 为真且条件满足时，z 近似服从标准正态分布（零分布）。
p-value from z由 z 求 p 值
Hₐ: p > p₀ → P(Z ≥ z) · Hₐ: p < p₀ → P(Z ≤ z) · Hₐ: p ≠ p₀ → 2·P(Z ≥ |z|). Use Table A or technology (normalcdf, 1-PropZTest).
Hₐ 为 >：右尾；<：左尾；≠：两尾之和 2P(Z ≥ |z|)。用表 A 或计算器。
z=\frac{\hat p-p_0}{\sqrt{\frac{p_0(1-p_0)}{n}}}

📝 Example 3 · 例题★★★★EK 3.7 · Skill 4.G

Potatoes: should the truck go back? 土豆：退货吗？

A potato-chip producer sends a truckload back if there is convincing evidence that more than 8% of the potatoes have blemishes. The truck contains 10,000 potatoes. A supervisor takes a random sample of 500 potatoes from the truck: 47 have blemishes. 500 个中 47 个有瑕疵。

Carry out a significance test at α = 0.05. What should the producer conclude?
Step 1. State: H₀: p = 0.08, Hₐ: p > 0.08, α = 0.05, where p = the true proportion of potatoes in this shipment with blemishes.
Step 2. Plan: one-sample z-test for p. Random sample ✓; 500 < 10% of the potatoes in the truck ✓; 500(0.08) = 40 and 500(0.92) = 460 ≥ 10 ✓.
Step 3. Do: p̂ = 47/500 = 0.094; z = (0.094 − 0.08)/√(0.08·0.92/500) = 0.014/0.01213 ≈ 1.15; p-value = P(Z ≥ 1.15) ≈ 0.124.
Step 4. Conclude: 0.124 > 0.05 → fail to reject H₀. There is not convincing evidence that more than 8% of the potatoes in the shipment have blemishes. The producer should use the truckload.
🔑 State → Plan → Do → Conclude.

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★EK 3.7 · 3.6.A.1.iii

Never smoked? (two-sided) 从未吸烟？（双侧）

A national website reports that 68% of high school students have never smoked. Taeyeon takes an SRS of 150 students at his high school of 2,000 students: 90 say they have never smoked.

Is there convincing evidence at α = 0.05 that the proportion at his school differs from 0.68?
Step 1. State: H₀: p = 0.68, Hₐ: p ≠ 0.68, α = 0.05; p = true proportion of all students at Taeyeon’s school who have never smoked.
Step 2. Plan: one-sample z-test for p. SRS ✓; 150 ≤ 10% of the 2,000 students ✓; 150(0.68) = 102, 150(0.32) = 48 ≥ 10 ✓.
Step 3. Do: p̂ = 90/150 = 0.60; z = (0.60 − 0.68)/√(0.68·0.32/150) = −0.08/0.0381 ≈ −2.10; p-value = 2·P(Z ≤ −2.10) ≈ 0.0357.
Step 4. Conclude: 0.0357 < 0.05 → reject H₀. Convincing evidence that the true proportion of students at Taeyeon’s school who have never smoked differs from 0.68.
🔑 “Differs” → Hₐ: p ≠ 0.68 → two tails.

📝 Example 5 · 例题★★★★EK 3.7 · Skill 4.G

Prize in the box 盒中奖品

A cereal company claims that 20% of its boxes contain a prize. Lena suspects the true proportion is lower. She buys a random sample of 120 boxes from many stores; 16 contain a prize. 120 盒中 16 盒有奖品。

Do the data give convincing evidence at α = 0.05 that fewer than 20% of boxes contain a prize?
Step 1. State: H₀: p = 0.20, Hₐ: p < 0.20, α = 0.05; p = true proportion of all the company’s boxes that contain a prize.
Step 2. Plan: one-sample z-test for p. Random sample ✓; 120 < 10% of all boxes ✓; 120(0.20) = 24, 120(0.80) = 96 ≥ 10 ✓.
Step 3. Do: p̂ = 16/120 ≈ 0.133; z = (0.133 − 0.20)/√(0.20·0.80/120) ≈ −0.0667/0.0365 ≈ −1.83; p-value = P(Z ≤ −1.83) ≈ 0.034.
Step 4. Conclude: 0.034 < 0.05 → reject H₀. Convincing evidence that fewer than 20% of the company’s boxes contain a prize.
🔑 Hₐ: p < 0.20 → left tail.

Tests vs. Confidence Intervals 检验与区间的联系

More information区间信息更多
A test only gives a decision (reject / fail to reject). A confidence interval also gives a range of plausible values for p.
检验只给出“拒绝 / 不拒绝”的决定；置信区间还给出 p 的合理取值范围。
The link联系
A two-sided test at level α and a 100(1 − α)% confidence interval often agree, but the usual proportion interval and test use different standard errors. For matching procedures: if p₀ is outside the interval → reject H₀; if p₀ is inside → fail to reject.
方法匹配时，水平 α 的双侧检验与 100(1−α)% 置信区间对应；常用比例方法并非严格匹配：p₀ 在区间外 → 拒绝 H₀；在区间内 → 不拒绝。

Use the interval 用区间判断

For Taeyeon’s data (90 of 150), a 95% confidence interval for p is (0.522, 0.678).

aInterpret the interval.
We are 95% confident that the interval from 0.522 to 0.678 captures the true proportion of students at Taeyeon’s school who have never smoked.
bBased on the interval, would a two-sided test at α = 0.05 reject H₀: p = 0.68? H₀: p = 0.55?

🔑 Inside the interval → plausible → fail to reject.

Power 检验功效

Power功效
The power of a test is the probability that the test correctly rejects a false H₀ — computed for a specific alternative value of p.
功效：H₀ 为假时检验正确拒绝 H₀ 的概率（针对某个具体的备择值）。
Type II and power第二类错误与功效
P(Type II error) = 1 − power (sometimes called β). Ideally P(Type II) ≤ 0.20, i.e., power ≥ 0.80.
P(第二类错误) = 1 − 功效（记作 β）。一般希望 β ≤ 0.20，即功效 ≥ 0.80。

📝 Example 7 · 例题★★★★EK 3.8.A.3 · 3.8.B.2

Potatoes: is the test powerful enough? 土豆检验的功效

For the potato test (H₀: p = 0.08, Hₐ: p > 0.08, n = 500, α = 0.05), the power against the alternative p = 0.11 is about 0.76.

aInterpret this value.
If 11% of the potatoes in a shipment actually have blemishes, there is about a 0.76 probability that the test (random sample of 500, α = 0.05) will find convincing evidence that p > 0.08 — and send the truck back.
bFind the probability of a Type II error for this alternative and describe the error in context.
Step 1. P(Type II error) = 1 − 0.76 = 0.24.
Step 2. Type II error here: the shipment really has 11% blemished potatoes, but the test does not find convincing evidence that p > 0.08 — the bad truckload is used.
🔑 Power = P(reject H₀ | specific alternative true).

What Increases Power? 什么能提高功效？

Four factors四个因素
With everything else unchanged, power increases (and P(Type II) decreases) when: ① sample size n increases; ② the standard error decreases; ③ the true value of p is farther from p₀; ④ the significance level α increases.
其他条件不变时，以下任一情况功效增大：① 样本量 n 增大；② 标准误减小；③ 真实 p 离 p₀ 更远；④ 显著性水平 α 增大。
Trade-off权衡
Raising α increases power but also P(Type I). The best way to raise power without more Type I errors is a larger sample — so the cost of a Type II error helps decide n.
增大 α 提高功效，但也增加第一类错误；不增加第一类错误的最好办法是增大样本。

Which change increases power? 哪种改变能提高功效？

The potato test uses n = 500 and α = 0.05; power against p = 0.11 is 0.76. For each change (others unchanged), does the power increase or decrease?

aSample 1000 potatoes instead of 500.
a. Larger n → smaller SE → increases (≈ 0.95).
bUse α = 0.01 instead of 0.05.
b. Smaller α → harder to reject → decreases (≈ 0.55).
cFind the power against p = 0.14 instead of 0.11.

Compute the test statistic

A random sample of 150 students gives p̂ = 0.46. What is the test statistic for H₀: p = 0.40 vs. Hₐ: p > 0.40?
  • (A) (0.46 − 0.40) / √(0.46·0.54/150) ≈ 1.47
  • (B) (0.46 − 0.40) / √(0.40·0.60/150) = 1.50
  • (C) (0.46 − 0.40) / √(0.40·0.60) ≈ 0.12
  • (D) (0.40 − 0.46) / √(0.40·0.60/150) = −1.50
  • (E) (0.46 − 0.40) / (0.40·0.60/150) = 37.5

z = (p̂ − p₀)/√(p₀(1 − p₀)/n) = 0.06/√(0.0016) = 0.06/0.04 = 1.50 → (B).

(A) uses p̂ in the SD (that is for intervals); (C) forgets n; (D) reverses the order; (E) forgets the square root.

Interval and test

A 95% confidence interval obtained by inverting the specified two-sided test for the proportion of voters who support a measure is (0.47, 0.55). Which is a correct conclusion for a two-sided test of H₀: p = 0.50 at α = 0.05?

  • (B) Fail to reject H₀, because 0.50 is in the interval.
  • (C) Reject H₀, because the interval is wider than 0.05.

Interpreting power

A test of H₀: p = 0.5 vs. Hₐ: p > 0.5 has power 0.85 against p = 0.6. Which is the correct interpretation?

A district report says 25% of students bike to school. A principal suspects the proportion is lower at her school of 2,000 students. In a random sample of 150 students, 27 bike to school.

1Carry out a test at α = 0.05 (all four steps).
Step 1. State: H₀: p = 0.25, Hₐ: p < 0.25, α = 0.05; p = true proportion of students at her school who bike. Plan: one-sample z-test for p; random ✓; 150 ≤ 10% of the 2,000 students ✓; 150(0.25) = 37.5, 150(0.75) = 112.5 ≥ 10 ✓.
Step 2. Do: z = (0.18 − 0.25)/√(0.25·0.75/150) = −0.07/0.0354 ≈ −1.98; p-value = P(Z ≤ −1.98) ≈ 0.024.
Step 3. Conclude: 0.024 < 0.05 → reject H₀. Convincing evidence that fewer than 25% of students at her school bike to school.
2Name one way the principal could increase the power of her test.
Increase power: survey a larger random sample (or use a larger α, which raises P(Type I)).
🔑 p̂ = 27/150 = 0.18.

A test statistic measures signal / noise把差异换成抽样波动单位

Begin with the null model. Measure the observed difference from its prediction, then divide by the typical sampling variation under that model.

\text{Test statistic}=\frac{\text{estimate}-\text{null value}}{\text{null-model SD or estimated SE}}

Example: p₀ = 0.40, n = 100, p̂ = 0.50. The null SD is √(0.4 × 0.6/100) ≈ 0.049.

z=\frac{0.50-0.40}{0.049}\approx2.04

The estimate is about 2.04 null-model SDs above the null value. A p-value is a tail area under that model, in the direction(s) specified by Hₐ.

检验不是只看“差了多少”，而是看“相对随机波动，差得有多远”。p 值以 H₀ 为条件，不是 H₀ 为真的概率。

Intervals and tests: a precise link比例方法为何可能不一致

For matching two-sided procedures, a 100(1 − α)% confidence set contains exactly the null values not rejected at level α. But common one-proportion procedures do not use the same denominator.

\operatorname{SE}_{\text{Wald}}=\sqrt{\frac{\hat p(1-\hat p)}{n}}
\operatorname{SD}_{H_0}=\sqrt{\frac{p_0(1-p_0)}{n}}

With x = 12 and n = 50, the 95% Wald interval is about (0.1216, 0.3584). It excludes p₀ = 0.36. The z test gives z ≈ −1.768 and a two-sided p ≈ 0.0771: fail to reject at 0.05.

区间和检验的精确对应要求方法匹配。比例题接近临界值时，应按指定检验计算，不能只看普通 Wald 区间。
