“I’m an 80% shooter!” “我罚球命中率 80%！”

A basketball player claims he makes 80% of his free throws. We think he might be exaggerating. He shoots 50 free throws and makes only 32 (p̂ = 0.64). 50 投 32 中。

Q1Give two possible explanations for p̂ < 0.80.
① The claim is true (p = 0.80) and he had a bad day — chance variation. ② The claim is false (p < 0.80).
Q2How could we decide which explanation is more believable?
Step 1. Simulate 400 sets of 50 shots by an 80% shooter (graph). Only 3 of 400 sets had 32 or fewer makes.
Step 2. An 80% shooter would almost never do this badly → convincing evidence that he makes fewer than 80% of his free throws.
🔑 Assume the claim is true, then ask: how surprising is 32/50?
💡 That probability (3/400 ≈ 0.0075) is called a p-value. That is the whole idea of a significance test!

Null and Alternative Hypotheses 零假设与备择假设

Hypothesis test假设检验
A hypothesis (significance) test is a procedure for using sample data to make a decision about the value of a population parameter.
假设检验：用样本数据对总体参数的取值做出决策。
H₀ · Hₐ零假设 · 备择假设
The null hypothesis H₀ is the status quo — assumed correct unless there is convincing evidence otherwise; it contains equality: H₀: p = p₀. The alternative hypothesis Hₐ is the claim we are seeking evidence for: p < p₀, p > p₀ (one-sided), or p ≠ p₀ (two-sided).
零假设 H₀：现状，除非有充分证据否则认为成立，含等号：p = p₀。备择假设 Hₐ：研究者想找证据支持的说法：p < p₀、p > p₀（单侧）或 p ≠ p₀（双侧）。
⚠️ Hypotheses are about the population parameter (p), never the statistic (p̂). Write them before looking at the data. Define p in context: “p = the true proportion of …”. 假设关于参数，不关于样本统计量；在看数据前写。

📝 Example 2 · 例题★★★★EK 3.5.B · Skill 2.E

Write the hypotheses 写出假设

Define the parameter and state H₀ and Hₐ.

aA website says 68% of high school students have never smoked. Taeyeon wonders whether this is different at his school.
a. p = true proportion of all students at Taeyeon’s school who have never smoked. H₀: p = 0.68, Hₐ: p ≠ 0.68 (two-sided).
bA potato-chip producer sends a truck back if there is convincing evidence that more than 8% of the potatoes have blemishes.
b. p = true proportion of potatoes in the shipment with blemishes. H₀: p = 0.08, Hₐ: p > 0.08.
cA company claims that at most 3% of its phones are defective. A consumer group suspects the rate is higher.
c. p = true proportion of the company’s phones that are defective. H₀: p = 0.03 (tested at the boundary), Hₐ: p > 0.03.
dA teacher wonders if fewer than half of students eat breakfast.
d. p = true proportion of all students at the school who eat breakfast. H₀: p = 0.5, Hₐ: p < 0.5.
🔑 Look for “different”, “more than”, “less than”.

Spot the mistake 找错误

Each set of hypotheses has a mistake. Circle it and fix it.

1H₀: p̂ = 0.4, Hₐ: p̂ > 0.4
1. Uses the statistic p̂ — must use the parameter p.
2H₀: p > 0.25, Hₐ: p = 0.25
2. Reversed — equality belongs in H₀: H₀: p = 0.25, Hₐ: p > 0.25.
3H₀: p = 0.6, Hₐ: p ≥ 0.6
3. Hₐ must be strict: Hₐ: p > 0.6 (or ≠ / <).
4A sample of 200 gives p̂ = 0.58, so we test H₀: p = 0.58, Hₐ: p ≠ 0.58.
4. The null value must come from the claim, not from the data.
🔑 Hypotheses: parameter, equality in H₀, strict inequality in Hₐ.

The p-Value p 值

Null distribution零分布
Assuming H₀ is true, the test statistic has a probability distribution called the null distribution (simulated or a normal model).
假设 H₀ 为真时检验统计量的分布叫零分布（可模拟或用正态模型）。
p-valuep 值
The p-value is the probability of getting a statistic as extreme as or more extreme than the observed one (in the direction of Hₐ), assuming H₀ is true. For ≠: count both tails.
p 值：假设 H₀ 为真，得到与观测值一样极端或更极端（按 Hₐ 方向）的统计量的概率。双侧检验算两尾。
Small vs. large小与大
Small p-value → the result would be unusual if H₀ were true → evidence for Hₐ. Not small → not unusual → no convincing evidence for Hₐ — and no evidence that H₀ is true.
p 值小 → 若 H₀ 为真结果很少见 → 支持 Hₐ 的证据。p 值不小 → 没有充分证据支持 Hₐ，也不是 H₀ 为真的证据。

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★EK 3.6.A.3

Interpret the p-value 解释 p 值

For the free-throw shooter (H₀: p = 0.80, Hₐ: p < 0.80), the p-value is about 0.0075.

aInterpret the p-value.
“Assuming the player really makes 80% of his free throws in the long run, there is about a 0.0075 probability of making 32 or fewer of 50 shots just by chance.”
bA student writes: “There is a 0.0075 probability that the player is an 80% shooter.” What is wrong?
The p-value is not the probability that H₀ is true. It is calculated assuming H₀ is true: P(data this extreme | H₀), not P(H₀ | data).
🔑 The p-value assumes H₀ is true.
💡 p-value = P(result this extreme or more | H₀ true).

📝 Example 5 · 例题★★★★EK 3.6.A.1.iii · 3.6.A.2.iii

Two-sided p-value 双侧 p 值

Test H₀: p = 0.5 vs. Hₐ: p ≠ 0.5 with a random sample of 40 people. The dotplot shows p̂ for 200 simulated samples assuming p = 0.5. The observed p̂ = 0.65.

Which simulated values count toward the p-value? Estimate the p-value.
Step 1. 0.65 is 0.15 above 0.5, so values ≥ 0.65 and values ≤ 0.35 are at least as extreme.
Step 2. Count both tails in the simulation (pink) and divide by 200.
Step 3. p-value ≈ (count in both tails) ÷ 200 = 10/200 ≈ 0.05 — borderline, not very small.
🔑 ≠ → count BOTH tails: as far from 0.5 as 0.65 is.

Significance Level and Decisions 显著性水平与决策

Significance level α显著性水平 α
The significance level α is the predetermined probability of rejecting H₀ when it is true (often 0.05). Set it before collecting data.
显著性水平 α：H₀ 为真时拒绝 H₀ 的事先设定的概率（常用 0.05），在收集数据前确定。
Decision决策
p-value ≤ α → reject H₀: convincing evidence for Hₐ (statistically significant). p-value > α → fail to reject H₀: not convincing evidence for Hₐ. We never “accept” or “prove” H₀. Conclude in context, in terms of Hₐ.
p ≤ α → 拒绝 H₀，有充分证据支持 Hₐ（统计显著）。p > α → 不拒绝 H₀。永远不说“接受”或“证明”H₀；结论结合语境、用 Hₐ 的语言。
“Because the p-value of 0.0075 < α = 0.05, we reject H₀. There is convincing evidence that the true proportion of free throws the player makes is less than 0.80.”

📝 Example 6 · 例题★★★★EK 3.7.B

Reject or fail to reject? 拒绝还是不拒绝？

Write a decision and conclusion for each.

aPotatoes: H₀: p = 0.08, Hₐ: p > 0.08, α = 0.05, p-value = 0.124.
a. 0.124 > 0.05 → fail to reject H₀. There is not convincing evidence that more than 8% of the potatoes in the shipment have blemishes. (The truck is accepted.)
bTaeyeon: H₀: p = 0.68, Hₐ: p ≠ 0.68, α = 0.05, p-value = 0.036.
b. 0.036 ≤ 0.05 → reject H₀. There is convincing evidence that the true proportion of students at Taeyeon’s school who have never smoked differs from 0.68.
cSame as (b) but α = 0.01.
c. 0.036 > 0.01 → fail to reject H₀ — the same data give a different decision at a stricter α.
🔑 p ≤ α → reject; p > α → fail to reject.

Type I and Type II Errors 第一类与第二类错误

Type I error第一类错误
Finding convincing evidence that Hₐ is true (rejecting H₀) when it isn’t — a “false alarm”. P(Type I error) = α.
H₀ 实际为真却拒绝了它（“误报”）。P(第一类错误) = α。
Type II error第二类错误
Not finding convincing evidence that Hₐ is true (failing to reject H₀) when it is — a “missed detection”.
Hₐ 实际为真却没有拒绝 H₀（“漏报”）。
Decision ↓ · Truth →H₀ trueHₐ true
Reject H₀Type I error (prob. α)✓ correct (power)
Fail to reject H₀✓ correctType II error
Unit 3 · Lesson 5
📝 Example 7 · 例题★★★★EK 3.8.A · 3.8.D

Potato errors 土豆检验的两类错误

Potato-chip producer: H₀: p = 0.08, Hₐ: p > 0.08, where p = the true proportion of potatoes in the truckload with blemishes. If H₀ is rejected, the truck is sent back.

Describe a Type I error and a Type II error in context, and give a possible consequence of each.
Step 1. Type I: the true proportion is 0.08, but the producer finds convincing evidence that it is more than 0.08.
Step 2. Consequence: a good truckload is sent back — wasted time, lost money for the supplier.
Step 3. Type II: the true proportion is greater than 0.08, but the producer does not find convincing evidence of this.
Step 4. Consequence: chips are made with too many blemished potatoes — unhappy customers, lower sales.
🔑 Type I: reject a true H₀. Type II: fail to reject a false H₀.

Difficulty 难度 ★★★★

Choosing α: Consequences Matter 根据后果选择 α

Which error is worse?哪种错误更严重？
In some studies a Type I error is more serious; in others a Type II error is. Consider the consequences before the study. If a Type I error is very costly → choose a smaller α (e.g., 0.01). If a Type II error is very costly → a larger α (e.g., 0.10) or a larger sample.
有时第一类错误更严重，有时第二类更严重。研究前考虑后果：第一类错误代价大 → 选更小的 α；第二类错误代价大 → 选较大的 α 或增大样本。

📝 Example 8 · 例题★★★★EK 3.8.D · Skill 4.D

Which error is worse? 哪种错误更糟？

A new rapid test is used at an airport. H₀: the passenger does not carry a dangerous item; Hₐ: the passenger carries a dangerous item. Rejecting H₀ means the bag is searched by hand.

aDescribe a Type I and a Type II error.
Type I: a safe passenger’s bag is searched (delay, annoyance). Type II: a dangerous item is not detected.
bWhich is more serious? Should α be large or small?
Step 1. A Type II error is far more serious here.
Step 2. To make Type II errors less likely, use a larger α (search more bags) — accepting more false alarms.

Meaning of the p-value

A test of H₀: p = 0.30 vs. Hₐ: p > 0.30 gives p̂ = 0.36 and a p-value of 0.04. Which is the correct interpretation of the p-value?
  • (A) There is a 4% chance that H₀ is true.
  • (B) There is a 4% chance that p = 0.36.
  • (C) Assuming p = 0.30, there is a 4% chance of getting a sample proportion of 0.36 or more by chance.
  • (D) There is a 96% chance that Hₐ is true.
  • (E) 4% of samples will give p̂ = 0.36.

The p-value is computed assuming H₀ is true and refers to results at least as extreme as observed → (C).

(A) and (D) treat the p-value as the probability of a hypothesis — a classic error.

📝 AP Practice 10 · AP 选择题★★★★EK 3.7.B.2–4

Fail to reject

A test at α = 0.05 gives a p-value of 0.21. Which conclusion is correct?
  • (A) Accept H₀; the null hypothesis is true.
  • (B) Reject H₀; there is convincing evidence for Hₐ.
  • (C) Fail to reject H₀; there is not convincing evidence for Hₐ.
  • (D) Fail to reject H₀; we have proved H₀ is true.
  • (E) The result is statistically significant.

p-value > α → fail to reject H₀ → not convincing evidence for Hₐ → (C).

Lack of evidence for Hₐ is not evidence for H₀ — (A) and (D) are wrong.

Type I error

A school tests H₀: p = 0.60 vs. Hₐ: p < 0.60, where p is the proportion of all students who are satisfied with lunch. A Type I error would occur if the school
  • (A) concludes that fewer than 60% are satisfied when actually 60% are
  • (B) concludes that fewer than 60% are satisfied when that is true
  • (C) fails to conclude that fewer than 60% are satisfied when fewer than 60% are
  • (D) fails to conclude that fewer than 60% are satisfied when actually 60% are

Reject H₀ (conclude p < 0.60) when H₀ is true (p = 0.60) → (A).

(C) is a Type II error; (B) and (D) are correct decisions.

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

A coin-flipping app claims its virtual coin is fair. Mia suspects it lands heads more than half the time. In 200 flips she gets 114 heads; the p-value is 0.024. Use α = 0.05.

1State the hypotheses (define p).
p = true proportion of all flips of the app’s coin that land heads. H₀: p = 0.5, Hₐ: p > 0.5.
2Interpret the p-value, then give a decision and conclusion.
Assuming the coin is fair, there is a 0.024 probability of getting 57% or more heads in 200 flips by chance. 0.024 ≤ 0.05 → reject H₀: convincing evidence that the coin lands heads more than half the time.
3Which error could Mia have made? Describe it in context.
She rejected H₀, so she could have made a Type I error: the coin is actually fair, but she found convincing evidence that it favors heads.
🔑 p̂ = 114/200 = 0.57.
