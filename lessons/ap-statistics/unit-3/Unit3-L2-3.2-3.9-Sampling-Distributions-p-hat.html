📝 Warm-up · 例题★★★★Warm-up

What’s a typical sample? 什么样的样本结果算“正常”？

Suppose exactly 50% of the students at a large school like the new cafeteria menu. You survey a random sample of 20 students. 全校 50% 喜欢新菜单，随机调查 20 人。

Q1Would you be surprised if p̂ = 0.60? p̂ = 0.85?
With n = 20, p̂ = 0.60 (12 of 20) happens often — not surprising. p̂ = 0.85 (17 of 20) is very rare.
Q2What would change if you surveyed 200 students instead?
Step 1. With n = 200, the sample proportions cluster much more tightly around 0.5 — even 0.60 becomes unusual.
Step 2. To decide what is “surprising”, we need the sampling distribution of p̂: its center, spread, and shape.
🔑 p = 0.5, n = 20 vs. n = 200.

Shape, Center, and Variability of p̂ p̂ 的形状、中心与变异

Center中心
The mean of the sampling distribution of p̂ equals the population proportion p — p̂ is an unbiased estimator of p.
p̂ 抽样分布的均值等于 p —— p̂ 是 p 的无偏估计量。
Variability变异
The SD is largest when p is near 0.5 and gets smaller as n gets larger: multiplying the sample size by 4 cuts the SD in half.
p 接近 0.5 时标准差最大；n 越大标准差越小：样本量乘 4，标准差减半。
Shape形状
When n is small and p is close to 0 (or 1), the distribution is skewed right (or left). It becomes more normal when p is closer to 0.5 or n is larger.
n 小且 p 接近 0（或 1）时右偏（或左偏）；p 接近 0.5 或 n 变大时越来越接近正态。

The Sampling Distribution of p̂ p̂ 的抽样分布

Mean & SD均值与标准差
For a population with proportion p, when sampled values are independent: μ_{p̂} = p and σ_{p̂} = √(p(1 − p)/n).
若样本观测相互独立：μ_{p̂} = p，σ_{p̂} = √(p(1−p)/n)。
Conditions条件
Randomization: a random sample. 10% (when sampling without replacement): n ≤ 10% of N — so the SD formula is about right. Large Counts: np ≥ 10 and n(1 − p) ≥ 10 — the expected numbers of successes and failures — so the distribution is approximately normal.
随机：随机样本。10%（无放回）：n ≤ 10%N，保证标准差公式近似正确。大计数：np ≥ 10 且 n(1−p) ≥ 10（期望成功、失败数），保证近似正态。
\mu_{\hat p}=p\qquad\sigma_{\hat p}=\sqrt{\frac{p(1-p)}{n}}
np ≥ 10 · n(1 − p) ≥ 10 · n ≤ 0.10N

📝 Example 2 · 例题★★★★EK 3.2.A–C

Backpacks 书包

Suppose 84% of students at a large high school regularly use a backpack. Take an SRS of 100 students; p̂ = proportion in the sample who use a backpack.

aIdentify the mean of the sampling distribution of p̂.
μ_{p̂} = p = 0.84.
bCalculate and interpret the standard deviation. Verify the 10% condition.
Step 1. 100 is less than 10% of all students at a large school → σ_{p̂} = √(0.84 × 0.16 / 100) ≈ 0.0367.
Step 2. Interpretation: in SRSs of 100 students, the sample proportion who use a backpack will typically vary by about 0.0367 from the true proportion 0.84.
cDescribe the shape. Justify.
Large Counts: 100(0.84) = 84 ≥ 10 and 100(0.16) = 16 ≥ 10 → approximately normal.
🔑 p = 0.84, n = 100.

📝 Example 3 · 例题★★★★EK 3.2.B · Skill 4.E

Which condition fails? 哪个条件不满足？

For each situation, decide whether we can use the normal model with σ_{p̂} = √(p(1−p)/n). If not, name the condition that fails.

aA school has 600 students; 30% walk to school. SRS of 80 students.
a. 10%: 80 > 10% of 600 = 60 → fails (the SD formula overstates the variability).
b3% of all phones from a factory are defective. SRS of 200 phones.
b. Large Counts: np = 200(0.03) = 6 < 10 → fails (distribution skewed right).
c45% of a city’s 50,000 voters support a bond. SRS of 400 voters.
c. Random ✓; 400 ≤ 5,000 ✓; 400(0.45) = 180 and 400(0.55) = 220 ✓ → normal model OK.
dA teacher surveys the 30 students in her first-period class about a city with p = 0.5.
d. Not a random sample → randomization fails (and can’t generalize).
🔑 Check: random? 10%? Large Counts?

Interpreting & Using the Normal Model 解释与正态概率

Interpret in context结合语境解释
The mean, SD, and probabilities for the sampling distribution of p̂ should be interpreted in the context of a specific population.
p̂ 抽样分布的均值、标准差和概率都要结合具体总体的语境来解释。
Normal probabilities正态概率
If the Large Counts condition is met, use N(p, σ_{p̂}): standardize z = (p̂ − p) ÷ σ_{p̂}, or use normalcdf(lower, upper, p, σ_{p̂}).
若满足大计数条件，用 N(p, σ_{p̂})：标准化或用 normalcdf。
SD: “In SRSs of size n, the sample proportion of context typically varies by about σ from the true proportion p.”

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★EK 3.2.C.1

Within 2 percentage points? 误差在 2 个百分点以内的概率

A polling organization asks an SRS of 1,500 first-year college students how far away their home is. Suppose 35% of all first-year students attend college within 50 miles of home.

Find the probability that the sample will give a result within 2 percentage points of the true value.
Step 1. 10%: 1,500 < 10% of all first-year students → σ_{p̂} = √(0.35 × 0.65 / 1500) ≈ 0.0123.
Step 2. Large Counts: 1500(0.35) = 525 ≥ 10 and 1500(0.65) = 975 ≥ 10 → approximately normal.
Step 3. z = ±0.02 ÷ 0.0123 ≈ ±1.62 → P(−1.62 ≤ z ≤ 1.62) ≈ 0.896. (normalcdf(0.33, 0.37, 0.35, 0.0123))
Step 4. About 90% of all such SRSs give a p̂ within 2 points of the truth.
🔑 P(0.33 ≤ p̂ ≤ 0.37).

📝 Example 5 · 例题★★★★EK 3.2.C · Skill 4.D

Is 75% unusual? 75% 不寻常吗？

The school claims that 84% of its students use a backpack. In an SRS of 100 students, only 75 use one.

aIf the claim is true, find P(p̂ ≤ 0.75).
z = (0.75 − 0.84) ÷ 0.0367 ≈ −2.45 → P(p̂ ≤ 0.75) ≈ 0.007.
bDoes this sample give convincing evidence that the claim is too high?
Step 1. If 84% really used backpacks, a sample proportion this low would happen only about 7 times in 1,000 samples.
Step 2. That is very unusual → convincing evidence that fewer than 84% use backpacks. (Preview of a significance test!)
🔑 Use μ = 0.84, σ ≈ 0.0367 (from the backpack example).

Difficulty 难度 ★★★★

The Sampling Distribution of p̂₁ − p̂₂ p̂₁ − p̂₂ 的抽样分布

Mean & SD均值与标准差
For two independent populations with proportions p₁ and p₂ (independent sampled values): μ = p₁ − p₂ and σ = √(p₁(1 − p₁)/n₁ + p₂(1 − p₂)/n₂). (Variances add!)
两个独立总体：均值 = p₁ − p₂；标准差 = √(p₁(1−p₁)/n₁ + p₂(1−p₂)/n₂)（方差相加）。
Conditions条件
Randomization: two independent random samples (or, in an experiment, treatments randomly assigned — that is the only condition needed then). 10%: n₁ ≤ 10%N₁ and n₂ ≤ 10%N₂. Large Counts: n₁p₁, n₁(1−p₁), n₂p₂, n₂(1−p₂) all ≥ 10.
随机：两个独立随机样本（实验中则只需随机分配）。10%：各样本 ≤ 各自总体的 10%。大计数：四个期望次数都 ≥ 10。

Formula · 公式

The Sampling Distribution of p̂₁ − p̂₂ p̂₁ − p̂₂ 的抽样分布

\mu_{\hat p_1-\hat p_2}=p_1-p_2\qquad\sigma_{\hat p_1-\hat p_2}=\sqrt{\frac{p_1(1-p_1)}{n_1}+\frac{p_2(1-p_2)}{n_2}}

Read the numerator and denominator separately, then identify each parameter or statistic in context. 先看分子和分母，再说明各符号的含义。

📝 Example 6 · 例题★★★★EK 3.9.A · 3.9.C

Homework at two schools 两所学校的作业完成率

At School 1, 70% of students did their homework last night; at School 2, only 50% did. Counselor 1 takes an SRS of 100 students; counselor 2 takes an independent SRS of 200. Both schools are large.

aFind the mean and SD of the sampling distribution of p̂₁ − p̂₂.
Step 1. μ = 0.70 − 0.50 = 0.20.
Step 2. σ = √(0.70·0.30/100 + 0.50·0.50/200) = √(0.0021 + 0.00125) ≈ 0.058 (both samples < 10% of large schools).
bInterpret the SD in context.
The difference (School 1 − School 2) in sample proportions who did their homework typically varies by about 0.058 from the true difference of 0.20.
🔑 p₁ = 0.70, n₁ = 100; p₂ = 0.50, n₂ = 200.

📝 Example 7 · 例题★★★★EK 3.9.A–C

Goldfish crackers 金鱼饼干

Bag 1 has 25% red goldfish crackers and Bag 2 has 35% red. Each bag contains more than 1,000 crackers. The teacher takes an SRS of 50 from Bag 1 and a separate SRS of 40 from Bag 2. Let p̂₁ − p̂₂ be the difference in sample proportions of red crackers.

aWhat is the shape of the sampling distribution? Why?
Large Counts: 50(0.25) = 12.5, 50(0.75) = 37.5, 40(0.35) = 14, 40(0.65) = 26 — all ≥ 10 → approximately normal.
bFind its mean and SD.
μ = 0.25 − 0.35 = −0.10; σ = √(0.25·0.75/50 + 0.35·0.65/40) ≈ 0.0971 (50 and 40 are < 10% of each bag).
cEstimate the probability that the proportion of red crackers in the sample from Bag 1 is greater than in the sample from Bag 2.
Step 1. P(p̂₁ − p̂₂ > 0): z = (0 − (−0.10)) ÷ 0.0971 ≈ 1.03 → P(z > 1.03) ≈ 0.15.
Step 2. About 15% of the time, the Bag 1 sample would have the higher proportion of red crackers — even though Bag 2 really has more red.
🔑 p̂₁ > p̂₂ ⇔ p̂₁ − p̂₂ > 0.

Samples or an experiment? 抽样还是实验？

📋 Two independent random samples

Randomization: two independent random samples.
10%: n₁ ≤ 10%N₁ and n₂ ≤ 10%N₂.
Large Counts: all four expected counts ≥ 10.

两个独立随机样本：随机、10%、大计数。

🧪 Randomized experiment

Randomization: treatments randomly assigned to the units. That’s the only randomization condition needed — no 10% condition (we aren’t sampling from a population).
Large Counts still needed for normality.

随机分配处理即可，不需要 10% 条件；仍需大计数条件。

⚠️ Write conditions in context with numbers: “50(0.25) = 12.5 ≥ 10 …”, not just “Large Counts ✓”. 条件要结合语境写出具体数字。

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

In a large city, 22% of adults do not own a car. Take an SRS of 300 adults.

1Find the mean and SD of the sampling distribution of p̂. Check the 10% condition.

Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
3 min · derivation optional

A proportion is an averagep̂ 的标准差从哪里来

With independent Bernoulli trials, p̂ = X/n, where X is the number of successes. Multiplying a random variable by c multiplies its variance by c².

\operatorname E(\hat p)=\frac{\operatorname E(X)}{n}=p
\operatorname{Var}(\hat p)=\frac{\operatorname{Var}(X)}{n^2}=\frac{np(1-p)}{n^2}=\frac{p(1-p)}{n}
\operatorname{SD}(\hat p)=\sqrt{\frac{p(1-p)}{n}}

This is the exact SD in the independent-trial model. Large Counts is needed for a normal approximation, not for this algebra.

先有抽样波动的大小，再判断能否用正态曲线近似。无放回抽样还要考虑独立性近似。

Check your understanding · Why does n × 4 halve the SD?

The denominator is √n, so √(4n) = 2√n.
