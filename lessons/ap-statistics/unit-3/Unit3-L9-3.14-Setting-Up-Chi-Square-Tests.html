Wine ↓ · Music →NoneFrenchItalianTotal
French30393099
Italian1111931
Other433535113

Q1What percent of bottles were French wine under each type of music?
French wine: none 30/84 ≈ 35.7%, French music 39/75 = 52%, Italian music 30/84 ≈ 35.7%.

Two Chi-Square Tests for Two-Way Tables 双向表的两种卡方检验

Homogeneity同质性检验
Several independent random samples (or treatment groups in an experiment), one categorical variable. Question: is the distribution of the variable the same in every population / treatment?
多个独立随机样本（或实验的多个处理组），一个分类变量：各总体/处理中该变量的分布是否相同？
Independence独立性检验
One random sample, two categorical variables recorded for each individual. Question: is there an association between the two variables in the population?
一个随机样本，每个个体记录两个分类变量：两变量在总体中是否有关联？
🔑 Ask: How many samples? Several samples or groups → homogeneity. One sample, classified two ways → independence. The calculations are identical. 数样本个数。

Hypotheses for Chi-Square Tests 卡方检验的假设

Homogeneity同质性
H₀: There is no difference in the distribution of (variable) among (populations / treatments). Hₐ: There is a difference in the distribution of (variable) among (populations / treatments).
H₀：各总体/处理中（变量）的分布没有差异；Hₐ：有差异。
Independence独立性
H₀: There is no association between (variable 1) and (variable 2) in (population). Hₐ: There is an association between (variable 1) and (variable 2) in (population).
H₀：总体中两变量没有关联（独立）；Hₐ：有关联。
No direction没有方向
Hₐ is always “a difference / an association” — there is no one-sided version. Hypotheses are written in words, not symbols.
Hₐ 只写“有差异/有关联”，没有单侧；一般用文字写假设。

Expected Counts 期望频数

Idea思路
If H₀ is true, every group should have the same distribution as the whole table. E.g., 99 of 243 bottles (40.7%) were French wine, so we expect 40.7% of the 84 “no music” bottles to be French: 84 × 99/243 ≈ 34.22.
若 H₀ 为真，每组的分布都应与整体相同。如 99/243 的酒是法国酒，则 84 瓶“无音乐”中预计 84×99/243 ≈ 34.22 瓶。
Formula公式
Expected count = (row total)(column total) / (table total). Expected counts need not be whole numbers — don’t round them.
期望频数 = 行合计 × 列合计 / 总数。期望频数可以不是整数，不要取整。
E=\frac{\text{row total}\times\text{column total}}{\text{table total}}

The Chi-Square Statistic 卡方统计量

χ²卡方统计量
χ² = Σ (Observed − Expected)² / Expected, summed over all cells (not the totals). It measures how far the observed counts are from what H₀ predicts.
χ² = Σ(观测 − 期望)²/期望，对所有格子（不含合计）求和；衡量观测值与 H₀ 预测的差距。
Reading χ²读懂 χ²
χ² ≥ 0. A small χ² → data close to H₀. A large χ² → evidence against H₀. Each term is a component; big components show where the differences are.
χ² ≥ 0。χ² 小 → 数据接近 H₀；χ² 大 → 反对 H₀ 的证据。每一项叫一个分量，大的分量说明差异在哪里。
\chi^2=\sum\frac{(O-E)^2}{E}

Chi-Square Distributions and Conditions 卡方分布与条件

χ² distributions卡方分布
If H₀ is true (and conditions are met), χ² follows approximately a chi-square distribution with df = (r − 1)(c − 1). Right-skewed, nonnegative values, mean = df; becomes more symmetric as df increases.
H₀ 为真时 χ² 近似服从卡方分布，df = (行数 − 1)(列数 − 1)；右偏、取非负值、均值 = df，df 越大越对称。
Conditions条件
Random: random samples / random assignment. 10%: n ≤ 10% of each population when sampling without replacement. Large Counts: all expected counts > 5 (CED: “greater than 5”).
随机；10%；大计数：所有期望频数 > 5。

Check the conditions; find df 检验条件，求自由度

For each study, check the Large Counts condition and find df.

aMusic and wine (3 × 3 table; smallest expected count 9.57).
a. All expected counts ≥ 9.57 > 5 ✓. df = (3 − 1)(3 − 1) = 4.
bAnger and heart disease (2 × 3 table; expected counts 69.73, 106.08, 14.19, 3040.27, 4624.92, 618.81).
b. All expected counts ≥ 14.19 > 5 ✓. df = (2 − 1)(3 − 1) = 2.
cA random sample of 40 students: left- or right-handed × plays an instrument (yes / no). 8 are left-handed and 12 play an instrument.
c. Expected (left, plays) = 8 × 12 / 40 = 2.4 < 5 ✗ — the χ² test is not appropriate. df would be (2 − 1)(2 − 1) = 1.
🔑 Large Counts: all EXPECTED counts > 5.

Why (rows − 1)(columns − 1)?列联表的自由度

Hold both row totals and column totals fixed. In this 2 × 3 table, choose a and b. Every remaining cell is forced.

C1C2C3Total
R1ab40 − a − b40
R230 − a20 − b10 + a + b60
Total302050100
\mathrm{df}=(2-1)(3-1)=2

In an R × C table, choose the upper-left (R − 1) × (C − 1) cells. The last row and column follow from the margins; counts must remain nonnegative.

行列合计固定后，剩余格子不是都能自由填。这解释了检验中偏离独立模型的独立方向数。

Check your understanding · a = 12, b = 8: complete the table.

First row: 12, 8, 20. Second row: 18, 12, 30.

Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
3 min · derivation optional

Expected counts under independence独立模型如何产生 E

Under independence, the probability of belonging to both categories is the product of their marginal probabilities. Estimate those probabilities using the table margins.

P(R_i\cap C_j)=P(R_i)P(C_j)
E_{ij}=N\cdot\frac{\text{row total}}{N}\cdot\frac{\text{column total}}{N}
E_{ij}=\frac{\text{row total}\times\text{column total}}{N}

For a row total of 40 and column total of 30 out of 100, the expected count is 12. This is a model-based reference, not a required observed count.

期望频数来自“假设独立时应出现多少”；卡方统计量再衡量实际频数偏离它有多远。

Check your understanding · Can E be a decimal?

Yes. An expected count need not be an integer, even though an observed count is.
