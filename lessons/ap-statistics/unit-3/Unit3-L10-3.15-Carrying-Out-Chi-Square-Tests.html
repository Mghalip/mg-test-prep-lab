p-Values and Conclusions for χ² Tests 卡方检验的 p 值与结论

p-valuep 值
p-value = P(χ² ≥ observed χ²) — always the right tail of the χ² distribution with df = (r − 1)(c − 1). Calculator: χ²cdf(χ², 1E99, df) or STAT → TESTS → C:χ²-Test (enter the observed table in a matrix).
p 值 = P(χ² ≥ 观测 χ²)，永远是卡方分布的右尾。计算器：χ²cdf(χ², 1E99, df) 或 χ²-Test。
Conclusion结论
p ≤ α → reject H₀: convincing evidence of a difference in distributions (homogeneity) / an association (independence). p > α → fail to reject H₀: not convincing evidence. Never “prove” H₀.
p ≤ α → 拒绝 H₀：有充分证据表明分布有差异 / 变量有关联。p > α → 不拒绝 H₀。
“Because the p-value of ___ < α = ___, we reject H₀. There is convincing evidence of a difference in the distribution of (variable) among (groups) / an association between (var 1) and (var 2) in (population).”

Music and wine: the test 音乐与葡萄酒：检验

Music played on randomly chosen days (none / French / Italian) and wine purchased (French / Italian / other), 243 bottles. Expected counts are all ≥ 9.57.

Wine ↓ · Music →NoneFrenchItalianTotal
French30393099
Italian1111931
Other433535113
Total847584243
Carry out a test at α = 0.05: does the type of music affect what wine customers buy?
Step 1. State: H₀: There is no difference in the distribution of wine purchases among no music, French music, and Italian music. Hₐ: There is a difference. α = 0.05.
Step 2. Plan: χ² test for homogeneity. Random: music was randomly assigned to days ✓. Large Counts: all expected counts ≥ 9.57 > 5 ✓.
Step 3. Do: χ² = (30 − 34.22)²/34.22 + (39 − 30.56)²/30.56 + ⋯ + (35 − 39.06)²/39.06 ≈ 18.28; df = (3 − 1)(3 − 1) = 4; p-value = P(χ² ≥ 18.28) ≈ 0.0011: assuming the music has no effect, there is about a 0.0011 probability of getting a χ² of 18.28 or larger by chance.
Step 4. Conclude: 0.0011 < 0.05 → reject H₀. Convincing evidence that the type of music played affects the distribution of wine purchases (random assignment → cause and effect, for customers at this store).
🔑 Homogeneity (three treatments, one variable).

Follow-Up Analysis 后续分析

Where is the difference?差异在哪里？

拒绝 H₀ 后，找分量最大的格子，说明观测值比期望值多还是少。
Wine study葡萄酒研究
Italian wine with French music: observed 1 vs. expected 9.57 (component 7.67) — far fewer. Italian wine with Italian music: 19 vs. 10.72 (6.40) — more. French wine with French music: 39 vs. 30.56 (2.33) — more.

📝 Example 3 · 例题★★★★Topic 3.15 · Skill 4.G

Anger and heart disease 愤怒与心脏病

A random sample of 8474 adults was classified by anger level and whether they developed coronary heart disease (CHD).

CHD ↓ · Anger →LowModerateHighTotal
CHD5311027190
No CHD305746216068284
Total311047316338474
aCarry out a test at α = 0.05 (expected counts: 69.73, 106.08, 14.19, 3040.27, 4624.92, 618.81).
Step 1. State: H₀: no association between anger level and CHD among adults like these; Hₐ: there is an association; α = 0.05. Plan: χ² test for independence; random sample ✓; 8474 < 10% of all adults ✓; all expected counts ≥ 14.19 ✓.
Step 2. Do: χ² = (53 − 69.73)²/69.73 + ⋯ ≈ 16.08, df = (2 − 1)(3 − 1) = 2, p-value ≈ 0.0003. Conclude: reject H₀ — convincing evidence of an association between anger level and heart disease.
bWhich cell contributes most to χ²? Describe it.
High anger with CHD: observed 27 vs. expected 14.19 (component ≈ 11.56) — many more high-anger people developed CHD than expected. (Low anger with CHD: 53 vs. 69.73 — fewer.)
cCan we conclude that anger causes heart disease? Which error could we have made?
No — this is an observational study (no random assignment), so we cannot conclude causation. We rejected H₀, so we could have made a Type I error: concluding there is an association when there really isn’t.
🔑 One sample, two variables → independence.

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★Bonus · TPS 12.2

2 × 2 tables: χ² and z 2×2 表：χ² 与 z

Perry Preschool (Lesson 8): 38 of 62 preschool children and 49 of 61 control children needed social services. The two-sample z test gave z = −2.32.

aA χ² test for homogeneity on the same 2 × 2 table gives χ² ≈ 5.38. Compare with z².
z² = (−2.32)² ≈ 5.38 = χ² (df = 1).
bHow do the p-values compare?
χ² p-value = P(χ² > 5.38) ≈ 0.020 = the two-sided z test p-value (2 × 0.010). The χ² test cannot do a one-sided alternative — use the z test for that.
🔑 For a 2 × 2 table, χ² = z².

📝 AP Practice 5 · AP 选择题★★★★Topic 3.15

Finding the p-value

A χ² test for independence on a 3 × 4 table gives χ² = 14.2. What is the p-value?
  • (A) P(χ² ≥ 14.2) with df = 12
  • (B) P(χ² ≥ 14.2) with df = 6
  • (C) P(χ² ≤ 14.2) with df = 6
  • (D) 2P(χ² ≥ 14.2) with df = 6
  • (E) P(χ² ≥ 14.2) with df = 11

df = (3 − 1)(4 − 1) = 6; the p-value is always the right tail → (B) (≈ 0.027).

📝 AP Practice 6 · AP 选择题★★★★Topic 3.15

Conclusion for independence

A χ² test for independence between music genre preference and favorite subject (random sample of students) gives a p-value of 0.31. Which conclusion is correct at α = 0.05?
  • (A) Music preference and favorite subject are independent.
  • (B) There is convincing evidence of an association.
  • (C) There is not convincing evidence of an association between music preference and favorite subject.
  • (D) Music preference causes favorite subject.
  • (E) The distributions are proved to be the same.

0.31 > 0.05 → fail to reject H₀ → not convincing evidence of an association → (C).

(A) and (E) “prove” H₀ — never allowed.

📝 AP Practice 7 · AP 选择题★★★★Topic 3.15

Largest component

In a χ² test, the cell for “seniors who prefer online classes” has observed count 48, expected count 30, and the largest component. Which statement is the best follow-up?
  • (A) Fewer seniors preferred online classes than expected if H₀ were true.
  • (B) More seniors preferred online classes than expected if H₀ were true.
  • (C) Seniors caused the difference.
  • (D) This cell should be removed.
  • (E) The expected count is too small for the test.

O = 48 > E = 30, and (48 − 30)²/30 = 10.8 is the largest component → more seniors than expected → (B).

For a 2 × 2 table, the two-sample z statistic is z = 1.80. What is the χ² statistic for a test for homogeneity on the same table?

A random sample of 300 adults (Lesson 9 exit ticket): age group × shopping method.

Age ↓ · Shop →OnlineIn-storeBothTotal
Under 30582240120
30+626850180
Total1209090300
1Expected counts are 48, 36, 36, 72, 54, 54. Calculate χ², df, and the p-value. What do you conclude at α = 0.05?
Step 1. χ² = (58 − 48)²/48 + (22 − 36)²/36 + (40 − 36)²/36 + (62 − 72)²/72 + (68 − 54)²/54 + (50 − 54)²/54 = 2.08 + 5.44 + 0.44 + 1.39 + 3.63 + 0.30 ≈ 13.29; df = 2; p-value ≈ 0.0013.
