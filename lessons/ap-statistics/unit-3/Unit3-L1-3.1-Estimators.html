📝 Warm-up · 例题★★★★Warm-up

How many tanks? 有多少辆坦克？

In World War II, the Allies wanted to know how many tanks Germany had made. Tanks were numbered 1, 2, 3, …, N. Suppose four captured tanks had serial numbers 19, 40, 42, 60. 缴获的 4 辆坦克编号为 19、40、42、60，估计总数 N。

Q1Give your best estimate of N.
Step 1. Rule A: use the largest number → 60. But N must be at least 60, so this rule is always too low (or exactly right).
Step 2. Rule B: twice the mean − 1 → 2(40.25) − 1 = 79.5. The mean of 1…N is (N + 1)/2.
Step 3. Rule C: max + (max ÷ n) − 1 → 60 + 15 − 1 = 74 (add the typical gap).
Q2Describe the rule (formula) you used. Would your rule tend to be too low, too high, or about right?
Different rules (estimators) give different estimates. Which rule is best? We judge a rule by how it behaves over many samples → today’s lesson!
🔑 Serial numbers 19, 40, 42, 60 — estimate N.
💡 An estimator is a rule; an estimate is the number the rule gives for one sample.

Parameters, Statistics & Point Estimators 参数、统计量与点估计量

Parameter · statistic参数 · 统计量
A parameter describes a population (usually unknown). A statistic describes a sample. Remember: statistics come from samples, parameters come from populations.
参数描述总体（通常未知）；统计量描述样本。
Point estimator点估计量
A sample statistic is a point estimator of the corresponding parameter; its value for a sample is the point estimate — a single point on the number line. Example: the sample proportion p̂ is a point estimator for the population proportion p.
样本统计量是对应总体参数的点估计量，由一个样本算出的具体数值叫点估计值。例如 p̂ 是 p 的点估计量。
Parameter (population)pμσp₁ − p₂
Statistic (sample)p̂x̄sp̂₁ − p̂₂

📝 Example 2 · 例题★★★★EK 3.1.B.1

Point estimates 点估计值

a. In a random sample of 1,502 U.S. adults, 1,067 said they use social media every day. Identify the parameter and find its point estimate.

b. A random sample of 8 AP students reported weekly study hours: 6 9 4 11 7 8 5 6. Find point estimates for the mean μ and the standard deviation σ of study hours for all AP students.

Step 1. a. Parameter: p = the proportion of all U.S. adults who use social media every day.
Step 2. Point estimate: p̂ = 1067 ÷ 1502 ≈ 0.710.
Step 3. b. x̄ = 56 ÷ 8 = 7 hours estimates μ; s ≈ 2.20 hours estimates σ (calculator: 1-Var Stats).
🔑 Statistic → point estimate of the matching parameter.

The cabin in winter 冬天的小屋

To keep pipes from freezing, Mrs. Starnes sets the cabin thermostat at 50°F. She wants to know how low the temperature actually gets. A digital thermometer records the indoor temperature at 20 randomly chosen times during a given day. The minimum reading is 38°F.

Identify the population, the parameter, the sample, and the statistic.
Step 1. Population: all times during that day.
Step 2. Parameter: the true minimum temperature in the cabin at all times that day.
Step 3. Sample: the 20 randomly selected times. Statistic: the sample minimum, 38°F.
🔑 Not every parameter is a mean or a proportion!

Three Different Distributions 三种不同的分布

Population distribution总体分布
The values of the variable for all individuals in the population.
总体中所有个体的变量取值的分布。
Distribution of sample data样本数据分布
The values of the variable for the individuals in one sample.
一个样本中个体取值的分布。
Sampling distribution抽样分布
The values of a statistic from all possible samples of the same size from the same population.
同一总体中所有相同大小样本的统计量取值的分布。

Unbiased Estimator 无偏估计量

Unbiased无偏
When estimating a parameter, an estimator is unbiased if, on average, its value does not underestimate or overestimate the parameter: the mean of its sampling distribution equals the parameter.
若估计量平均来说既不低估也不高估参数，即其抽样分布的均值等于参数，则称它是无偏的。
Biased有偏
If the center of the sampling distribution is consistently above or below the parameter, the estimator is biased.
若抽样分布的中心系统性地高于或低于参数，则估计量有偏。

📝 Example 5 · 例题★★★★EK 3.1.A.1 · Skill 4.B

Are x̄ and the range unbiased? 样本均值与极差无偏吗？

Use the sampling distributions from the sons’ heights (71, 75, 72, 68).

aIs the sample mean x̄ an unbiased estimator of the population mean μ? Justify.
μ = (71 + 75 + 72 + 68) ÷ 4 = 71.5. Mean of the 6 sample means = 429 ÷ 6 = 71.5 = μ → x̄ is unbiased.
bIs the sample range an unbiased estimator of the population range? Justify.
Step 1. Population range = 75 − 68 = 7. Mean of the 6 sample ranges = 22 ÷ 6 ≈ 3.67 ≠ 7.
Step 2. 3.67 < 7 → the sample range is a biased estimator: it consistently underestimates the population range.
🔑 Unbiased ⇔ mean of the sampling distribution = parameter.

📝 Example 6 · 例题★★★★EK 3.1.A.1 · Skill 4.B

Why divide by n − 1? 为什么除以 n − 1？

Population: 2, 4, 6. The population variance is σ² = [(2−4)² + 0² + (6−4)²] ÷ 3 = 8/3 ≈ 2.67.

Take all 9 samples of size 2 with replacement: (2,2), (2,4), (2,6), (4,2), …, (6,6).

aFor each sample, find s² = Σ(x − x̄)² ÷ (n − 1). Is s² unbiased for σ²?
s² values (÷ 1): 0, 2, 8, 2, 0, 2, 8, 2, 0 → mean = 24 ÷ 9 = 8/3 = σ² → unbiased ✓.
bWhat if we divided by n instead?
Step 1. Dividing by n = 2 halves every value → mean = 4/3 < 8/3 → biased low.
Step 2. That’s why the sample variance (and our calculator’s s) divides by n − 1.
🔑 For a sample (a, b): Σ(x − x̄)² = (a − b)² ÷ 2.

Bias and Variability 偏差与变异性

Bias (accuracy)偏差（准确性）
Bias: the sampling distribution is not centered at the parameter → estimates are not accurate. Fix it by choosing a better estimator or a better (random) sampling method.
偏差：抽样分布的中心不在参数上，估计不准确。
Variability (precision)变异性（精确性）
High variability: repeated samples give very different results → estimates are not precise. Larger random samples give less variability; bias is not fixed by a larger sample.
变异大：重复抽样结果差别大，估计不精确。增大随机样本量可减小变异，但不能消除偏差。

Which estimator is best? 哪个估计量最好？

The dotplots show simulated sampling distributions of three statistics (A, B, C) used to estimate the same parameter. The dashed line marks the parameter value.

aWhich statistics are unbiased?
A and C are centered at the parameter → unbiased. B is centered too high → biased.
bWhich statistic is the best estimator? Explain using bias and variability.
Step 1. B has low variability, but it is consistently wrong.
Step 2. C is best: unbiased and the least variable of the unbiased ones → precise and accurate.

📝 Example 8 · 例题★★★★TPS 7.1

Mrs. Lin’s chips 林老师的筹码

Mrs. Lin claims her bag has 200 chips and 100 are red. Jenna takes an SRS of 20 chips and gets 7 red (p̂ = 0.35). The dotplot shows p̂ for 500 simulated SRSs of 20 chips from a bag with p = 0.5.

aGive two explanations for Jenna’s p̂ < 0.5.
① Mrs. Lin is telling the truth and Jenna got a low p̂ by sampling variability; ② Mrs. Lin is wrong and fewer than half are red.
bIs there convincing evidence that fewer than half the chips are red?
Step 1. In the simulation, p̂ ≤ 0.35 happened in about 1 in 6 of the 500 samples — not unusual.
Step 2. So there is not convincing evidence that fewer than half the chips are red. (This is the logic of a significance test — Lesson 5!)
🔑 Use the sampling distribution assuming the claim is true.

📝 AP Practice 9 · AP 选择题★★★★EK 3.1.A.1

Unbiased means…

A statistic is an unbiased estimator of a parameter when
  • (A) its value equals the parameter for every sample
  • (B) the mean of its sampling distribution equals the parameter
  • (C) it comes from a large random sample
  • (D) its sampling distribution has a small standard deviation
  • (E) its sampling distribution is approximately normal

Unbiased: on average, it neither overestimates nor underestimates → the mean of the sampling distribution = parameter → (B).

(A) is not required: estimates can vary across samples. (C) large n alone does not remove bias; (D) concerns variability; (E) concerns shape.

Reduce the variability

A researcher wants the sampling distribution of p̂ to have less variability. Which action helps?
  • (A) Use a larger random sample
  • (B) Use a convenience sample of the same size
  • (C) Use a 99% confidence level
  • (D) Use the sample median instead
  • (E) Use a smaller random sample

Larger random samples → statistics vary less from sample to sample → (A).

A larger sample does not remove bias; a convenience sample adds bias.

📝 AP Practice 11 · AP 选择题★★★★EK 3.1.B.1

Name the point estimate

In an SRS of 400 adults in a city, 132 said they ride a bike at least once a week. Which is the point estimate of the proportion of all adults in the city who ride a bike weekly?
  • (A) p = 132
  • (B) p = 0.33
  • (C) p̂ = 0.33
  • (D) p̂ = 400
  • (E) μ = 0.33

p̂ = 132 ÷ 400 = 0.33 is the statistic (point estimate) → (C).

p (no hat) is the unknown parameter — we never know its value from the sample.

Sample maximum

To estimate the maximum weight of all packages shipped by a company in a day, a clerk uses the maximum weight in a random sample of 15 packages. This estimator is
  • (A) unbiased, because the sample is random
  • (B) biased; it tends to overestimate the true maximum
  • (C) biased; it tends to underestimate the true maximum
  • (D) unbiased, because n ≥ 10
  • (E) unbiased if the weights are normally distributed

The sample maximum can never be larger than the population maximum and is usually smaller.

So it consistently underestimates → biased → (C).

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

To estimate the proportion of students at her school who own a pet, Ana takes an SRS of 40 students; 26 own a pet.

1Define the parameter and give the point estimate.
p = proportion of all students at Ana’s school who own a pet; p̂ = 26 ÷ 40 = 0.65.
2Ana says, “Because p̂ is unbiased, my estimate equals the true proportion.” Is she right?
No. Unbiased means p̂ is correct on average over many samples; one sample’s p̂ usually misses p a little (sampling variability).
3How could Ana get a more precise estimate?
Take a larger random sample → less variability → more precise.

Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
4 min · derivation optional

Why s² estimates σ²n−1 的代数依据

Assume independent observations from one population with mean μ and finite variance σ². Centering at x̄ makes the sum of squares smaller than centering at μ.

\sum_{i=1}^{n}(X_i-\bar X)^2=\sum_{i=1}^{n}(X_i-\mu)^2-n(\bar X-\mu)^2

Take long-run averages (expectations). Each squared deviation from μ averages σ², and E[(X̄ − μ)²] = Var(X̄) = σ²/n.

\operatorname E\!\left[\sum_{i=1}^{n}(X_i-\bar X)^2\right]=n\sigma^2-n\frac{\sigma^2}{n}=(n-1)\sigma^2
s^2=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(X_i-\bar X)^2}{n-1}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname E(s^2)=\sigma^2

自由度解释“约束”；这个等式解释为什么除以 n−1 能校正方差的低估。s² 无偏，不代表开平方后的 s 也严格无偏。
