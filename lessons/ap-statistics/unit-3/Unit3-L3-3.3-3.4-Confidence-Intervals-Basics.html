📝 Warm-up · 例题★★★★Warm-up

One number or a range? 一个数还是一个范围？

Two weather apps forecast tomorrow’s high temperature. 两个天气 App 的预报：

App A“The high will be 25°C.”
App A (a point estimate) is almost certainly not exactly right — the true high could be 24.6°C.
App B“The high will be between 23°C and 27°C.”
App B (an interval estimate) is much more likely to capture the truth, and still useful.
QWhich forecast is more likely to be correct? Which is more useful? What would a forecast of “between −10°C and 60°C” be worth?
“−10°C to 60°C” is certain to be right but useless. Good intervals balance confidence and precision → today’s lesson.
🔑 A single number is almost never exactly right.

Confidence Interval 置信区间

Interval estimate区间估计
A confidence interval is an interval estimate for a population parameter: a range of plausible values. For a proportion, it is based on the sample proportion p̂.
置信区间是总体参数的区间估计：一个由合理取值组成的范围。
Form形式
point estimate ± margin of error. The parameter must be defined in context: the proportion, the response variable, and the population.
点估计 ± 误差界。参数要结合语境说明：比例、响应变量、总体。
“Plausible” ≠ “possible”: plausible means we wouldn’t be surprised if any value in the interval were the true parameter. 合理 ≠ 可能。

Margin of Error & Standard Error 误差界与标准误

Standard error (SE)标准误
The standard error of a statistic estimates the SD of its sampling distribution: SE_{p̂} = √(p̂(1 − p̂)/n). It quantifies the typical amount the statistic varies from the parameter.
标准误是统计量抽样分布标准差的估计：SE = √(p̂(1−p̂)/n)，表示统计量与参数之间的典型差距。
Margin of error误差界
The margin of error is half the width of the interval: (critical value) × (SE). The critical value z* makes the interval wide enough to have the stated capture rate.
误差界是区间宽度的一半 = 临界值 × 标准误；临界值 z* 让区间达到所需的覆盖率。
\begin{gathered}\text{statistic}\pm(\text{critical value})(\text{standard error})\\\hat p\pm z^*\sqrt{\frac{\hat p(1-\hat p)}{n}}\end{gathered}

📝 Example 2 · 例题★★★★EK 3.3.C–D · 3.4.A

An unexpected $1,000 bill 突如其来的 1000 美元账单

A poll asked a random sample of U.S. adults how much difficulty they would have paying an unexpected $1,000 bill right away. 65% said they would have “a little” or “a lot” of difficulty. The 95% confidence interval for the proportion of all U.S. adults who would admit some difficulty is 0.613 to 0.687.

aFind the point estimate and the margin of error.
Step 1. Point estimate = (0.613 + 0.687) ÷ 2 = 0.650.
Step 2. Margin of error = (0.687 − 0.613) ÷ 2 = 0.037.
bInterpret the interval.
“We are 95% confident that the interval from 0.613 to 0.687 captures the true proportion of all U.S. adults who would admit to some difficulty paying an unexpected $1,000 bill right away.”
🔑 Point estimate = midpoint; margin of error = half the width.

Interpreting Intervals and Levels 解释区间与置信水平

Confidence interval置信区间
“We are C% confident that the interval from a to b captures the [parameter in context].” The interval from one sample may or may not contain the parameter.
“我们有 C% 的信心，区间 (a, b) 包含了[语境中的参数]。”一个样本得到的区间可能包含、也可能不包含参数。
Confidence level置信水平
In repeated random sampling with the same sample size, about C% of the confidence intervals calculated will capture the population proportion. It describes the method, not one interval.
在相同样本量的重复随机抽样中，大约 C% 的置信区间会包含总体比例。描述的是方法，不是某一个区间。
Interval: “We are 95% confident that the interval from a to b captures the true proportion of all [population] who [response].”
Level: “If we took many random samples of size n and made a 95% CI from each, about 95% of them would capture the true proportion of ….”

📝 Example 3 · 例题★★★★EK 3.4.A · 3.4.B

Candidate A 候选人 A

Two weeks before a presidential election, a polling organization asked a random sample of registered voters whether they would vote for Candidate A or Candidate B. The 95% confidence interval for the proportion who favor Candidate A is (0.48, 0.54).

aInterpret the confidence interval.
“We are 95% confident that the interval from 0.48 to 0.54 captures the true proportion of all registered voters who favor Candidate A.”
bFind the point estimate and the margin of error.
p̂ = 0.51; margin of error = 0.03.
cA reporter claims that a majority of registered voters favor Candidate A. Use the interval to evaluate this claim.
The interval contains plausible values ≤ 0.50 (0.48 to 0.50) → it does not give convincing evidence that a majority favor Candidate A. (It doesn’t prove the opposite either!)
🔑 Plausible values: 0.48 to 0.54.

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★EK 3.4.A.2

Interpret the level 解释置信水平

For the Candidate A poll, interpret the confidence level of 95%.

Then decide whether each statement is correct:

1There is a 95% probability that p is between 0.48 and 0.54.
Level: “If we took many random samples of registered voters and constructed a 95% CI from each, about 95% of the intervals would capture the true proportion who favor Candidate A.”
295% of registered voters favor Candidate A.
1 ✗ — p is fixed; this interval either captures it or not. 2 ✗ — confuses the level with a proportion of voters.
3If we repeated the poll many times, about 95% of the intervals would capture p.
3 ✓ — that is exactly the meaning of the confidence level.
495% of samples will have p̂ between 0.48 and 0.54.
4 ✗ — the intervals vary; the level is not about where future p̂’s fall.
🔑 The level describes the method over many samples.
💡 “95% confident” is shorthand for “we used a method that captures p in 95% of samples.”

Justify a Claim with an Interval 用置信区间判断说法

Plausible values合理取值
A confidence interval gives a range of plausible values for p. If the claimed value is outside the interval → convincing evidence against the claim. If it is inside → the claimed value is plausible; we have no convincing evidence against it (but we haven’t proved it!).
置信区间给出 p 的合理取值。声称值在区间外 → 有充分证据反对；在区间内 → 该值合理，没有充分证据反对（但也没有证明它）。

What Affects the Margin of Error? 什么影响误差界？

Confidence level ↑置信水平增大
For a given sample, increasing the confidence level → larger critical value → larger margin of error → wider interval. (100% confidence: the interval 0 to 1 — useless!)
置信水平提高 → 临界值变大 → 误差界变大 → 区间变宽。
Sample size ↑样本量增大
Increasing n decreases the standard error → narrower interval. Width is approximately proportional to 1/√n: quadruple n → half the width.
样本量增大 → 标准误减小 → 区间变窄。宽度约与 1/√n 成正比：n 乘 4，宽度减半。

📝 Example 5 · 例题★★★★EK 3.4.C

Narrower or wider? 变窄还是变宽？

A survey of 400 randomly selected students gives a 95% CI for the proportion who use public transportation: (0.27, 0.36). What happens to the interval if…

a…we use 99% confidence instead (same data)?
99%: z* goes from 1.96 to 2.576 → margin of error × 1.31 → wider (about 0.25 to 0.38).
b…we survey 1,600 students instead (95%, similar p̂)?
n × 4 → SE ÷ 2 → margin of error 0.045 → about 0.0225 → half as wide.
c…we want 100% confidence?
100% confidence → the interval 0 to 1 — certain but useless.
🔑 Margin of error = z* × SE.

Using confidence intervals wisely 明智地使用置信区间

① Random data required

The method assumes the data come from a random sample (or a randomized experiment). A convenience sample → no confidence interval makes sense.

方法要求随机样本或随机实验。

② The margin of error covers only chance

It accounts only for sampling variability (or random assignment) — not for undercoverage, nonresponse, or response bias. Those can make estimates far worse than the margin of error suggests.

误差界只包括抽样随机误差，不包括覆盖不足、无回应、回应偏差。

Example: a phone poll of 1,000 adults reports “52% ± 3%”, but only 9% of people called agreed to answer. The true margin of uncertainty is probably much larger than 3%. 回应率很低时，真实误差远大于报告的误差界。

📝 AP Practice 6 · AP 选择题★★★★EK 3.4.A.2

Meaning of 95% confidence

A researcher reports a 95% confidence interval for the proportion of teens who own a smartwatch: (0.18, 0.24). Which statement correctly interprets the confidence level?
  • (A) There is a 95% chance that the true proportion is between 0.18 and 0.24.
  • (B) 95% of teens own a smartwatch.
  • (C) If many random samples were taken and a 95% CI made from each, about 95% of the intervals would capture the true proportion.
  • (D) 95% of all sample proportions will fall between 0.18 and 0.24.
  • (E) The true proportion is 0.21 with 95% certainty.

The confidence level describes what happens in repeated sampling → (C).

(A) is the classic mistake: the parameter is fixed, not random.

📝 AP Practice 7 · AP 选择题★★★★EK 3.4.C.1

Raise the confidence level

Using the same sample data, a 99% confidence interval for p compared with a 95% interval is
  • (A) narrower, because it is more confident
  • (B) wider, because the critical value is larger
  • (C) the same width, because the data didn’t change
  • (D) shifted to the right
  • (E) narrower, because the standard error is smaller

Same data → same SE. A higher level needs a larger z* (2.576 vs. 1.96) → wider → (B).

Halve the margin of error

A poll of n = 300 has a margin of error of about 5.6 percentage points. Approximately how many people are needed to cut the margin of error to 2.8 points (same confidence level)?
  • (A) 150
  • (B) 424
  • (C) 600
  • (D) 1,200
  • (E) 2,400

Half the margin of error → √n must double → n × 4 = 1,200 → (D).

📝 AP Practice 9 · AP 选择题★★★★TPS 8.1

What the margin of error covers

A news report says “58% of residents support the new stadium (margin of error ± 4%).” The survey was conducted online, and anyone could respond. The margin of error accounts for
  • (A) voluntary response bias
  • (B) undercoverage of people without internet
  • (C) variation due to random sampling only
  • (D) people who lied about their opinion
  • (E) all possible errors in the survey

The margin of error measures only sampling variability → (C).

Here the sample wasn’t even random (voluntary response) — the reported margin of error is not meaningful.

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

A random sample of teenagers gives a 95% confidence interval for the proportion of all teenagers who sleep at least 8 hours on school nights: (0.27, 0.35).

1Find the point estimate and the margin of error.
p̂ = 0.31, margin of error = 0.04.
2Interpret the interval.
We are 95% confident that the interval from 0.27 to 0.35 captures the true proportion of all teenagers who sleep at least 8 hours on school nights.
3Is it plausible that one-third of teenagers sleep 8+ hours? That half do?
1/3 ≈ 0.333 is inside → plausible. 0.50 is outside → not plausible; convincing evidence that fewer than half do.
