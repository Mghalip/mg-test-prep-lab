The Correlation Coefficient r 相关系数 r

Meaning含义
r summarizes the strength and direction of the linear association between two quantitative variables.
r 概括两个数值变量之间线性关联的强度与方向。
Values取值
−1 ≤ r ≤ 1. r > 0 → positive; r < 0 → negative. The closer r is to −1 or 1, the stronger. r = 0 → no linear association; r = ±1 → perfect linear association.
−1 ≤ r ≤ 1。正负号表示方向；越接近 ±1 越强；r = 0 表示没有线性关联；r = ±1 表示完全线性。
Unit-free无单位
r has no units; changing units (inches → cm) does not change r. Swapping x and y does not change r.
r 没有单位；换单位不改变 r；交换 x 与 y 也不改变 r。
“r = 0.82 indicates a strong, positive, linear association between (x in context) and (y in context).”

📝 Example 2 · 例题★★★★EK 5.2.A · technology

Interpret r 解释 r

For the 10 students in Lesson 1 (high school GPA x, college GPA y), technology gives r = 0.817.

aInterpret r.
There is a strong, positive, linear association between high school GPA and first-year college GPA for these students.
bWhat would r be if GPAs were recorded on a 100-point scale instead?
Still 0.817 — r has no units, so a change of scale does not change it.
cWhat would r be if we used college GPA to predict high school GPA?
Still 0.817 — r does not depend on which variable is called x.
🔑 r is unit-free and symmetric.

Three Cautions About r 关于 r 的三个提醒

r is not resistantr 不抗离群值
A single outlier can greatly increase or decrease r.
一个离群点就能大幅改变 r。
r ≈ ±1 ≠ linearr 接近 ±1 不代表线性
A curved relationship can have r close to 1. Always look at the scatterplot (and later the residual plot).
曲线关系也可能 r 接近 1。一定要看散点图（之后还要看残差图）。
Correlation ≠ causation相关不等于因果
A real relationship between two variables does not mean changes in x cause changes in y — a lurking (confounding) variable may explain both.
两个变量有关联不代表 x 的变化导致 y 的变化——可能有潜在（混杂）变量同时影响两者。

📝 Example 3 · 例题★★★★EK 5.2.A.3

Strong r, wrong model r 很大，模型却不对

A ball is dropped and its distance fallen (m) is recorded every 0.1 s for 1 second. The correlation between time and distance is r = 0.97.

aA student says, “r is very close to 1, so a straight line is a great model.” Respond.
r = 0.97 indicates a strong positive linear association, but does not guarantee a suitable linear model. Here the data are strongly increasing. The scatterplot is clearly curved (distance grows like time²), so a line is not the most appropriate model even though r is large.
bWhat should the student do first?
Always make a scatterplot first (and, in Lesson 3, a residual plot) before using r or a line.
🔑 Look before you compute.

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★EK 5.2.A.4

Ice cream and shark attacks 冰淇淋与鲨鱼袭击

Over the months of a year, a beach town’s ice-cream sales and number of shark attacks have r = 0.88.

aDoes eating ice cream cause shark attacks?
No — r measures association only.
bSuggest a lurking variable.
Temperature / season: in hot months more people buy ice cream and more people swim in the sea.
cWhat kind of study could show cause and effect?
A randomized experiment (Unit 1) — this observational example provides no randomized evidence for the proposed causal effect.
🔑 Association is not causation.

📝 AP Practice 5 · AP 选择题★★★★EK 5.2.A.1–2

Interpreting r

The correlation between the number of hours worked per week and GPA for a sample of students is r = −0.45. Which is the best interpretation?
  • (A) Working more hours causes lower GPA.
  • (B) There is a moderate negative linear association between hours worked and GPA.
  • (C) 45% of the variation in GPA is explained by hours worked.
  • (D) GPA decreases by 0.45 for each extra hour worked.
  • (E) There is no association.

|r| = 0.45 is moderate, sign negative → (B). (C) describes r², (D) describes a slope, (A) claims causation.

Which statement is true?

Which statement about correlation is true?
  • (A) r = 0 means there is no relationship of any kind.
  • (B) r = 0.9 means a linear model is definitely appropriate.
  • (C) A strong correlation proves cause and effect.
  • (D) r = −1 means a perfect negative linear association.

Exit ticket 🎟️ 离堂小测

For 30 countries, the correlation between the number of TVs per person and life expectancy is r = 0.80.

1Interpret r.
There is a strong, positive, linear association between TVs per person and life expectancy for these countries.
2A politician says, “Send TVs to countries with low life expectancy.” Explain the flaw.
Correlation is not causation — national wealth is a lurking variable: richer countries have more TVs and better health care. These data do not establish that sending TVs would raise life expectancy.

Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
3 min · derivation optional

Build r from paired deviations从共同变化到相关系数

For each person, compare x and y with their own sample means. Products are positive when both deviations have the same sign, and negative when their signs differ.

s_{xy}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-\bar x)(y_i-\bar y)}{n-1}

Covariance depends on units. Divide by both sample SDs to remove the units. Assume sₓ > 0 and sᵧ > 0.

r=\frac{s_{xy}}{s_xs_y}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}z_{x_i}z_{y_i}}{n-1}

先看每一对数据是否一起高、一起低，再去掉单位。r 是标准化后的共同变化。

Check your understanding · One variable is constant. What is r?

Undefined: its sample SD is zero, so standardization is impossible.

Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
4 min · derivation optional

Why −1 ≤ r ≤ 1不用微积分的界限推导

For sample-standardized values, Σzₓ² = Σzᵧ² = n − 1. Every square is nonnegative.

\begin{aligned}0&\leq\sum_{i=1}^{n}(z_{x_i}-z_{y_i})^2=2(n-1)-2\sum_{i=1}^{n}z_{x_i}z_{y_i}\\&\Longrightarrow\sum_{i=1}^{n}z_{x_i}z_{y_i}\leq n-1\Longrightarrow r\leq1\end{aligned}
\begin{aligned}0&\leq\sum_{i=1}^{n}(z_{x_i}+z_{y_i})^2=2(n-1)+2\sum_{i=1}^{n}z_{x_i}z_{y_i}\\&\Longrightarrow\sum_{i=1}^{n}z_{x_i}z_{y_i}\geq-(n-1)\Longrightarrow r\geq-1\end{aligned}

Equality requires zᵧ = zₓ for every pair (r = 1), or zᵧ = −zₓ for every pair (r = −1): a perfect straight-line relationship.

上下界来自“平方和不能为负”。需要至少两对数据，而且两个变量都有非零变异。

How r determines the regression slope回归预告：标准化斜率

Preview after learning least squares: write uᵢ = xᵢ − x̄ and vᵢ = yᵢ − ȳ. A fitted line through (x̄, ȳ) predicts v using bu.

\begin{aligned}\operatorname{SSE}(b)&=\sum_{i=1}^{n}(v_i-bu_i)^2\\&=\sum_{i=1}^{n}v_i^2-2b\sum_{i=1}^{n}u_iv_i+b^2\sum_{i=1}^{n}u_i^2\end{aligned}

Completing the square gives the minimum at b = Σuᵢvᵢ/Σuᵢ². The best intercept is a = ȳ − bx̄ because residuals sum to zero.

b=\frac{s_{xy}}{s_x^2}=r\frac{s_y}{s_x}\qquad\frac{\hat y-\bar y}{s_y}=r\frac{x-\bar x}{s_x}

r 是标准化单位下的回归斜率；原单位斜率还要乘 sᵧ/sₓ。r 没有单位，b 有单位。

Check your understanding · r = 0.6, sₓ = 2, sᵧ = 10: slope?

b = 0.6 × 10/2 = 3 y-units per x-unit.

Why r² measures explained variation回归预告：平方和分解

For ordinary least-squares regression with one predictor and an intercept, let eᵢ = yᵢ − ŷᵢ. The fitted deviations and residuals are orthogonal: Σ(ŷᵢ − ȳ)eᵢ = 0.

\begin{aligned}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_i-\bar y)^2&=\sum_{i=1}^{n}(\hat y_i-\bar y)^2+\sum_{i=1}^{n}e_i^2\\\operatorname{SST}&=\operatorname{SSR}+\operatorname{SSE}\end{aligned}
\operatorname{SSR}=b^2\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-\bar x)^2=r^2\frac{s_y^2}{s_x^2}(n-1)s_x^2=r^2\operatorname{SST}
Derivation optional / 推导不作 AP 必背要求
Behind the Formula · 公式背后的逻辑
4 min · derivation optional

Interpreting the explained fraction解释变异的比例

For a one-predictor least-squares line with an intercept, SST = SSR + SSE. Dividing by SST gives the fraction explained by the fitted line.

R^2=\frac{\operatorname{SSR}}{\operatorname{SST}}=1-\frac{\operatorname{SSE}}{\operatorname{SST}}=r^2

r² 衡量这条回归线解释的样本 y 变异比例；不是预测正确率，也不是因果贡献比例。这个等式限定为含截距的一元最小二乘回归。

Check your understanding · r = −0.8: explained variation?

64%. The negative sign describes direction; squaring removes it.
