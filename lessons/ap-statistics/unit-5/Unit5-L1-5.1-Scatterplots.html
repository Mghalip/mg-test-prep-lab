Bivariate Data and Scatterplots 双变量数据与散点图

Bivariate quantitative data双变量数值数据
Ordered pairs (x, y) of two quantitative variables measured on the same individuals.
对同一批个体测量的两个数值变量形成的有序对 (x, y)。
Explanatory & response解释变量与响应变量
The explanatory variable (x) is used to explain or predict the response variable (y). Put x on the horizontal axis, y on the vertical axis.
解释变量 x 用来解释或预测响应变量 y。x 放横轴，y 放纵轴。
Scatterplot散点图
Each individual is one point. Label both axes with the variable names and units, and use evenly spaced scales.
每个个体是一个点。两条轴都要标出变量名和单位，刻度均匀。

Describing a Scatterplot 描述散点图

Form · Direction形式 · 方向
Linear or non-linear. Positive: as x increases, y tends to increase; negative: as x increases, y tends to decrease.
线性或非线性；正：x 增大 y 倾向增大；负：x 增大 y 倾向减小。
Strength强度
How closely the points follow the pattern: strong, moderate, weak.
点贴近模式的程度：强、中等、弱。
Unusual features异常特征
Outliers (points outside the pattern) and clusters.
离群点与聚集群。
“There is a strong, positive, linear association between (x in context) and (y in context). (Unusual features).”

📝 Example 4 · 例题★★★★EK 5.1.B

SAT participation SAT 参加率

The scatterplot shows, for 50 states, the percent of high school graduates who took the SAT (x) and the state’s mean SAT Math score (y). 各州 SAT 参加率与平均数学分。

Describe the relationship.
Step 1. Direction: negative — states where a higher percent of graduates take the SAT tend to have lower mean Math scores.
Step 2. Form: somewhat curved (non-linear).
Step 3. Strength: moderately strong.
Step 4. Unusual features: two clusters (states with ≤ 25% taking the test and states with more than 40%), and one state with a much lower mean score than others with similar participation (an outlier).
🔑 Direction · unusual features · form · strength — in context.

5.1.C · Key ConceptEK 5.1.C.1
Difficulty 难度 ★★★★

Justifying Claims with Scatterplots 用散点图支持结论

Justify论证
Refer to specific features of the graph (direction, strength, particular points) and state the conclusion in context. A scatterplot shows an association — it does not show that x causes y.
引用图中的具体特征（方向、强度、具体点），在语境中下结论。散点图只显示关联，不能说明 x 导致 y。
Weak answer ✗

“The graph shows that the claim is true.”

没有引用图中的特征。

Strong answer ✓

“The claim is supported: there is a strong positive linear association — students who studied more hours tended to have higher quiz scores (e.g., 9 hours → 88 vs. 1 hour → 52).”

方向 + 强度 + 具体点 + 语境。

📝 Example 5 · 例题★★★★EK 5.1.C · Skill 4.B

Is the claim supported? 这个说法有依据吗？

The scatterplot shows hours of sleep (x) and reaction time in ms (y) for 20 randomly selected students. 睡眠与反应时间。

a“Students who sleep more tend to react faster.”
Supported — there is a moderately strong negative association: more sleep goes with lower reaction times.
b“Every student who slept more than 8 hours reacted faster than every student who slept less than 6 hours.”
Not supported — one student who slept about 8.5 hours had a reaction time (≈ 300 ms) slower than a student who slept 5.5 hours (≈ 290 ms). The pattern is a tendency, not a rule.
c“Sleeping more causes faster reactions.”
Not supported — this is an observational study, so it shows association, not causation.
🔑 Use the graph and the study design.

📝 AP Practice 6 · AP 选择题★★★★EK 5.1.B.3

Describing direction

In a scatterplot of car age (years) and price ($), older cars tend to have lower prices. The association is
  • (A) positive
  • (B) negative
  • (C) non-linear
  • (D) causal
  • (E) zero

As age increases, price tends to decrease → negative → (B).

📝 AP Practice 7 · AP 选择题★★★★EK 5.1.A.2

Explanatory variable

A researcher wants to predict a student’s final exam score from the number of classes the student missed. Which is correct?
  • (A) Final exam score goes on the x-axis.
  • (B) Classes missed is the explanatory variable.
  • (C) Both variables are categorical.
  • (D) Classes missed is the response variable.
  • (E) Neither variable can be explanatory.

We use classes missed to predict score → classes missed is explanatory (x-axis) → (B).

The scatterplot shows the age (years) and maximum heart rate (bpm) of 15 adults.

1Identify the explanatory and response variables.
Explanatory: age; response: maximum heart rate.
2Describe the relationship.
There is a strong, negative, linear association between age and maximum heart rate: older adults tend to have lower maximum heart rates. One adult (age ≈ 40, ≈ 150 bpm) is somewhat below the pattern.
