What is it?

What is a paragraph main point question?

Definition

These questions ask what one paragraph (or a few paragraphs, or a set of lines) mainly says, or which choice best summarizes it.

ACT describes this skill as “summarize information and ideas accurately.” It belongs to Key Ideas & Details.

这类题问的是某一段(或几段、几行)的主要意思,或者哪个选项最准确地概括了它。

How it differs from a main idea question

How paragraphs are built

Common paragraph patterns

Pattern · 结构Example · 例子Main point · 概括
Claim → support观点→支持The park benefits residents. It offers shade and space to exercise.The park serves residents in several ways.概括两种益处，不只写“有树荫”。
Old view → turn旧观点→转折The task seemed easy. However, it required months of practice.The task was harder than it appeared.
Question → answer问题→回答Why did the plants wilt? Their roots lacked water.Lack of water caused the plants to wilt.
Event → response事件→反应Mia lost her notes. She calmly rebuilt the outline.Mia responded calmly to a setback.

概括整段的共同重点，不是逐句抄写，也不是只选其中一个真实细节。

How to approach it

Four steps for a paragraph point question

1

Read the whole paragraph

Do not stop after the first sentence. Read every line in the range given. 读完整段,不要只读第一句。

2

Find the sentence the others support

Ask: “Which sentence would the others be examples or evidence for?” Watch for turn words. 找到被其他句子支撑的那一句,注意转折词。

3

Say the point in your own words

One short sentence. Include the attitude if there is one (praise, doubt, surprise). 用一句话说出这段的意思,带上态度。

4

Choose the answer that covers the whole paragraph

A choice about only one example or one sentence is too narrow, even if it is true. 只覆盖一个例子或一句话的选项,即使正确也不选。

Wrong answers

How wrong answers are built

One detail only

A true detail from the paragraph, but not its point.

例:F11 Q15 选项 B 只是列出了几位顾问是谁。

Only half of a turn

Describes what comes before the But, and misses the point after it.

例:G01 Q37 选项 D 只说了转折前的内容。

Wrong strength

The paragraph says “many possible reasons … we don’t know”; the choice says “the primary reasons.”

程度不对:可能 → 一定。

From another paragraph

A true idea from the passage, but it belongs to a different part.

信息来自别的段落。

Summary

What to remember about paragraph point questions段落主旨题要点

Read the full section

These questions name a paragraph or a set of lines. Read all of it.

读完指定整段／范围，不能只摘一句。

Find the central point

Find the sentence that the other sentences support. Look carefully after But, However, Yet.

找出其他句子共同解释或支持的核心。

Include the attitude

Say the point in your own words, including any attitude.

把段落重点和态度压缩成一句话。

Cover the whole paragraph

The correct choice covers the whole paragraph. One true detail is not enough.

概括须覆盖整段，不是只选一个例子。

Match the strength

Watch for wrong strength: may → must, some → most.

some 不等于 most；may 不等于 must。

说出本课规则／方法，指出依据，并解释一个错误选项为什么错。
