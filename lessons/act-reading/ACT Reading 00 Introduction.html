What you will see

  • Each passage has a short introduction: genre, title, author.
  • Lines are numbered every five lines so questions can refer to them.
  • Each question has four answer choices.
  • Three passages are scored; one is a field-test passage that looks exactly the same.

4 篇文章,每篇 9 题;其中 1 篇不计分,但看不出来。

Good news

  • Everything you need is in the passage. No outside knowledge is tested.
  • There is no penalty for wrong answers: never leave a question blank.
  • You can go back to the passage as often as you need.

不考背景知识;答错不扣分;可以随时回看原文。

Passage types

Kinds of passages

By genre

Literary narrativeStories and memoirs: characters, events, feelings. One per test.
InformationalHumanities (art, music, history of ideas), social science (economics, culture, history), natural science (biology, physics). Two per test.

文学叙事(小说、回忆录)和说明性文章(人文、社科、自然科学)。

By format

SingleOne passage from one source.
PairedPassage A and Passage B, two shorter texts on a related topic.
Visual (VQI)A passage with a graph, table, or figure.

单篇、双篇、带图表的文章。

The passages come from real books and magazines, written for educated adult readers. They are usually 650–750 words. 文章选自真实的书籍和杂志,每篇约 650–750 词。

Anatomy of a passage

What a passage looks like

1

Passage number

Passage I, II, III, IV.

2

Introduction

The genre (LITERARY NARRATIVE), the title, and the author. Sometimes a sentence of background. Read it — it tells you what kind of text this is. 导语要读:告诉你文体和背景。

3

Line numbers

Printed every five lines (5, 10, 15…). Questions use them: “lines 79–83.” 每五行标一次行号。

4

Paragraphs

Indented. Questions often ask about “the third paragraph.”

Anatomy of a question

What a question looks like

ACT 选项字母交替:奇数题 A B C D,偶数题 F G H J。

1

The stem

The question itself. It tells you what kind of question this is (“can reasonably be inferred” → inference).

2

A location

Line numbers, a paragraph, or a key word tell you where to look.

3

Four choices

Odd-numbered questions use A–D; even-numbered use F–J. Only one is fully supported by the passage.

4

“Best” answer

Words like best, most nearly, most strongly mean: pick the choice the passage supports most clearly.

What it measures

What the Reading section measures

Key Ideas & Details

44–52% of scored questions

Read closely; find central ideas; summarize; draw inferences; understand sequence, comparison, and cause-effect.

关键信息与细节:主旨、概括、细节、推断、逻辑关系。

Craft & Structure

26–33%

Word and phrase meanings; word choice; text structure; the author’s purpose and perspective; characters’ points of view.

写作技巧与结构:词义、结构、目的、视角。

Integration of Knowledge & Ideas

19–26%

Authors’ claims; facts vs. opinions; evidence; connections between related texts; graphics.

知识与观点整合:论点论据、事实观点、双篇、图表。

Source: ACT, Preparing for the ACT (2025).

How to read a passage

How to read an ACT passage

Read for structure, not for memory

  • Read the introduction line first.
  • Read the first paragraph carefully: what is the topic, the situation, the question?
  • For each paragraph, ask: what is this paragraph doing? Write 3–5 words in the margin.
  • Read the last paragraph carefully: conclusion or ending.
  • Do not try to memorize details. You can always go back.

读结构而不是背细节:读导语、首段、每段写几个字的笔记、细读末段。

What to mark

  • Names and new terms
  • Numbers and dates
  • Opinion words: remarkable, dumbest, sadly
  • Turns: but, however, yet, in fact
  • Absolute words: only, never, all
  • Quotations, dashes, parentheses

标记:名字、数字、态度词、转折词、绝对词、引号/破折号/括号。

How to answer

A process for every question

1

Read the question and identify its type

Detail? Inference? Purpose? The wording tells you what kind of thinking is needed. 先判断题型。

2

Go back to the passage

Use line numbers, paragraph numbers, or key words. Read a little before and after. 回到原文定位。

3

Answer in your own words first

Before reading the choices, say what the answer should be. 先自己说出答案。

4

Eliminate and choose

Cross out choices with a clear problem. The answer you keep must be supported by specific words in the passage. 排除有明确问题的选项;留下的答案必须有原文支持。

Wrong answers

How wrong answers are usually built

Opposite

Says the reverse of the passage.

Too strong

always, only, proves, revolutionized — more than the passage says.

Not in the passage

Sounds reasonable, but the text never says it. This is the most common trap.

Wrong place / person

A real detail, attached to the wrong person, time, paragraph, or passage.

True but not the answer

Accurate, but it does not answer this question (often too narrow).

One wrong word

Mostly right; one word makes it false.

反向、过度、无中生有、张冠李戴、答非所问、一词之差。各课会结合具体题型再讲。Sources: Kaplan, Magoosh, APGuru ACT Reading guides.

Pacing

Pacing and guessing

About 10 minutes per passage

  • About 3 minutes to read and make notes.
  • About 7 minutes for the nine questions.
  • Check the time once per passage, not after every question.
  • Start with the passage type you find easiest, if you like.

每篇约 10 分钟:读 3 分钟,做题 7 分钟。

When you are stuck

  • If you cannot find a detail in about 30 seconds, mark it and move on.
  • Never leave a question blank: there is no penalty for guessing.
  • Eliminate one or two choices first, then guess.

卡住就先跳过;不留空,先排除再猜。

Pacing suggestions: Magoosh and Princeton Review guides to the enhanced ACT.
