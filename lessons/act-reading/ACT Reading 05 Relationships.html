What is a relationship question?

Cause & effect

Asks why something happened, or what resulted from it.

one reason … is that
what caused …
resulted in …

因果:为什么发生?导致了什么?

Comparison

Asks how two people, ideas, or things are alike or different.

compared to …
in contrast to / unlike …
the relationship between …

比较:相同点或不同点。

Sequence

Asks about the order of events.

before / after …
occurred first / last …
then …

顺序:先后关系。

Name the relationship, identify the two ideas it connects, and check its direction. A signal word is a clue; the surrounding sentences establish the connection.

Signal words

Words in the passage that show relationships

Cause → effect · 因果

Heavy rain flooded the road. Because the road was closed, the bus took a detour.
Rain降雨
→
Road closed封路
→
Detour绕行

题目问“绕行的直接原因”，应答封路；不是把所有相关事件都当同一个原因。

Sequence ≠ cause · 顺序不等于因果

Mia opened the window. A bird sang outside.

The events are sequential. The text does not say that opening the window caused the bird to sing.

先发生 A、后发生 B，不自动证明 A 导致 B。倒叙文章还要区分讲述顺序与实际时间顺序。

How to approach it

Steps for relationship questions

1

Decide which relationship is asked

Cause, effect, similarity, difference, or order? Read the question twice: “what caused X” is not “what X caused.” 先判断问的是哪种关系,别把因果方向弄反。

2

Find the stated relationship in the passage

Look for the signal word near the key words. The ACT tests relationships the passage states, not ones you assume from real life. 找原文里明确写出的关系,不靠常识。

3

For comparisons, check both sides

Find what the passage says about each thing. A difference must be true of one and not the other. 比较题两边都要核对。

4

For sequence, make a quick timeline

Write the events in order with their dates or time words. Passages do not always tell events in order. 画时间线;文章叙述顺序不一定等于发生顺序。

Wrong answers

How wrong answers are built

Reversed

Cause and effect swapped, or before and after swapped.

因果或先后颠倒。

Real but unrelated

Both facts are in the passage, but the passage does not connect them.

两件事都在文中,但文章没有说它们有关系。

True of both

In a difference question, the choice is true of both things, so it is not a difference.

比较题:两边都有的特点不是“不同点”。

Common-sense cause

A reason that sounds natural but that the passage never gives.

听起来合理的原因,但原文没说。

Summary

What to remember about relationship questions逻辑关系题要点

Identify the relationship

Three kinds: cause/effect, comparison, sequence. Decide which one the question asks.

先区分因果、比较和时间顺序。

Locate the connection

Find the relationship the passage states — look for signal words and verbs like drove, led to, caused.

分清原因与结果，核对方向。

Check the direction

Watch the direction: “what caused X” ≠ “what X caused”; before ≠ after.

比较两者同一维度，不错配对象。

Compare both sides

For a difference, the choice must be true of one thing and not the other.

讲述顺序可能与实际事件顺序不同。

Build a timeline

For sequence, make a quick timeline; the passage may not tell events in order.

前后发生不自动证明因果关系。
