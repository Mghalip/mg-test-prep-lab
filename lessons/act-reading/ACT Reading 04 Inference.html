What is it?

What is an inference question?

Definition

An inference question asks what the passage suggests or implies, even though it does not say it directly.

ACT describes this skill as “draw logical inferences and conclusions.” It belongs to Key Ideas & Details.

推断题问的是文章“暗示”的内容:原文没有直接写,但根据原文可以合理得出。

An ACT inference is small

On the ACT, an inference is not a guess or a creative idea. It is a conclusion that is almost stated: if you read the lines carefully, the answer must be true.

Test: Can you point to the words that make the answer true? If not, it is not a good inference.

ACT 的推断“很近”:读懂原文后,答案几乎是必然成立的。必须能指出支撑它的原文。

Where inferences come from

Common kinds of inference on the ACT

Clue · 线索Text · 原句Supported inference · 可支持的推断
Actions → feeling动作→情绪Before the audition, Mia’s hands shook and she checked the clock repeatedly.Mia was anxious.有紧张行为支持；不能推成“她一定会失败”。
Combine details组合两处信息The museum is closed on Mondays. The visit is scheduled for Monday.The visit will need to be rescheduled.结论来自两条明确条件。
Behavior → belief行为→想法The growers added powder because they expected brighter petals.They believed the powder could change the flowers.说明他们的信念，不证明粉末实际有效。
Contrast → interpretation反差→含义A careful planner became known for studying failed plans.There is an irony between his role and his subject.

推断应是证据最支持的结论。most likely 不等于“逻辑上百分之百必然”，也不允许凭常识扩写故事。

How to approach it

Four steps for an inference question

1

Find the lines the question is about

Use the line reference or the key words. Read the whole sentence and the one after it. 先定位,读整句和后一句。

2

Collect the clues

Underline the exact words that point to the answer: actions, descriptions, contrasts. 划出线索词:动作、描写、对比。

3

Take one step

Ask: “What conclusion is best supported by these words?” Stop there. Do not build a long chain of reasoning. 只往前推一步,不连续推理。

4

Check the choice against the clues

The correct answer is supported by specific words. A choice that “could be true” but has no support is wrong. “可能对”不够,要有原文支撑。

Wrong answers

How wrong answers are built in inference questions

Too far

The choice takes several steps beyond the text, or adds a strong claim the passage does not support.

推得太远。

Possible but unsupported

It might be true in real life, but nothing in the passage points to it.

现实中可能对,但原文没有依据。

Wrong direction

Positive instead of negative, cause instead of effect, before instead of after.

方向反了:正负、因果、先后。

Wrong person

A real detail, but about a different person than the question asks about.

张冠李戴:说的是别人。

Summary

What to remember about inference questions推断题要点

Recognize an inference

Inference questions ask what the passage suggests. Signals: infer, suggest, imply, most likely.

infer / suggest / imply 提示需要推断。

Keep the inference small

An ACT inference is small: one step from the text, almost stated.

选原文最支持的结论，避免连续补设情节。

Name the evidence

Always point to the words that support your answer.

每个结论都能指出对应的证据。

Combine the clues

Common kinds: feelings from actions, combining two details, identifying a role, beliefs from behavior.

可以组合多处信息，但不能添加原文没有的前提。

Reject unsupported claims

Wrong answers go too far, have no support, point the wrong direction, or describe the wrong person.

可能发生不等于有证据支持。
