What is a detail question?

Stated information · 直接陈述

The museum closes at six on Fridays.

Question: When does the museum close on Fridays?
Answer: At 6 p.m.

直接信息可以换表达；正确答案未必重复原文词汇。

A detail checklist · 五项核对

Check who, what, when, where, and how much.

Wrong: “It opens at six.”
Wrong: “It closes at six every day.”

一个改了动作 closes → opens；另一个扩大范围 Fridays → every day。相同数字不能保证正确。

Signals

How to recognize a detail question

Question words

According to the passage…the most common form
The passage states / indicates…“says directly”
The author claims / notes…what the author writes
The narrator states…what the narrator says
…because / one reason…a stated reason

Clues that help you locate

  • Names · 人名、地名、团体名如 Ficken、Hoosac Mountain：先找到名字，再核对是谁做了什么；同一名字可能出现多次。
  • Numbers and dates · 数字与日期如 1882、3 million years：连同单位、时间和比较对象一起读，别把两个年份或数量对错对象。
  • Special terms · 特殊术语如 “living fossil”：找术语及紧邻的定义或解释；答案可能换一种说法，不照抄原词。
  • Line references · 题干给出的行号如 lines 36–44：先读指定范围，再读完跨行的句子；遇到 this / they，向前找指代对象。
  • Quoted words · 题干引用的原文词句找到引号中的表达后，读整句及必要的前后文，核对否定、原因和转折，不能只靠词语相同选答案。
Example · 例子

The museum opened in 1882, but the new wing opened in 1910.

问新馆何时开放：找到 opened 后，还要核对 new wing，答案是 1910，不能选博物馆最初开放的 1882。

How to approach it

Five steps for a detail question

1

Find the key words in the question

Underline the name, number, or term the question is about. 圈出题干里的关键词。

2

Locate the lines

With a line reference, read a few lines before and after it. Without one, use your paragraph notes or scan for the key word. 有行号读前后几行;没有行号就用段落笔记或扫描关键词。

3

Read the sentence carefully and say the answer in your own words

Do this before you look at the choices, so the choices do not confuse you. 先自己说出答案,再看选项。

4

Match the meaning, not the words

The correct choice restates the passage. Be careful with a choice that copies words but changes the meaning. 匹配意思,而不是匹配单词。

5

Check why the others are wrong

Each wrong choice has a specific problem. If you can name it, you can be confident. 能说出每个错误选项错在哪,才算真正做对。

Wrong answers

How wrong answers are built in detail questions

1 · Right words, wrong place

The choice uses a real detail from the passage, but it belongs to a different person, time, or paragraph.

例:F12 Q39 选项 “8 and 13 million years ago” 是“所有现存鳄鱼”的时间,不是这两个物种的。

2 · Opposite

The choice says the reverse of the passage.

例:G01 Q7 原文 dim and silent,选项 colorful and noisy。

3 · Not in the passage

The choice sounds reasonable, but the passage never says it.

听起来合理,但原文没有说。

4 · One word is wrong

Most of the choice is correct, but one word (often almost, always, only, most) makes it false.

大部分对,一个词错了,整个选项就错。

Skill drill · 技能训练

Same meaning or changed?同义改写还是改了意思？

The correct answer says the same thing in different words. Watch the small words. 看清程度词。

1Text: “The plant rarely flowers in cold years.” → “The plant seldom blooms when the weather is cold.”
2Text: “The plant rarely flowers in cold years.” → “The plant never flowers in cold years.”
3Text: “Sales rose slightly after the redesign.” → “Sales increased a little following the new design.”
4Text: “Sales rose slightly after the redesign.” → “The redesign caused sales to soar.”
5Text: “Most of the volunteers were students.” → “A majority of the volunteers were students.”
6Text: “Most of the volunteers were students.” → “All of the volunteers were students.”

Summary

What to remember about detail questions细节题要点

Recognize a detail question

Detail questions ask what the passage states. Signal words: According to the passage, states, claims, indicates.

定位原文的具体信息，再做同义匹配。

Read enough context

Use key words (names, numbers, terms, line numbers) to locate the sentence. Read a little before and after.

按行号、段落或关键词返回原文。

Predict the answer

Say the answer in your own words before reading the choices.

读完整句及必要上下文，防止只截半句。

Match meaning

The correct answer is a paraphrase. A choice that copies words may still be wrong.

核对谁、做什么、时间、地点、数量和否定词。

Check common traps

Common wrong answers: right words in the wrong place, opposite, not in the passage, one wrong word.

题目问 NOT / EXCEPT 时，找不符合原文的一项。

30-second check · 30 秒自检

Name the skill, cite the evidence, and explain why one wrong choice fails.

说出本课规则／方法，指出依据，并解释一个错误选项为什么错。
