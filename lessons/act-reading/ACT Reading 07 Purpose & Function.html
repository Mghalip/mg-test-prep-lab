What is a purpose or function question?

Definition

These questions ask why the author included a part of the passage — a paragraph, a sentence, a detail, an example, a quotation, or even one word — and what job it does in the passage.

ACT describes this as “analyze text structure” and “understand the author’s purpose.” It belongs to Craft & Structure.

问的是“为什么写”和“起什么作用”,而不只是“写了什么”。

“What it says” vs. “What it does”

Main point asks…Purpose asks…
What does this paragraph say?What does this paragraph do?
“Shells have had many uses.”“To specify some of the roles shells have played.”

主旨题问“内容”,功能题问“作用”。在新版 ACT 中,这类题出现得很多。

How to approach it

Four steps for a function question

1

Read the part itself

Know what it says. Then ask: is it an example, a definition, a scene, a claim, a contrast? 先读懂这一部分说什么,再判断它是什么类型的内容。

2

Read what comes before and after

Function = relationship. What does this part do for the sentences around it or for the whole passage? 功能就是关系:它对上下文起什么作用。

3

Say the job in your own words

Use a verb: “It gives an example of…”, “It sets up…”, “It shows that…”. 用“动词 + 内容”说出它的作用。

4

Check both halves of each choice

The verb must match the job, and the content must match the passage. Many wrong choices get one half right. 动词和内容两部分都要对。

Wrong answers

How wrong answers are built

Right content, wrong verb

The topic is correct, but the action is not: argues when the part only describes.

内容对、动词错。

Right verb, wrong content

It does introduce something — but not what the choice says.

动词对、内容错。

Too strong

prove, revolutionize, dismiss — stronger than the passage.

动词过强。

From the wrong part

Describes what another paragraph does.

说的是另一段的作用。

Summary

What to remember about purpose and function questions目的与功能题要点

Content versus function

These questions ask what a part of the passage does, not only what it says.

内容问“说了什么”，功能问“为什么放在这里”。

Read the surroundings

Read the part, then read before and after it: function is about relationships.

读前后文，找出这部分与上下文的关系。

Name the job

Name the job with a verb: introduce, illustrate, define, support, contrast, transition.

用引入、举例、支持、限定、对比等动词概括。

Check verb and content

Check both halves of each choice: the verb and the content.

动词准确，后面的内容也必须准确。

Match the strength

Beware of verbs that are too strong: prove, revolutionize, dismiss.

support 不等于 prove；注意指向前文还是后文。
