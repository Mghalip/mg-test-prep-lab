Warm-up · 热身

Fact or opinion?事实还是观点？

A fact can be checked; an opinion is a judgment. 事实可以核实，观点是判断。

Which statement is an opinion?
What is it?

What are claims-and-evidence questions?

Key terms

ClaimA statement the author wants you to accept: an idea, a judgment, an argument.
EvidenceWhat supports the claim: data, examples, experiments, quotations, stories.
FactCan be checked and shown true or false.
OpinionA judgment or feeling; words like best, lucky, beautiful, should.

论点 = 作者想让你接受的说法;论据 = 支撑它的数据、例子、实验、引语。

Why it matters on the new ACT

ACT describes these skills as “analyze how authors construct arguments,” “evaluate reasoning and evidence,” and “differentiate between facts and opinions.” They belong to Integration of Knowledge & Ideas.

Practice linking a specific detail to a specific claim. A relevant detail supports an argument only when the connection is clear.

先说清论点，再解释具体证据为什么支持它；支持不等于完全证明。

How to approach it

Steps for claims-and-evidence questions

1

Restate the claim in your own words

What exactly needs support? (“Weddell lives in a vast world.” “Shells are now worth far less than Vermeers.”) 先用自己的话复述论点。

2

Find where the claim appears and read around it

Evidence is usually in the same sentence or the next few lines. 证据通常就在论点附近。

3

Test each choice: does it directly support the claim?

Ask: “If I only knew this detail, would it make this claim more credible?” Choose the most direct support, not something merely related. 选“最直接”的证据,不选“只是相关”的。

4

For theory questions, decide: support or contradict?

Does the evidence agree with the theory, go against it, or show something mixed? Look for however, if … then. 证据是支持还是反驳理论?

Fact vs. opinion

Telling facts from opinions

Fact claim · 可核查的陈述

The bridge is 300 meters long.

A measurement can test this claim. Calling it factual does not mean it has already been verified.

The scientist predicted that the bridge would last 100 years.

可核查不等于必然真实。“有人作出预测”可以是事实，但预测内容本身尚未被证明。

Opinion / judgment · 评价或判断

The bridge is the most beautiful structure in the city.

“Most beautiful” expresses an evaluation.

The author calls the bridge elegant, while a quoted critic calls it dull.

评价词要结合谁在说、评价什么。不能把引用人物的态度直接当作作者态度。

证据支持程度也要准确：一次结果可能加强主张，却未必证明因果或适用于所有情形。

One more format

Visual and quantitative information (VQI)

What ACT says

On the enhanced ACT, one passage may include a graph, table, or figure. Some questions ask you to read the graphic, or to combine information from the graphic and the text.

新版 ACT 可能有一篇文章附带图表,题目要求读图,或把图表和文字结合起来。

How to approach a graphic

  1. Read the title, labels, units, and key first.
  2. Find the overall trend (rising, falling, highest, lowest).
  3. Find the sentence in the passage that discusses the same data.
  4. Choose an answer supported by both the graphic and the text.

先读标题、坐标、单位;找趋势;再回到文中找对应的句子。

Summary

What to remember about claims and evidence论据题要点

Claim versus evidence

A claim is what the author wants you to accept; evidence is what supports it.

论点是待接受的说法；论据是支持它的信息。

Locate relevant support

Restate the claim, then look for the most direct support — usually close to the claim.

先复述需要证明什么，再寻找直接证据。

Explain the connection

Authors often state the link: “This made us confident that…”, “And he was right:…”.

说清证据为何支持结论，不只说“相关”。

Evaluate the theory

For theories, decide whether the evidence supports or contradicts them.

新证据可能支持、挑战或限定原理论。

Distinguish fact and opinion

Fact: can be checked, stated plainly. Opinion: a judgment (best, dumbest, lucky).

事实陈述可核查，评价是观点；还要区分谁在说。
