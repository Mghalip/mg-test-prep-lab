What is it?

What is a main idea question?

Definition

A main idea question asks about the whole passage: what it is mainly about, what point it makes, or why the author wrote it.

ACT describes this skill as “determine central ideas or themes.” It belongs to Key Ideas & Details.

主旨题问的是“整篇文章”:它主要讲什么、作者想表达什么观点、写作目的是什么。

Three things a question can ask for

Main idea / pointWhat does the author say about the topic?
Main purposeWhy did the author write it? (to explain, to describe, to review…)
Story summaryIn a literary passage: who, what happens, and how the character changes or feels

主旨 = 观点;目的 = 为什么写;文学类 = 人物 + 事件 + 情感变化。

Key concepts

Topic, main idea, and purpose

Topic

What the passage is about — usually a few words.

Crocodiles and the “living fossil” idea

话题:文章讲的对象。

Main idea

What the author says about the topic — a full sentence.

New DNA evidence shows that crocodiles are not living fossils after all.

主旨:作者对这个话题的观点。

Purpose

Why the author wrote it — begins with a verb.

To explain how new research changed scientists’ view of crocodile evolution.

目的:作者写这篇文章想“做什么”。

Example passage: Form F12, Passage IV (“The Rise and Fall of the Living Fossil”). A choice that only names the topic (“crocodiles”) is not enough; the correct main idea also includes the point. 只说出话题的选项不够,正确答案还要包含观点。

Where to look

Where the main idea usually appears

Informational text · 说明文

Old view旧观点
→
New evidence新证据
→
Revised view修正观点
People once thought the island was empty. New excavations revealed homes and tools. The island had supported a community.

Main idea: New evidence shows that people once lived on the island.

首末段和转折常是线索，但主旨必须覆盖全文；不能机械选最后一句。

Literary text · 叙事文

Situation处境
→
Event事件
→
Change变化
Mia feared the audition. Her friend encouraged her. After performing, Mia felt proud.

Summary: A nervous performer gains confidence after facing an audition.

概括人物、核心事件和变化；不必把故事强行改成作者提出的论点。

How to approach it

Four steps for a main idea question

1

After reading, write one sentence

Informational: topic + point. Literary: who + what happens + how they feel. Write it before you read any questions. 读完先用一句话概括。

2

Answer it last in the set

The whole-passage question is easier after you have worked through the detail questions. 全文题可以放在这篇的最后做,那时你对文章更熟悉。

3

Compare each choice with your sentence

The correct choice covers the whole passage, not one paragraph or one example. 正确答案覆盖全文,而不是某一段或某一个例子。

4

Check the beginning and the end

Does the choice fit both the opening and the conclusion? Is the attitude right? 核对开头和结尾,态度也要一致。

Wrong answers

How wrong answers are built in main idea questions

Too narrow

True, but only about one paragraph, one example, or one detail.

太窄:只是某一段或某一个细节。

Too broad

Goes beyond the passage, for example “an overview of plant viruses” for a passage about one kind of tulip.

太宽:超出了文章范围。

Wrong focus

Mentioned in the passage, but not what the passage is mainly about.

偏题:文中提到了,但不是重点。

Distorted

Changes the author’s point, attitude, or strength: proves instead of suggests, always instead of often.

歪曲:观点、态度或程度不对。

Summary

What to remember about main idea questions主旨题要点

Whole-passage scope

Main idea questions ask about the whole passage: main idea, main purpose, or what the story is about.

范围是全文；用“话题＋作者的重点”概括。

One-sentence summary

After reading, write one sentence: topic + point (informational) or who + what + feeling (literary).

文学类概括“人物＋事件＋变化”，说明文概括“话题＋观点”。

Check the structure

The main idea is often in the first and last paragraphs and after turn words like But and However.

首末段与转折是线索，仍需检查其余段落。

Eliminate distortions

Wrong answers are usually too narrow, too broad, off-focus, or distorted.

真实细节也可能太窄；核对范围与态度。

Choose your order

Do the whole-passage question last in the set.

全文题可在更熟悉文章后做，按个人效率安排。

说出本课规则／方法，指出依据，并解释一个错误选项为什么错。
