What is it?

What is a words-in-context question?

Two versions

1 · A single word. “As it is used in line 20, the word put most nearly means:”

2 · A phrase or statement. “When the narrator says ‘every square inch had its drama’ (line 53), he most nearly means that:”

ACT describes this as “determine word and phrase meanings” and “analyze an author’s word choice.” It belongs to Craft & Structure.

两种形式:单个词的意思;一个短语或一句话的意思。

What makes these questions hard

The ACT does not test rare vocabulary. It tests common words with less common meanings:

put→ assign (not “place”)
standard→ accepted (not “normal”)
spring→ activate (not “jump”)
drew on→ referenced (not “sketched”)

ACT 不考生僻词,考的是常见词在语境中的“非常见意思”。官方说明:新版不考脱离语境的词汇。

Phrases and images

Phrases, statements, and literal vs. figurative

Literal or figurative?

Literal: the words mean exactly what they say.

Figurative: the words create an image to express another idea (metaphor, simile, personification).

“redraw the map of how crocodilians evolved in space and time” — no one draws a real map; scientists change the story of crocodile evolution → figurative.

字面 = 字面意思;比喻 = 用画面表达别的意思。

How to explain a phrase

  1. Read the phrase in its full sentence and the sentences around it.
  2. Ask: what is being described? (water, music, a person’s feeling…)
  3. If it is an image, find what quality it points to (a tornado → force; color → beauty).
  4. Choose the plain-English explanation that fits the surrounding lines.

先看它描述的对象,再找“画面”要表达的特征。

Summary

What to remember about words and phrases in context词义与句意题要点

Word or phrase?

Two versions: one word (“As it is used in line…”) and one phrase or statement (“most nearly means that…”).

先读语境，再判断这里需要哪一种意思。

Use contextual meaning

The ACT tests common words with less common meanings. Distrust the everyday meaning.

用自己的简单词替换原词。

Substitute and test

Cover the choices, read the full sentence, use your own word, then substitute each choice.

把选项代回完整句，核对语法、语气和程度。

Explain the image

For phrases: find what is being described, then explain the image in plain English.

比喻先找描述对象，再解释其含义。

Literal or figurative?

Decide literal vs. figurative before reading the explanations.

熟悉的词典义未必符合本句。
